Hikayat & Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa

Prince Diponegoro's Makassar Manuscripts (1838)

Digital archival preservation, 1:1 scan-concordant diplomatic transcription, Latin transliteration, and historical English translations of two major manuscripts composed or dictated by Prince Diponegoro during his exile in Fort Rotterdam, Makassar. Transliterated by Sastrasuganda in 1973.

Hikayat Ratu Tanah Jawa

Treatise (Sufism & Java War recollections)

📘 Original Pegon Manuscript (125 Folios) 📗 1973 Typescript & English Translation (47 Pages)

Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa

Chronicle (Pre-Majapahit History)

📕 Original Pegon Manuscript (90 Folios) 📙 1973 Typescript & English Translation (57 Pages)

Important Note for Local Viewing

To view the transcriptions locally, you must run a local web server to bypass CORS restrictions for fetching the markdown files. From your terminal in this directory, run:

python -m http.server

Then open http://localhost:8000 in your browser.

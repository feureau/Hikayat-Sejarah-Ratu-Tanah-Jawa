The **Alif Mutakalimu Wanid** (The Singular I) is what encompasses everything in this world up to and including the Hereafter, namely the **Alif Mutakalimu Wanid** itself. This **Alif** is truly threefold. The first is the **Alif Nyata Ngazhim** (the Manifest Great Alif), which means the **Alif Nyata Ngazhim** is the **Bā** in ***Bismillah***. This is called the **Bahru Muhit** (Surrounding Ocean), its nature being **Qutb**. The second is the **Alif Mutafawir** (the Wavering Alif), meaning the **Alif Mutafawir** is the **Mīm** in ***Bismillah***, the **Mīm** in ***Bismillah*** being called the **Badaru Alim** (Wise Moon). The **Badaru Alim** means it is the Prophet **Muhammad** *sallallahu 'alaihi wasallam*. The third is the **Alif Tamasur** (the Resembling Alif), meaning this **Alif** is the word **Allah** in ***Bismillah***. The word **Allah** in ***Bismillah*** is called the **Nuqot Ghoib** (Hidden Point). The **Nuqot Ghoib** means it is the Prophet **Adam**, **Khalifatullah** *alaihis salam*. Thus, these three **Alif**s are what manifest the entirety of existence from this world to the Hereafter: **Qur’an, Muhammad, Adam**. These three function as the outward manifestation of the essential nature of the Essence that is **Wajibul Wujud** (Necessary Existent), which is hidden and eternal, unchanging? Yes, it is the **Alif Sjaiun Muhit** (The All-Encompassing Alif). This is the region.

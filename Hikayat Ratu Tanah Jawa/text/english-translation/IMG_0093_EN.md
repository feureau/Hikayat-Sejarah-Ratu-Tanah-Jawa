### [Folio 105]
1. [p.198.] Yes, remember / all descendants of Adam, male and female,
2. because after **Kanjeng Nabi Mohammad Salallahu Ngalaihi Wassalam**,

5. of Adam who uses the title of Prophet, because indeed the Seal of the
6. Prophets is your Prophet all the way to the hereafter, who serves as
7. the outward manifestation of the Necessarily Existent Essence
8. (*zat kang wajibul wujud*) in the world all the way to the hereafter;
9. yes, that is the Seal of the Prophets. Thus, what remains in the
10. world all the way to the Day of Judgment is only faith. /* [margin: iya] */

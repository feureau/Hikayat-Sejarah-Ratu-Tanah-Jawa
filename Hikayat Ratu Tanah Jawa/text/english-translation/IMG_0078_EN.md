## Text

### [Folio 124]
1. *tabtholu akmalakum*." O, remember all you descendants of Adam, you who are Muslims,
2. male and female, towards the evidence of the text in the Qur'an. Meaning that all of you Muslims
3. must have faith in all the commands of Lord Allah Subhanahu Watangala the nguza wajalla
4. (Mighty and Majestic) the wahidul kohhar which are in the text of the Qur'an entirely in
5. [p.109.] the world and / even until the hereafter, and all of you Muslims must have faith
6. in all the words of Lord Prophet Mohammed rosulullah sollallohu ngalaihi wassalam (peace be upon him).
7. Meaning all that is stated in the Hadith. Meaning what is called Hadith is yes,
8. all the books of testament of the /* ^Lord */ Prophet Mohammad Rosulullah sollallahu ngalaihi wasalam,
9. all of you Muslims, male and female, must inevitably carry out
10. all the commands of the hadith of the rosulullah.
11. Then what serves as the rule of the commands of the hadith of the rosul for all of you Muslims
12. [p.110.] in the world is, it is inevitable / that all of you must be great in your repentance,
13. feeling that you have many faults towards the Robbilngalamin, and all of you
14. descendants of Adam who are Muslims remaining in the world must be able
15. to guard all of your *iman* (faith) along with all of your *ngamal* (deeds)
16. for as long as you live in the world; then the meaning
17. of guarding your *iman* is to be very careful not to dare
18. approach all that is forbidden in *iman*. Because if you dare to edge
19. [p.111.] towards the prohibitions / then if you have edged towards those prohibitions,
20. it is truly dangerous; if you have edged towards them, it is as if you are plunging
21. into those prohibitions; if you have plunged into those prohibitions, what will become of you,
22. if you do not remember your *iman*; then the meaning of guarding your *ngamal* in the world
23. is yes, your conduct must be dissolved along with guarding your *nafsu* (desires), /* [X] */
24. meaning *nafsu* is whatever your desires are that align with the commands of the evidence of the hadith,
25. if your desires become mixed with the desires of the iblis laknatullah. Likewise
26. [p.112.] with the speech / of *tekabur* (arrogance) that penetrates the heart, yes that is iblis.
27. If you are arrogant towards fellow Muslims, what will become of you if your forgetfulness
28. is constant and you do not repent for all your forgetfulness, if you do not remember your *iman*,
29. then all your *ngamal* throughout your life in the world will be void.
30. So if it is certain that your *iman* and *ngamal* in the world are void, it is truly impossible
31. for you to be able to return to heaven, for all the descendants of Adam who are Muslims
32. remaining in the world, except those who attain the path
33. [p.113.] of *sirullah* (the secret of Allah), but *iman* and *ngamal* are what serve as / your provisions to return
34. to your glory. So all the descendants of Adam who are Muslims in the world
35. who are perfect are those who are given revelation by the Robbilngalamin as stated
36. in the evidence of the text in the Qur'an: " *Qul lilmukhalifina minal maghribi satadnguna*
37. *ila qaumi ula baksa sadid tukotilu nahum au yuslimun*." O, remember all
38. the descendants of Adam who are Muslims still in the world, what serves as the sign
39. of a Muslim who receives the grace of Lord Allah Subhanahu Watangala the nguza wajalla
40. the wahidulkohar, what has become the sign of the chosen one is firstly

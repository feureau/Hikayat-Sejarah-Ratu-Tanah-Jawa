## Text

### [Folio 31]
1. the Essence of Subhanahu wa Tangala. The fourth is the
2. *zikir* (*remembrance*) of the ten states (*hal*), its name is, if you are about
3. to step onto the valley, the first is your
4. right foot, the second step is your left foot; as for
5. the purpose of walking, it is only three things, the
6. first is walking in congregation (*jamangan*), the second is walking when
7. advised by your relatives who are both *Mukmin* (*believers*) two or
8. dead, the third is walking in the war of Sabilullah (*holy war*), the
9. fifth is the *zikir* of the ten states, its analogy is that you should not
10. mistakenly cast your sight
11. and your hearing toward anything, it is
12. certain that everything does not escape

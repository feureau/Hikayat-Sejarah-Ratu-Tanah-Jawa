## Text

[p. 4.] as for the purpose of walking, it is only three things, the first is walking in congregation (*jamangan*), the second is walking when advised by your relatives who are both *Mukmin* (*believers*) two or dead, the third is walking in the war of Sabilullah (*holy war*), the fifth is the *zikir* of the ten states, its analogy is that you should not mistakenly cast your sight and your hearing toward anything, it is certain

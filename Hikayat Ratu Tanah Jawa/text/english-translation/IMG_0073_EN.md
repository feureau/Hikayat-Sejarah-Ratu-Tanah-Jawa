4. [p.86.] / it is truly based on the teachings of Iblis in his instruction, but
5. it is not the world belonging to Subhanahu Watangala which remains hidden. Meaning in
6. heaven; whereas the world is definitively bounded and cannot be touched by everything,
7. because the world is the intermediate between heaven and hell; as for what is called inward marriage,
8. it involves two matters: first, during descendants (procreation), second, the entering of the spirit into the
9. body; that is why Iblis's desire in his teaching of male and female is that as long as they both
10. desire it, that is called inward marriage, and there is no limit to *ghofarolahum* (may He forgive them) /* (X) */

14. commands of the Rabbil 'Alamin which are already in the Qur'an, all that is commanded.
15. As for the forbidden actions, they are avoided; regarding the matters of the world, one no longer
16. calculates the fortune, pain, or death of the body, for there is none other than Subhanahu Watangala,
17. because this body is one /* (X) */ alike a reward, similarly this material world and

20. [p.88.] the sign of your ultimate fortune or misfortune. / That is the conviction
21. of a human being who already has *amantubillah*, there is none other than Allah who is loved;

29. [p.89.] Subhanahu Watangala the Wahidul Qahhar, as long as / one does not

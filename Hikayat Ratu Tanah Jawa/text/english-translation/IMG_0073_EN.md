        so an inward marriage is the most excellent, as well as eating whatever is your desire, yes that is good because it takes the form of worldly bounty, whatever is your delight, as long as you are not hindered by people. As for such speech,
[p.86.] / it is truly based on the teachings of Iblis in his instruction, but it is not the world belonging to Subhanahu Watangala which remains hidden. Meaning in heaven; whereas the world is definitively bounded and cannot be touched by everything, because the world is the intermediate between heaven and hell; as for what is called inward marriage, it involves two matters: first, during descendants (procreation), second, the entering of the spirit into the body; that is why Iblis's desire in his teaching of male and female is that as long as they both desire it, that is called inward marriage, and there is no limit to *ghofarolahum* (may He forgive them) /* (X) */ As for this chapter explaining the root of acceptance, it is *iman*; the meaning of what is called *iman* is *iman amantubillah*
[p.87.] / not *iman taghiyur*, meaning *iman amantubillah* is already trusting and believing in the commands of the Rabbil 'Alamin which are already in the Qur'an, all that is commanded. As for the forbidden actions, they are avoided; regarding the matters of the world, one no longer calculates the fortune, pain, or death of the body, for there is none other than Subhanahu Watangala, because this body is one /* (X) */ alike a reward, similarly this material world and all its contents, yes it is a reward, while the ones being rewarded by all the contents of the material world are human beings; that serves as
[p.88.] the sign of your ultimate fortune or misfortune. / That is the conviction of a human being who already has *amantubillah*, there is none other than Allah who is loved; whatever is seen, whatever is heard is none other than the wondrous signs of the Rabbil 'Alamin; that is the sight and hearing of the true *Mukmin* (believer), because each and every outward and inward thing is definitively nothing other than the power and will (*qudrat* and *iradat*) of Subhanahu Watangala, the Wahidul Qahhar. As for the conduct in the world, there are three things: *sabar* (patience), *syukur* (gratitude), and *ridha* (contentment); meaning *sabar* is patience when afflicted by trials, meaning *syukur* is gratitude when receiving blessings, meaning *ridha* is contentment towards the decree of Allah, because the conviction is that there is none other than Subhanahu Watangala
[p.89.] the Wahidul Qahhar, as long as one does not / separate from *iman*, meaning *iman* is yes, the commands of the Rabbil 'Alamin which are in the Qur'an, that is its outward manifestation, thus the conduct becomes three things, because one has surrendered entirely and believed in Subhanahu Watangala; that is its form when one is firmly steadfast in *iman*. Because there is no other command besides the Qur'an, therefore it cannot be avoided at all, those three matters belonging to Allah Ta'ala. Furthermore, what serves as the roof: first is *solat* (prayer), second is *zhikir* (remembrance), third is reciting the Qur'an, which serves as the sign that one can be called a human being who firmly
[p.90.] and truly guards the possessions / of Subhanahu Watangala; those three things in the world definitely invoke fear if one opposes them, because they are *af'alullah* (Actions of Allah), while the Qur'an must inevitably be opened every single day, because it is like the body, the board of the Qur'an, while its writing is like its form, while its letters are like the movements of the body, and the sound of the Qur'an is yes, like the life force of the truth of Subhanahu Watangala, *ghofarolahum*; therefore, it is forbidden for you to touch the board of the Qur'an if you have not yet /* [nu] */ purified yourself by taking your *wudlu* (ablution) water, unless there is a severe *'uzur* (valid excuse), because

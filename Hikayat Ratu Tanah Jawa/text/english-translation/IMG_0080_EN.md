## Text

### [Folio 126]
1. [p. 23.] [p.114.] they are unwilling to speak arbitrarily,
2. if there is no result from it towards excellence
3. in the end, their hearts have intended for the destruction of their bodies
4. in the world and become devoted to nothing but whatever comes
5. from those commands, which are in the Arabic language.
6. Meaning the evidence (*dalil*) and *khadits* (hadith), only that which is in their hearts
7. which is seen or doubted by people, and that has become
8. the sign of the descendants of Adam if they receive the grace of the Robbilngalamin.
9. Only those five matters: firstly feeling (*rasa*), secondly courage (*kendel*),
10. thirdly firmness (*mantep*), [p.115.] truthfulness without lying / in all their words,
11. fourthly Islam, fifthly reverence (*taklim*); yes, that is if Lord Allah
12. Subhanahu Watangala the ngaza wajalla the wahidul kohhar wills to give
13. the revelation of *perang sabilullah* (holy war in the path of Allah) which serves as the guide
14. for war for all the descendants of Adam who are Muslims, along with the evidence of the text
15. in the Qur'an: " /* ^Fa */*'in tathing*/* ^u */*-ayuk tikumullahu ajrun akhsana
16. wa in tatawallu kama tu litum minkobli yungi*/* ^yu */*zadkum ngazhaban alima*."
17. O, remember all descendants of Adam who are Muslims, you must cherish
18. the command of that *aprang sabilullah* [p.116.] entirely / you will be even better
19. in the world up to the hereafter, all you descendants of Adam who are Muslims,
20. yes in that *aprang sabilullah*. So if all the descendants of Adam
21. who claim to be Muslims have not yet carried out the *perang sabilullah*,
22. it is truly a lie that they are Muslims, but in truth, if they knew
23. the truest command, this is the true command of Lord Allah Subhanahu Watangala
24. the ngaza wajalla the wahidul kohhar x the evidence of the text in the Qur'an:
25. " *Huwallazhi arsala rosuluhu bilhadu dinil khaq liyuthohiruhu ngalalazhi kullihi*
26. [p.117.] *wakafa billahi sjahidan Muhammadar*/ *rosulullahi wallazhina manganu*
27. *asjaddaa wa ngalal ngafaro ronmaa bainahum taronum rukangisujud yabtaghuna*
28. *fadl lamminallah wa ridlwanan sam ngihim fi' wujuhikumin afsarisujud*
29. *zhalika matsaluhum fitauroti wanatsaluhum filinzil, kazamgu akhroju sjatluhu*
30. *fazronu fastaqnalazna fastawa ngala suggini yakjibuzaronga layazhumullanufa*
31. *rawa ngidullanu llazhima amanu wangamilussolikhati minham maqhfiratun wa ajron ngazhima*."
32. O, remember all you descendants of Adam who are still Muslims, male and female,
33. towards the messenger of Lord Allah Subhanahu Watangala the nguza wajalla
34. [p.118.] the wahidul / kohhar, meaning Lord Prophet Mohammad rosullallah
35. sollallahu ngalaihi wassalam who was sent down to the material world
36. bringing the commands of the Robbilngalamin, and the absolute greatest
37. is the command that was sent down, that is the *kalamullah* (Word of Allah) in its reality,
38. yes the truest command, and surpassing in purity are all those commands.
39. Meaning yes, that is the religion of Islam by the will of Lord Allah Subhanahu Watangala
40. the ng/* ^a */za wajalla the wahidul kohhar, and yes, the Religion of Islam
41. is sufficient for all religions [p.119.] in the world up to the hereafter.
42. Then what serves as / the witness (*sjahid*) of the descent of the religion of Islam to the material world
43. from the l/* ^o */akhil mahfud (Lauhul Mahfuz) is Lord Prophet Mohammad khabibullan ngalaini wassalam
44. who was sent to spread all the commands of the religion of Islam to all the descendants
45. of Adam, humans and jinn, as well as if all the kafir (disbelievers)

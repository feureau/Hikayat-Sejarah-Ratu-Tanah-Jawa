[p. 23.] [p.114.] they are unwilling to speak arbitrarily, if there is no result from it towards excellence in the end, their hearts have intended for the destruction of their bodies in the world and become devoted to nothing but whatever comes from those commands, which are in the Arabic language. Meaning the evidence (*dalil*) and *khadits* (hadith), only that which is in their hearts which is seen or doubted by people, and that has become the sign of the descendants of Adam if they receive the grace of the Robbilngalamin. Only those five matters: firstly feeling (*rasa*), secondly courage (*kendel*), thirdly firmness (*mantep*), [p.115.] truthfulness without lying / in all their words, fourthly Islam, fifthly reverence (*taklim*); yes, that is if Lord Allah Subhanahu Watangala the ngaza wajalla the wahidul kohhar wills to give the revelation of *perang sabilullah* (holy war in the path of Allah) which serves as the guide for war for all the descendants of Adam who are Muslims, along with the evidence of the text in the Qur'an: " /* ^Fa */*'in tathing*/* ^u */*-ayuk tikumullahu ajrun akhsana wa in tatawallu kama tu litum minkobli yungi*/* ^yu */*zadkum ngazhaban alima*." O, remember all descendants of Adam who are Muslims, you must cherish the command of that *aprang sabilullah* [p.116.] entirely / you will be even better in the world up to the hereafter, all you descendants of Adam who are Muslims, yes in that *aprang sabilullah*. So if all the descendants of Adam who claim to be Muslims have not yet carried out the *perang sabilullah*, it is truly a lie that they are Muslims, but in truth, if they knew the truest command, this is the true command of Lord Allah Subhanahu Watangala the ngaza wajalla the wahidul kohhar x the evidence of the text in the Qur'an: " *Huwallazhi arsala rosuluhu bilhadu dinil khaq liyuthohiruhu ngalalazhi kullihi* [p.117.] *wakafa billahi sjahidan Muhammadar*/ *rosulullahi wallazhina manganu asjaddaa wa ngalal ngafaro ronmaa bainahum taronum rukangisujud yabtaghuna fadl lamminallah wa ridlwanan sam ngihim fi' wujuhikumin afsarisujud zhalika matsaluhum fitauroti wanatsaluhum filinzil, kazamgu akhroju sjatluhu fazronu fastaqnalazna fastawa ngala suggini yakjibuzaronga layazhumullanufa rawa ngidullanu llazhima amanu wangamilussolikhati minham maqhfiratun wa ajron ngazhima*." O, remember all you descendants of Adam who are still Muslims, male and female, towards the messenger of Lord Allah Subhanahu Watangala the nguza wajalla [p.118.] the wahidul / kohhar, meaning Lord Prophet Mohammad rosullallah sollallahu ngalaihi wassalam who was sent down to the material world bringing the commands of the Robbilngalamin, and the absolute greatest is the command that was sent down, that is the *kalamullah* (Word of Allah) in its reality, yes the truest command, and surpassing in purity are all those commands. Meaning yes, that is the religion of Islam by the will of Lord Allah Subhanahu Watangala the ng/* ^a */za wajalla the wahidul kohhar, and yes, the Religion of Islam is sufficient for all religions [p.119.] in the world up to the hereafter.

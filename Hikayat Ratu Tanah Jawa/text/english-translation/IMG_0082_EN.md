2. serving as its deed in this world, without following whatever becomes [p.136.] the desire
3. of animals. /* [(X)] */ As for the meaning of *zakat* (alms), it is purifying one's intention each time after
4. the obligatory fasting in the month of Ramadan, which serves as a deed in this world by humbling oneself
5. toward fellow Muslims—in walking, sitting, and speaking. If toward fellow Muslims, it is certain
6. that one must humble oneself; but toward the cursed ones (*laknatullah*), it is certain
7. that one must not be willing to be humbled even a little; this is the true nature of a Muslim. /* [(X)] */
8. As for the meaning of *hajj* (pilgrimage), it is bringing one's body to Mecca, because that is the command of
9. **Subhanahu Watangala** (Glory be to Him and Exalted is He) and the truth; this is the perfection of what is called the pillar of Islam, [p.137.]
10. the five matters. However, regarding the *hajj*, there are three kinds of summons /
11. First, the summons of the Prophet; second, the summons of the Angels; third, the summons of the cursed *Iblis* (Satan).
12. The meaning of performing the *hajj* by the summons of the Prophet is that arriving in Mecca and returning home remain
13. exactly the same in holding firmly to the command of Islam in behavior; this is the one whose Islam is perfect.
14. As for what serves as the deed in this world, it is gratitude and acting for the sake of **Allah** in all actions,
15. continuously outward and inward. As for the *hajj* that receives the summons of the Angels,
16. the meaning is that immediately after the *hajj*, one dies in Mecca, or dies on the journey before reaching Mecca; [p.138.]
17. that death is indeed the same / in the perfection of its foundation, its purpose, and its intention, but
18. outwardly there are differences. As for the *hajj* that receives the summons of *Iblis*,
19. the meaning is arriving in Mecca, and after returning to one's own country, becoming very arrogant,
20. because one feels one's self is perfect, and there is no one who equals them except their fellow
21. *hajj* pilgrims. Although the command of the **Robbilngalamin** (Lord of the Worlds) regarding the five obligatory
22. pillars of Islam has been fulfilled, as for what serves as the deed in this world, it only becomes a cause for pride,
23. and so all of their religion is ruined, *Insya Allah* (God willing), if such is their belief; that is [p.139.]
24. the form of / *Iblis* clearly manifesting as a human being, the form of Satan called *maknowiya*,
25. because they will infect those who do not guard their faith with their speech. That is the subtle
26. manifestation of the power of **Subhanahu Watangala** in this world, and furthermore, both of them are eternal (*kadim*) and unchanging.
27. The meaning of faith and Satan: faith is the outward manifestation of knowledge, while Satan is the outward manifestation of intellect. /* [(X)] */

As for the meaning of *hajj* (pilgrimage), it is bringing one's body to Mecca, because that is the command of
**Subhanahu Watangala** (Glory be to Him and Exalted is He) and the truth; this is the perfection of what is called the pillar of Islam,
[p.137.] the five matters. However, regarding the *hajj*, there are three kinds of summons.
First, the summons of the Prophet; second, the summons of the Angels; third,
the summons of the cursed *Iblis* (Satan). The meaning of performing the *hajj* by the summons of the
Prophet is that arriving in Mecca and returning home remain exactly the same in holding firmly to the command of
Islam in behavior; this is the one whose Islam is perfect. As for what serves as
the deed in this world, it is gratitude and acting for the sake of **Allah** in all actions,
continuously outward and inward. As for the *hajj* that receives the summons of the Angels, the meaning
is that immediately after the *hajj*, one dies in Mecca, or dies on the journey before reaching Mecca;
[p.138.] that death is indeed the same / in the perfection of its foundation, its purpose, and its intention, but
outwardly there are differences. As for the *hajj* that receives the summons of
*Iblis*, the meaning is arriving in Mecca, and after returning to one's own country,
becoming very arrogant, because one feels one's self is perfect, and there is
no one who equals them except their fellow *hajj* pilgrims. Although the command of the
**Robbilngalamin** (Lord of the Worlds) regarding the five obligatory pillars of Islam has been fulfilled, as for
what serves as the deed in this world, it only becomes a cause for pride, and so
all of their religion is ruined, *Insya Allah* (God willing), if such is their belief; that is
[p.139.] the form of / *Iblis* clearly manifesting as a human being, the form of Satan called *maknowiya*,
because they will infect those who do not guard their faith with their speech. That is
the subtle manifestation of the power of **Subhanahu Watangala** in this world, and furthermore,
both of them are eternal (*kadim*) and unchanging. The meaning of faith and Satan: faith is the outward manifestation of
knowledge, while Satan is the outward manifestation of intellect. /* [(X)] */ A reminder: the poor one **Ngabdulhamid**
gathered in the palace of the *kadipaten* (duchy) on the night of Friday Wage, after

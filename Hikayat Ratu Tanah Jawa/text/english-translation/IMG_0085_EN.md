### [Folio 131]
1. - 28 -
2. **Robbilngalamin**; all that exists in the world up to the hereafter, yes, [p.153.]
3. the *Ba* in *Bismillah* / is what is called the *nuqot ghoib* (hidden point). Thus,
4. manifesting the *nuqot ghoib* is called the *nuqot nurullah* (point of divine light). Yes, the *nuqot nurullah* is the *Mim*
5. of *Bismillah*. Yes, the *Mim* of *Bismillah* serves as the container for all the majesty
6. of **Subhanahu Watangala**, and serves as the seed for all
7. created beings (*mahluq*) in the world and the hereafter. There is nothing else
8. that serves as the seed except the *ayamu nurullah*. The meaning of the *Mim* of *nurullah* is **Kanjeng Nabi kita
9. Mohammad Salallahu Ngalaihi Wassalam**, but all of that *Mim* is still hidden. [p.154]
10. The meaning of being still hidden is that the form of that *Mim* has not yet been clearly revealed in each /
11. individual entity, nor the names of all those *Mim*. Thus, the outward manifestation of all those *Mim*
12. is the clear revelation of the form of each *Mim* and the name of each entity; all the *Mim*, everything that exists
13. in the world and everything that exists in the hereafter, emerges from the word of **Gusti Allah
14. Subhanahu Watangala**, the Exalted and Majestic, the *Wahidul Kohhar*, in the explicit text (*dalil nas*) of the Quran:
15. *kun fayakun* ("Be, and it is"). Thus, the form of each *Mim* in the world immediately becomes manifest,
16. as well as all the *Mim* that are certain to become the equipment of the hereafter. All of it is instantly
17. perfect, nothing is lacking from all the *Mim* in the world up to [p.155.] the hereafter /
18. all equipment. The meaning of the word *Allah* in the *Bismillah* is the *Alif mutakalimu wahid*
19. (the Alif speaking as the One). Yes, the *Alif* of the *Bismillah* that becomes the word *Allah* is
20. *Allahuwa-sjaiun mukhith* (Allah encompasses all things). The word *Allah* in *Bismillah* is merely used for mentioning
21. the *Alif mutakalimu wahid* in *Bismillah*, because it is what determines existence and negates non-existence,
22. as it can no longer be conceptualized in any way. Yes, the *Alif mutakalimu wahid*
23. in *Bismillah* has no place, no shape, no color, no children, nor is it begotten.
24. It stands by its own will, great [p.156.] without being made high, not /
25. overshadowed, and glorious without being named. It is miraculous without being sought,
26. becoming whatever it wills without being forced. It is one (*ahad*), unique (*wahid*), living without dying,
27. eternal without changing, powerful without instruments. Yes, the *Alif sjaiun muhith* is one (*ahat*),
28. unique (*wahid*), only forgiving (*ghofur*). The attribute of the *Alif mutakalim wahid* is *Allahuwa sjaiun muhith*.
29. The meaning of *Allahuwa sjaiun muhith* is that it already encompasses (*muhith*) from the world to the hereafter;
30. it is not manifest like all creations (*mahluq*); thus, the Creator (*kholiq*) only encompasses from the world up
31. to the hereafter. Thus, the [p.157.] *Alif mutakalimu wahid* in *Bismillah* that serves as the reflection /
32. of the *Alif mutakalimu wahid* in *Bismillah* is called *Alif tamasur*. This is the outward manifestation of
33. the word *Allah* in *Bismillah*. The word *Allah* in *Bismillah* is that which serves as the reflection of the attribute
34. of the Necessarily Existent Essence (*zhat kang wajibul wujud*). The meaning is **Kanjeng Nabi Adam Kholifatullah Ngalaihissalam**;
35. yes, that is the outward manifestation of the attribute of the *Alif tamasur* in *Bismillah*, the word *Allah*.
36. Yes, that word is what serves as the seed for all those possessing human nature. Yes, it is **Kanjeng Nabi Adam Kholifatullah
37. Ngalaihissalam** who serves as the seed for all those possessing human nature, who serves as the reflection
38. of the hidden *sifatullah* (attribute of God), [p.158.] acknowledging the replacement of **Kanjeng Nabi Adam Ngalaihissalam** /

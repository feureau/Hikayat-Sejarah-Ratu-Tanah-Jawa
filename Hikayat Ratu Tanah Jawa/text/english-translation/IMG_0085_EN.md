**Robbilngalamin**; all that exists in the world up to the hereafter, yes,
[p.153.] the *Ba* in *Bismillah* / is what is called the *nuqot ghoib* (hidden point). Thus, manifesting
the *nuqot ghoib* is called the *nuqot nurullah* (point of divine light). Yes, the *nuqot nurullah*
is the *Mim* of *Bismillah*. Yes, the *Mim* of *Bismillah* serves as the container
for all the majesty of **Subhanahu Watangala**,
and serves as the seed for all created beings (*mahluq*)
in the world and the hereafter. There is nothing else that serves as the seed
except the *ayamu nurullah*. The meaning of the *Mim* of *nurullah* is **Kanjeng Nabi kita Moh-
ammad Salallahu Ngalaihi Wassalam**, but all of that *Mim* is still hidden.
[p.154] The meaning of being still hidden is that the form of that *Mim* has not yet been clearly revealed in each /
individual entity, nor the names of all those *Mim*. Thus, the outward manifestation of all those *Mim* is the clear
revelation of the form of each *Mim* and the name of each entity; all the
*Mim*, everything that exists in the world and everything that exists in the hereafter,
emerges from the word of **Gusti Allah Subhanahu Watangala**, the Exalted
and Majestic, the *Wahidul Kohhar*, in the explicit text (*dalil nas*) of the Quran: *kun fayakun* ("Be, and it is").
Thus, the form of each *Mim* in the world immediately becomes manifest,
as well as all the *Mim* that are certain to become the equipment of the hereafter.
All of it is instantly perfect, nothing is lacking from all the *Mim* in the world up to
[p.155.] the hereafter / all equipment. The meaning of the word *Allah* in the
*Bismillah* is the *Alif mutakalimu wahid* (the Alif speaking as the One). Yes, the *Alif* of the *Bismillah*
that becomes the word *Allah* is *Allahuwa-sjaiun mukhith* (Allah encompasses all things). The word *Allah* in
*Bismillah* is merely used for mentioning the *Alif mutakalimu wahid*
in *Bismillah*, because it is what determines existence and negates
non-existence, as it can no longer be conceptualized in any way. Yes, the *Alif mutakalimu
wahid* in *Bismillah* has no place, no shape, no color, no
children, nor is it begotten. It stands by its own will, great
[p.156.] without being made high, not / overshadowed, and glorious
without being named. It is miraculous without being sought, becoming whatever it wills
without being forced. It is one (*ahad*), unique (*wahid*), living without dying, eternal
without changing, powerful without instruments. Yes, the *Alif sjaiun muhith* is one (*ahat*), unique (*wahid*),
only forgiving (*ghofur*). The attribute of the *Alif mutakalim wahid* is *Allahuwa sjaiun
muhith*. The meaning of *Allahuwa sjaiun muhith* is that it already encompasses (*muhith*)
from the world to the hereafter; it is not manifest like all creations (*mahluq*); thus, the Creator (*kholiq*)
only encompasses from the world up to the hereafter. Thus, the *Alif muta-
[p.157.] -kalimu wahid* in *Bismillah* that serves as the reflection / of the *Alif muta-
kalimu wahid* in *Bismillah* is called *Alif tamasur*. This is the outward manifestation of the
word *Allah* in *Bismillah*. The word *Allah* in *Bismillah* is that which
serves as the reflection of the attribute of the Necessarily Existent Essence (*zhat kang wajibul wujud*). The meaning is
**Kanjeng Nabi Adam Kholifatullah Ngalaihissalam**; yes, that is the outward manifestation of the attribute of the
*Alif tamasur* in *Bismillah*, the word *Allah*. Yes, that word is what serves as
the seed for all those possessing human nature. Yes, it is **Kanjeng Nabi
Adam Kholifatullah Ngalaihissalam** who serves as the seed for all those
possessing human nature, who serves as the reflection of the hidden *sifatullah* (attribute of God),
[p.158.] acknowledging the replacement of **Kanjeng Nabi Adam Ngalaihissalam** / for **Gusti
Allah Subhanahu Watangala**, the Exalted and Majestic, the *Wahidul Kohhar*. Thus,

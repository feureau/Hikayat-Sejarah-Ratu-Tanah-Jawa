13. for people going to the market if they do not bring merchandise, or if people sail without
14. /* ^badoh */ a compass? Thus, if people go to the market without bringing merchandise,
15. they are truly foolish, if they hope to trade and get something in return, because in the market, all kinds of beautiful *Mim*

20. for the safety of such a person. /* ^badoh */ Likewise, for people who sail,

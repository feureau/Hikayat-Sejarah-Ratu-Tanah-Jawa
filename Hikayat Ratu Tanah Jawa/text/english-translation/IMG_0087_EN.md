if they hope to trade and get something in return, because in the market, all kinds of
beautiful *Mim* are complete. So if people just stroll into the market without
bringing anything, what is the point? This is unless the person does not desire to see
[p.166.] the beautiful *Mim* / and has no desire to buy or trade.
There is nothing to trade; what is the point if the person does not want to see
or grasp them? Thus, if a person wants to see and grasp them, it is truly impossible
to hope for the safety of such a person. /* ^badoh */
Likewise, for people who sail, if they do not use a compass, they are truly foolish
if they expect their boat to find safety. Inevitably, it will become
aimless. For the boat to be safe and not wrecked, it is certain that if it is
[p.167.] confused, it will not be able to return home to its house / it is truly foolish.
If they are not warned, all descendants of Adam who have become like that
towards the cursed *Iblis*—meaning the cursed infidels, the descendants of
Adam in the world who act like that—it is truly foolish for them to think they can
return to their glory again. Yes, remember, all descendants of Adam, male
or female, above all, be /* [sakehezxhuriyat] */ vigilant in
the world towards all the *Mim* that become the body of the cursed *Iblis*.
Whatever is wondrous in the world, it will be destroyed. Above all, do not be amazed by it,

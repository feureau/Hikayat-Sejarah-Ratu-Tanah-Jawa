        Watangala who is 'Azza wa Jalla (Mighty and Majestic) and Wahidul Qahhar (The One, the Subduer), that is without
[p.73.] difference / for the *Wali* and *Mukmin*. Thus before the Qur'an was taken back, its writing came to the material world, because what serves as the witness of the Qur'an, is the *Wali* and the *Mukmin* after the Prophet, by the will of Lord Allah Subhanahu Watangala who is 'Azza wa Jalla and Wahidul Qahhar, with the intention of being commanded to witness, yes the *Wali* and *Mukmin* for all that is in the world, the bad and the good, belonging to the Rabbil 'Alamin (Lord of the Worlds). Then the sign that the end of the age of the material world is approaching is that the structure will change, meaning
[p.74.] the world will change / it is not eternal, there is nothing that cannot undergo changes, all of it means that when it is near for the world of the Qur'an to have its writing taken back to the Lauhul Mahfuz (Preserved Tablet), the *Wali* and *Mukmin* have all returned to heaven, and none remain in the world. Thus the descendants of Adam who still remain in the world are all the wicked ones. It means those who become the cursed disbelievers (*kafir laknatullah*), their bodies have all become like animals, and what serves as the soul for the cursed disbelievers is
[p.75.] the cursed Iblis (Satan). Yes, that is the sign when the world approaches its end, all that is good and right will have been taken back to the Rabbil 'Alamin, all that was in the world has been made to vanish entirely back to Lord Allah Subhanahu Watangala who is 'Azza
[p.76.] wa Jalla and Wahidul Qahhar /* (X) */ /* (X) */ /* (X) */ / That is the *Nabi*, the *Wali*, the *Mukmin*; that is why each consists of three adornments, /* [i] */ because the *Nabi*, *Wali*, and *Mukmin*, their three manifestations are truly one, equally serving as the *kastuba* building made as the shadow of the unseen *Zatullah* (Essence of Allah). Then outwardly, the *nuqot nurullah* (point of divine light) becomes the two Kalimah Shahadat, which form the seed of *iman* (faith), formed like this /* [box: 0] */ Alif serves as the *iman amantubillah* (faith in believing in Allah), meaning *iman amantubillah* is the *iman maksum* (infallible faith), *maksum* meaning protected, meaning the Prophets, even before they were born, were already endowed with *iman amantubillah*, which is why they are far from all sins,
[p.77.] great or small. / Shin means shin is what becomes the *iman hidayat* (guided faith), meaning *iman hidayat* is the faith of the *Wali*, who receive guidance to enter into *iman amantubillah* after they boldly destroy their worldly desires. Ha means it is what becomes the *iman makbul* (accepted faith) and *iman taghiyur* (changing faith), which is why ha represents two faiths, *makbul* and *taghiyur*, because it is an empty space (*awang-uwung*), meaning an empty space is knowing the bottom and the top, meaning the top is towards the void, the bottom is towards existence. Dal means Dal is what becomes the *iman mardud* (rejected faith), meaning
[p.78.] *iman mardud* belongs / to all the cursed disbelievers, because Dal means the world (*donya*), which is why if *iman taghiyur* regresses, it becomes *mardud*, and if it progresses, it becomes *makbul*, because those two faiths are balanced by the will of Subhanahu Watangala to be the conclusion, *iman maksum* and *iman mardud*; meaning *iman maksum* is Lord Prophet Adam 'Alaihissalam (peace be upon him), yes *iman amantubillah*, that is the meaning of *iman maksum*. The meaning of *iman mardud* is the cursed Iblis, who definitely holds the *mardud* faith. As for the faith whose existence is certain, there are only three; *iman maksum* belongs to Lord Prophet Adam 'Alaihissalam, starting
[p.79.] with all / the Prophets because all the Prophets were endowed with prophetic light; while they were still unseen, the Prophets were already destined to have *iman maksum*. As for *iman taghiyur*, it is destined to belong to all the children of Adam, because all the children of Adam have two paths, half becoming inhabitants of heaven

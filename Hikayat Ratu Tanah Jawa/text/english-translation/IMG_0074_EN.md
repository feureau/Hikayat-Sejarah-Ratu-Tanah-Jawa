        thus it is called Insan Kamil, which serves as the building that manifests the possessions of the Rabbil 'Alamin which are hidden entirely within the Qur'an, meaning the *Nabi*, *Wali*, and *Mukmin*. As for the explanation of the difference between the Qur'an and the scriptures that likewise sent down one hundred boards, they are included in the Qur'an, the possession of Subhanahu Watangala, the true one, yes the Qur'an is what serves as the mother of the one hundred and three, which is why the Qur'an was the last to be revealed, because the will of Subhanahu Watangala made it a sign that its core is complete. As for the explanation of the difference
[p.92.] between the Qur'an and the one hundred and three scriptures: "Bismillah", yes Bismillah is the Wahidul Qahhar, which is why /* [every] */ ^every *surah* uses Bismillah in the Qur'an, ____________ which serves as the sign to utter the name of Subhanahu Watangala, the Wahidul Qahhar, which is why every *surah* uses Bismillah in the Qur'an, meaning the *surah* is yes, the form of the command of the Rabbil 'Alamin to all the *ummah* (community) of Lord Prophet Muhammad s.m./* ^a */, male believers and female believers, who have been endowed with *iman*, which is why it is obligatory (*fardu*) for the board of the Qur'an to be kept well because /
[p.93.] so that you remember that the root of each and every thing is why the letters of the Qur'an use large, small, long, short, thick, and thin; that is the sign of the forms of all creation, as well as the ranks of each and every thing; long and short refer to age, thick and thin refer to sin, *tasjid* (tashdid) refers to severe illness; that is the sign that it cannot be avoided, each and every thing is definitely the path of death or destruction for all creation in the world, therefore be sure not to forget the Qur'an every single day
[p.94.] because the Qur'an is what serves as the outward manifestation of the *sjahadat* (shahadat), therefore / the form of the *sjahadat* is not plainly visible because it is the seed of all knowledge, then when the seed is planted, its form is not seen; when it grows, what is seen is the stem along with its fruit and its taste /* [sjahadat is it, zhikir,] */; while what serves as the stem of the *sjahadat* is *solat*, what serves as the fruit of the *sjahadat* is *zhikir*, what serves as the taste of the *sjahadat* is the Qur'an, but those three are the manifest forms of the stem, fruit, and taste, which are seen first by sight; if the stem is yes, the five daily prayers, that is the form of the stem of knowledge, while its fruit is the *zhikir* of the Kalimah Toyibah, that is the form
[p.95.] of the fruit of knowledge; while its taste is the Qur'an, which / serves as the taste of knowledge, its form is because the taste is *iman*, meaning *iman* is the command of the Qur'an, therefore it cannot be said that the forms of the taste and the stem, the seed and its fruit, are two; it is certain that they are the same form. While their gathering is in the Insan Kamil, its manifestation serves as the outward manifestation of *sifatullah* (Attributes of Allah), while the actions of the Insan Kamil serve as the outward manifestation of *af'alullah*, while the commands of the Insan Kamil serve as the outward manifestation of *zhatullah*, that means Lord Prophet Muhammad Rasulullah s.m./* ^a */, who is called the Insan Kamil, serves as the outward manifestation of ________________
[p.96.] all the attributes of knowledge, likewise / the manifestation of the body of the *Mukmin* and the board of the Qur'an are truly two /* [exist] */ yet not two, one yet not one, the board of the Qur'an and the body of the *Mukmin*, which is why all *Mukmin* cannot be separated at all from the board of the Qur'an, because the board of the Qur'an serves as the body of *iman*, while all the commands within the Qur'an are what is called *iman*; so if you perform the prayer, the board of the Qur'an is not visible because it has transferred to become your body which serves as the board of the Qur'an, because all
[p.97.] the recitations of the *solat* are nothing other than the Qur'an until the perfection of the *solat* is reached, meaning the *zhikir* of the Kalimah / Toyibah

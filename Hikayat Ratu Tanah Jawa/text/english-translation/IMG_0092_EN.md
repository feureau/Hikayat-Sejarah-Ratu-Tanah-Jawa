**Subhanahu Watangala**, the Exalted and Majestic, the *Wahidul Kohhar*, willed to
perfect all His will in the world. The meaning of perfecting
all His will is because all the commands of the **Robbil-
ngalamin** were perfected. All of the one hundred and three Books were abrogated (*mansukh*),
[p.193.] meaning they were abrogated / because they were no longer used for any commands
contained within the one hundred and three Books, which would only serve as a shield
for the cursed *Iblis*, because what was sent down from the *Lauhil
Mahfudh* at the very end was the Quran. The meaning of the Quran is the true
*Kalamullah* (Word of God). The meaning of the *Kalamullah* is all the will and commands of the **Robbilngalamin**;
everything is already contained within the Quran: the beginning, the end,
the outward, the inward. It is absolutely certain that the Quran is the mother of all the Books that
had been sent down before, the one hundred and three Books. Then the will of **Gusti Allah
Subhanahu Watangala**, the Exalted and Majestic, the *Wahidul Kohhar*, sent a Messenger
[p.194.] / to the world to clearly explain the *Kalamullah* in the world to
all the descendants of Adam, both Jinn and humans. If there were any who dared to
oppose the commands of the *Kalamullah*, they were then ordered to be killed, yes,
that is the manifestation of the cursed *Iblis*. Thus, the descent of **Kanjeng Nabi Mohammad
Habibullah Mustafa Solallahu Ngalaihi Wasalam**, who was sent down
himself to the world, served as the seal for all
the Prophets. That is why after **Nabi Ngisa**, it was forbidden in the world for all descendants of Adam who were Muslims
[p.195.] to hold the title of Prophet, because **Gusti Allah Subhanahu /
Watangala**, the Exalted and Majestic, the *Wahidul Kohhar*, would send the Prophet of the End Times (*Nabi akir jaman*), who is truly the beginning,
the outward, and the inward; indeed, there are no longer two. Even more so, after **Nabi
Ngisa**, if there was anyone who had a child and named him Mohammad, he would immediately
die. Yes, that was the sign that it was forbidden, because the descent of the
Seal of the Prophets (*Kanjeng Nabi Panutub*) to the world was the will of the **Robbilngalamin**;
he was caused to experience being part of the descendants of Adam. The Seal of the Prophets was made
to serve simultaneously as a guide for all the *Mim* in the world possessing clear attributes.
In the world, it is certain that all the *Mim* in the world are caused
[p.196.] to experience infancy and are / caused to experience death. So when
**Kanjeng Nabi kita Mohammad Kabilullah kang Mustafa Solollahu
Ngalaihi Wassalam** stood in the world, he then commanded all the
commands of **Gusti Allah Subhanahu Watangala**, the Exalted and Majestic, the *Wahidul
Kohhar*, all that is stated within the Quran, to the Jinn
and all humans in the world all the way to the hereafter.
Anything else was forbidden from being a command besides the Quran. Then, after all
the commands of the **Robbilngalamin** contained within the Quran were evenly spread
to all the *Mim* in the world, as well as those already in the hereafter,
[p.197.] in heaven / or in hell—when faith had already stood in the world
all the way to the hereafter—the will of **Gusti Allah
Subhanahu Watangala**, the Exalted and Majestic, the *Wahidul Kohhar*, no longer changed. So after
all the commands were evenly spread, **Kanjeng Nabi Mohammad Habibullah kang
Mustafa Solollahu Ngalaihi Wassalam** was then summoned back to
His mercy (*rokmatullah*). Yes, he was caused to pass away (*mupud*), serving as a sign
that the final infant had died. Thus, what still remains in the world is

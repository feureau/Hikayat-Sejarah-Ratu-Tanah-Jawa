[p.143.] / In the world (*ngalam donya*), yes, in the world, its form is the *naraka wel* (the abyss of hell); then
there is none other than **Kanjeng Nabi Adam Ngalaihissalam** who serves as
the seed for all those possessing human nature. Thus, all
those possessing human nature emerge into the world—yes,
into the world called *naraka wel*. The reason the world is called *naraka wel*
is because it is only made as a brief trial for faith.
The meaning of what is called faith is that **Kanjeng Nabi Adam** and *Iblis*
are compared by the will of **Gusti Allah Subhanahu Watangala** the Exalted
and Majestic (*ngaza wajalla*), the *Wahidul Kohhar*, with the will to manifest and see
[p.144.] His / attributes, which are four matters: *jalal* (majesty), *jamal* (beauty), *kohhar* (dominance), and *kamal* (perfection).
Therefore, the essence of the *naraka wel* is the outward manifestation of **Kanjeng Nabi Adam**,
the Caliph of Allah (*khalifatullah*), peace be upon him, compared against the cursed *Iblis*
in the world. The meaning of the world is the realm of destruction, which
is why **Kanjeng Nabi Adam Ngalaihissalam** experienced the shame of being subjected
to wrath. He was then cast down from heaven to the *naraka wel*
because he ate the fruit of eternity (*khuldi*) in heaven, which
would become the seed for all forms of sustenance (*rizqi*) in the world. Thus,
[p.145.] **Kanjeng Nabi Adam** was named /* ^al */ / _______________________
*Bismillahir-ahmanirohim albab*. As for this chapter, it is the explanation of the majesty of **Gusti
Allah Subhanahu Watangala**, the Exalted and Majestic, the *Wahidul Kohhar*. What
is called the universe (*ngalam*) is none other than the Quran, yes, the Quran that is
called *majmu mukhith* (the encompassing collection), because the Quran is the *kalamullah* (Word of God), the will of **Gusti
Allah Subhanahu Watangala**, the Exalted and Majestic, the *Wahidul Kohhar*. The Quran
[p.146.] is made / as faith. The meaning of faith being made as the foundation (*panggeran*)
for the commands of the **Robbilngalamin** from the world all the way to the hereafter:
what is true, what is false, what is evil, what is good, what is fortunate, what is unfortunate,
from the world to the hereafter—all of this is the Quran, which serves as the foundation for the commands of the **Robbilngalamin**.
The beginning, the end, the outward, the inward, all the way to the Day of Judgment (*kiyamat*);
there is no longer any will of **Gusti Allah Subhanahu Watangala**, the Exalted and Majestic,
[p.147.] the *Wahidul Kohhar*, commanding / anything other than the Quran. Therefore, it is
not allowed to be touched or read if one has not taken ablution water (*banyu wulud/wudlu*)
to purify oneself, because the Quran is the seed of extraordinary purity, unmixed. Yes, the Quran
is the outward manifestation of the *zhatullah* (Essence of God). The hidden will of the **Robbilngalamin**, the Quran,
is made as the sun in the *ngalam kabir* (macrocosm). The meaning of the *ngalam kabir*
is the hearts of humans, encompassing three matters, namely: Prophet (*Nabi*), Saint (*Wali*),
and Believer (*Mukmin*). Therefore, the world of the Prophet, Saint, and Believer becomes bright, no longer subject to
obscurity

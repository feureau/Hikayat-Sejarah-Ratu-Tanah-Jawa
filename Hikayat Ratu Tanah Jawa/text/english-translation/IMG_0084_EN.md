**Kanjeng Nabi Sjis Ngalaihissalam** (Seth) was given fifty Books;
**Kanjeng Nabi Idris Ngalaihissalam** (Enoch) was given thirty Books;
**Kanjeng Nabi Ibrahim Kholilullah Ngalaihissalam** (Abraham, Friend of God) was given ten
Books; **Kanjeng Nabi Musa Kalamullah Ngalaihissalam** (Moses, Word of God) was given one

llah Ibnu Marjam Ngalaihissalam** (Jesus, Spirit of God, Son of Mary) was given one Book named
[p.150.] the Gospel (*Injil*). **Kanjeng Nabi Mo- / Mohammad Khabibullah Solallahu Ngalaihissalam** (Muhammad, Beloved of God, peace be upon him),
the chosen one (*mustafa*), served as the seal of all the Prophets who descended to
the world in the end times. He was given the title of the Prophet of the End Times (*Nabi akir jaman*),
the beginning, the end, the outward, the inward; it is certain that
there are not two in the world or the hereafter, because the full moon of the universe, the **Kanjeng Nabi**,
was given one Book named the Quran. Yes, the Quran is the seed
of the one hundred and three Books. The one hundred and three Books descended
to the world from the Preserved Tablet (*Lauhil Mahfudh*). All of them, the Angel
**Jabarail Ngalaihissalam** (Gabriel) was commanded to deliver to the Prophets who
[p.151.] were given the Books. That is why / if the one hundred and three Books
are read or touched using purification, it is merely preferable (*utama*), because
they are only entrusted and involve an intermediary in their descent to
the world from the *Lauhil Mahfudh*. Yes, the Angel **Jabarail Ngalaihissalam** was
commanded by **Gusti Allah Subhanahu Watangala**, the Exalted and Majestic,
the *Wahidul Kohhar*. As for the descent of the Quran from the *Lauhil
Mahfudh* to the world to **Kanjeng Nabi Mohammad Solall-
ahu Ngalaihi Wassalam**, it did not use the Angel as an intermediary, so it was already
*kalamullah* (the Word of God). On the contrary, that is why it is *haram* (forbidden) to touch or read the
[p.152.] Quran / if one's body is not yet pure. The Quran has thirty *juz'* (parts).
These thirty *juz'* serve as the seed for all the one hundred and three Books.
As for what serves as the seed for the Quran, it is the *Fatekah* (Al-Fatihah).
What serves as the seed for the *Fatekah* is the *Bismillah*. What serves /* [k] */
as the seed for the *Bismillah* is the *Ba* in *Bismillah*. As for the *Ba* in *Bismillah*,
it is the will of **Gusti Allah Subhanahu Watangala**, the Exalted and Majestic, the *Wa-
hidul Kohhar*, which serves as the seed for all the majestic knowledge of the
**Robbilngalamin**.

## Text

### [Folio 130]
1. - 27 -
2. obscurity regarding all the majesty of **Subhanahu Watangala** in their sight,
3. from the world all the way to the hereafter. Therefore, anyone who is not penetrated by the Quran, [p.148.]
4. it is certain that their world is dark, // so they do not see the majesty of the **Robbilngalamin**
5. in all its beautiful manifestations. All of this means it is certain their sight is blurred
6. from the world to the hereafter. What they understand of the majesty of **Subhanahu Watangala**
7. consists only of four matters: clothing, food, illness, and comfort. This belongs to the nature
8. of animals that survive, to the evil nature, to the nature of *Iblis*. As for the majesty of **Gusti Allah
9. Subhanahu Watangala**, the Exalted and Majestic, the *Wahidul Kohhar*, the Books number
10. one hundred and four. The meaning of the Book is faith. The meaning of faith is the command
11. of the **Robbilngalamin** to all the descendants of Adam. Thus, **Kanjeng Nabi Adam**,
12. the Caliph of Allah, peace be upon him, who serves as the seed [p.149.] for all /
13. those possessing human nature, was brought down from heaven to the world to become the first Prophet,
14. given ten Books. **Kanjeng Nabi Sjis Ngalaihissalam** (Seth) was given fifty Books;
15. **Kanjeng Nabi Idris Ngalaihissalam** (Enoch) was given thirty Books; **Kanjeng Nabi Ibrahim
16. Kholilullah Ngalaihissalam** (Abraham, Friend of God) was given ten Books; **Kanjeng Nabi Musa
17. Kalamullah Ngalaihissalam** (Moses, Word of God) was given one Book named the Torah (*Toret*);
18. **Kanjeng Nabi Dawud Kholifatul Ardhi Ngalaihissalam** (David, Caliph of the Earth) /* [x] */ was given
19. one Book named the Psalms (*Zabur*); **Kanjeng Nabi Ngisa Ruhullah Ibnu Marjam Ngalaihissalam**
20. (Jesus, Spirit of God, Son of Mary) was given one Book named [p.150.] the Gospel (*Injil*).
21. **Kanjeng Nabi Mo- / Mohammad Khabibullah Solallahu Ngalaihissalam** (Muhammad, Beloved of God, peace be upon him),
22. the chosen one (*mustafa*), served as the seal of all the Prophets who descended to the world in the end times.
23. He was given the title of the Prophet of the End Times (*Nabi akir jaman*), the beginning, the end,
24. the outward, the inward; it is certain that there are not two in the world or the hereafter,
25. because the full moon of the universe, the **Kanjeng Nabi**, was given one Book named the Quran.
26. Yes, the Quran is the seed of the one hundred and three Books. The one hundred and three Books
27. descended to the world from the Preserved Tablet (*Lauhil Mahfudh*). All of them, the Angel **Jabarail Ngalaihissalam**
28. (Gabriel) was commanded to deliver to the Prophets who [p.151.] were given the Books. That is why /
29. if the one hundred and three Books are read or touched using purification, it is merely preferable (*utama*),
30. because they are only entrusted and involve an intermediary in their descent to the world from the *Lauhil Mahfudh*.
31. Yes, the Angel **Jabarail Ngalaihissalam** was commanded by **Gusti Allah Subhanahu Watangala**,
32. the Exalted and Majestic, the *Wahidul Kohhar*. As for the descent of the Quran from the *Lauhil Mahfudh*
33. to the world to **Kanjeng Nabi Mohammad Solallahu Ngalaihi Wassalam**, it did not use the Angel as an intermediary,
34. so it was already *kalamullah* (the Word of God). On the contrary, that is why it is *haram* (forbidden) to touch
35. or read the [p.152.] Quran / if one's body is not yet pure. The Quran has thirty *juz'* (parts).
36. These thirty *juz'* serve as the seed for all the one hundred and three Books. As for what serves
37. as the seed for the Quran, it is the *Fatekah* (Al-Fatihah). What serves as the seed for the *Fatekah*
38. is the *Bismillah*. What serves /* [k] */ as the seed for the *Bismillah* is the *Ba* in *Bismillah*.
39. As for the *Ba* in *Bismillah*, it is the will of **Gusti Allah Subhanahu Watangala**, the Exalted and Majestic,
40. the *Wahidul Kohhar*, which serves as the seed for all the majestic knowledge of the **Robbilngalamin**.

**Kanjeng Nabi Adam**. He was commanded to scatter the fruit of the *khuldi*, so that all forms of sustenance (*rizqi*) in the world would grow. Yes, from the *khuldi*, various forms grew from the *khuldi* fruit that was thrown into the perishable world. Thus, **Kanjeng Nabi Adam Khalifatullah Ngalaihissalam** and his wife, **Kanjeng [p.175.] Babu Khowa**, after / a full thousand years of undergoing judgment in the *naraka wel*, the sins of **Kanjeng Nabi Adam** and his wife, **Kanjeng Babu Khowa**, were forgiven. They were summoned back to heaven, both of them. But the descendants of Adam who were born in the *naraka wel* were not summoned back to heaven, because they were left behind, only given clothing and food in the *naraka wel*. But the descendants of Adam were commanded to walk on the path that was replaced by **Kanjeng Nabi Adam Kholifatullah Ngalaihissalam**. The meaning is the religion of Islam. [p.176.] / Because **Kanjeng Nabi Adam**, yes, was already infused with the religion of Islam, but **Kanjeng Nabi Adam** was not given an additional Book that descended, only outward commands. As for all the children of Adam who were willing to heed the commands of **Kanjeng Nabi Adam**, yes, they were summoned back to heaven. The meaning of returning to heaven is that when they die, they return to their place of origin. Thus, all the descendants of Adam who were not willing to heed the commands of **Kanjeng Nabi Adam**, yes, they remained eternally in the *naraka wel*. The meaning is that when they die, they then go to the hell of *Jahanam*. [p.177.] Because from the *naraka wel* / they then go to the hell of *Jahanam*. The meaning is they become cursed infidels, because *Iblis* has permanently established his place in the hell of *Jahanam*. Thus, the one hundred and four Books were sent down to the world by **Gusti Allah Subhanahu Watangala**, the Exalted and Majestic, the *Wahidul Kohhar*. All of them were made as guidance and as the sustenance of the heart for the descendants of Adam who are Muslims. The meaning of being made the sustenance of the heart is: *"Bismillahir-rohmanir-rohim"*. The meaning is: the hidden *Mim* made as a replacement [p.178.] in the world. All of this is why it was made the sustenance of / the heart for the descendants of Adam, because the physical body of the descendants of Adam comes from the water of destiny (*qodlo*), so the subtle spirit becomes the sustenance of the hidden *Mim*. The meaning of the hidden *Mim* is the hidden subtle sustenance. Thus, if the subtle spirit becomes dry because it does not eat the subtle sustenance, the subtle spirit dies, and the person is no longer a Muslim, becoming like an animal. If they have not yet died, the meaning is when they meet the time of death, they become a cursed infidel. Therefore, Islam cannot be devoid of the sustenance of [p.179.] the heart. The meaning of the sustenance of the heart is: / *"Bismillahir-rohmanir-rohim"*. *"Bismillahir-rohmanir-rohim"* is the gathering of only the *naqtu ghoib* (hidden point). Yes, the *naqtu ghoib* is the *Mim* in *Bismillah*. Yes, the *Mim* in *Bismillah* outwardly is the name of **Kanjeng Nabi Mohammad Salallahu Ngalaihi Wassalam**. Thus, the sustenance of the heart is reciting blessings (*salawat*), or reciting the Quran, or the *zhikir nafi isjabat*. All of these gather to become the sustenance of the heart. So if the hearts of the descendants of Adam who are Muslims are not devoid of these three kinds of sustenance, it is certain they become true Muslims, their hearts shining brightly. [p.180.] / Thus, none of the cursed *Iblis* can enter the heart of such a person. But the will of the **Robbil'ngalamin** is to choose the descendants of Adam whose hearts are dry, because they do not bring the sustenance of their hearts. Thus, their hearts are belatedly closed off by the cursed *Iblis*, unwilling to listen to true guidance, so their hearts are filled with the sustenance of the cursed *Iblis*. Yes, that is the *Mim* of the world that does not cause them to remember **Gusti Allah**.

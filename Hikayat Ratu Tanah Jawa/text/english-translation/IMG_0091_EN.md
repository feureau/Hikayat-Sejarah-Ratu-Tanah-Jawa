## Text

                                - 34 -

[p.185.] / Thus, **Kanjeng Nabi Nuh** agreed to the request of *Iblis*. Then
**Kanjeng Nabi Nuh** prostrated and spoke to **Subhanahu Watangala**, presenting
the request of *Iblis*. So the **Robbil Ngalamin** commanded **Nabi Nuh**
to tell the cursed *Iblis* to prostrate to **Nabi Adam**; if
*Iblis* was forgiven by **Nabi Adam**, **Subhanahu Watangala** would accept
the repentance of *Iblis*, because the sin of *Iblis* was against **Nabi Adam**. Thus,
it was then relayed to *Iblis* that such was the will of the **Robbil-
ngalamin**. Then *Iblis* departed from the presence of **Kanjeng Nabi
[p.186.] Nuh** with a scowling face, and *Iblis* said: "I will go and become /
dust. I will endure being in hell; I am not willing to be subdued by mud."
Thus, after **Kanjeng Nabi Nuh Rosulullahi Ngalaihissalam**, a long time passed,
and the cursed infidels increased again. Then **Subhanahu Watangala** chose again
among the descendants of Adam one who was commanded to guide all the cursed infidels.
He was given the name **Kanjeng Nabi Ibrahim Kholilullah Ngalaihissalam** (Abraham, Friend of God, peace be upon him), and
the **Kanjeng Nabi** was given another ten Books. Thus, after **Kanjeng
Nabi Ibrahim Kholilullah Ngalaihissalam**, a long time passed, and the cursed *Iblis*
[p.187.] increased again. So **Subhanahu Watangala** chose / among the descendants of
Adam one who was commanded to guide all the cursed infidels. He was given
the name **Kanjeng Nabi Musa Kalamullah Ngalaihissalam** (Moses, Word of God, peace be upon him)...
peace be upon him / and was given one Book named the Torah (*Toret*). Thus,
after **Kanjeng Nabi Musa Kalamullah**, a long time passed, and the cursed
*Iblis* increased again. So the **Robbilngalamin** chose among the descendants of Adam
one who was commanded to guide all the cursed infidels. He was given
the name **Kanjeng Nabi Dawud Kholifatullardi Ngalaihissalam** (David, Caliph of the Earth, peace be upon him) and was given
one Book named the Psalms (*Zabur*). Thus, after **Kanjeng Nabi Dawud
[p.188.] Kholifatulardi**, a long time passed, and the cursed *Iblis* increased again. /
So **Subhanahu Watangala** chose among the descendants of Adam one who was commanded to guide
all the cursed infidels. He was given the name **Kanjeng Nabi Ngisa
Ruhullah Ibnu Marjam Ngalaihissalam** (Jesus, Spirit of God, Son of Mary, peace be upon him) and was given one Book
named the Gospel (*Injil*). Thus, after **Kanjeng Nabi Ngisa Ruhullah**, a long time passed,
and the cursed *Iblis* increased again. So, starting from **Kanjeng Nabi Adam Kholi-
fatullah Ngalaihissalam** being brought down from heaven to the
world up to **Kanjeng Nabi Ngisa Ruhullah Ibnu Marjam Ngalaihi-
ssalam**, the number of those /* ^riyadlan */ given the title of Prophet from among all the descendants of Adam
[p.189.] by the **Robbilngalamin** was one hundred and twenty-
four thousand; the Prophets who became Messengers (*utusan*) were three hundred and thirteen. The Prophets who held
command were six. That is why **Gusti Allah Subhanahu Watangala**, the Exalted
and Majestic, the *Wahidul Kohhar*, sent down Books to **Kanjeng Nabi Adam Kholifatullah
Ngalaihissalam** in the world from the *Lauhil
Mahfudh*, one hundred and three in number, which served as the help of the **Robbil-
ngalamin** to **Kanjeng Nabi Adam** and all his descendants who were Muslims
who remained in the world. Thus, the Six Prophets served as the pillars for
all the descendants of Adam who still remained in the world who were
[p.190.] Muslims. Thus, between the Six Prophets who were commanded / by the **Robbilngalamin**,
if after a Prophet became a Messenger among the Six Prophets, then came
the *jaman pitrah* (era of emptiness/interregnum) in the world. The meaning of the *jaman Pitrah* is that all
the commands of **Subhanahu Watangala** that were already stated in the Books
were referred to by those who obeyed any one of the one hundred and
three Books. Because all the one hundred and three Books functioned similarly;
whichever was practiced regarding religion, suddenly they would be opposed. Even if
there were Prophets, Saints, and Believers who explained all the commands of the
[p.191.] Books, / still many encountered difficulties. And the children and grandchildren of
the Prophets, Saints, and Believers, if they happened to be in the *jaman Pitrah* in the world—
the meaning of the *jaman Pitrah* being that the world was currently empty—
the cursed *Iblis* ruled the world and claimed to be King, so many
descendants of Adam who remained were deceived, if it happened to be in that *jaman Pitrah*.
Then **Gusti Allah Subhanahu Watangala**, the Exalted and Majestic, the *Wahidul
Kohhar*, after **Kanjeng Nabi Ngisa Ruhullah Ibnu Marjam Ngalaihissalam**,
there were no longer any descendants of Adam remaining in the world
who held the title of Prophet among all true Muslims; there only remained the titles of
[p.192.] Saint (*Wali*) and Believer (*Mukmin*) / for all the descendants of Adam who were Muslims remaining
in the world. Thus, after **Kanjeng Nabi Ngisa Ruhullah Ibnu Marjam**,
it was indeed infidelity if there were any descendant of Adam who claimed the title of Prophet, because
after **Kanjeng Nabi Ngisa Ruhullah Ibnu Marjam**, **Gusti Allah
                                                          Subhanahu**

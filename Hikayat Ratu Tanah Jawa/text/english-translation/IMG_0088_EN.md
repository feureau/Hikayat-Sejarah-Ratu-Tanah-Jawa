Those *Mim*, other than the four matters, it is certain that the sun

towards the falsehood. Thus, the meaning in the world is that there are two paths.
The descendants of Adam who choose the true *Mim*—meaning
those who do not separate from faith—yes, that is the sign that they will be
able to return to their origin again. That means in heaven. But
all the descendants of Adam who choose the false *Mim*—yes, those are the ones
who will no longer be able to return to their origin again, yes, in the world
which is called the *naraka wel* (the abyss of hell), all the descendants of Adam who choose
what is false. Thus, **Gusti Allah Subhanahu Watang-
[p.172.] -ala**, the Exalted and Majestic, the *Wahidul Kohhar*, takes out all /
the descendants of Adam from the *naraka wel*. This means in the world,
all descendants of Adam begin—Muslims and infidels are
the same until they die. So when all the descendants die, yes,
it is exactly the same, their arrival and their state as Muslims or infidels. They do not
bring anything from all the *Mim* in the world; yes, all of that still remains in the world.
Thus, what is carried by all the descendants of Adam is
only their Islam and their infidelity. So when all the descendants of Adam die,
yes, they find the path of two matters. The Muslims find eternal life
[p.173.] that is everlasting and unchanging. Meaning, yes, they return to heaven /

to **Kanjeng Nabi Adam**, only the fruit of eternity (*khuldi*) was
/* [den] */ not permitted. Because if he ate it, he would inevitably need to
urinate and defecate, and **Nabi Adam** would cause impurity in heaven. Thus,
**Nabi Adam** transgressed the prohibition of **Subhanahu Watangala** in that way,
because he believed the words of **Babu Khowa** (Mother Eve). **Babu Khowa** believed
the words of the cursed *Iblis*, because of the oath of the cursed *Iblis*.

### [Folio 118]
1. and half becoming inhabitants of hell. If the *iman taghiyur* regresses to the *iman*
2. *mardud*, it is certain that the child of Adam will become an inhabitant of hell. Because the
3. *iman mardud* belongs to the cursed Iblis, if it still exists, but the *iman*
4. [p.80.] *taghiyur* will certainly face / the final judgment (*hisab*) for the child of Adam.
5. As for the two faiths, *makbul* and *hidayat*, they hang solely upon the will
6. (*iradat*) of the Rabbil 'Alamin. As for *iman taghiyur*, the sign
7. that it will progress to *iman makbul* is holding firmly to *iman amantubillah*;
8. if it receives help, it gains *iman makbul*, and if it progresses further, it becomes *iman hidayat*;
9. these three faiths are a sequence: *maksum*, *hidayat*, and *makbul*, all
10. outwardly are *iman amantubillah*. The meaning of *iman amantubillah* is that which hangs above
11. [p.81.] the *iman taghiyur* / which serves as the path to
12. those three faiths. As for the difference between the *Nabi*, *Wali*, and *Mukmin*: for the *Nabi*,
13. when young, their faith is already *amantubillah*, and upon coming of age (*baligh*), it becomes *maksum*;
14. the *Wali* and *Mukmin* when young have *iman taghiyur*, and upon coming of age have *iman amantubillah*;
15. their elevation to *iman makbul* or *iman hidayat* is determined by the will of Subhanahu Watangala Himself.
16. As for those three faiths, they each have their own behaviors: *iman maksum*, *iman hidayat*,
17. and *iman makbul*, because *iman maksum* applies after being in this world, after
18. [p.82.] Lord Prophet Muhammad s.m.w (peace be upon him) / it is called Satan /* (X) */
19. Likewise, in speech, do not say 'take a major ritual bath (*jinabat*)', 'I have already taken
20. a major ritual bath'; because the inward state has already taken the bath, that is the purest water,
21. called the *jinabat sinarawedi* (the esoteric major ritual bath), and as long as you remember
22. those three things, your inward state is called *jinabat sinarawedi*; there is no need to use water
23. which will only make you cold, yet it is perfect, yes that *jinabat sinarawedi* is the purest
24. and most perfect water and is not cold. As for saying such a thing, it is indeed clear
25. that what is called *jinabat sinarawedi* is purer; if weighed against your
26. [p.83.] *jinabat niyat* (ritual bath by intention) alone, / it is like comparing the five daily prayers
27. to those five prayers, but for both of them, prayer and the ritual bath /* [dead/mati] */,
28. what serves as their rule is your outward intention, indeed it is valid for both,
29. but it is just lacking in excellence; whereas what is called *jinabat sinarawedi*
30. is like the five prayers, its method requires you to be mindful of the place of
31. that purest water, because it passes through two earths (*siti*), and also passes through
32. its unseen realm inside the *Bait al-Makmur* (The Inhabited House). Therefore, if you wish for
33. it to descend into the heart after the merging of feelings inside the *cupu manik astagina*
34. [p.84.] (jeweled casket of eight virtues), / what makes it so that if your desire is improper, it incurs the
35. great wrath of the Rabbil -'Alamin because this is the most chosen among your trusts
36. from Subhanahu Watangala, that water of life; therefore, if it is not done with
37. proper etiquette, its descent becomes haram, meaning you must marry and remember
38. the intention when you carry out your command, the conduct of a Muslim person.
39. So if you already know the intricacies of what is called the pure water
40. and the guidance of the teacher. As for the method of what is called *jinabat sinarawedi*,
41. if you have performed the *jinabat* bath, then intend to perform the *kifarat*
42. [p.85.] (expiation) prayer ideally, or / just remember the Rabbil 'Alamin, asking for forgiveness
43. because of your brief forgetfulness, that is called *jinabat sinarawedi*, so both
44. are achieved. Likewise regarding the matter of prayer. If both the outward and
45. the inward have become one, yes that is called the *sinarawedi* prayer /* (X) */

The meaning of *iman amantubillah* is that which hangs above the *iman taghiyur* [p.81.] / which serves as the path to those three faiths. As for the difference between the *Nabi*, *Wali*, and *Mukmin*: for the *Nabi*, when young, their faith is already *amantubillah*, and upon coming of age (*baligh*), it becomes *maksum*; the *Wali* and *Mukmin* when young have *iman taghiyur*, and upon coming of age have *iman amantubillah*; their elevation to *iman makbul* or *iman hidayat* is determined by the will of Subhanahu Watangala Himself. Therefore, if you wish for it to descend into the heart after the merging of feelings inside the *cupu manik astagina* (jeweled casket of eight virtues), what makes it so that if your desire is improper, it incurs the great wrath of the Rabbil [p.84.] -'Alamin / because this is the most chosen among your trusts from Subhanahu Watangala, that water of life; therefore, if it is not done with proper etiquette, its descent becomes haram, meaning you must marry and remember the intention when you carry out your command, the conduct of a Muslim person. So if you already know the intricacies of what is called the pure water and the guidance of the teacher.

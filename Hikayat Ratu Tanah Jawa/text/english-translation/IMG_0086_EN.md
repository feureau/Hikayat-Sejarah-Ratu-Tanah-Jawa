                                - 29 -

*mutakalimu wahid* can no longer be conceptualized in any way in the world
all the way to the hereafter, it is only *sjaiun muhith*. Yes, the *Alif muta-
kalimu wahid* is the *Alif* that manifests as three. First, the *Alif* manifesting as
*ngazhim* (great); the meaning of the *Alif* manifesting as *ngazhim* is the *Ba* in *Bismillah*.
Yes, that is what is called the *bahru muhith* (encompassing ocean), its attribute is the Quran. Second, the *Alif muta-
firu*; the meaning of the *Alif mutafiru* is the *Mim* in *Bismillah*. Yes, the *Mim* in
[p.159.] *Bismillah* is what is called / *badaru ngalim* (the full moon of the universe). The meaning of *badaru ngalim*
is **Kanjeng Nabi Mohammad Solkallohu Ngalaihi Wassalam**. Third,
the *Alif tamasur*; the meaning of that *Alif* is the word *Allah* in *Bismillah*.
The word *Allah* in *Bismillah* is what is called the *nuqot ghoib* (hidden point). The meaning of the *nuqot
ghoib* is **Kanjeng Nabi Adam Kholifatullah Ngalaihissalam**. Thus,
there are only three *Alif* whose forms manifest in the world all the way to the
hereafter: the Quran, Mohammad, and Adam. Yes, /* [maring] */ there are only those three that
serve as the outward manifestation of the attributes of the Necessarily Existent Essence (*zhat kang wajibul wujud*), which is hidden
and eternal (*kodim*), everlasting, and unchanging. Yes, it is the *Alif sjaiun muhith*. That is the *Da'irah* (circle).
[p.160.] /* [marane] */ Let everyone be vigilant / regarding the intent and movement of that *Da'irah*, as well as
the shape of its letters: /* [diagram: م الله ] */ . O remember, all descendants of Adam,
never forget the majestic knowledge of **Gusti Allah
Subhanahu Watangala**, the Exalted and Majestic, the *Wahidul Kohhar*. Yes, those
three matters manifest in *Bismillah* as *sifatullah* (the attributes of God): faith,
Mohammad, and Adam. But that is the true majesty of **Subhanahu Watangala**,
the beginning, the end, the outward, the inward, the hidden, and the manifest.
There is nothing else that emerges except from the seed of those three matters. Thus, those three
[p.161.] matters take turns, remaining constant in the world up to /
the hereafter. O remember, all descendants of Adam who still remain
in the world, Muslim men and Muslim women, never forget that faith,
all of you. Because faith is the foundation of your life from the world up to
the hereafter. The hereafter is like a city, and what serves as the light of
that city is faith moving to the hereafter, called the *ngalam kabir* (macrocosm). The meaning of
the *ngalam kabir* is the great and eternal universe, unchanging. Thus, the world
is called the *ngalam saqhir* (microcosm). The meaning of the *ngalam saqhir* is the
small, lowly universe, dark and changing, which will eventually be destroyed—the world
[p.162.] along with everything in it. / As for what serves as the light in the world,
it is the sun and the moon. Yes, both also serve to measure
the age of the world. By analogy, it is just like that in the world,
/* [bodho] */ ^very foolish. It is true, if a market were to be compared to a city, or
the sun /* [x] */ compared to faith in its brightness. Yes,
remember, all descendants of Adam who still remain in the world. Yes, the sun
is what serves as the light in the world, and what
provides guidance—yes, the sun—towards all *Mim*, as well as towards all
[p.163.] directions (*qiblat*), whether true or false in the / *ngalam soghir* (microcosm). The sun is what
provides guidance, but it is just the sun. If you have obtained faith, it is certain
that the sun will become your helper in the world, or your body, because the sun
will join in witnessing all your actions in the world,
because it is the means by which you perceive the majesty of **Gusti Allah Subhanahu
Watangala**, the Exalted and Majestic, /* [kohhar] */ the *Wahidul Kohhar*, towards all
*Mim*.

2. *mutakalimu wahid* can no longer be conceptualized in any way in the world all the way to the hereafter,
3. it is only *sjaiun muhith*. Yes, the *Alif mutakalimu wahid* is the *Alif* that manifests
4. as three. First, the *Alif* manifesting as *ngazhim* (great); the meaning of the *Alif* manifesting as *ngazhim*
5. is the *Ba* in *Bismillah*. Yes, that is what is called the *bahru muhith* (encompassing ocean), its attribute is the Quran.
6. Second, the *Alif mutafiru*; the meaning of the *Alif mutafiru* is the *Mim* in *Bismillah*. Yes, the *Mim* in [p.159.]
7. *Bismillah* is what is called / *badaru ngalim* (the full moon of the universe). The meaning of *badaru ngalim* is
8. **Kanjeng Nabi Mohammad Solkallohu Ngalaihi Wassalam**. Third, the *Alif tamasur*; the meaning of that *Alif*
9. is the word *Allah* in *Bismillah*. The word *Allah* in *Bismillah* is what is called the *nuqot ghoib* (hidden point).
10. The meaning of the *nuqot ghoib* is **Kanjeng Nabi Adam Kholifatullah Ngalaihissalam**. Thus, there are only three *Alif*
11. whose forms manifest in the world all the way to the hereafter: the Quran, Mohammad, and Adam. Yes,
12. /* [maring] */ there are only those three that serve as the outward manifestation of the attributes of the Necessarily Existent Essence
13. (*zhat kang wajibul wujud*), which is hidden and eternal (*kodim*), everlasting, and unchanging. Yes, it is the *Alif sjaiun muhith*.
14. That is the *Da'irah* (circle). [p.160.] /* [marane] */ Let everyone be vigilant / regarding the intent and movement of that *Da'irah*,
15. as well as the shape of its letters: /* [diagram: م الله ] */ . O remember, all descendants of Adam,
16. never forget the majestic knowledge of **Gusti Allah Subhanahu Watangala**, the Exalted and Majestic, the *Wahidul Kohhar*.
17. Yes, those three matters manifest in *Bismillah* as *sifatullah* (the attributes of God): faith, Mohammad, and Adam.
18. But that is the true majesty of **Subhanahu Watangala**, the beginning, the end, the outward, the inward, the hidden,
19. and the manifest. There is nothing else that emerges except from the seed of those three matters. Thus, those three [p.161.]
20. matters take turns, remaining constant in the world up to / the hereafter. O remember, all descendants
21. of Adam who still remain in the world, Muslim men and Muslim women, never forget that faith, all of you.
22. Because faith is the foundation of your life from the world up to the hereafter. The hereafter is like a city,
23. and what serves as the light of that city is faith moving to the hereafter, called the *ngalam kabir* (macrocosm).
24. The meaning of the *ngalam kabir* is the great and eternal universe, unchanging. Thus, the world is called
25. the *ngalam saqhir* (microcosm). The meaning of the *ngalam saqhir* is the small, lowly universe, dark and changing,
26. which will eventually be destroyed—the world [p.162.] along with everything in it. /
27. As for what serves as the light in the world, it is the sun and the moon. Yes, both also serve to measure the age

31. all *Mim*, as well as towards all [p.163.] directions (*qiblat*), whether true or false in the / *ngalam soghir* (microcosm).

# Hikayat Ratu Tanah Jawa — IMG_0006

## Folio 14 (Visual Right)

1: unto the sacred law and the foundation of all of Islam,
2: all five pillars; injustice (al-zhulm) is when the Lord God,
3: Blessed and Exalted, the Just, the One,
4: the Subduer (al-Qahhār), decrees His will to grant the command of struggle
5: in the cause of God (sabīlillāh), standing firm in humility in the sacred battle
6: among all the progeny of Adam, all who
7: are Muslims, according to the scriptural proof and text of the Qur'an:
8: "...and if you turn to the help of God for a reward
9: in excellence; but if you turn away as you turned away
10: before, He will punish you with a painful punishment..." [Qur'an 48:16];
11: the meaning is: all the progeny of Adam who are Muslims,
12: all of you Muslims, hold fast unto
13: the command of struggle in the cause of God (sabīlillāh), all of you,

## Folio 15 (Visual Left)

1: you have nothing more noble in this world unto
2: the hereafter; it is certain that all the descendants of Adam who are
3: Muslims, all of you, should engage in the struggle in the cause
4: of God; for if any of the descendants of Adam claim
5: to be Muslims, yet have not fulfilled
6: the struggle in the cause of God, they have not yet attained
7: true Islam, for indeed in this era, if you know
8: the righteous command, that is indeed
9: the command of this hour from the Lord God, Blessed and Exalted,
10: the Just, the One, the Subduer (al-Wāhid al-Qahhār), as the scriptural text
11: in the Qur'an states: "It is He who sent
12: His Messenger with guidance and the religion of truth to make it superior over
13: all religions, and sufficient is Allah as a Witness." [Qur'an 48:28]

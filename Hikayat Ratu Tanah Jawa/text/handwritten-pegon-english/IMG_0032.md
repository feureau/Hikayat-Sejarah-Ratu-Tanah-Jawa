# Hikayat Ratu Tanah Jawa - Scan IMG_0032

## Folio 66 (Visual Right)
1. unto Gusti Allah, the Glorified and Exalted, the Mighty  
2. and Majestic, the One, the Subduer (*al-Qahhār*); thus the Day in the *Bismillāh*  
3. here becomes three that manifest His attributes; because  
4. the Day of Primordial Origin (*al-Yawm al-Tilād*), the One, can never be doubted or questioned,  
5. in this world reaching unto the Hereafter truly,  
6. encompassing all, even as the Day of Origin, the One.  
7. Now the Day which is made three, named the Great  
8. Day (*al-Yawm al-'Aẓīm*); this Great Day is indeed  
9. the letter Bā' [ب]; the meaning of the Great Day is the Bā' in the  
10. *Bismillāh*, which is named the Encompassing Ocean (*Baḥr al-Muḥīṭ*),  
11. its attribute being boundless; and second, the Day of Manifestation (*al-Yawm al-Maẓhar*); the meaning  
12. of the Day of Manifestation is the letter Mīm [م] in the *Bismillāh*;  
13. this Mīm in the *Bismillāh* is what is named the Full Moon of the World...  

## Folio 67 (Visual Left)
1. unto the aspects of conduct in this cosmic sphere, as well as the  
2. sacred forms of its letters: *Bi-smi-llāh* [بِسْمِ اللهِ],  
3. behold, all the descendants and communities of the children of Adam  
4. reflect upon that which is encompassed within the divine knowledge of Gusti  
5. Allah, the Glorified and Exalted, the Mighty and Majestic, the One,  
6. the Subduer (*al-Qahhār*); these three realities encompass  
7. the divine richness within the *Bismillāh*, the holy attributes of Allah:  
8. Faith, Muhammad, Adam; these three in true spiritual reality  
9. are encompassed by the Glorified and Exalted, the First, the Last,  
10. the Manifest (*al-Ẓāhir*), the Inward (*al-Bāṭin*), the Hidden and the Revealed;  
11. none can escape following these three realities;  
12. their essence brings forth these three principles, side by side,  
13. mutually confirming and steadfast in this world reaching unto the Hereafter.

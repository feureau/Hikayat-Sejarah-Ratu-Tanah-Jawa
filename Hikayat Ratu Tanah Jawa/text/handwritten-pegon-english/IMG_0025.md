## Folio 52 (Visual Right)
1: Majestic, the One, the Subduer (*al-Qahhār*); the command of this Holy Qur'an, therefore
2: it is prohibited to touch or recite this Qur'an if
3: one has not yet touched the water of ritual purification; because this Qur'an
4: is sublime, meaning infinitely pure without blemish; indeed
5: this Qur'an is the outward manifestation of the Essence of Allah the Unseen, the Will
6: of the Lord of the Worlds. This Qur'an is encompassed by the Shari'ah in the Macrocosm
7: (*'Ālam Kabīr*), meaning that this Macrocosm is the human self.
8: Three categories are comprised therein: namely the Prophet, the Saint (*Walī*), and the
9: Believer (*Mu'min*); therefore all these worlds—Prophet, Saint, Believer—
10: are never separate from the divine majesty of Allah,
11: the Glorified and Exalted, whose presence fills this world unto the Hereafter;
12: therefore, whosoever does not hold fast to this
13: Qur'an will surely find his world engulfed in darkness,

## Folio 53 (Visual Left)
1: thus having no true knowledge regarding the majesty of the Lord of the
2: Worlds, who gathers all these beings together.
3: Indeed, if their spiritual sight is blinded in this world,
4: then unto the Hereafter truly they are deprived of divine forgiveness,
5: far from the majesty of the Glorified and Exalted, falling into four debased states:
6: only feeding appetite and seeking animal ease, acting like beasts,
7: wrapped in evil like Iblis. As for
8: the divine majesty of Gusti Allah, the Glorified and Exalted, the Mighty and Majestic,
9: the One, the Subduer (*al-Qahhār*), one hundred and four scriptures were revealed;
10: the meaning of the name of this Book is Faith; the meaning of Faith is
11: indeed the command of the Lord of the Worlds unto all the
12: descendants of Adam. And Kangjeng Prophet Adam, Caliph of Allah, upon him
13: be peace, was the first root and forefather unto all [generations].

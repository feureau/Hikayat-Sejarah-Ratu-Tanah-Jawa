# Hikayat Ratu Tanah Jawa - Scan IMG_0047

> [!NOTE]
> Textual sequence note: Folio 97 (on IMG_0046) flows into Folio 99 (Visual Left: Moses/Torah & David/Psalms), which continues into Folio 98 (Visual Right: Jesus/Gospel), and then flows into Folio 100 (on IMG_0048).

## Folio 98 (Visual Right)
1. Approaching, He chose, glorified and exalted be He, among the descendants  
2. of Adam one who would govern, confronting all the disbelievers,  
3. cursed of Allah; there was bestowed revelation upon Kangjeng Prophet Jesus (*'Īsā*),  
4. the Spirit of Allah (*Rūḥullāh*), son of Mary, peace be upon him, and  
5. he was granted a scripture, namely named the Gospel (*Injīl*); approaching,  
6. after Kangjeng Prophet Jesus, Spirit of Allah,  
7. as time passed, Iblis, cursed of Allah, rampaged once more;  
8. approaching the dispensation of Kangjeng Prophet Adam, Caliph of Allah, peace be upon  
9. him, sent down from Heaven into  
10. this earthly world, until in the end Kangjeng Prophet Jesus,  
11. Spirit of Allah, son of Mary, peace be upon him, was the culmination  
12. who sustained the faith of all the descendants of Adam  
13. unto the Lord of the Worlds, being a radiant light in truth...  

## Folio 99 (Visual Left)
1. ...unto the descendants of Adam, one to govern, confronting  
2. all the disbelievers, cursed of Allah; there was bestowed revelation upon  
3. Kangjeng Prophet Moses (*Mūsā*), who spoke with Allah (*Kalīmullāh*), peace be upon him,  
4. and he was granted a scripture, namely named the Torah (*Tawrāt*);  
5. approaching, after Kangjeng Prophet Moses, who spoke with Allah,  
6. as time passed, his people were overcome again by Iblis, cursed  
7. of Allah; approaching, the Lord of the Worlds chose among  
8. the descendants of Adam one to govern, confronting all  
9. the disbelievers, cursed of Allah; there was bestowed revelation upon Kangjeng Prophet  
10. David (*Dāwūd*), Caliph of Allah, peace be upon him, and  
11. he was granted a scripture, namely named the Psalms (*Zabūr*); approaching,  
12. after Kangjeng Prophet David, Caliph of Allah,  
13. as time passed, Iblis, cursed of Allah, rampaged once more...

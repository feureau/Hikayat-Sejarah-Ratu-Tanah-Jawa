# Hikayat Ratu Tanah Jawa - Scan IMG_0017

## Folio 36 (Visual Right)
1. Submitting and yielding towards the infidels, indeed it was so because  
2. their condition was in great agitation and distress,  
3. at that precise time it became the reason that Abdul Hamid,  
4. you [were sent], and therefore there came the divine aid of Gusti Allah,  
5. the Glorified and Exalted, the One, the Subduer (*al-Qahhār*). Abdul Hamid,  
6. [turning] towards you: said Panji Argakusuma / Danureja, "Listen, Abdul  
7. Hamid: indeed you speak truly. If Danureja  
8. is so, if he is likened unto your own brother, that is true,  
9. because both hold firmly to the command against the infidels.  
10. Thus even though both are holding steadfastly,  
11. hold fast to the command of Danureja, Abdul Hamid;  
12. especially since she is your elder, because  
13. what is held firmly by Danureja is the command [of the Lord]."  

## Folio 37 (Visual Left)
1. That which illuminates the Hajj prayer, its light is majestic,  
2. flowing from the divine Truth (*Haqq*); the Middle Prayer (*Ṣalāt al-Wusṭā*) contains within it  
3. its outward manifestation, witnessing the manifestation that preserves  
4. the attribute of the True One, appearing in pure and sublime goodness,  
5. the light of Divine Reality (*nūr Allāh*), surely when His actions encompass the servant.  
6. The fulfillment of the Middle Prayer, already filled with radiant light,  
7. and the manifestation of the actions of the Truth; this prayer is established  
8. within the inner and outer dimensions of the soul (*nafs*), witnessing that the *nafs* enters  
9. the attribute of the Living (*Ḥayy*) belonging to the True One, gathering all  
10. sentiments, feelings, praises, seeking forgiveness, and sincere devotion,  
11. yearning for the Exalted, returning to the highest good, returning  
12. to the sixth soul, obtaining its share among all  
13. perceptions, becoming purely good, the eternal light (*nūr al-qadīm*).

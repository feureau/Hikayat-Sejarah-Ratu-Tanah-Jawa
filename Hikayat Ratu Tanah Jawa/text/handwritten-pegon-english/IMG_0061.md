## Folio 126 (Visual Right)
1: ...your breath gently; and when it has completely exited,
2: then draw it in once more through the mouth, and your inhalation,
3: anchor it firmly unto the Heavenly Temple (*Bayt al-Ma'mūr*) again; then when it has
4: reached the *Bayt al-Ma'mūr*, let your breath descend toward
5: the House of God (*Baytullāh*), just as the movement was earlier; then proceed toward
6: divine guidance (*tawfīq*), and then return to the *Bayt al-Ma'mūr* once more;
7: repeat this three times, with its course being the same as
8: earlier: its entry is through the mouth and its exit is through the
9: nostrils; and when that breath returns to the *Bayt al-Ma'mūr* again,
10: let your breath exit through the nostrils; when you
11: feel your breath has rested, then raise it up once more,
12: draw in your breath and anchor it firmly unto the *Bayt al-Ma'mūr*
13: again, and let your breath exit through the nostrils gently...

## Folio 127 (Visual Left)
1: ...and when you feel your breath has rested, then
2: raise it up again and draw in your breath
3: once more; when it has reached the *Bayt al-Ma'mūr*, then let your breath
4: descend unto the Hidden Point (*Nuqṭah Ghayb*); as for the manner
5: of the entry and exit of that breath, it is just as earlier:
6: exiting from the nostrils and entering from the mouth; repeat
7: this three times; then from the Hidden Point (*Nuqṭah Ghayb*) proceed
8: toward the coccyx (*balung 'ajbu 'dh-dhanab*), likewise repeated
9: three times; as for the course of your breath, it does not differ
10: from earlier; the difference is that when it has reached
11: the third time of that breath within the coccyx (*balung 'ajbu 'dh-dhanab*),
12: then form the intention to spread it evenly throughout all
13: the outward bodily limbs (*anggotané kang ẓāhir*) completely...

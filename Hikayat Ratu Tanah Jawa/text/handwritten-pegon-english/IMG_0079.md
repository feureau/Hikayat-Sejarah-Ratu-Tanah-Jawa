## Folio 162 (Visual Right)

1: reaching unto the commands of a ruler who has already passed away
2: from ancient times in this world, be diligent in holding firmly;
3: truly that is good; and moreover, if
4: in this world there is a ruler who has passed away, the meaning thereof is
5: that if there is an Islamic ruler in that land who acknowledges
6: the proper guidance, namely the textual proof and purpose of the commands of that
7: ruler, if it reflects the hadith, the discourse
8: of that ruler can no longer be doubted at all; if
9: in an Islamic land, the ruler acts unjustly or transgresses, then
10: such being so, take heed, all descendants
11: of Adam who still consider themselves Muslims, both men
12: and women: it is best for you to come close and examine carefully
13: whether in that land there still remains separation (*mufāraqah*)

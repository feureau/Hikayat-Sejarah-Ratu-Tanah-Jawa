## Folio B (Visual Left — Numbered `237`, Pencil `P. 96`)

**1:** the physical frame of the body of a true believer (*jêngkêrungé badané mu’min*) and the place/book
**2:** of the Qur’ān (*papaning Qur’ān*): in reality (*sayêktiné*), if they are called two, they are not two (*loro ora loro*),
**3:** and if they are called one, they are not one (*siji ora siji*)—the place/book of the Qur’ān and the body
**4:** of the true believer (*papaning Qur’ān lan badané mu’min*). Therefore, all true believers (*sarupané mu’min*)
**5:** may not separate (*ora kêna pisah*) from the place/book of the Qur’ān (*papané Qur’ān*), because
**6:** the place/book of the Qur’ān serves as the body of faith (*minangka badaning īmā-*
**7:** *-n*), whereas all the commands within the Qur’ān (*sabarang paréntahé ing sajroning Qur’ān*)
**8:** are what is called faith (*kang ngaran īmān*). Then, when you perform the ritual prayer (*lamun sêmbahyang sira*),
**9:** the place/book of the Qur’ān is no longer seen (*dadi ora katon papané Qur’ān iku*), because
**10:** it has already been transposed (*wus ngalih*) so that your own body (*dadi badanira iku*) is what serves as
**11:** the place/book of the Qur’ān (*minangka papaning Qur’ān*), because all the recitations (*sarupané wawacané*)
**12:** of the ritual prayer (*ṣalāt*) are none other than the Qur’ān (*ora ana kang liya Qur’ān*), reaching unto
**13:** the perfection of the ritual prayer (*sampurnané ṣalāt*), which means the remembrance of the Word (*dhikir kalimah*)

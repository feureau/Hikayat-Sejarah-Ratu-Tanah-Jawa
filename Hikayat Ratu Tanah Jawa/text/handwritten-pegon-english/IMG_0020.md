# Hikayat Ratu Tanah Jawa - Scan IMG_0020

## Folio 42 (Visual Right)
1: From here, unto
2: all the communities, you
3: are the reality of Kangjeng Nabi, peace
4: and blessings be upon him, unto
5: Abu Bakr, Umar, Uthman, and Ali, who
6: all possessed knowledge concerning the self,
7: while other people who have come to know
8: their Lord—for when they have attained discovery,
9: those people who have come to know their Lord,
10: then such a person becomes humble and self-effacing before
11: his own self, meaning humble in regard to
12: himself, because he no longer harbors personal desire,
13: that person turning entirely away from anything other than Gusti Allah.

## Folio 43 (Visual Left)
1: And each year, following the obligatory fast in the month
2: of Ramadan, which becomes one's righteous action in this world,
3: humbling oneself before one's fellow
4: Muslims, in walking, sitting, and speaking; when towards
5: a fellow Muslim, it is certain that if
6: he humbles himself, he is elevated; whereas if [he incurs]
7: the curse of Allah, surely the light of understanding
8: is eclipsed; yet even so, such is the character of
9: an upright Muslim, especially regarding the pilgrimage of Hajj,
10: journeying with his physical body to Mecca,
11: obeying the command of the Glorified and Exalted; and this truth
12: is an established pillar among the five pillars of Islam;
13: as for this pilgrimage of Hajj, there are three essential preparations.

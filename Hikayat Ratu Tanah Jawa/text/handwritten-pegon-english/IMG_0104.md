## Folio A (Visual Right — Numbered `212`, Pencil `P. 69`)

**1:** those two adversaries (*satru loro iku*) were like people receiving a guest (*kaya wong nampani*);
**2:** then straightway the mendicant ‘Abdu 'l-Ḥamīd was told
**3:** to sit up (*lungguh*), and straightway the mendicant ‘Abdu 'l-Ḥamīd sat up,
**4:** while those two figures both stood (*padha ngadêg*). Then
**5:** the mendicant ‘Abdu 'l-Ḥamīd asked: "What do you wish to do (*arêp ngapapaké*)
**6:** with me?" Then that adversary answered: "I wish
**7:** to devour (*mangsa*)!" It was the one in the form of a tiger that answered, whereas the one in the form
**8:** of a human being remained silent (*mênêng baé*), standing on the left (*ana kiwa*). Then
**9:** the mendicant ‘Abdu 'l-Ḥamīd spoke: "If you wish to swallow (*nguntal*)
**10:** me, come, swallow me without delay (*mara nguntala aja suwé*)!"
**11:** Then it opened its jaws wide (*dèn-angapaké*), about to swallow him;
**12:** yet the mendicant ‘Abdu 'l-Ḥamīd no longer beheld anything
**13:** other than God (*tan ana kang katingal nangging Allāh*), and in his heart he recited *Sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ* (*maca Sūrat Ikhlāṣ*).

## Folio B (Visual Left — Numbered `213`, Pencil `P. 70`)

**1:** Then there came the mercy of God the Exalted (*marmāning Allāh ta‘ālā*), like a hood/shield (*kudhung*)
**2:** over the back (*ẓahr*) of the mendicant ‘Abdu 'l-Ḥamīd; then that adversary trembled (*gumêtêr*)
**3:** and was unable to touch/harm (*ora bisa ngungkuli*) the mendicant
**4:** ‘Abdu 'l-Ḥamīd. Then that adversary spoke: "If
**5:** you, ‘Abdu 'l-Ḥamīd, are able to kill (*matèni*) me,
**6:** then it is certain (*pêsthi*) that the Land of Java (*Tanah Jawa*) shall belong
**7:** to you!" Then the mendicant ‘Abdu 'l-Ḥamīd answered,
**8:** and looked closely (*waspada*): that which had become the adversary was already old (*wus tuwa*).
**9:** "If God wills (*in shā’a 'llāh*), I know the way to kill (*ucaping patiné*) you!"

**12:** ‘Abdu 'l-Ḥamīd stepped forward (*nyêdhak*) toward that adversary,

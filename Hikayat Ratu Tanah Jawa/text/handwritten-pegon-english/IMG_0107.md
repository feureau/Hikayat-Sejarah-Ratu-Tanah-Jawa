## Folio A (Visual Right — Numbered `218`, Pencil `P. 75`)

**1:** *-aké*) the approaching end (*dina wêkasan*): all that is  
**2:** good and right (*bêcik bênêr*), all of it has already  
**3:** been lifted up and taken back (*padha pinundhut*) by  
**4:** the Lord of the Worlds (*Rabbi 'l-‘Ālamīn*); everything that was  
**5:** in the world (*ing dunyā*) has been caused to vanish (*wus ingilang-*  
**6:** *-akên kabèh*) by  
**7:** the Lord God, glorified and exalted is He (*subḥānahu wa-ta‘ālā*),  
**8:** who is Mighty and Majestic (*‘azza wa-jalla*), who is the One, the Subduer (*Wāḥidu 'l-Qahhār*),  
**9:** all of it. ۞

## Folio B (Visual Left — Numbered `219`, Pencil `P. 76`)

**1:** Indeed, the prophet (*nabī*), the saint (*walī*), and the true believer (*mu’min*), the reason why they become one (*dadi siji*)  
**2:** in their three stations (*têlung pangkaté*) is because the prophet, the saint, and the true believer are three  
**3:** in their physical frames (*jêngkêrungé*), yet in reality (*sayêktiné*) they are but one (*tunggal baé*),  
**4:** all abiding in the union of servant and Lord (*kêtêmu kawula Gusti*), that is, as the shadow (*wayang-*)  
**5:** (*-ané*) of the Essence of God (*Dhātullāh*) who is Unseen (*kang ghā’ib*). Then there becomes manifest (*ẓāhir*) the two dots (*nuqṭah lo-*  
**6:** *-ro*) of *Allāh*: that becomes the two words of the Testimony (*kalimah shahādat loro*),  
**7:** which serves as the seed of faith (*wijining īmān*); thus is its form:  
**8:** `[Diagram: Two vertical strokes inside a black rectangle: ا ا]` (*Alif* — that is what becomes the faith of *"I believe in God"* [*īmān āmantu bi-'llāh*]).  
**9:** The meaning of the faith of *"I believe in God"* (*īmān āmantu bi-'llāh*) is indeed Infallible/Protected Faith (*īmā-*  
**10:** *-n ma‘ṣūm*); the meaning of *ma‘ṣūm* is "guarded/preserved" (*rinêksa*), meaning that  
**11:** the prophets (*para nabī*), while they were not yet outwardly born (*durung ẓāhir*), had already  
**12:** been entered (*kapanjingan*) by the faith of *"I believe in God"* (*īmān āmantu bi-'llāh*), and therefore  
**13:** they are far removed from all sins, whether great or small (*adoh marang sakèhé dosa gêdhé utawa cilik*).

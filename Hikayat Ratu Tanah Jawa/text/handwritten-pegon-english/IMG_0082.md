## Folio 169 (Visual Left)

1: the noon prayer time (*ẓuhur*), walking toward the afternoon prayer time (*‘aṣar*),
2: walking toward the sunset prayer time (*maghrib*), walking toward the
3: night prayer time (*‘ishā’*); as long as it is not yet completed and you still re-
4: -main in this world, therefore you must certainly return to walk again
5: toward the dawn prayer time (*ṣubuḥ*), thus returning just as what has
6: already transpired, its five appointed times in a single day and
7: night; and likewise in each of those prayer times, its obligatory units (*farḍu*)
8: are not the same in their number, and it is not permissible that they be
9: less or more—there are two, there are four, there are three;
10: the reason the sacred law (*saréngat*) employs specific numbers is because,
11: by the will of Him who is glorified and exalted (*subḥānahu wa-ta‘ālā*), that obligatory prayer (*farḍu*) which is
12: the root of the standing of the sacred law also observes its own
13: respective seasons and times, as a sign in this world

## Folio 35 (Visual Left)

1. in truth, even though you might yield, you must proceed  
2. first [circle] with that which is affirmed with certainty: the Friday prayer;  
3. second, the prayer of pilgrimage (salāt al-ḥajj); third, the middle prayer (ṣalāt al-wusṭā);  
4. fourth, the peaceful dawn prayer; fifth, the prayer of the Sublime  
5. Name (al-Ism al-'Aẓīm); [circle] as for the Friday prayer, it is  
6. upon the tongue, outwardly a praise, meaning praise  
7. that if it were not for My decree, Java would have fallen into apostasy;  
8. and indeed the Friday prayer, because the praise  
9. is outwardly the Word of God (Qalamullāh); [circle] while the pilgrimage prayer  
10. is outwardly the manifestation of submission, meaning  
11. submission confirmed in the spiritual vision of the Divine Names upon the servant  
12. who receives divine grace, which surely cannot be  
13. except through the Omnipotent Power (Qudrat) and Divine Will (Irādat) of Him, Blessed and Exalted.

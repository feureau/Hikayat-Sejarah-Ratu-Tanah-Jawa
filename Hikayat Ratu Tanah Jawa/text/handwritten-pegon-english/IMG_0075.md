## Folio 154 (Visual Right)
1. ...within [the grave] and the holy war in the path of Allah (*prang sabilullāh*), each for himself;  
2. whoever among all the descendants of Adam who are Muslims,  
3. passing through your existence here in this world, if you obtain the path  
4. of the war in the path of Allah, then never  
5. let your heart hesitate concerning the destruction of your bodily form  
6. meeting in the war in the path of Allah; for your Islam,  
7. facing the curse of Allah, never let it be swept away  
8. from all of your affirmation of Divine Unity (*tawḥīd*) unto  
9. the Lord of the Worlds, and let your ultimate goal  
10. of Islam be preserved, all of you; and if you fall,  
11. meaning falling as a martyr (*syuhadā'*) directly upon the path  
12. of Allah, then your actions will not be damaged  
13. by all your former worldly deeds; all of that is...

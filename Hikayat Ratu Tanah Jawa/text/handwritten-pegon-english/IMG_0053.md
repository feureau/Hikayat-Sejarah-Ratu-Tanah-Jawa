# Hikayat Ratu Tanah Jawa - Scan IMG_0053

## Folio 110 (Visual Right)
1. ...all the descendants of Adam who are Muslims; none  
2. other can interpret the Qur'an truly, save as confirmed by Gusti Allah,  
3. glorified and exalted be He, the Mighty and Majestic, the One, the Subduer (*al-Qahhār*);  
4. [as He says]: *Fa-ta'ālallāhu al-Maliku 'l-Ḥaqqu wa lā ta'jal*  
5. *bi 'l-Qur'āni min qabli an yuqḍā ilayka waḥyuhu*  
6. *wa qul Rabbi zidnī 'ilmā* [Qur'an 20:114]; therefore, the loss of all  
7. descendants of Adam who are Muslims, male and female, [is averted]  
8. by the proofs of the Qur'an; obey and heed  
9. truly what is bestowed by Gusti Allah, glorified and exalted be He,  
10. the Mighty and Majestic, the One, the Subduer (*al-Qahhār*), the Sovereign  
11. Who in absolute truth reveals the two paths, neither of which  
12. is hidden, in this world, in the Hereafter, in Heaven,  
13. in Hell, upon the Throne, the Footstool (*Kursī*), and the seven tiers of the earth...  

## Folio 111 (Visual Left)
1. ...the sovereign light and will, all fulfilled, held fast and embraced  
2. in every circumstance; approaching, indeed the Qur'an has encompassed  
3. all of faith and prophethood; indeed the Qur'an stands  
4. establishing its pillars without dispute;  
5. the writing of the Qur'an shall never perish, before giving life  
6. and resounding brightly across this earthly world;  
7. approaching, when it gives life, resounding throughout  
8. this earthly world; the meaning is, when giving life  
9. on the Day of Resurrection, the script of the Qur'an returns  
10. unto the Preserved Tablet (*al-Lawḥ al-Maḥfūẓ*); therefore it is established  
11. from of old for all the descendants of Adam remaining in this world;  
12. both your nourishment and spiritual sustenance come from Kangjeng Prophet,  
13. the Seal, and through the Qur'an your faith shall never perish...

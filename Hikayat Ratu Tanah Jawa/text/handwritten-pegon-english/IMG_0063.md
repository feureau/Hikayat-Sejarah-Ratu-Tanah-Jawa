## Folio 130 (Visual Right)
1: ...my Lord, my Master, my Sovereign, my Guide, my Teacher
2: in this world unto the Hereafter with certainty; I present the reward
3: of my prayer and my praise and remembrance unto you; may
4: they be accepted by Allah, and may you ever remain
5: my refuge and shelter unto you in this world and in the Hereafter
6: with certainty." And after all those degrees (*darajat*) have been
7: completed, if there is the grace of Allah, glorified
8: and exalted be He, unto you, the sign is that you still feel
9: longing toward the Lord of the Worlds, your love not yet sated,
10: still yearning; then utter the remembrance once more,
11: without any fixed count and without needing to observe
12: formal ceremony again, according to your own inclination alone;
13: yet it must never cease that you choose the formula...

## Folio 131 (Visual Left)
1: ...*Lā ilāha illallāh*, which is called negation and affirmation (*nafy ithbāt*), because
2: that serves as the vessel for all the supreme spiritual sciences
3: of the Lord of the Worlds, all of which are encompassed within that Good Word (*Kalimah Ṭayyibah*);
4: and if you have a pressing hindrance (*'udzur*), then at
5: dawn (*ṣubḥ*) recite it thirty times, and at night (*'isyā'*) and noon (*ẓuhr*)
6: ten times, and at afternoon (*'aṣr*) and sunset (*maghrib*) even less is permissible;
7: yet at each of the five prayer times it must never be abandoned,
8: because that remembrance serves as a perpetual lamp and a constant reminder
9: unto Gusti Allah, glorified and exalted be He, the One,
10: the Subduer (*al-Wāḥid al-Qahhār*). [Ornament] As for this, it is the explanation
11: of the remembrance of the spiritual path (*dhikr ṭarīqah*) at its appointed times: if at dawn (*ṣubḥ*),
12: after finishing the remembrance of the prayer time and perfecting
13: your remembrance of that time, then proceed to the remembrance...

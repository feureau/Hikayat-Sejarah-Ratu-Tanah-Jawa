# Hikayat Ratu Tanah Jawa - Scan IMG_0037

## Folio 78 (Visual Right)
1. all these descendants of Adam, brought into this realm of existence,  
2. meaning here in this earthly world, where all are tested;  
3. all the descendants of Adam, both Muslims and disbelievers,  
4. are alike until death; then when they have met  
5. death, all descendants of Adam are equal in undergoing mortality;  
6. but regarding their final destination, the Muslim and the disbeliever do not share  
7. the same station; all worldly possessions in this earthly life, even  
8. while they are still dwelling here in this world, whatever is held  
9. and possessed by all these descendants of Adam, both  
10. in their Islam and in their disbelief; then when  
11. all these descendants of Adam have died, they encounter one of two paths:  
12. those who are Muslim meet with eternal, unbroken light without  
13. decay, meaning that they return home unto Heaven.  

## Folio 79 (Visual Left)
1. Of these two paths, towards that which is true or towards that which  
2. is false: the meaning in this earthly world is that indeed  
3. there are two paths for the children of Adam: those who choose  
4. the Truth (*Haqq*), meaning those who never separate from Faith,  
5. are the ones who attain salvation, being able to return  
6. back unto their primordial origin, meaning unto Heaven;  
7. whereas all descendants of Adam who choose  
8. the false are those who will never be able  
9. to return unto their exalted origin, but in this world that which  
10. is called Hell (*Nār al-Wayl*); all descendants of Adam who  
11. choose what is false and vain are all seized  
12. and subjected to the wrath of Gusti Allah, the Glorified and Exalted, the Mighty  
13. and Majestic, the One, the Subduer (*al-Qahhār*), towards all creations.

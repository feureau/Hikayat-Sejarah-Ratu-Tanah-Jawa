## Folio 104 (Visual Right)
1. ...into this earthly world, conveying the Word of Allah (*Kalāmullāh*),  
2. unto none other than all the descendants of Adam;  
3. that light stands as a guide, so that if in reality  
4. they heed the teachings and commandments of the Word of Allah,  
5. then whatever evil schemes Iblis,  
6. cursed of Allah, desires [will fail]; approaching, there was sent down Kangjeng Prophet  
7. Muhammad, the Beloved of Allah (*Ḥabībullāh*), the Chosen (*al-Muṣṭafā*), may Allah bless him  
8. and grant him peace, who was sent down alone unto  
9. this earthly world, standing as the Seal (*Khātam*) over  
10. all the prophets; a radiant light, therefore  
11. after Prophet Jesus, when prophecy had departed from this earthly  
12. world, all descendants of Adam who embraced  
13. Islam attained salvation; because Gusti Allah, glorified and exalted...  

## Folio 105 (Visual Left)
1. ...illuminated with authority the commandments contained within  
2. those one hundred and three scriptures; increasing in power  
3. to overcome Iblis, cursed of Allah, exceedingly; because  
4. that which was sent down from the Preserved Tablet (*al-Lawḥ al-Maḥfūẓ*)  
5. as the final revelation is the Holy Scripture, the Qur'an; the meaning of the Qur'an  
6. is the true Word of Allah (*Kalāmullāh al-Ḥaqīqī*); the meaning of the Word of Allah  
7. is the unfolding and fulfillment of the divine purpose of the Lord of the Worlds;  
8. its radiance is all-encompassing within the Holy Qur'an:  
9. the First, the Last, the Manifest, the Hidden (*al-Awwal, al-Ākhir, al-Ẓāhir, al-Bāṭin*);  
10. for the Scripture of the Qur'an is the source and mother of all scriptures that had been  
11. revealed previously, those one hundred and three scriptures; approaching  
12. the will of Gusti Allah, glorified and exalted be He, the Mighty  
13. and Majestic, the One, the Subduer (*al-Qahhār*), Who sent him...

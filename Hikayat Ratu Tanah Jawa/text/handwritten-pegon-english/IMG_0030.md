## Folio 62 (Visual Right)
1. The Day of Origin, the One, in the *Bismillāh*:  
2. the Day of Manifestation (*al-Yawm al-Masyhūr*) is the outward form of the word  
3. *Allāh* in the *Bismillāh*; the word *Allāh* in the *Bismillāh*  
4. is what became the vessel containing the attributes of the Divine Essence  
5. whose existence is necessary (*Wājib al-Wujūd*); its meaning is Kangjeng Prophet Adam,  
6. Caliph of Allah, peace be upon him; that is the outward manifestation  
7. of the attribute of the Day of Manifestation in the *Bismillāh*, the sacred name  
8. *Allāh*; this word is what became the seed unto  
9. all human nature and character, which is Kangjeng  
10. Prophet Adam, Caliph of Allah, peace be upon him, who became  
11. the seed of all mankind,  
12. bearing the divine richness within the attributes of Allah the Unseen,  
13. acknowledged in the high dignity of Kangjeng Prophet Adam, peace be upon him.  

## Folio 63 (Visual Left)
1. the deception of Satan, who is the ancient adversary of the Prophet,  
2. who continuously opposes the command of Allah towards all  
3. the children of Adam who still dwell in this world; the sign  
4. of a true and upright Muslim is the fulfillment of the twofold Shahādah;  
5. as for the meaning of prayer (*ṣalāt*), it is steadfast adherence to the command of  
6. the five obligatory daily prayers, which is the outward sign of  
7. the Essence of Allah the Unseen, whose practice of prayer becomes established  
8. in this world through patient endurance. As for the meaning  
9. of fasting (*ṣawm*), it is the fulfillment of the command of the Glorified and  
10. Exalted, obligatory for thirty days in the month of  
11. Ramadan, which becomes one's righteous deed in this world,  
12. refusing to surrender to the inclinations of base passion (*hawā*).  
13. As for the meaning of Zakat, it is the purification and distribution of worldly wealth.

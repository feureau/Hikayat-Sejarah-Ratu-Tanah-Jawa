## Folio 82 (Visual Right)
1: the divine attributes received in this matter are Jalāl (Majesty), Jamāl (Beauty), Qahhār (Subduing),
2: and Kamāl (Perfection); therefore, it becomes populated in Hellfire (*Wayl*);
3: that is the outward manifestation of Kangjeng Prophet Adam, Caliph of Allah,
4: peace be upon him, who was summoned to confront Iblis,
5: cursed of Allah, in this earthly world; the culmination of the earthly world
6: is the realm of the grave (*'Ālam Kubūr*); drawing near, therefore Kangjeng
7: Prophet Adam, peace be upon him, fulfilled the decree of created life, replete,
8: subjected to trials and bonds, thus proceeding straightway until reaching
9: either Heaven or the torment of Hellfire (*Wayl*); this is because
10: both destinations lie before them within; in Heaven,
11: that is where the singular reality becomes the manifestation of all
12: forms existing in this earthly world, an approaching divine light;
13: such was the divine will, descending through the lineage of Kangjeng Prophet Adam...

## Folio 83 (Visual Left)
1: ...in this earthly world, indeed in this earthly world, its appearance unto
2: the torment of Hell, approaching its lights, other than Kangjeng Prophet Adam, peace
3: be upon him, who stood firm, becoming the embodiment of all
4: human attributes, an approaching light of all
5: who share the attributes of humanity, a light that has been transmitted
6: into this world; indeed, in this world which encompasses the boundaries,
7: the decree of torment; therefore, in encompassing the boundaries of Hell's decree
8: in this earthly world, because of the devotion of the servant in taking hold
9: of faith, because of attaining the true inner meaning which
10: binds faith fast: that is Kangjeng Prophet Adam opposing
11: Iblis, summoned by the decree of Gusti Allah, glorified
12: and exalted be He, the Mighty and Majestic, the One, the Subduer (*al-Qahhār*);
13: and by His Exalted Will towards all of this sovereign domain.

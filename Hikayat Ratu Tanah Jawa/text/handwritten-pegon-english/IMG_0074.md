## Folio 152 (Visual Right)
1. ...the textual proof of the Qur'an: that the supreme path attained  
2. by you is complete and perfect: *"And spend in*  
3. *the path of Allah and cast not yourselves with your own hands into destruction,*  
4. *into destruction; and do good; indeed, Allah loves*  
5. *the doers of good"* (Q. 2:195); whoever among all  
6. the descendants of Adam who are Muslims, all of you,  
7. the most supreme and perfect life for you here in  
8. this world is if you meet your death on the path of Allah;  
9. that is what is attained by you unto Gusti Allah, glorified and  
10. exalted be He, the Mighty and Sublime (*'Azza wa-Jall*), the One, the Subduer (*al-Wāḥid al-Qahhār*);  
11. because the path of holy war in the path of Allah (*prang sabilullāh*) surpasses  
12. all other paths leading unto  
13. the Hereafter, all of them; there is none that weighs equal...  

## Folio 153 (Visual Left)
1. ...the time of Islam: do not ever apostatize! If you  
2. repent unto all of your sins,  
3. continuously unto the Lord of the Worlds; and if  
4. your heedlessness still compels you, let your fear be directed toward  
5. the curse of Allah (*la'natullāh*); beware! Truly, if there still  
6. remains a trace of your faith, do not apostatize;  
7. understand that if you are not plunged into  
8. the following of Iblis, may the curse of Allah be upon him, all of you,  
9. in this world unto the Hereafter with certainty, that is already  
10. certain: if all of that disappears from you, then that which  
11. remains steadfast is if Gusti Allah, glorified and exalted be He,  
12. the Mighty and Sublime, the One, the Subduer, grants that worldly realm,  
13. that you serve as a spiritual mendicant (*faqīr*) in the war in the path of Allah (*prang sabilullāh*)...

## Folio 23 (Visual Left)

1: among all Muslims that their faith is not ruined; because
2: all is annulled if one becomes a disbeliever accursed of God,
3: such as those who still obstinately cling to the religion of the Torah
4: and the Gospel; all who profess and claim
5: to hold a separate religion of their own, in defiance of the commands
6: of the Lord of the Worlds, all of them; for when the Jews
7: and Christians have been confuted, then unto you, all Muslims,
8: it is an absolute duty, and you must never adopt or practice their ways,
9: but hold fast to the command of the Lord God, Blessed and Exalted,
10: the Mighty and Majestic, the One, the Subduer (al-Wāhid al-Qahhār), concerning prayer,
11: all of it; for when one has fulfilled and practiced
12: the commands of prayer entirely, no longer
13: shall there remain even a fraction of the sins of the accursed disbelievers.

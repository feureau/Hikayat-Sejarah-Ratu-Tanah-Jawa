# Hikayat Ratu Tanah Jawa - Scan IMG_0028

## Folio 58 (Visual Right)
1: which is named the Hidden Dot (*Nuqṭah Ghā'ib*), manifesting
2: this Hidden Dot named the Dot of Divine Light (*Nuqṭah Nūr Allāh*);
3: and this Dot of Divine Light is the letter Mīm [م] in the *Bismillāh*;
4: and this Mīm in the *Bismillāh* is what became
5: the vessel containing all that is encompassed within
6: the divine knowledge of the Glorified and Exalted, becoming
7: the seed of all praiseworthy attributes (*ṣifāt maḥmūdah*),
8: all that exist in this world and in the Hereafter;
9: the sublime light which became the seed is the Light of Allah (*Nūr Allāh*),
10: meaning that the Light of Allah is none other than our lord the Prophet
11: Muhammad, peace and blessings of Allah be upon him; while yet
12: hidden, together with the Mīm; the meaning of remaining hidden is
13: before the outward form of this Mīm was made manifest in that primordial time...

## Folio 59 (Visual Left)
1: its reality, its nature, and all the names of this Mīm; now
2: the manifestation of all these Mīms makes clear their essential forms:
3: one Mīm represents its inner reality and name, while another
4: reality encompasses all created entities that exist
5: in this world and all that exist in the Hereafter, placed
6: in submission to the decree of Gusti Allah, the Glorified
7: and Exalted, the Mighty and Majestic, the One, the Subduer (*al-Qahhār*),
8: by the clear textual proof (*naṣṣ*) of this Qur'an, which stands firmly established,
9: so that all things are brought forth in pristine clarity,
10: all that exist in this world; and all those who have
11: been predestined shall find no burden in the Hereafter;
12: all being already brought to ultimate perfection, without deficiency,
13: all creations in this world reaching unto the Hereafter.

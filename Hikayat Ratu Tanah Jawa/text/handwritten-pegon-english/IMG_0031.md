2: Prophet Muhammad, peace and blessings of Allah be upon him; third,
3: the Day of Manifestation (*al-Yawm al-Masyhūr*); the meaning of the Day of Manifestation is the sacred name
4: *Allāh* in the *Bismillāh*; the word *Allāh* in the *Bismillāh*
5: is what is named the Hidden Dot (*Nuqṭah Ghā'ib*); the meaning of the Hidden
6: Dot is Kangjeng Prophet Adam, Caliph
7: of Allah, peace be upon him; so these three Primordial Days
8: are exalted and glorified in this world reaching unto the Hereafter
9: truly: the Qur'an, Muhammad, and Adam; these three
10: are what became the outward manifestation of the divine attributes of the Essence

12: eternal, beyond all doubt, being the All-Encompassing Reality;
13: this is the cosmic sphere wherein all are set in divine balance.

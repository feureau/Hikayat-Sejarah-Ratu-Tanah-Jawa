## Folio 90 (Visual Right)
1. and she who accompanied him, Mother Eve (*Babu Hawa*); approaching the will of Gusti  
2. Allah, glorified and exalted be He, the Mighty and Majestic, the One,  
3. the Subduer (*al-Qahhār*); approaching Kangjeng Prophet Adam, he was bestowed  
4. ten scriptures, yes, ten scriptures [scrolls] which  
5. stood firm as faith, granted by the Lord of the Worlds;  
6. that is the true realization of faith; and the succession of revelation was granted,  
7. while Kangjeng Prophet Adam was in Heaven; he did not  
8. intentionally choose to eat of the fruit  
9. of the Khuldi tree; thus again it was granted  
10. by Him, glorified and exalted be He, the Heir (*al-Wārith*), unto Prophet Adam;  
11. and its radiance was cut off from  
12. this earthly world for those who desire power, its light already  
13. certain; whereas that which became the deceit of Iblis...  

## Folio 91 (Visual Left)
1. ...unto her who accompanied him, revered Mother Eve (*Babu Hawa*); approaching  
2. then the offering of the fruit of the Khuldi tree to be eaten,  
3. which Iblis [orchestrated], pretending to appear like an angel;  
4. Iblis manifested, presenting as lawful the forbidden fruit of  
5. the Khuldi tree unto Mother Eve; approaching then, Mother  
6. Eve straightway offered it unto her husband, Prophet Adam;  
7. therefore it came to pass that they fell under the decreed trial and bond,  
8. subjected to reckoning, Prophet Adam and she who accompanied him, Mother Eve;  
9. and they were cast down unto the earthly world; the meaning of  
10. this earthly world is that it borders upon the pit of Hell (*Wayl*), because  
11. their descent halted the scheming deceit of Iblis; that is,  
12. this world draws near to the perception of the perils of Hellfire  
13. (*Wayl*), [yet guided by] Kangjeng Prophet Adam, Caliph of Allah, peace be upon him.

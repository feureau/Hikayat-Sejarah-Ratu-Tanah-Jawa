# Hikayat Ratu Tanah Jawa - Scan IMG_0021

## Folio 44 (Visual Right)
1. matters which are prepared: the preparation by the Prophet; second,  
2. the preparation by the angels; third, the preparation by  
3. Iblis, cursed by Allah. Now, the meaning:  
4. the meaning, if a pilgrim receives the preparation from the Prophet,  
5. when going to Mecca and upon returning, both are blessed with goodness,  
6. upholding the commands of Islam, upright in conduct;  
7. that is indeed the person whose Islam is perfected; whereas  
8. that which becomes his righteous deed in this world is renowned  
9. and solely for the sake of Allah, and his attainment penetrates both outward  
10. and inward. As for the pilgrim who receives the preparation of the angels,  
11. that means that either after the Hajj he dies there in Mecca, or  
12. before performing the Hajj he dies there in Mecca, or along the way  
13. before reaching Mecca he dies, yet he has already attained equal blessing,  

## Folio 45 (Visual Left)
1. its perfection being completed, recorded on the preserved tablet,  
2. while outwardly his state was that of a living human being.  
3. As for the pilgrim who receives the preparation from Iblis,  
4. that means that from Mecca, after returning  
5. to his own homeland, he grows excessively arrogant and proud,  
6. feeling in his heart that his person is already perfected,  
7. considering none equal to him except his fellow  
8. pilgrims; whereas the pilgrimage has already fulfilled the command of the Lord  
9. of the Worlds, which is an obligatory pillar among the five  
10. pillars of Islam, so that it becomes one's deed  
11. in this world; yet because of his arrogance, it becomes  
12. ruined before the judgment of *Inshā' Allāh*, so that  
13. if he persists in such pride, it leads to utter loss and degradation.

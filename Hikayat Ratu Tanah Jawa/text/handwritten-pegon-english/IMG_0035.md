## Folio 75 (Visual Left)
1: O foolish and fallen ones, if you do not beware against
2: all descendants of Adam who have strayed thus
3: following Iblis, cursed by Allah; its meaning is the disbelievers
4: cursed by Allah, descendants of Adam who live in this world in such
5: conduct: foolish, heedless, and disgraced; unless they
6: are able to return unto the path of salvation once again. Behold,
7: all descendants of Adam, male and female,
8: all communities: look carefully and watchfully, all of you, in
9: this world against all traps set as snares
10: by Iblis, cursed by Allah, which entice and deceive
11: in this world; for when ruin falls upon communities, confounding
12: their minds, it is because all the tempting illusions
13: in this world are manifold, becoming snares in the path of righteousness.

## Folio B (Visual Left — Numbered `209`, Pencil `P. 66`)

**1:** Sunan Mangkurat [Pugêr], and Sunan Prabu: ❁ those seven  
**2:** are the forefathers of Java (*wiyuning Jawa*). ❁ On the eve of Thursday (*malêm Khamīs*), at the hour  
**3:** of eleven, in the second month (*sasi Rowa* / *Ruwah*), on the first day (*tanggal pisan*),  
**4:** in the Year Wawu (*tahun Wawu*), ❁ in the city of Makassar (*kutha Makasar*), at that moment (*maka mangsa*)  
**5:** by the grace of God the Exalted (*mīlāni-hi 'llāhu ta‘ālā*), the mendicant ‘Abdu 'l-Ḥamīd (*faqīr ‘Abdu 'l-Ḥamīd*)  
**6:** beheld (*mulat*) that which had become his adversary/trial (*satru*): it was exceedingly  
**7:** terrifying (*kagila-gila*), for its appearance was utterly awe-inspiring (*awèh haybah*),  
**8:** accompanied by a voice (*kang swara*); then it returned (*mêngko bali*)  
**9:** unto the place where the mendicant ‘Abdu 'l-Ḥamīd was sleeping (*nendra*).  
**10:** When the mendicant ‘Abdu 'l-Ḥamīd simultaneously heard the voice  
**11:** and simultaneously beheld an appearance of such overwhelming  
**12:** awe (*haybah*), the mendicant ‘Abdu 'l-Ḥamīd was sorely afraid (*bangêt wêdi*),  
**13:** and all the hairs of his body stood on end (*kumênyut badané kabèh*), and he recited

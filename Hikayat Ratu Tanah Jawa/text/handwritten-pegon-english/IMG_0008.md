# Hikayat Ratu Tanah Jawa — IMG_0008

## Folio 18 (Visual Right)

1: both of these latter commands; and indeed understand
2: that not all descendants of Adam who still dwell here
3: in this world, all of them heed and observe the prayer
4: at the five appointed times, both jinn and mankind.
5: For it is not permissible under the command of this religion of Islam if
6: one adheres obstinately to a religion other than
7: this religion of Islam; meaning that this religion of Islam is what must be
8: held with firm conviction, and one must not waver, but must truly
9: submit unto the two phrases of the Shahadah (the creed of faith), meaning
10: that the two phrases of the creed cannot be divided;
11: and if this conviction is held firmly in these two parts in this world,
12: unto the hereafter, surely with the Holy Prophet Muhammad,
13: who stands firm, becoming manifest in this world unto the end.

## Folio 19 (Visual Left)

1: Thus the Shahadah and the descent of this religion of Islam
2: upon the earthly world came from the Preserved Tablet (Lawḥ al-Maḥfūẓ),
3: unto the Holy Prophet Muhammad, Beloved of God—upon him
4: be peace—who was sent to spread abroad
5: the commands of this religion of Islam unto all
6: the progeny of Adam, all human beings and jinn,
7: as well as unto all the disbelievers;
8: it is not permissible for any to follow merely whatever they desire;
9: all of that is forbidden; all must
10: implement this final command, namely the religion
11: of Islam, this last dispensation willed by the Lord God,
12: Blessed and Exalted, the Mighty and Majestic, the One, the Subduer (al-Qahhār);
13: that is to say, the command that must be believed in and obeyed.

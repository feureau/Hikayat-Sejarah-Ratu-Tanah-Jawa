# Hikayat Ratu Tanah Jawa — IMG_0015

## Folio 32 (Visual Right)

1. the One, the Subduer (al-Wāhid al-Qahhār), thus your spiritual sight remains steadfast  
2. and firmly established is your place with the Lord of the Worlds, without ceasing;  
3. the entry unto Paradise is assured because it is firmly rooted;  
4. remember it well in this world: namely the hidden remembrance (dhikr ghayb)  
5. of the most concealed of the unseen, termed the secret (khafī), namely  
6. facing with the soul, having discernment over those who falter,  
7. throughout the lifespan of mankind;  
8. because the unseen remembrance of the hidden is practiced  
9. in such a manner of inward voice, audible only to the discerning heart;  
10. perfected are those who have attained this spiritual illumination;  
11. and those united with divine favor; it cannot be grasped by worldly men,  
12. but only by rare and sincere souls, seven or five or  
13. three, nor can it be imparted except unto one chosen soul.

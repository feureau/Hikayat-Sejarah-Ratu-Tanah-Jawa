## Folio 28 (Visual Right)

1: that which is left empty [circle] surely [circle] does not become
2: the lineage; then let them meet with
3: you, Pangeran Suryengalaga by name; [circle] because
4: at that time, when all things are prepared, then immediately report
5: unto you who were fallen; [circle] as for the people
6: who are with Abdul Hamid, who hold the duty firmly,
7: they merely surrender the letter whose contents declare: [circle]
8: 'O Abdul Hamid, behold those who fell, unto
9: you they bear witness, directed to seek honor through
10: you; [circle] if you offer assistance, let it become your petition, that those
11: who fell will surely follow
12: you, indeed, if you advance;' [circle] turn
13: yourself, O Abdul Hamid, towards those who have fallen;

## Folio 29 (Visual Left)

1: it is certain that nothing is permitted except absolute obedience [circle]
2: unto the decree of God, Blessed and Exalted, O Abdul Hamid,
3: which has been revealed unto you; [circle] because what serves as your witness
4: is the Shaykh who fulfills what God, Blessed
5: and Exalted, has willed: that Abdul Hamid, when the land of Java was
6: laid waste; [circle] if it comes to the matter of Pajang, O Abdul Hamid,
7: you shall answer: 'It is to abolish extortion and unjust levies upon
8: the land of Java, throughout all its administration until now;'
9: this is [ornamental vignette] following the deep inner essence
10: of the words spoken at Tegalrejo: 'O Abdul Hamid, if
11: anyone inquires why you remained among those who fell, [circle]
12: when Prince Purbaya was present, you shall
13: answer: "I have returned; hold fast to your petition with conviction."'

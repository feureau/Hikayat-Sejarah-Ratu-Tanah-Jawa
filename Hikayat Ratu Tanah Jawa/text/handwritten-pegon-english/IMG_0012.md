# Hikayat Ratu Tanah Jawa — IMG_0012

## Folio 26 (Visual Right)

1. the sensation of spiritual yearning, when one has rendered honor unto thanksgiving;  
2. for thanksgiving becomes the contemplation of the Essence of God (Dhāt Allāh);  
3. that is what becomes the contemplation of the Divine Acts (Af'ālullāh), because  
4. the virtue of thanksgiving gathers all focus upon the Essence of God,  
5. which is vast within, and does not require calculating the payment of thanks;  
6. it does not distract, nor is it done merely for the sake of giving thanks;  
7. that is what becomes firm within you. For indeed, the virtue of  
8. all fulfilled obligations (qaḍā') concentrates upon the Acts of God (Af'ālullāh),  
9. because all manner of qaḍā' is known  
10. to be a spiritual striving and exalts the inward meaning;  
11. in the hereafter, all of these fulfilled obligations  
12. remain steadfast as divine acts; for the confirmation of paying  
13. the qaḍā' is established in every state of spiritual contemplation.

3. of you: "For what reason do you have no  
4. request, Abdul Hamid?" If

7. no request, it was improbable that any petition  
8. should be asked of you, Abdul Hamid; you may say so;

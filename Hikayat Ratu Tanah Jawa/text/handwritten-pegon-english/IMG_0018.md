## Folio 38 (Visual Right)
1. is the patih, who is held firmly by you in obedience to commands,  
2. the court of the ruler; if he is likened unto your elder brother,  
3. Danureja, that is right, because truly it is certain that it becomes  
4. your resolution and intent. Whereupon at that time, Abdul  
5. Hamid: Danureja has already grown reluctant if he is to  
6. follow you, so that there would be no end to the king's distress;  
7. as for the matter that has become your resolve,  
8. my brethren, indeed you speak truly, Danureja, if  
9. you are guided by your father, the revered prince,  
10. his state being overwhelmed with grief, surely if you are defeated  
11. you shall fall; and as for what you shall demand from that grief,  
12. even if Danureja is lost, yet it is because  
13. he causes continuous suffering, this Danureja."  

## Folio 39 (Visual Left)
1. Its light is in the manifestation upon the Essence of Allah (*Dhāt Allāh*),  
2. after which the progeny of Adam are gathered  
3. upon this world, where it becomes the witnessing  
4. of the Essence of Allah, which is the saint (*walī*).  
5. Thus it becomes one, united in two aspects:  
6. namely between the *walī* and the true believers (*mu'minīn*), placed  
7. as the outward manifestation of the Essence whose existence is necessary (*Wājib al-Wujūd*),  
8. which is the *walī* and the believers who are deemed  
9. to possess the rich divine attributes of Allah,  
10. reality (*ḥaqīqī*) being the *walī* and the believers,  
11. witnessing that the righteous believers are acknowledged as possessing  
12. the divine self-sufficiency (*Ghanī*) of Gusti Allah, the Glorified and Exalted, the Mighty,  
13. the One, the Subduer (*al-Qahhār*), who is fourfold in manifestation.

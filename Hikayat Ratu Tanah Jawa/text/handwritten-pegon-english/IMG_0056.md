## Folio 116 (Visual Right)
1: In accordance with this spiritual practice, both of them [conjoined]... Praise be to Allah (*al-ḥamdu li'llāh*), completely:
2: becoming two yet not two, one yet not one;
3: this is the way of Islam in this world unto the Hereafter with certainty.
4: Here 'Abd al-Hamid, regarding that which is practiced in this world:
5: this is the spiritual knowledge of Gusti Allah, glorified and exalted be He;
6: these are three essential principles that remain, namely
7: their forms: the seed of knowledge is the Creed (*Shahādah*); the seed of
8: action is intention (*Niyyah*); the seed of spiritual acceptance is Faith (*Īmān*);
9: the inner meaning of the Creed is the Good Word (*Kalimah Ṭayyibah*); that
10: which is called the Good Word is: "The summit of *Lā ilāha illallāh,*
11: *Muḥammadur Rasūlullāh*"; he who possesses such an apex
12: is the Prophet Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace; it is incumbent upon all
13: the community of Islam, male believers and female...

## Folio 117 (Visual Left)
1: ...believers, to utter and affirm that Good Word,
2: namely the Creed; that is the meaning of what is called
3: the Good Word (*Kalimah Ṭayyibah*); therefore it is obligatory for male believers
4: and female believers to hold fast and preserve
5: this Good Word; its meaning is the guidance of remembrance (*dhikr*), the command of
6: Kangjeng the Messenger of Allah, an obligation upon Muslim men and Muslim
7: women, that you practice the profound spiritual disciplines in complete readiness;
8: the meaning of practicing the complete spiritual discipline is that which has been
9: transmitted through the established tradition of spiritual initiation (*talaqqī*), derived from Kangjeng the Messenger of Allah,
10: having rightful authority over all aspects of practice,
11: in all forms of remembrance and the stations (*darajat*) of remembrance; because
12: this is what stands as an exalted, unshakeable pillar in this world unto
13: the Hereafter with certainty, if you have obtained the divine assistance...

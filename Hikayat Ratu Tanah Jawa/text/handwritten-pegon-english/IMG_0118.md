## Folio B (Visual Left — Numbered `241`, Pencil `P. 114`)

**1:** all those filthy impurities (*sakèhé rêrêgêd ya iku*), and as for the dark rainclouds (*méga mêndhung*)
**2:** that veil and screen (*kang ngaling-ngalingi*) the sun (*srêngéngé*), because that fire
**3:** rises up into the sky (*munggah marang langit*), it is certain that all your world becomes radiant and bright (*pêsthi lamun padhang jagad-*
**4:** *-ira kabèh*). Yet only if its wood is dry (*lamun garing kayuné*); if its wood is wet/green (*lamun tulus*
**5:** *kayuné*), that fire is unable to blaze up (*ora bisa murub gêni iku*), so that
**6:** it is unable to dispel those filthy impurities (*ora bisa ngilangaké rêrêgêd*) or
**7:** those dark rainclouds (*méga mêndhung*), because those filthy impurities are the likeness
**8:** of wax (*upama malêm*): when struck by fire (*lamun kêna gêni*), it is certain that the wax melts away and vanishes (*mêsthi lamun ilang malêm*
**9:** *iku*). Then, once all the forms of filthy impurities (*sarupané rêrêgêd*) have vanished,
**10:** all of your world becomes radiant and bright (*dadi padhang jagadira iku kabèh*)
**11:** by the radiance of the light of faith (*soroting cahyané īmān*); it is already
**12:** certain (*wus pêsthi*) that you behold the possessions of [God], glorified
**13:** and exalted is He (*wêruh sira marang kagungané subḥānahu wa-ta‘ālā*), which are unseen (*kang ghā’ib-ghā’ib*). Therefore, take care that you do not become lax/attached (*aja pakêt sira*)

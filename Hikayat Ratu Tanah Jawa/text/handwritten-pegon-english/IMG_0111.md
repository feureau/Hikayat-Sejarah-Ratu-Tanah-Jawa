## Folio B (Visual Left — Numbered `227`, Pencil `P. 86`)

**1:** (*-ruk*); yet this world (*dunyā*) is the possession of [God], glorified and exalted is He (*kagungané subḥānahu wa-ta‘ālā*),
**2:** who still holds it in invisible trust (*maksih gina’ib*), meaning in Heaven (*ing swarga*); whereas in this world (*dunyā*),
**3:** it is already certain (*wus pêsthi*) that nothing may be without a boundary/limit (*ora kêna barang ana wêtê-*
**4:** *-sé*), because this world serves as the intermediate realm (*antarané*) between Heaven (*swarga*)
**5:** and Hell (*lan naraka*). Whereas what is called "inward marriage" (*nikāḥ bāṭin*)
**6:** consists of two matters: first, while still in the loins/seed (*kala dhurriyyah*); and second,
**7:** the entering of the spirit into the body (*panjinging rūḥ marang badan*). Therefore, the desire (*karê-*
**8:** *-pé*) of Iblis in his teaching (*oléhé muruk*) a man and a woman
**9:** is merely that so long as they mutually desire one another (*anggeré padha karêpé*), that is what is called "inward marriage" (*nikāḥ bāṭin*
**10:** *arané*), and there is no reckoning for it (*tan ana étungané*)—*yā Ghafūra 'l-hamm* [O Forgiver of sorrow/care!].
**11:** ۞ Now this is the chapter explaining (*bāb pratélané*) the root
**12:** of acceptance (*witing panarima*), which is faith (*īmān*): the meaning of what is
**13:** called faith (*īmān*) is the faith of *"I believe in God"* (*īmān āmantu bi-'llāh*);

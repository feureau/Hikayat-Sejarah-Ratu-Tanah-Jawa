## Folio 56 (Visual Right)
1: As for those one hundred and three books, if they are
2: recited or touched...
3: in their completeness, because their contents were delivered through
4: intermediary transmission, all descending from the Preserved
5: Tablet unto the world of this earth, Angel
6: Gabriel, peace be upon him, being sent by Gusti
7: Allah, the Glorified and Exalted, the Mighty and Majestic, the One,
8: the Subduer (*al-Qahhār*). But the descent of the Book of the Qur'an
9: from the Preserved Tablet unto the world
10: of this earth, unto Kangjeng Prophet Muhammad, peace and blessings of Allah be upon him,
11: did not rely solely on intermediate angelic transmission,
12: but was the direct, uncreated Word of Allah (*Kalāmullāh*); therefore it becomes
13: forbidden (*ḥarām*) to touch or recite this Qur'an

## Folio 57 (Visual Left)
1: if one's physical body is not yet purified. Now this Qur'an
2: consists of thirty parts (*juz'*); these thirty parts
3: are what become the seed and essence of all the
4: one hundred and three scriptures; while that which becomes the seed
5: of the Book of the Qur'an is the *Fātiḥah*; that which becomes
6: the seed of the *Fātiḥah* is the *Bismillāh*; that which becomes
7: the seed of the *Bismillāh* is indeed
8: the dot of the letter Bā' [ب] of the *Bismillāh*. As for the dot of the Bā'
9: of the *Bismillāh*, it is the Will of Gusti Allah, the Glorified
10: and Exalted, the Mighty and Majestic, the One, the Subduer (*al-Qahhār*), which
11: becomes the seed of all the encompassed divine knowledge
12: of the Lord of the Worlds, all that exists in this world reaching
13: unto the Hereafter, truly residing within this dot beneath the Bā' of the *Bismillāh*.

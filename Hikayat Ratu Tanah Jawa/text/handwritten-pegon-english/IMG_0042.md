## Folio 89 (Visual Left)

2: the Lord of the Worlds was directed against Prophet Adam and his descent;
3: then straightway his deceitful entrapment was exposed at the boundary of torment;
4: approaching how the petition of Iblis was permitted, in that manner
5: it was allowed to test Adam; approaching the will of Gusti
6: Allah, glorified and exalted be He, Kangjeng Prophet Adam
7: and she who accompanied him, Mother Eve (*Babu Hawa*), were then willed
8: to reside in Heaven in fullness, before the Lord of the Worlds;
9: then came the envious intention of Iblis, cursed of Allah,
10: who could not praise or revere Prophet Adam while he remained
11: dwelling in Heaven with all blessings, accompanied [by Eve]; and exceedingly
12: burning was the fiery malice of that Iblis towards Kangjeng Prophet Adam,
13: seeing him enjoying glory and felicity radiant throughout Heaven.

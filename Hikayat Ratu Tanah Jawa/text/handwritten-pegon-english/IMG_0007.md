## Folio 16 (Visual Right)

1: "Muhammad is the Messenger of God; and those with him are forceful against the disbelievers,
2: merciful among themselves. You see them bowing and prostrating,
3: seeking bounty from God and His pleasure. Their mark
4: is on their faces from the trace of prostration. That
5: is their description in the Torah. And their description in the Gospel
6: is as a plant which produces its offshoots and strengthens them so they grow firm
7: and stand upon their stalks, delighting the sowers,
8: so that He may enrage by them the disbelievers. God has promised those who believe
9: and do righteous deeds among them forgiveness and a great
10: reward." [Qur'an 48:29]; wherefore all of the
11: progeny of Adam who still remain in Islam, both men and women,
12: all of you should turn unto the Messenger of the Lord
13: God, Blessed and Exalted, the Mighty and Majestic, the One,

## Folio 17 (Visual Left)

1: the Subduer (al-Qahhār); that is, the Holy Prophet Muhammad, the Messenger of God—
2: may God bless him and grant him peace—who was sent down
3: to descend upon this world as a guide;
4: the Holy Prophet, under the command of the Lord of the Worlds, brought down
5: both together as a support: that is, the divine command brought down,
6: namely the Word of God (Kalāmullāh) in its ultimate reality, and
7: the truest directives, both being utterly pure,
8: all of those commands in their entirety; that is to say,
9: the religion of Islam, by the decree of the Lord God, Blessed and Exalted,
10: the Mighty and Majestic, the One, the Subduer (al-Wāhid al-Qahhār), and
11: it suffices above all other ways; namely this religion of Islam,
12: above all other religions whatsoever, in this world
13: and in the hereafter, surely unto those who follow it.

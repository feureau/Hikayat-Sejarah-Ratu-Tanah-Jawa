## Frontispiece (Folio 1)

*[Full-page mystical calligraphic illumination in the form of a Tree of Life / Gunungan / Lampstand bearing Arabic inscriptions of the Shahadah and Basmala]*

1: [Top finial / apex] Al-Hamdu lillāh (Praise be to God)
2: [Upper branch cartouche] Rabbi al-'Ālamīn (Lord of the Worlds)
3: [Central branch transom] Lā ilāha illā Allāh (There is no god but Allah)
4: [Trunk / stem inscription] Muhammadun Rasūlu Allāh (Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah)
5: [Pedestal upper tier] Bismillāhi al-Rahmāni (In the name of God, the Most Gracious)
6: [Pedestal lower tier] al-Rahīm (the Most Merciful)
7: [Base inscription / floral vignette] Wa bihi nasta'īnu 'alā umūri al-dunyā wa al-dīn (And from Him we seek aid in the affairs of the world and religion)

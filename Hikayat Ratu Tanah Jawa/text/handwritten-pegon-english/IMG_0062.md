## Folio 128 (Visual Right)
1. ...so that nothing is left out, neither outside nor inside; and when  
2. the course of your breath is completed, then let it exit  
3. gently and unhurriedly through the mouth,  
4. because that already belongs to the category of the breath of divine inspiration (*ilham*).  
5. Then, when all the degrees (*darajat*)  
6. of that remembrance have been completed, return to reciting *istighfār* (seeking forgiveness)  
7. thirty times, and *ṣalawāt* (blessings upon the Prophet) thirty times; then perform the prostration  
8. of drawing near (*sujūd taqarrub*); after rising from prostration, recite the *Fātiḥah*;  
9. after the *Fātiḥah*, recite *Sūrah al-Ikhlāṣ* three  
10. times, *al-Falaq* three times, *an-Nās* three times, and the *Fātiḥah* three times,  
11. intending to present them as a spiritual offering (*ruba*) to your first teacher  
12. and to all the teachers of your teacher; and prostrate in this  
13. manner, with the words: "O my master, and the masters...  

## Folio 129 (Visual Left)
1. ...of my master, all of them, I present the reward  
2. of my prayer unto you, and my praise and remembrance  
3. unto you; may they be accepted by Allah and  
4. by the Messenger of Allah, and may you ever remain  
5. my refuge and shelter in this world unto  
6. the Hereafter with certainty, and may I obtain the help of Allah  
7. and the grace of Allah." Then rise from prostration and recite the *Fātiḥah*;  
8. then prostrate once more, intending an offering (*ruba*) to the Four Companions (*Ṣaḥābah Papat*)  
9. and all the Companions; as for the wording, it is the same as  
10. that for your teacher; after rising from  
11. prostration, recite the *Fātiḥah* again, intending an offering (*ruba*) to  
12. Kangjeng the Messenger of Allah, prostrating a third time; thus is  
13. its intention: "O Lover of Allah, O Muhammad, Messenger of Allah...

## Folio 80 (Visual Right)
1. Such is the manifestation of the children of Adam; thus those who are disbelievers  
2. subsequently follow [falsehood]; they do not understand the true inner meaning,  
3. which is following the analogies and deceits of Iblis; thus  
4. the end of those disbelievers, on account of their worldly discernment,  
5. is that they are overtaken and snared by Iblis, the cursed of Allah; approaching  
6. what brings ruin to all these descendants of Adam, brought close to Iblis;  
7. this causes the loss and spiritual death of the descendants of Adam  
8. who are brought close to disbelief, because worldly understanding has become  
9. their false sovereign, so that the disbeliever belongs to Iblis, the cursed of  
10. Allah, day and night; the disbeliever has become internally bound in his heart  
11. unto Iblis, cursed of Allah; thus from former times,  
12. void and empty of truth, unlike all descendants of Adam who  
13. remain steadfast in Islam, approaching ultimate bliss and deliverance...

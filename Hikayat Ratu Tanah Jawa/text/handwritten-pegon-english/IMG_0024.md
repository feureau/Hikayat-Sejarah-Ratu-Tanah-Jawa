## Folio 51 (Visual Left)
1: by faith: the meaning of faith here is being encompassed by the overarching
2: command of the Lord of the Worlds (*Rabb al-'Ālamīn*) in this world reaching
3: into the Hereafter, truly distinguishing what is right and wrong, evil
4: and good, fortunate and unfortunate in this world
5: unto the Hereafter; all these upon the earth
6: which become the manifestation of the command of the Lord of the Worlds,
7: the First, the Last, the Manifest (*al-Ẓāhir*), the Inward (*al-Bāṭin*), reaching
8: even unto the Day of Resurrection, all having already been determined by the Will
9: of Gusti Allah, the Glorified and Exalted, who is Ever-Watchful.

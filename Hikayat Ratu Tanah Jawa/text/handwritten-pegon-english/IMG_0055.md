## Folio 114 (Visual Right)
1: *Allāh, Allāh, Allāh, Allāh, Allāh, Allāh, Allāh,*
2: *Allāh, Allāh, Allāh, Allāh, Allāh, Allāh, Allāh;*
3: day and night, steadfast and unchanging, having
4: repeated utterance many thousands of times; thus firmly established
5: in the human being who is properly disposed and concentrated;
6: powerful in his faith, which obtains divine forgiveness;
7: the noble human being who aligns within the sanctuary
8: of his inner retreat, forgiven by the Eternal (*Ghafara 'l-Ṣamad*); the conclusion of
9: the section on Dhikr and Wirid: reaching the outward practice,
10: after the Sunnah Ba'diyah prayer, pronouncing the remembrance (*Dhikr*)
11: of Allah repeatedly, together with the outward remembrance after the obligatory (*farḍ*)
12: prayers, and all beings bow in prostration,
13: and with the wirid at the time of the dawn prayer (*ṣubḥ*), reaching its fulfillment...

## Folio 115 (Visual Left)
1: ...that is Kangjeng Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace; therefore
2: there are two phrases of the Creed (*Shahādah*), realizing
3: the true relationship between Servant and Lord (*Kawula lan Gusti*),
4: for 'Abd al-Hamid; it is not permissible if
5: one separates the two, the Prophet Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace,
6: and the Lord of the Worlds; for if one separates
7: the two, the summit of your contemplation becomes void,
8: because it falls into division; furthermore,
9: if they are separated; yet in truth, if they are regarded as merely one
10: alone, that too is void, because it confuses
11: the servant, 'Abd al-Hamid; what is perfect in contemplation
12: is that the two are conjoined into one, yet not one nor simply two,
13: distinguishing the two, so that one remains truly among the people of [Tauhid]...

## Folio B (Visual Left — Numbered `215`)

**1:** [and] what is meant in that manner (*kang dèn-sudukakên mangkono têgêsé*) is that the prophet (*nabī*), the saint (*walī*),  
**2:** and the true believer (*mu’min*)—those three have now already become one (*saiki wus nunggal*)  
**3:** in that; then after the war in this world (*ba‘da ing prang ing dunyā iku*), that which  
**4:** raises up (*nêdêgakên*) the intention (*niyaté*) becomes a mirror/example (*pangiloné*)  
**5:** unto all the forms of the descendants of Adam (*sarupané dhurriyyah Ādam*) who are Muslims (*padha Islām*),  
**6:** both men and women (*lanang wadon*)—all of them who still remain (*maksih ana*)  
**7:** in the world (*ing dunyā*), regarding what is right and wrong (*ing bênêr luputé*), or what is evil and  
**8:** good (*ing ala lawan bêciké*) among all the forms of the descendants of Adam (*sarupané dhurriyyah Ād-*  
**9:** *-am*) who dwell therein; indeed, that which serves as the witness (*kang mênêng dadi shā-*  
**10:** *-hid*) distinguishing a beast (*ḥayawān*) from a true believer (*mu’min*), and which confirms (*nêtêpakên*)  
**11:** the union of servant and Lord (*kêtêmuning kawula Gusti*), or of the saint and true believer (*walī mu’min*) unto  
**12:** the Lord God, glorified and exalted is He (*subḥānahu wa-ta‘ālā*), who is Mighty and Majestic (*‘azza wa-jalla*),  
**13:** who is the One, the Subduer (*Wāḥidu 'l-Qahhār*), is without using any intermediary (*lawan ora nganggo ba‘da* / * lantaran*)

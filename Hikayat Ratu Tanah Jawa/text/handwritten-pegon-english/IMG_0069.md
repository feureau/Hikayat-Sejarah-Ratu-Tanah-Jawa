## Folio 142 (Visual Right)
1. ...unto those who are slain in the path of Allah: "[they are] dead, nay, they are alive,  
2. but you perceive it not..." (Q. 2:154); whoever among  
3. all the descendants of Adam who are still Muslims,  
4. let all of you aspire to attain the preparation  
5. of perfection in this world; the meaning of the path of  
6. perfection is this: the person who attains the preparation  
7. of fighting in the path of Allah (*jihad fī sabīlillāh*); because they do not die, those who attain  
8. the preparation of fighting in the path of Allah, but rather are alive (*ḥayyun*) in both  
9. realms, meaning possessing two lives, thus attaining  
10. two lives: in this world and in the Hereafter;  
11. possessing two states; and if the Lord  
12. of the Worlds has not yet willed your departure, then  
13. in this material world you become a spiritual mendicant (*faqīr*)...

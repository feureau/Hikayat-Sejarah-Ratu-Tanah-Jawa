# Hikayat Ratu Tanah Jawa - Scan IMG_0019

## Folio 40 (Visual Right)
1: Here is the fourfold Will: the Will here is the Mighty
2: manifestation of life, present in the spirit (*rūḥ*),
3: present in the soul (*sukma*), which is your true reality,
4: meaning the inner bodily self; where indeed is that
5: which is named "man"? It is not something
6: that can be held apart from the realization of its Lord.
7: It is the perfect human being (*al-Insān al-Kāmil*),
8: the human being who has attained the true reality of man;
9: that is the one who has received divine revelation from
10: the Lord of the Worlds (*Rabb al-'Ālamīn*). The faith of his inward being
11: brings benefit to all his outward limbs and senses;
12: such is the faith of man who has reached
13: perfection, who is elevated and esteemed like a master.

## Folio 41 (Visual Left)
[Upper Margin: Glorified and Exalted is He, the Mighty... bewildered and confused, filled with the curse of Allah and dispersed.]
1: Among all the descendants of Adam who are
2: mature, having attained discernment over all things towards
3: everything in this world, all which is apportioned with
4: certainty, as stated in the Hadith of the Messenger:
5: The Prophet, peace and blessings of Allah be upon him, said:
6: "Whosoever knows himself knows his Lord; and whosoever knows
7: his Lord, while remaining ignorant of his own self..."
8: Behold, all the descendants of Adam who are
9: Muslims, male and female, all of them who still [abide in faith].

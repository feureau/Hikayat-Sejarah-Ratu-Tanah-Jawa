## Folio 30 (Visual Right)

1: decreed by Him, Blessed and Exalted; [circle] fourthly,
2: the recitation of the ten lines of remembrance; if you wish
3: to advance, walk with firm faith, your foot
4: on the right side, avoiding unbelief, while the left foot,
5: the obligation of this advancement comprises three things which
6: are held in certainty: walking to Friday prayer, turning away from disbelievers,
7: and if reminded of all unbelievers who mock your suffering,
8: you die; thirdly, marching in the holy struggle in the cause of God (sabīlillāh); fifthly,
9: the remembrance of the ten allegorical lessons that guide you;
10: let there be no mistake in applying them unto the spiritual
11: path wherein the Lord leads you towards Paradise;
12: for it is certain that Paradise cannot be attained
13: except through the Omnipotent Power (Qudrat) and Divine Will (Irādat) of Him, Blessed and Exalted.

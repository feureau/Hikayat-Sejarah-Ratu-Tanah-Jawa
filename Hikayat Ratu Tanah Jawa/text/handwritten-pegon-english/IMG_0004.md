## Folio 10 (Visual Right)

1. in the grave continuously until the hereafter; namely Satan,  
2. when he approaches the folk of Islam,  
3. lest if in that manner you become heedless and do not  
4. repent of all your heedlessness, and if you do not  
5. preserve your faith, then all of your deeds  
6. shall be nullified for as long as you remain  
7. in this world; and if you are thus, it is indeed certain  
8. that your faith and your deeds in this world shall be rendered void,  
9. and you shall find no place wherein you may be restored to honor again  
10. in Paradise; because all of the  
11. progeny of Adam who live as Muslims remaining here in this world,  
12. all of them, except those who find a way upon the path in the cause  
13. of God (sabīlillāh), namely faith and righteous deeds that stand firm,

## Folio 11 (Visual Left)

1. meaning that when you have reflected upon God,  
2. and do not plead frivolously, truly, when you have  
3. reckoned yourself, lest you fail to restrain yourself against  
4. the afflictions of temptation, when you have guarded yourself  
5. against those sorrows, lest  
6. your faith be lost, which means  
7. guarding your deeds here in this world;  
8. that is, you must know your conduct  
9. in guarding against your carnal passions, meaning the lower desires  
10. opposed to the sacred law, because they do not conform  
11. to the commands of the scriptural proofs and the Prophetic traditions, and your spirit  
12. becomes tainted because of Satan, accursed of God;  
13. and therefore hold firmly likewise unto the obligatory duties,

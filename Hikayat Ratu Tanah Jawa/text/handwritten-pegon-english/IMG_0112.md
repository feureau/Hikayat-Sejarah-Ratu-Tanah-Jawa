**3:** the commands of the Lord of the Worlds (*paréntahé Rabbi 'l-‘Ālamīn*) that are within
**4:** the Qur’ān, all of them: whatever is commanded (*kang kinon*) is carried out (*dèn-lako-*

**11:** rewarded (*kang ginanjar*) with all forms of the contents of the realm of this world (*sarupané isining ‘ālam dunyā*) is indeed
**12:** the human being (*manungsa*), which serves as the sign of good fortune or misfortune (*tandha bêja cilaka-*)

**5:** both the outward and the inward (*kang ẓāhir kang bāṭin*), are already certainly (*wus pêsthi*) none
**6:** other than the Power and Will (*qudrat irādaté*) of [God], glorified and exalted is He (*subḥānahu wa-ta‘ālā*), who is the One,

**9:** patience (*ṣabar*) is to be patient when struck by a trial (*kênèng coba*); the meaning of gratitude (*shukur*)

**11:** is to be content with the decree of God (*marang ḥukumé Allāh*), because

## Folio 3 (Visual Left)

1: This is the clear exposition (Hādhā al-bayān) concerning
2: mankind, humanity, and the secrets
3: of human life and all the progeny of Adam,
4: both male and female, who have embraced Islam in this
5: world, turning in submission towards the destiny decreed by our Lord
6: God, Blessed and Exalted, the Mighty and Majestic, the One,
7: the Absolute, the Subduer (al-Wāhid al-Qahhār), who brought forth mankind and perfected
8: its creation, revealing the true reality of human existence
9: that was fashioned into completion. And when it was brought into being,
10: all this occurred through the Omnipotent Power (Qudrat) and Divine Will (Irādat) of our Lord God,
11: Blessed and Exalted, the Mighty and Majestic, the One,
12: the Subduer (al-Qahhār), who created mankind

## Folio 76 (Visual Right)
1: all of your conduct and steps, whenever you
2: have attained the age of maturity (*bāligh*), all of you in this earthly world,
3: so that all of you possess understanding towards the
4: divine majesty of the Lord of the Worlds, which encompasses all
5: things; but if you lack vigilance, all of you,
6: towards the worldly entities that exist in this realm,
7: then this becomes the source of two diverging paths:
8: among all worldly things, because amidst the temptations of the adversary,
9: they point towards one of two paths;
10: now, whichever descendant of Adam,
11: having reached maturity, steps forward steadfastly
12: in faith, such a child of Adam attains supreme felicity
13: in this world reaching unto the Hereafter truly, because...

## Folio 77 (Visual Left)
1: their spiritual vision becomes radiant in this world reaching
2: unto the Hereafter truly; nothing can veil them, meaning
3: that the Prophet, the Saint (*Walī*), and the Believers (*Mu'minīn*) are illuminated without obscurity,
4: under the shelter of divine self-sufficiency: the Prophet, the Saint, and the Believers;
5: and regarding Islam, if one examines the state of Muslims,
6: it is manifested in diverse conditions among all mankind:
7: there are those who remain upright, true Muslims; there are sinful Muslims (*fāsiq*);
8: there are apostate Muslims (*murtadd*); there are hypocritical Muslims (*munāfiq*);
9: and there are those who fall completely into disbelief (*kāfir*); none of them
10: are other than the descendants of Adam, all of them;
11: their outward conditions in this world towards worldly things,
12: beyond these categories; it is certain
13: that the Shari'ah is what serves as the true guide unto [salvation].

# Hikayat Ratu Tanah Jawa - Scan IMG_0041

## Folio 86 (Visual Right)
1: the decree of striving in the way of Allah (*Sabīlillāh*) in this world for the descendants of Adam who
2: are not touched by the decree of unlawful death; if they are
3: called dead, that is the natural death of Adam's descendants who
4: are upright, so that it becomes contemplated and intended in this world;
5: in the hereafter, their upright portion becomes their true origin; for
6: it became an affliction unto Kangjeng Prophet Adam before the Heir (*al-Wārith*),
7: the Lord of the Worlds, while the bonds of Islam are an immense
8: favor; Prophet Adam and Mother Eve (*Babu Hawa*), when both of them ate
9: outwardly of the fruit of that Khuldi tree, were afflicted; approaching
10: Kangjeng Prophet Adam, Caliph of Allah, peace be upon him,
11: therefore it truly happened that he experienced shame and trial in that manner;
12: this was because of the entreaty and petition of Iblis, cursed of
13: Allah, which had been granted by Gusti Allah, glorified and exalted...

## Folio 87 (Visual Left)
1: ...that for one thousand years both of them were subject to reckoning [as if] at the verge of Hellfire
2: (*Wayl*); then straightway the transgression of Kangjeng
3: Prophet Adam and revered Mother Eve (*Babu Hawa*) was forgiven, and they were summoned,
4: called to return into Heaven once more,
5: by the grace of Gusti Allah, glorified and exalted be He,
6: the Mighty and Majestic, the One, the Subduer (*al-Qahhār*), decreeing also
7: for all the descendants of Kangjeng Prophet Adam;
8: His will is that those who have been liberated and granted nobility
9: are named among the holy saints (*Awliyā'*) and the faithful believers (*Mu'minīn*);
10: and for all creations, whether Muslim or disbeliever, His will reaches
11: into this earthly world throughout days and nights,
12: until death, when their return is directed unto their destined place;
13: by establishing and confirming it, except that there are two distinct paths...

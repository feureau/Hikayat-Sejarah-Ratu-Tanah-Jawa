## Folio 112 (Visual Right)
1: attaining ultimate contemplation, turning wholly unto Him, glorified and
2: exalted be He; you become the emissary of your own innermost summit; when heard
3: by others, it is not yours alone, but in truth
4: you feel nothing else save the phrase *Lā ilāha*
5: *illallāh*; my heart is my true existence (*qalbī wujūdiyā*), truly the summit of your being;
6: the silent, tranquil remembrance (*dhikr jatmika*), which you have chosen, reaching completion;
7: having realized this, you no longer feel anything else; if
8: your remembrance alters, if you feel other than God, then your remembrance
9: becomes void; for if you do so, in truth it is forbidden, becoming
10: disbelief (*kufr*), because you have fallen away unto that which is
11: other than Him; this indeed is the form of remembrance which constitutes true realization (*wujūdiyā*):
12: *Lā ilāha illallāhu, Allāhu, Allāhu, Allāhu, Allāhu,*
13: *Allāhu Huwa, Allāhu Huwa, Allāhu Huwa, Allāhu, Lā...*

## Folio 113 (Visual Left)
1: ...seven tiers, and all of them entirely, in every manifestation,
2: are certain; if both illuminations are established,
3: in all realms, that light illuminates and stands firm;
4: and by His will, He is the One, the Unique (*Aḥad, Wāḥid*);
5: the Attribute of Allah is the Encompassing Attribute (*Muḥīṭ*), which
6: brought forth His scriptures; indeed, the Attribute of Muḥīṭ encompasses all commands
7: unto all creations; the meaning is, indeed the Holy Qur'an
8: stands as the ultimate rectitude, for Allah is described as *al-Muḥīṭ* (the All-Encompassing);
9: therefore, in the dispensation of the Qur'an,
10: it is all-encompassing, because the Holy Qur'an is the manifestation
11: of the Divine Essence of Allah (*Dhāt Allāh*), the Unseen (*al-Ghāyib*), before the creation
12: of all forms, and after the passing of all forms;
13: unto that, indeed, it is all-encompassing, the supreme guidance...

## Folio 122 (Visual Right)

6: which must not be more or less, repeated three times;
7: then recite the remembrance of the Divine Essence (*Dhāt*), with the same
8: count, and the remembrance of the Divine Attribute (*Ism al-Ṣifah*); then
9: recite the remembrance of the Hidden Name (*Ism al-Ghayb*), likewise with the same count;
10: then the hidden remembrance within the Unseen of Allah (*Allāh ghayb*),
11: likewise with the same count, repeated three times;
12: the negation of that hidden essence, as the saying goes, is not permissible except through
13: the spiritual instruction of a master who guides and directs to its proper station.

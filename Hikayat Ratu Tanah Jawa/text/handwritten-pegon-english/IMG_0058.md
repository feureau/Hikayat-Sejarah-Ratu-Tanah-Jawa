## Folio 120 (Visual Right)
1. ...serving as the exemplar of the aforementioned person; such indeed is the form  
2. of a person who completes his spiritual conduct toward the presence of his Lord's receptacle,  
3. the ordinance of Islam for true believers (*wong mu'minīn kang ḥaqīqī*);  
4. that which is upright is that you attend to both. And if you follow  
5. such a person, do not harbor any doubt whatsoever  
6. in establishing your spiritual conduct upon all of his guidance;  
7. for in truth the human being who serves as an exemplar  
8. unto [Allah], glorified and exalted be He, in this world unto the Hereafter  
9. with certainty. This is the explanation of  
10. the remembrance of negation and affirmation (*dhikr nafy wa-ithbāt*), and its count, according to  
11. the times of the twenty-five hours and the repetitions of remembrance,  
12. one of which is the Good Word (*Kalimah Ṭayyibah*); therefore, that which is chosen  
13. is that you should choose the formula of negation and affirmation (*lafaẓ nafy ithbāt*)...  

## Folio 121 (Visual Left)
1. In a human being who has attained true realization (*sumahīqī*), there are firmly established three  
2. matters: the first is simplicity/humility and continuous praise; the second  
3. is constant worship without desiring ostentation, seclusion together with  
4. a single companion alone; because it is already safeguarded from being  
5. forbidden to any other than one's own self; thirdly,  
6. quiet contemplation, not wishing to mingle or associate with  
7. others, keeping desires minimal;  
8. because in deep contemplation, howsoever it may be, and in all  
9. that becomes one's total immersion, it is already certain that  
10. Iblis and destructive attachments will not disrupt the worship;  
11. while that which serves as the spiritual bond is no longer  
12. attached to anything whatsoever other than Allah, glorified and exalted be He;  
13. such is the human being who has reached spiritual perfection (*istakmīl*)...

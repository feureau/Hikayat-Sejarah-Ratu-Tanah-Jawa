# Hikayat Ratu Tanah Jawa — IMG_0005

## Folio 12 (Visual Right)

1: and not like a human being who is heedless, if he persists only in one manner
2: after knowing, lest his state slip away
3: from the true path, in fear of the final testimony; wherefore
4: it has been made manifest regarding the destruction of the body here
5: in this world, and it becomes a ruin unto you.
6: Those companions who follow that command are just like
7: those who comprehend the Arabic, that is the meaning of the scriptural proof of the Hadith, meaning:
8: that it resides within the worldly heart,
9: turning away from idle talk concerning other people; and those
10: who have become the trust of the progeny of Adam, if
11: they are guided by the Lord of the Worlds, which means
12: the covenant which is held with conviction, destroying the corruptions
13: of those who become disbelievers, whose resting place shall find no peace,

## Folio 13 (Visual Left)

1: after you return unto your heavenly honor,
2: unto all the descendants of Adam who are Muslims here
3: in this world; those who are perfected are those who are granted
4: divine inspiration by the Lord of the Worlds, as has
5: been agreed upon in the scriptural proofs and texts of the Qur'an:
6: "Say to those who remained behind of the bedouins: You will be called
7: to fight against a people of great military might,
8: or they will submit..." [Qur'an 48:16]; that is, instructing
9: their fellow descendants of Adam who are Muslims living here
10: in this world, who remain steadfast and uphold Islam,
11: granted by the Lord God, Blessed and Exalted, the
12: Necessary Being, the One, the Subduer (al-Wāhid al-Qahhār); it is indeed
13: a sign that is certain for those who possess firm conviction.

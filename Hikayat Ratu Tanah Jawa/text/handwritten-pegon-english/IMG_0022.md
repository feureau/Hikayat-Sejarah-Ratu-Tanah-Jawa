## Folio 46 (Visual Right)
1: Iblis who becomes the companion and ally of Satan;
2: its meaning and reason is that this light does not descend
3: upon a person whose faith is not guarded and preserved,
4: such being the state of one devoid of the divine richness of the Glorified
5: and Exalted in this world, not being preserved. Those
6: two matters, meaning faith versus Satan:
7: faith is outwardly in the intermediate knowledge,
8: outwardly in the intellect (*'aql*), reflecting upon the divine decree (*qaḍā'*).
9: Regarding the humble servant Abdul Hamid, his situation
10: at the court, it happened in the darkness of the night,
11: on Friday Wage, after the fourth hour of the month of Muharram,
12: the eighth day, in the year Bé, when he was struck with calamity,
13: departing together and fleeing

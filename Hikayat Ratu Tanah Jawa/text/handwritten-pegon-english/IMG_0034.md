# Hikayat Ratu Tanah Jawa - Scan IMG_0034

## Folio 72 (Visual Right)
1. the marketplace of all entities, wherein all things are gathered together,  
2. in this market, like the likeness of all things in this world;  
3. and the likeness of your physical body is like unto a camel;  
4. if this faith has no guide, its likeness is that of a riderless camel,  
5. falling into the hands of one devoid of understanding. A person going to market, if  
6. he lacks steadfast resolve, or if that person is distracted without  
7. true guidance, then when that person goes unto this market  
8. and lacks inner discernment, he falls into foolishness and deception;  
9. for if he is captivated by outward illusions, because  
10. in this market all manner of enticing worldly wares are gathered,  
11. then the traveler in the marketplace who lacks prudence and cannot  
12. hold firm or lacks true understanding, if he lacks certitude (*yaqīn*),  
13. such a person merely gazes blindly upon the fleeting illusions of things.  

## Folio 73 (Visual Left)
1. The certitude of the inner light which is encompassed within,  
2. this inner light which is revealed and made visible:  
3. unless a person takes firm hold of it,  
4. his likeness is like one who sets sail in a ship; if a person  
5. takes command and equips a ship, preparing its voyage,  
6. if it is properly steered according to Islam, no one  
7. can cause it to founder; likewise with all other men:  
8. if they do not hold fast to the compass of true guidance, they fall into error;  
9. and if that vessel is struck by tempests,  
10. lacking navigational insight, it will surely drift helplessly,  
11. caught in dangerous reefs and entangled in currents, unable  
12. to find safe passage; surely if it sinks into the depths,  
13. it will never be able to return home to its haven.

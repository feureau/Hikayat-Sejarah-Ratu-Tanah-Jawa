# Hikayat Ratu Tanah Jawa — IMG_0003

## Folio 4 (Visual Right)

1. and the purpose of existence becomes  
2. the path of all mankind.  
3. In this earthly life, whenever  
4. they walk upon this world, all of them,  
5. if they stray into error, they look only upon themselves.  
6. If they stumble, or meet with hardship,  
7. they lament their poverty and suffering, yet do not see  
8. that their distress arises because they  
9. lack true understanding, remaining unmindful of the eternal life  
10. in their worldly pursuits, striving after mortal pleasures.  
11. If their wealth increases, they boast of their riches,  
12. yet when adversity strikes, they lose all their patience.

## Folio 5 (Visual Left)

1. All of them must know and understand,  
2. turning in repentance, so that they may not be led astray  
3. from the path ordained by the Lord of the Worlds. All of them  
4. must stand together, united in brotherhood,  
5. all the descendants of Adam who hold fast to Islam, residing in this  
6. world, striving together in the perfection of  
7. their faith, with all steadfastness and devotion,  
8. and performing righteous deeds throughout their entire lives,  
9. sojourning upon this earth with true insight,  
10. protecting their faith from the whisperings of temptation,  
11. and repelling all wickedness that enters into the heart,  
12. through the light of true faith, avoiding sinful deeds,  
13. while reflecting upon the ultimate return unto the Almighty.

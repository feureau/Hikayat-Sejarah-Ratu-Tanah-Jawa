# Scan IMG_0123 (Folios 250 and 251)

## Folio 250 (Visual Right — P. 139)
1. of the river (*kali*) and rainwater; and whereas the rising  
2. and ebbing of the ocean water is only through the waves (*karêp*), the meaning  
3. of those waves is the wind, and the meaning of that wind is the will  
4. of the Lord God—glory be to Him and exalted is He, the Mighty and Majestic,  
5. the One, the Irresistible Subduer—which is able to move  
6. the ocean water. However, the surface alone of that ocean water,  
7. it cannot move within the depths of that ocean.  
8. As for the waves that appear large and small because of  
9. the movement of that ocean water, they follow along with the wind—  
10. only on its outer surface. Indeed, it is that wind which comes  
11. and becomes the passion (*nafsuné*) in that ocean, becoming the vicegerent (*khalīfah*) of the creatures (*makhlūq*)  
12. of the Most Exalted Lord of all the worlds who  
13. is in the world, and yet that ocean can be crossed by sails.

# Hikayat Ratu Tanah Jawa - Scan IMG_0029

## Folio 60 (Visual Right)
1: truly together with that Light; meaning the sacred name *Allāh* which
2: is in the *Bismillāh*, which is the Day of Primordial Origin (*al-Yawm al-Tilād*),
3: the One (*Wāḥid*); the Alif of the *Bismillāh* is what becomes
4: this word *Allāh*, which is *Allāhu Huwa* the All-Encompassing (*al-Muḥīṭ*); the name
5: *Allāh* in the *Bismillāh* is encompassed in its totality,
6: even as the Day of Origin, the One, in the *Bismillāh*,
7: because herein it is necessarily affirmed,
8: that impossibility and absolute non-existence (*'adam*) are negated;
9: for if it were asked: how is the Day of Origin the One in
10: this *Bismillāh*? It is without spatial direction, without boundary, without
11: deficiency, without end, but rather shining with light that illuminates,
12: creating the heavens and earth by the Will of the Almighty,
13: without addition or diminution, pure and immaculate, without flaw.

## Folio 61 (Visual Left)
1: The greatness of Nūn and the noble Mīm, this light and adoration
2: is wondrous light, turning back and becoming fully manifest;
3: by His Divine Will, without anything resembling or rivaling Him,
4: the Unique, the One (*al-Aḥad al-Wāḥid*), the Living who never dies, the Ever-Enduring,
5: none possessing sovereignty or power over Him, light upon light;
6: such is the all-encompassing reality: Aḥmad, the One,
7: while His attribute is Ever-Forgiving (*al-'Afuww*), the Day of Origin, the One,
8: which is *Allāhu Huwa* the All-Encompassing (*al-Muḥīṭ*); the meaning of *Allāhu Huwa*
9: *al-Muḥīṭ* is that He encompasses all existence in this world reaching unto
10: the Hereafter; this light is the reality of all praiseworthy qualities (*ṣifāt maḥmūdah*);
11: thus this living essence encompasses this world reaching unto
12: the Hereafter completely; so that the Day of Primordial Origin, the One,
13: in the *Bismillāh* is what becomes the origin and foundation of all reality.

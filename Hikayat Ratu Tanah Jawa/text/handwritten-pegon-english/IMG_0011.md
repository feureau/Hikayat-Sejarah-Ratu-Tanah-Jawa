# Hikayat Ratu Tanah Jawa — IMG_0011

## Folio 24 (Visual Right)

1. the progeny of Adam  
2. who remain as Muslims living here in this world, unto  
3. the Lord God, Blessed and Exalted, the Mighty and Majestic, the One,  
4. the Subduer (al-Wāhid al-Qahhār); that is the scriptural text: "He released  
5. the two bodies of water, meeting together; between them is a barrier they do not  
6. transgress." [Qur'an 55:19–20]; wherefore all of  
7. the descendants of Adam who remain as Muslims living here in this world,  
8. must look upon the scriptural text of the Qur'an without ceasing,  
9. directing your gaze while here in this world; for distinct from other  
10. things and from mere created beings, you must guard yourselves  
11. with watchful care, all you Muslims; which means that other conditions  
12. are like the gratitude of created beings unto their master,  
13. or like making up missed duties (qaḍā'), lest if through qaḍā' one can

## Folio 25 (Visual Left)

1. attain equality with the fulfillment of the obligatory duty; yet if it be  
2. termed the spiritual absorption of a person who has settled his missed debt (qaḍā'),  
3. then through that thanksgiving the taint of debt is removed,  
4. and the makeup duty and the gratitude become united as one state  
5. in the person, and his spiritual experience is transformed;  
6. such is the exalted state of the created servant,  
7. where the servanthood of the creature is not erased, but both  
8. states are united during the moment of prayer,  
9. specifically during the opening Takbīrat al-Iḥrām; for when  
10. that moment passes, one returns to ordinary creaturely state again,  
11. and the temporary passing away of creaturely identity departs; but when  
12. one stands in reverent awe within the Takbīrat al-Iḥrām,  
13. in like manner the burden of paying the missed debt (qaḍā') is lifted.

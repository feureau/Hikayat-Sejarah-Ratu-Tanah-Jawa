# Hikayat Ratu Tanah Jawa - Scan IMG_0023

## Folio 48 (Visual Right)
[Right Margin: Named Syabib al-Jamal, in truth Syabib al-'Awam.]  
1. This is the intention for the ritual bath of protection:  
2. This is the intention for the ritual bath of warding off afflictions (*talak bala*) in the month of Safar,  
3. on the last Wednesday: "I intend the ritual bath to remove  
4. three calamities, a sunnah for the sake of Allah the Exalted." This is the intention  
5. for the prayer of the last Wednesday: "I intend the prayer of obedience to ward off  
6. calamities, four rak'ahs, as a follower, for Allah the Exalted, *Allāhu  
7. Akbar*." This is the recitation in prostration and bowing: "*Subḥān-  
8. Allāh wal-ḥamdu lillāh... Subḥānallāh*,  
9. [water over which is recited and] which is drunk:  
10. 'Peace! A word from a Merciful Lord' [*Salāmun qawlam mir-Rabbir-Raḥīm*, Qur'an 36:58].  
11. 'Peace be upon Noah among all the worlds' [*Salāmun 'alā Nūḥin fīl-'ālamīn*, Qur'an 37:79].  
12. 'Peace be upon Abraham' [*Salāmun 'alā Ibrāhīm*, Qur'an 37:109].  
13. 'Peace be upon Lot among the messengers.'  

## Folio 49 (Visual Left)
1. 'Peace be upon Jonah among the messengers.'  
2. 'Peace be upon Elijah among the messengers.'  
3. 'Peace be upon the righteous among the messengers.'  
4. 'Peace be upon Hud among the messengers.'  
5. 'Peace be upon Moses and Aaron.'  
6. 'Peace be upon the archangels brought near (*al-Malā'ikat al-Muqarrabīn*).'  
7. 'Peace be upon the angels of the supreme assembly until the Day of Judgment.'  
8. Tamat (Concluded).

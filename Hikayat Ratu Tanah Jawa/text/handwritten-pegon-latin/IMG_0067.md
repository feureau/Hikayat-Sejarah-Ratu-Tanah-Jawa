## [Folio 136]

1: ts sng mḥyṭ ln kf mntd tw psntwny
2: ts bḥr mḥyṭ yt y yt ts brng
3: ‘lm tks brng ‘lm yt y yt knjng
4: nby mḥmd rswl Allāh ṣlā Allāh ‘lyh wslm
5: mry knjng nby mḥmd yt tmnwrwn mrng ng
6: ‘lm dny yt ddr fntwtn tks nby fntwt
7: yt wws b‘d njyyh srw psy fr’n yt
8: ddr nby wws cmn khyr ng ‘lm dny
9: yt kng mntwq nb kng wl ng ‘lm
10: dny yt knjng nby ādm khlqh Allāh ‘lyh
11: lslm yt kng tnwrwn hk ty ng swrg
12: mrng ng ‘lm dny yt nfyng m‘ẓhyr hkā mrng ng srw psy żryt b‘ḍ b‘ḍ tks żryt.
[catchword: yt lwn mp wmrgh]

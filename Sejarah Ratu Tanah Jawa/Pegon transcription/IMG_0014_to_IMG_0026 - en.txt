
The knowledge of the intermediate realm, the existence of the intermediate realm, is the bearer of the seed of the angels and what is called the seed of the gods, which in the future will descend as the Batara (Gods). There was the desire of Prophet Seth (Nabi Syis), upon him be peace, regarding the manifestation of the kingdom of the world, namely the Land of Java. When the world was manifested, there was the Land of Arabia, which was desired by Allah, Subhanahu wa Ta'ala (The Most Glorious and Exalted), the Lord of the Worlds. It was bestowed upon Lord Prophet Seth, upon him be peace, and then Angel Gabriel (Jibril), upon him be peace, descended and then vanished. Following this, Lord Prophet Seth prostrated to Allah the Exalted. From that prostration, a brilliant light emerged from the Prophet's face and hands. By the will of Allah, the Prophet felt a beautiful sensation, and a child was born to Prophet Seth. The child was named by the Prophet as **Hyang Nur Cahya** (The Divine Light).

After the light was perfected, the light ascended to his father, Prophet Seth, pleasing him greatly. Because Prophet Adam, upon him be peace, possessed a beautiful physical form by the will of Allah, this light was wondrous. Prophet Seth then went to Mount Nur (Jabal Nur). He surrendered to Allah, the One, the Subduer, the Mighty and Majestic, asking for a sign of his father's succession. By the mercy of the Lord of the Worlds, Angel Gabriel descended to Mount Nur to meet Prophet Seth. Angel Gabriel informed Prophet Seth that the Divine Light was sufficient to hold the knowledge of the world, and Prophet Seth was granted this spiritual knowledge.

Later, at Mount Sur (Jabal Sur in Mecca), Hyang Nur Cahya meditated for a long time. Hyang Nur Cahya then asked for his father's permission to be named Hyang Nur Rasa. With Allah's mercy, Hyang Nur Cahya and his son, Hyang Nur Rasa, met with Angel Gabriel. Gabriel decreed that the descendants of the light at Mount Nur and Mount Sur would have different destinies. By the will of Allah, the descendants in the Land of Arabia would become Prophets, while the descendants who possessed the knowledge of the world and kingdom would become the rulers (Gods/Kings), dwelling in the celestial realm.

## The Wisdom of Lord Prophet Adam, upon him be peace

*The Tale of Lord Prophet Adam, upon him be peace. And Allah knows best the truth. May Allah forgive us, O Lord, O Most Forgiving.*

This is the tale of the creation of the creatures of Allah, the One, the Subduer, the Mighty and Majestic, who are called the Gods and Goddesses (Dewa-Dewi), born from the light of the angels by Allah's will. This became the seed of Hyang Nur Cahya. Hyang Nur Cahya had completed his profound meditation at Mount Sur in Mecca, facing the Lord of the Worlds.

He was granted the title of a God, and his descendants would become Gods and Goddesses. When the Gods and Goddesses descended to the world, they manifested a kingdom in the Land of Java. The descendants were granted divine status by Allah, surpassing the angels, with grace from the Lord of the Worlds. Because they chose the knowledge of the intermediate realm, they were given the titles of Gods, and Hyang Nur Cahya was granted excellence by the Lord of the Worlds to rule and be obeyed by his descendants.

However, they were no longer permitted to ask for prophethood, though they were blessed with the Night of Decree (Lailatul Qadar). Hyang Nur Cahya and his son prostrated in gratitude to the Lord of the Worlds. Angel Gabriel then guided them from the worldly realm to the intermediate realm to establish their celestial kingdom, which is called **Jonggring Saloka** (the abode of the Javanese gods). After Gabriel showed Hyang Nur Cahya and his son Hyang Nur Rasa the way, the angel vanished.

Hyang Nur Cahya and his son Hyang Nur Rasa submitted to Allah, and descended to govern Jonggring Saloka. They built a grand audience hall. There, all the male and female deities gathered. Hyang Nur Cahya and Hyang Nur Rasa held court, granting titles and duties to the various Gods and Goddesses.

*The text then details the complex genealogy and succession of the Javanese supreme gods:*
Hyang Nur Cahya passed his authority to his son, **Sang Hyang Nur Rasa**.
Sang Hyang Nur Rasa passed it to **Sang Hyang Wening** (or Wenang).
Sang Hyang Wening passed it to **Sang Hyang Tunggal**.

During the era of Sang Hyang Tunggal, conflicts and battles erupted in the heavens (Jonggring Saloka). Weapons were drawn, and a curse (bendhu) fell upon them by the will of Allah. Because of their rebellion and warfare, some of the divine beings and celestial nymphs (bidadari) were cursed to become ogres, demons, and spirits, scattered across the realms without knowing their destination. Those who remained pure stayed in the heavens.

Sang Hyang Tunggal prostrated and repented to the Lord of the Worlds. He then had offspring, among them Batara Surya (God of the Sun), Batara Brahma (God of Fire), Batara Indra, and **Batara Wisnu** (Lord Vishnu). By divine decree, Batara Wisnu was chosen to be the supreme ruler and was commanded to descend to the Land of Java to become a king, alongside Goddess Sri (Dewi Sirmi). Batara Wisnu meditated deeply, accepted his destiny as the ruler of the world and the celestial realm (Suralaya), and his lineage became the kings of Java.

### The Tale of the Sultan of Rum

*Anapun hikayat Kanjeng Sultan ing Rum...*
Here is the tale of the Kanjeng Sultan of Rum (the Emperor of Rome/Turkey), who submitted completely to Allah, the One, the Subduer, the Mighty and Majestic.

The Sultan of Rum gathered all his ministers and captains. The Sultan was commanded by the Lord of the Worlds to populate the Land of Java with human beings. At that time, the Sultan of Rum had not yet seen the Land of Java. He ordered his captains to sail there, but the captains reported back that the Land of Java could not be inhabited by humans because it was filled with fierce, terrifying demons and spirits.

Hearing this, the Sultan of Rum prepared 20,000 men and ordered them to bring **Tumbal** (magical wards/talismans/sacrifices). The Sultan commanded that these *tumbal* be planted in all the mountains, forests, and across the entire Land of Java to subdue the spirits, accompanied by prayers to Allah.

When the *tumbal* were planted and the prayers were offered to the Lord of the Worlds, the entire Land of Java violently split and shook. The earth quaked, the mountains erupted with terrifying roaring sounds, and a deafening noise echoed for seven days and nights. All the spirits, demons, and man-eating beasts cried out in agony and fled in terror, driven away by the power of the *tumbal*.

After the chaos subsided and the land was finally safe and pacified, the emissaries returned to Rum to report to the Sultan that the Land of Java was now ready. Thus, humans were sent to safely inhabit the land.


**# 53**
Raden Dewata Cengkar grew up and desired to rule, but his father, Prabu Sindula, was still alive. Because of his immense ambition to take the throne, Dewata Cengkar forced his way and rebelled. By the decree of Allah, the Exalted, he was destined to face ruin and suffering because he had acted tyrannically and forced his father from power.

**# 54**
Meanwhile, Syekh Makura and Syekh Tikur traveled to report to the Kanjeng Sultan in the land of Rum (Turkey). They informed the Sultan that the land of Java was still devoid of true faith. Because of this, the Sultan of Rum ordered Syekh Makura and Syekh Tikur to bring 20,000 families to inhabit and settle in the land of Java. Upon returning to Java, they found a mystical cave known as Gua Terusan, which became a place of significance as the new settlers built their homes across the land.

**# 55**
Raden Dewata Cengkar had now taken the title of Prabu Dewata Cengkar, ruling the kingdom of Medang. Although he retained Adipati Tikur as his minister, the King grew exceedingly arrogant and tyrannical. He abandoned the wise teachings of his father. Meanwhile, Raden Daniswara, a youth of great handsome appearance and noble character, observed the sorrow of the people. Because of Dewata Cengkar's cruelty, the land of Java fell into ruin and despair. Adipati Tikur was deeply distressed seeing the King's daily behavior, which brought continuous sorrow to the realm.

**# 56**
Prabu Sindula was overwhelmed with grief seeing the actions of his son, Dewata Cengkar. The King even dared to confront his own father with harsh words. Prabu Sindula and Patih Makura wept over this tragedy. Dewata Cengkar cast aside all advice, ignoring his father and his ministers, thereby sealing his cursed fate for his great disobedience.

**# 57**
The narrative then shifts to the land of Arabia, during the time of the chosen Prophet Muhammad (Peace Be Upon Him), the Messenger of Allah, and his beloved companions: Abu Bakar ash-Shiddiq (RA), Umar al-Faruq (RA), Uthman Dhun-Nurayn (RA), and Ali al-Murtadha (RA). There was a man named Aji Saka, whom the cursed Iblis (Satan) sought to deceive. Iblis planned to enter Aji Saka's heart to mislead him and challenge the teachings of the Prophet.

**# 58**
Abu Bakar ash-Shiddiq respectfully asked the Prophet about the deceit of Iblis and how his dark knowledge works. Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) explained that Allah allows Iblis two primary methods to tempt mankind into arrogance and rebellion. If a human lets their guard down, Iblis will enter their heart and claim them, clouding their mind with ancient, misguided knowledge.

**# 59**
Aji Saka's heart was successfully influenced by Iblis, altering his spiritual knowledge. The companions wept upon seeing Aji Saka's state. Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) told his companions to observe how a human looks when they lose their divine guidance and are consumed by arrogance. Abu Bakar asked further, and the Prophet explained that Aji Saka was now filled with dark, worldly magic rather than divine truth.

**# 60**
Because the cursed Iblis had completely settled in his heart, Aji Saka became a disbeliever (*kafir*). Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) ordered Aji Saka to be exiled from the land of Arabia. Aji Saka begged for forgiveness, but the Prophet knew that Iblis had taken full control of him. If he were allowed to stay in Arabia, he would disrupt the Islamic faith. Therefore, he had to be banished.

**# 61**
Aji Saka, now marked as an exile, left Arabia. Had he stayed, he would have faced execution for his heresy. He wandered until he reached an island called Pulau Majeti. There, he was met by the Angel Azrael (Izrail). Aji Saka wept as the Angel Azrael spoke to him about his destiny.

**# 62**
Azrael told him that it was the will of Allah that he be sent to the land of Java. He was chosen to bring a specific kind of knowledge and civilization to Java. Azrael explained that Javanese knowledge and Arabian knowledge would differ, but this was the divine decree. Aji Saka was instructed to accept his fate and cross over to the Javanese mainland.

**# 63**
Aji Saka arrived in Java from Pulau Majeti with his loyal followers. He traversed Mount Merapi, Mount Sindoro, and Mount Kendeng. He introduced the twenty letters of the script (*Hanacaraka*) to the land of Java. These letters represented the essence of human destiny: *Hanacaraka* (there were messengers), *Datasawala* (they fought/argued), *Padajayanya* (they were equal in strength), *Magabathanga* (they both died/became corpses). This script became the foundation of Javanese knowledge.

**# 64**
Meanwhile, the situation in Medang Kamulan had worsened; Prabu Dewata Cengkar had become a cannibal, eating his own people. Adipati Tikur desperately searched for a holy ascetic who possessed the power to stop the King's cruelty. Hearing of a powerful new ascetic (Aji Saka) whose knowledge was unparalleled, Adipati Tikur hoped he could be the salvation of the realm, while Raden Daniswara remained deep in meditation.

**# 65**
Adipati Tikur met with the ascetic (Aji Saka) and pleaded for his help to save the people from being devoured. Aji Saka agreed to confront the King, but he stated that he would only ask the King for a piece of land as wide as his turban. Adipati Tikur then went to Prabu Dewata Cengkar to deliver the ascetic's seemingly humble request.

**# 66**
Prabu Dewata Cengkar arrogantly agreed to the ascetic's request. Aji Saka then unrolled his turban. Miraculously, the turban kept stretching endlessly, pushing the King backward. Dewata Cengkar was pushed further and further until he fell into the violent waves of the Southern Sea, where he transformed into a terrifying white crocodile (*Bajul Putih*).

**# 67**
With the tyrant defeated, Aji Saka was crowned as the new King of Medang Kamulan, taking the title Prabu Widayaka (Prabu Jaka). He ruled justly and wisely. Adipati Tikur remained as his minister. Peace and prosperity returned to Java, and the people rejoiced greatly under their new, righteous king.

**# 68**
In the meantime, Raden Daniswara, accompanied by his two loyal servants, Dora and Sembada (the *Punakawan*), chose the path of asceticism. Seeking spiritual perfection, Raden Daniswara went into a deep cave to meditate, leaving behind all worldly desires alongside his two faithful companions.

**# 69**
Prabu Aji Saka ruled with the blessing of the Almighty. He tended to the victims of Dewata Cengkar's reign, praying for the souls of those who had been unjustly killed. Through divine permission, order was restored to the spiritual and physical realms of Medang Kamulan.

**# 70**
Later, a mystical event occurred involving a snake-like creature named Jaka Linglung, who claimed to be the son of Prabu Aji Saka. He was subjected to a test to prove his lineage. He was ordered to go to the Southern Sea to kill the white crocodile (the manifestation of Dewata Cengkar). Jaka Linglung traveled underground, succeeded in his mission, and brought back the proof, thereby fulfilling his destiny.

**# 71**
While Raden Daniswara was deep in meditation, he heard the ethereal voice of his ancestor, Prabu Sindula. Raden Daniswara bowed in reverence and wept. Prabu Sindula comforted him, telling him not to grieve, for everything was the will of the Almighty. He warned Daniswara that Java would face many future trials, but he must remain steadfast.

**# 72**
As Raden Daniswara's meditation grew immensely powerful, the heavens decided to test him. Two beautiful heavenly nymphs (*bidadari*), Dewi Sangkaningrat and Dewi Tunjungkara, were sent down to tempt him. They used all their beauty and charms to break his concentration, but Raden Daniswara remained entirely unmoved. Realizing they could not sway him, the nymphs eventually returned to heaven.

**# 73**
To the south of the forest lived a poor widow named Mbok Randa Kasihan. She lived with her adopted son, a simple but strong-willed youth named Jaka. They lived in extreme poverty. Despite his simple mind, Jaka worked tirelessly in the forest every day to provide for his adoptive mother.

**# 74**
After the nymphs departed to report to the gods, Raden Daniswara awakened from his long meditation. His loyal servants, who had been resting nearby, also awoke, ready to serve their master as he prepared to step back into the world.

**# 75**
Mbok Randa Kasihan spoke lovingly to Jaka, telling him about his potential and that he was destined for more than just a life of poverty. She advised him that one day he must serve a true and just king. Jaka listened earnestly and promised his mother that he would follow her advice and seek his true calling.

**# 76**
Their hut was very fragile; the roof leaked heavily when it rained, and they barely had enough to eat, surviving on whatever Jaka could forage. Yet, Jaka never complained. He took care of Mbok Randa Kasihan with absolute devotion, working day and night without demanding anything in return.

**# 77**
One day, while wandering in the forest, Jaka unexpectedly encountered Raden Daniswara and his servants. Seeing the noble and glowing aura of Raden Daniswara, Jaka immediately bowed in deep respect. Raden Daniswara was touched by the young man's sincere humility and saw his hidden potential.

**# 78**
Mbok Randa Kasihan was overjoyed when she learned Jaka had met such a noble figure. Raden Daniswara spoke kindly to them, promising to guide Jaka. Jaka swore his absolute loyalty to Raden Daniswara, intertwining their fates as they prepared for the next chapter in the history of the realm.

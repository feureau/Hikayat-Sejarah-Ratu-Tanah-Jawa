# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — Scan IMG_0049 Translation

### Folio 97 (Visual Right)
1: After they had arrived at the court, all the chiefs and nobles
2: paid homage, bowing their heads in deep reverence before the king.
3: The king of Pajajaran sat upon the throne, radiant as the morning sun,
4: surrounded by his counselors, warriors, and high dignitaries.
5: Ki Ageng Sela stepped forward, offering his humble greetings and counsel.
6: The king was pleased with his presence and commanded that he be seated
7: on a place of honor, beside the royal throne.
8: Then they discussed matters concerning the peace and defense of the realm,
9: and how to bring prosperity to all the subjects throughout the provinces.
10: Ki Ageng Sela spoke with great wisdom and insight,
11: and all who heard him marveled at the clarity of his understanding.
12: The king presented him with rich gifts of gold, fine garments, and fragrant oils,
13: but the sage accepted only what was necessary for his simple needs.
14: He took leave of the monarch with respectful bows,
15: and returned to his quiet village to continue his prayers and contemplation.
16: The king watched him depart with admiration and respect,
17: knowing that such righteous men were the true pillars of the realm.

### Folio 98 (Visual Left)
1: Now we record the succession of rulers in the kingdom of Pajajaran.
2: After the passing of the king, his son mounted the throne,
3: continuing to uphold the laws and traditions of his ancestors.
4: The realm flourished in peace, and trade expanded across the seas.
5: Envoys from distant kingdoms arrived bearing valuable tributes,
6: seeking alliances of friendship and mutual commerce.
7: The young king received them with regal dignity and generosity,
8: establishing treaties that brought security to the maritime routes.
9: In those days, scholars and bards composed songs of praise,
10: celebrating the chivalry, justice, and nobility of the royal house.
11: Temples were restored, and sanctuaries for learning were endowed,
12: where youths were taught the ancient scripts, philosophy, and virtue.
13: The people rejoiced in the fairness of their governors,
14: and agriculture thrived in the fertile valleys watered by pristine mountain streams.
15: Thus the chronicle of Pajajaran was written with honor and renown,
16: serving as a guide and model for the kingdoms that followed after.
17: And God's benevolence shone upon the land, bestowing long-lasting peace.

# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — Scan IMG_0054 Translation

### Folio 107 (Visual Right)
1. Then it was recorded that Dewi Suradewati spoke to King Brawijaya,
2. saying: "Sire, my heart is filled with joy, for we have seen the wonders
3. of the realm and the loyalty of the people toward the throne."
4. The king smiled and replied: "The love of the subjects is the true treasure
5. of a king; without their devotion, the grandest palace is but dust."
6. In those days, Raden Patah had grown to manhood in Palembang,
7. excelling in wisdom, martial skill, and spiritual learning.
8. He spoke to Arya Damar, expressing his deep desire to visit Java,
9. the land of his royal father and the sacred cradle of his lineage.
10. Arya Damar, loving the young prince as his own son, granted permission,
11. equipping him with ships, provisions, and trusted retainers for the voyage.
12. He advised him: "Go first to Mount Ampel Denta in Surabaya,
13. and seek the spiritual guidance of the venerable Sunan Ampel."
14. Raden Patah took leave of Arya Damar with profound gratitude,
15. setting sail across the calm waters toward the shores of Java.
16. The journey was favored by gentle winds and clear skies,
17. and soon the white sails appeared before the bustling harbor of Tuban.

### Folio 108 (Visual Left)
1. When Raden Patah landed in Tuban, he was received with honor
2. by the local governor, who recognized his noble bearing and royal lineage.
3. The prince did not linger long in the port, but proceeded directly
4. toward Surabaya, seeking the hermitage of Sunan Ampel.
5. When he arrived at Ampel Denta, he was welcomed warmly by the venerable saint,
6. who embraced him and smiled, already knowing his destiny through inner vision.
7. Sunan Ampel instructed the young prince in the deeper mysteries of the faith,
8. teaching him how to govern with compassion, humility, and righteousness.
9. He advised him: "Do not seek power through violence or pride,
10. but establish your dwelling in the glade of Glagah Wangi, in the forest of Demak."
11. There, where the fragrant reeds grew, he was to found a new settlement,
12. welcoming all who sought refuge, justice, and the true knowledge of God.
13. Raden Patah received these instructions with a devoted heart,
14. bowing low before the holy master and pledging his complete obedience.
15. Accompanied by his loyal followers, he set forth toward the wilderness of Demak,
16. clearing the thick jungle and laying the foundations of the future kingdom.
17. And the blessing of the saints accompanied him in all his endeavors.

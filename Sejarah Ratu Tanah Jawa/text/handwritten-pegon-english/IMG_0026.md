### Folio 51 (Visual Right)

1: Talismans were planted, all of them prepared by the priests,
2: those talismans being by order of Kangjeng Sultan of Rum: on the mountain
3: peaks: on Mount Tidar, Mount Wilis, Mount Semeru, and
4: on all the mountains, including the northern mountains, throughout all the land of Java.
5: After these talismans were planted, accompanied by prayers and petitions,
6: the Lord of the Worlds answered the supplications:
7: then the land of Java shook violently, like a vessel tossed on waves,
8: emitting loud roaring noises from all the mountains in the land of Java.
9: In great terror, lightning flashed, and torrential rains fell,
10: accompanied by storms that terrified all human beings.
11: The earth trembled, thunder crashed, and darkness covered the land,
12: obscuring the sun. And afterwards, all the noise ceased,
13: and the spirits fled, scattered across the entire land of Java.
14: Then they returned home to Rum, all forty
15: of them, reporting to Kangjeng Sultan that
16: Java was now peaceful. Thereupon Kangjeng Sultan
17: sent twenty thousand families once again, together with artisans

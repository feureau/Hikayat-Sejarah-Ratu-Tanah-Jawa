### Folio 15 (Visual Right)  

1. Thereupon the angel Jabrā’īl, peace be upon him, arrived bearing the command  
2. of Subḥānahu wa-Ta‘ālā to Kangjeng Prophet Adam, peace be upon him, and  
3. to his consort Kangjeng Babu Eve, may Allāh be pleased with her, conveying:  
4. "If it pleases the Lord of the Worlds, Kangjeng the Prophet is commanded to dwell in Mecca,  
5. he and his consort; and Kangjeng Prophet Adam, peace be  
6. upon him, is commanded to build the Dome of Allāh [the Ka‘bah]." And Kangjeng Prophet Adam was given books,  
7. ten scrolls, and the khuldī fruit from Paradise, but only a single seed.  
8. Then Kangjeng Prophet Adam was commanded by the Lord  
9. of the Worlds to plant that seed in the earthly world.  
10. Kangjeng Prophet Adam was also given cattle from Paradise: a pair of horses,  
11. male and female, from Paradise, and a pair of sheep, male and female,  
12. likewise from Paradise. And that single grain of khuldī was planted,  
13. whereupon it sprouted and grew, bearing various colors of fruit,  
14. spreading across the world—all originating from that single khuldī fruit of Paradise  
15. which grew from the seed. As for the chronicle of Kangjeng Prophet Adam,  
16. peace be upon him, after he had long dwelt  
17. in the earthly world before Subḥānahu wa-Ta‘ālā,

4. the chronicle: Kangjeng Prophet Adam, peace be upon him, had arrived  
5. in Mecca, fragrant with musk, sitting at the edge of the Masjid

7. consort's place. Babu Eve had already entered the Masjid al-Ḥarām,  
8. wearing clothing of gold, and Kangjeng Babu Eve saw  
9. her husband, Kangjeng Prophet Adam, arrive, also adorned in garments of gold,  
10. fragrant with musk. Then Kangjeng Babu Eve,  
11. may Allāh be pleased with her, embraced Kangjeng Prophet Adam, peace be  
12. upon him. Then Kangjeng Babu Eve embraced  
13. Kangjeng Prophet Adam, meeting him within the Masjid  
14. al-Ḥarām on Friday after the forgiveness [*ghufiralahum*].

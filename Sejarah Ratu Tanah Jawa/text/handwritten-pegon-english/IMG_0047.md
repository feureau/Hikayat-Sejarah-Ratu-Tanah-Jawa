# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — Scan IMG_0047 Translation

### Folio 93 (Visual Right)
1: Then it was told that there was a great sage dwelling in the southern forest,
2: named Kiyai Ageng Sela, who was revered for his spiritual power and knowledge.
3: He commanded the spirits of the woods and streams, and no harm could befall him.
4: To him came many seekers from east and west, desiring to learn the inner wisdom.
5: Kiyai Ageng Sela possessed deep insight into the movements of heaven and earth,
6: and could catch the lightning from the sky and hold it fast in his hand.
7: In those days, the court was troubled by portents and signs in the heavens,
8: and the king sent messengers to request the sage's counsel in the capital.
9: When the messengers arrived at the hermitage, they bowed respectfully,
10: delivering the royal letter asking for guidance and spiritual intercession.
11: Kiyai Ageng Sela smiled gently and said: "The destiny of Java is written
12: by the Almighty; righteous deeds bring peace, while wickedness brings ruin."
13: He advised the king to rule with mercy, to care for the poor and weak,
14: and to keep the sacred covenants established by the founding fathers.
15: Then he gave them a talisman of protection, inscribed with sacred letters,
16: instructing them to place it at the northern gate of the palace.
17: The messengers returned with joyful hearts, reporting all the sage's words.

### Folio 94 (Visual Left)
1: Both kingdoms, Janggala and Kediri, lived in peace and close alliance,
2: bound together by the sacred bond of the royal marriage.
3: Raden Panji and Dewi Sekartaji were blessed with noble offspring,
4: who inherited the virtues and valor of their illustrious parents.
5: When the old kings passed away, the realm remained strong and united,
6: under the wise leadership of their successors, who followed the ancient customs.
7: The people throughout Java lived in plenty, free from fear and strife,
8: praising the memory of the heroic ancestors whose deeds brought peace.
9: Here begins the chronicle of the subsequent generations of rulers,
10: who held sway over the fertile plains and mountain valleys of the island.
11: They established fair laws, supported learning, and honored the sages,
12: so that wisdom flourished alongside worldly power and military strength.
13: Trade continued to expand across the waters to foreign kingdoms,
14: bringing riches of spices, silk, and porcelain to the busy ports.
15: Thus the golden age of Java continued through many generations,
16: leaving behind monuments and chronicles that testified to their glory.
17: And God's blessing rested upon the land, nurturing its people and prosperity.

# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa - English Translation
## Source: British Library Or. 14371

### Folio 41 (Visual Right)  

1. served him, and he took a wife named the daughter of Nila-  
2. kanta in the intermediate realm, and by her had four children.  
3. The first of his children and his consort was Batara Durga; then Batara  
4. Kala, and then Rūpabuta. Then Kanyaka,  
5. who was also named Batara Surya, Batara Bima, and  
6. Batara Sambu, then Batara Mahadewa, and then Batara Mahadipa.  
7. They were of diverse appearances, and in their meditation and contemplation  
8. on Mount Lawu they were revered, their faces shining upon Mount Candramuka.  
9. Such was the assembly upon Mount Candramuka. Then the jinn were gathered  
10. before Nur Adam Ratna. And he gave birth to children of various appearances,  
11. brought to Suralaya. Then they were taken to  
12. Sang Hyang Jaka, and through his consort he had children of good appearance,  
13. named Batara Ismara, and another of good appearance  
14. named Batara Kamajaya. Then, by the will of Sang  
15. Hyang Tunggal, Batara Guru was commanded to become king on Mount  
16. Candramuka in the land of Java, as was also the son of Batara Brama;  
17. they served Sang Hyang Tunggal, and were favored.

### Folio 42 (Visual Left)  

1. Sang Hyang Ismaya toward his father Batara Nila Kanta; for  
2. he was the elder brother, and Batara Caturboja, because he was very  
3. obedient, had children and grandchildren who were also rulers,  
4. numerous. Sang Hyang Tunggal, by the will of his father Sang Hyang Tunggal,  
5. was commanded to marry, taking to wife the daughter  
6. of the lineage of royal princes, because  
7. they were wise, noble in demeanor, and without equal. And after  
8. the command was given, Sang Hyang Tunggal then dwelt on Mount Candramuka,  
9. on Mount Sukasrana,  
10. on the summit of Mount Candramuka. Sang Hyang Guru  
11. and Sang Hyang Kanyaka had nine sons:  
12. all the gods bowed down, prostrating before them. And after  
13. the nine sons were grown, they were separated. And after  
14. a long time had passed, the elder son was named  
15. Batara Brama, then Batara Ind[ra], then Batara Sukra,  
16. and Batara Wisnu, who were the sons of Sang Hyang Tunggal;  
17. the youngest among them was loved by his father.

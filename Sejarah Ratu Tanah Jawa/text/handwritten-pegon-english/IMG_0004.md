# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa - English Translation
## Source: British Library Or. 14371
### Scan Concordance: IMG_0004.jpg (Folios 7 & 8)

---

### Folio 7 (Visual Right)

1: then fell down to the earth, dropping upon the land of Isfahan. Such was
2: the descent of Iblīs. Kangjeng Prophet Adam wept bitterly;
3: for forty years his tears streamed continuously without ceasing. Wherever his tears fell
4: upon Mount Surandīl, by the power of Allāh subḥānahu wa-ta‘ālā, they became
5: Ceylon rubies of red hue. And Kangjeng Eve wept; her tears
6: became Syrian rubies, also of red hue, upon Mount ‘Arafah.
7: And the dragon, wherever its tears fell, they became Yemeni carnelian agates.
8: And the peacock, wherever its tears fell, they became emerald gems.
9: And the wood of the anjir tree that fell upon the island of Java became sandalwood.
10: Thus is the narrative of the descent of Kangjeng Prophet Adam and Kangjeng Eve
11: and the dragon and the peacock and the anjir tree.
12: All of them were cast out from Paradise, because
13: Kangjeng Prophet Adam had eaten the khuldī fruit.

### Folio 8 (Visual Left)

1: Now the degrees of guilt of each one were explained:
2: Iblīs was the instigator, plotting the deception and bearing the prime malice;
3: the peacock allowed entry, opening the way through vain pride;
4: the dragon, by its drowsy yawning, allowed Iblīs to slip past hidden beneath its tongue;
5: Kangjeng Eve, heedless of the prohibition, urged her lord;
6: and Kangjeng Prophet Adam momentarily forgot the command of his Lord and consumed the fruit.
7: Therefore, each received retribution according to the measure of their deed.
8: For forty years Kangjeng Prophet Adam remained in solitary remorse upon Mount Surandīl,
9: eating nothing save wild herbs, weeping until rivers of rubies formed,
10: crying out in repentance: "O our Lord! We have wronged our own souls!
11: If Thou forgive us not and bestow not Thy mercy upon us,
12: surely we shall be among the lost!"
13: Thereupon the forgiveness of Allāh Almighty descended upon him.

### Folio 37 (Visual Right)

1: Sang Hyang Tunggal, that is Sang Hyang Caturboja. As for
2: the four sons of Sang Hyang Tunggal who were placed
3: upon the throne of stone beside their father Sang Hyang Tunggal,
4: each had distinct forms, wondrous and perfect. All of them,
5: having been blessed by their father and by the will of the
6: Lord of the Worlds, were greeted by all the gods of Mount
7: Mercukunda, who came before the four sons.
8: And after they grew to maturity, the four sons
9: of Sang Hyang Tunggal were deeply loved by their parents: Sang Hyang Pu-
10: gung and Sang Hyang Punggung, because Sang Hyang Pugung was exceedingly foolish,
11: unable to speak well, while Sang Hyang Punggung was also crude. If instructed,
12: they refused to comprehend, and constantly disputed with each other.
13: Their hearts were unruly, causing disorder within the pa-
14: lace. Then the gods turned away in disappointment,
15: and they were dismissed from the presence of their parents. All
16: the gods turned their faces away from them. And from the heavenly nymphs

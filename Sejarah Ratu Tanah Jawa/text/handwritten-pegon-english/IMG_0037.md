# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — Scan IMG_0037 Translation

### Folio 73 (Visual Right)
1: After the sister was married, she was placed on the southern side of the court.
2: Her elder sister had died, leaving behind only the memories of past days
3: in the palace. There was also a sister of the king who was exceedingly lovely,
4: residing in the inner court, secluded from the sight of all common folk.
5: She was accompanied by her handmaidens, and they dwelled on the mountain slopes,
6: living in harmony, beloved by all who beheld them. If they requested
7: anything from the king on the mountain, their requests were graciously granted.
8: She was surrounded by many attendants, and her brother was greatly honored,
9: regarded as a hero, skilled in the art of war, so that whoever
10: came to oppose him in battle was defeated. All submitted to him,
11: and all people held him in high esteem and affection.
12: When the king went forth to the countryside, everyone
13: followed him to the middle of the field on the southern mountain,
14: honoring the king, who was accompanied by Raden Daniswara.
15: Then, to continue the tale, here begins the account of the mountain
16: of Mount Kendeng, beloved by all, standing firm in the south,
17: stretching between the south and east, with a river flowing through it.

### Folio 74 (Visual Left)
1: unto the royal realm. His sister was given in marriage to the lord of
2: Mount Kelud, who was very prosperous and revered. Then
3: she was established as queen in the palace of King Sindula.
4: Her sister was married to King Sindula and became his royal consort,
5: living in splendor on the golden throne. Tomorrow
6: they would journey back to Medang. When they returned,
7: they were accompanied by their retinue, returning peacefully
8: to the palace. She was beloved by all her attendants,
9: so that all the court admired her beauty and grace.
10: She was given the title of Queen of the Southern Realm,
11: and her two brothers ruled over the realm
12: of Jemini. When the time of their departure came,
13: they took leave, saying: "May your journey be blessed,
14: and may you remain safe under the guidance of King Sindula." The two
15: brothers and the youngest sister, who had stayed in the cave,
16: had accompanied their sister, sitting together beside the royal dais,
17: and their journey was peaceful, watched over by the gods.

# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — IMG_0064

## Folio 127 (Visual Right)

1. Their arrival there was observed by Pangeran Lowan and all his companions,  
2. who were standing ready, fully equipped and armed. Then  
3. the King looked upon the council of chiefs and nobles;  
4. all the dignitaries who had arrived were invited to come forward,  
5. while the scouts and soldiers were stationed along the perimeter.  
6. The King then commanded that a royal proclamation be read,  
7. praising the valor of the army and the steadfastness of the chiefs;  
8. the noble ladies and royal attendants in the pavilion  
9. looked on with admiration, and all the people rejoiced.  
10. Then Buyut Seta and Pangeran Lowan stepped forward,  
11. bowing reverently before the King with hands clasped in homage;  
12. all the dignitaries also bowed down, offering their respects.  
13. The King was exceedingly pleased by their fidelity and courage,  
14. and reaffirmed their authority over the surrounding lands;  
15. he urged them to maintain vigilance and guard the peace of the realm,  
16. so that no foreign intruder or rebel could disrupt the tranquility of Java;  
17. to which all the gathered commanders replied with obedient assent.

## Folio 128 (Visual Left)

1. There were eight hundred men assembled under the command of the elders,  
2. all armed with lances and dressed in battle attire. Then Kyai Seta  
3. ordered the heralds to announce the royal decree to the populace,  
4. and they proceeded to the outer courtyard, where the people were gathered;  
5. all listening attentively to the words spoken by the herald.  
6. The King then came forth, accompanied by his personal guard,  
7. and the entire assembly rose to pay homage to the sovereign.  
8. The trumpets sounded and drums rolled in great celebration,  
9. while the people shouted acclamations of loyalty and joy.  
10. The King distributed gifts of rice, gold, and fine garments  
11. to the soldiers and common people who had gathered from every district;  
12. all returned home with joyful hearts, blessing the name of the King.  
13. Pangeran Lowan was then instructed to oversee the northern borders,  
14. establishing outposts and garrison troops at key mountain passes,  
15. so that order was firmly secured against any further disturbance;  
16. and peace returned once more to the entire country,  
17. while the King continued to reign in justice and prosperity.

## [Folio 1]

1. [illuminated / decorated heading band]  
2. [illuminated / decorated heading band]  
3. [In the name of God, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful]  
4. Praise be to God, Lord of the Worlds, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful, Master of  
5. the Day of Judgment, and the ultimate outcome is for the pious. Now follows the story  
6. of the history of Prophet Adam, peace be upon him, when he was punished by  
7. the Lord God, glory be to Him and exalted is He, the One, the Subduer,  
8. because he fell prey to the plotting of Iblis, the accursed of God. This came to pass through  
9. his noble wife, Kangjeng Babu Hawa, may God be pleased with her, because  
10. Kangjeng Prophet Adam, peace be upon him, readily listened to the words of his  
11. noble wife, Kangjeng Babu Hawa; and Kangjeng Babu Hawa had readily listened  
12. to the words of Iblis, the accursed of God, when she was offered to eat the fruit  
13. of the Khuldi tree; and so Kangjeng Babu Hawa complied, because  
14. Iblis, the accursed of God, disguised himself as an angel and claimed he was sent,  
15. Iblis the accursed of God claiming to be sent by the Exalted, the One,  
16. the Subduer, accompanied by numerous solemn oaths of Iblis.  
17. Thus it came to pass that Kangjeng Babu Hawa ate of the fruit  
18. of the Khuldi tree.  
[catchword: of the Khuldi tree]

## [Folio 2]

1. of the Khuldi tree. Now the fruits growing upon the Khuldi tree  
2. in Paradise were only three; one was offered to her husband,  
3. the revered Prophet Adam, peace be upon him, so that only one fruit of the  
4. Khuldi tree remained. Thereupon Kangjeng Prophet Adam, peace be upon him, was greatly  
5. startled when he was offered the fruit of the Khuldi tree by his noble wife,  
6. Kangjeng Babu Hawa, because previously Kangjeng Prophet  
7. Adam had been warned by the Exalted that Paradise had been surrendered  
8. with all its contents, except for one thing which Kangjeng Prophet Adam, peace be upon him,  
9. was not permitted by the Lord of the Worlds, namely that which was called the Khuldi  
10. tree; indeed, far from eating its fruit, even if Kangjeng the Prophet  
11. were permitted to wander, simply being in the vicinity of the Khuldi fruit  
12. was not permitted. Thereupon Kangjeng Babu Hawa said to Kangjeng Prophet  
13. Adam, peace be upon him, that it was the will of the Lord of the Worlds, an angel  
14. having been sent to proclaim it, accompanied by many solemn oaths;  
15. thus Kangjeng the Prophet complied with the urging of his noble wife,  
16. and ate the Khuldi fruit. But as soon as it was swallowed, Kangjeng  
17. Prophet Adam, peace be upon him, remembered that no  
[catchword: angel]

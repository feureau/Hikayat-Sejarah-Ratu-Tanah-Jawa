# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — IMG_0068

## Folio 135 (Visual Right)

1. Now in the palace court, the dignitaries and royal relatives  
2. gathered to witness the formal reception of the newly arrived emissaries;  
3. the King sat in full majesty upon the golden throne,  
4. dressed in magnificent garments adorned with precious gems,  
5. while the Queen and the princesses sat upon the inner dais.  
6. The master of ceremonies stepped forward to announce the proceedings,  
7. and the tribute from the outlying districts was presented before the sovereign:  
8. vessels of carved silver, rolls of fine silk, and rare aromatics,  
9. which the King received with satisfaction and gracious acknowledgment.  
10. Then the high priests offered solemn prayers for the health of the royal house  
11. and for continuous prosperity throughout the land of Java;  
12. all the ministers bowed low, expressing their heartfelt agreement and loyalty.  
13. The King then addressed the gathering on the duties of governance,  
14. emphasizing the need for mutual trust between the ruler and his subjects,  
15. and warning against arrogance and negligence in public administration;  
16. each official listened with profound respect, committing the advice to memory.  
17. Following the audience, a lavish feast was served to all who were present.

## Folio 136 (Visual Left)

1. Now in those days, the administration of the outlying regions  
2. was entrusted to reliable regents who reported regularly to the court;  
3. among these was Ki Tumenggung of the western territories,  
4. renowned for his courage, firmness, and fairness in judgment.  
5. He maintained strong defensive outposts along the mountain passes,  
6. preventing banditry and ensuring the safe passage of traveling merchants.  
7. The villagers lived in security, tilling their rice fields and orchards,  
8. so that the markets were constantly supplied with agricultural produce.  
9. When reports of his successful governance reached the ears of the King,  
10. the sovereign bestowed upon him a ceremonial kris and robe of honor,  
11. as tokens of royal appreciation for his unswerving dedication.  
12. Other regional administrators were encouraged to follow his example,  
13. fostering a spirit of diligent service throughout the administrative hierarchy.  
14. Thus the entire realm enjoyed stability and domestic tranquility,  
15. and the authority of the central government was respected everywhere;  
16. while the King continued to oversee all matters with vigilant care,  
17. ensuring that justice was administered without fear or partiality.

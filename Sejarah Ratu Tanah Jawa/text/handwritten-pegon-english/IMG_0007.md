### Folio 13 (Visual Right)  

1. Adam was reunited with Babu Eve. Then  
2. Kangjeng Prophet Adam prostrated himself before Subḥānahu wa-Ta‘ālā,  
3. prostrating in gratitude, and Kangjeng the Prophet was clothed with garments brought  
4. from Paradise. After the angel Jabrā’īl, peace be  
5. upon him, had clothed Kangjeng Prophet Adam, peace be upon him,  
6. the angel Jabrā’īl, peace be upon him, went to Mount Surandīl  
7. to Kangjeng Prophet Adam, peace be upon him. And Kangjeng Prophet Adam,  
8. peace be upon him, went to the peak of Mount Surandīl, resting upon  
9. Ceylon, wearing his garments, fragrant with musk and civet.  
10. Kangjeng Babu Eve was on the peak of Mount ‘Arafah in the land of Syria,  
11. weeping in sorrow. Thereupon came the angel  
12. Jabrā’īl, peace be upon him, to Kangjeng Babu Eve, may Allāh be pleased with her,  
13. conveying the news: "Babu Eve, you have been forgiven  
14. by the Lord of the Worlds!" And Kangjeng Babu Eve was clothed with the garments  
15. of Paradise, may Allāh be pleased with her, brought from Paradise. Then Kangjeng

### Folio 14 (Visual Left)  

1. the Prophet prayed the Dawn prayer. After the Prophet prayed,  
2. he prostrated himself, weeping with profound emotion. And  
3. the angel Jabrā’īl, peace be upon him, arrived before Kangjeng Prophet Adam, peace be  
4. upon him, who was still prostrating. Then the angel Jabrā’īl  
5. greeted him, saying: "Peace be upon you, O Adam!" Kangjeng Prophet Adam answered the greeting,  
6. and embraced the angel Jabrā’īl, peace be upon him. After  
7. embracing him, the angel Jabrā’īl, peace be upon him, said to  
8. Prophet Adam: "You have been forgiven by  
9. the Lord Subḥānahu wa-Ta‘ālā for forty days, or one thousand  
10. years of the Prophet's earthly life; if it were not for this, after one thousand years  
11. the Prophet would have returned to Paradise!" And Prophet Adam was granted

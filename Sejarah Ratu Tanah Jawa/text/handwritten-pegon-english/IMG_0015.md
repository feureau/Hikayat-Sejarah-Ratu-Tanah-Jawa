### Folio 29 (Visual Right)

1: Long he dwelt upon Mount Thawr, and after Nur Cahya
2: was born on Mount Thawr in Mecca, he was called Sayyid
3: Anwas. After Nur Rasa reached adulthood,
4: the command of Subḥānahu wa-Ta‘ālā came to Nur Cahya
5: and his brother Nur Rasa: the angel Jabrā’īl, peace be upon him,
6: was sent down to Mount Thawr, commanding them
7: to depart: Nur Cahya was instructed: "If you do not find
8: light in the land of the Arabs, depart!" Then the angel Jabrā’īl,
9: peace be upon him, arrived at Mount Thawr, meeting with Nur Cahya,
10: who was accompanied by his brother Nur Rasa. The angel Jabrā’īl, peace be
11: upon him, spoke to Nur Cahya and Nur Rasa:
12: "Jabrā’īl, peace be upon him, says: 'If you no longer abide
13: in the land of the Arabs, it is by the decree of Subḥānahu wa-Ta‘ālā;
14: for the appointed time has come. Therefore,
15: if you are not content to remain among men in this manner,
16: in the earthly world, establishing a kingdom granted by the Lord of the Worlds,
17: depart into the intermediate realm [*‘ālam antara*], that is the realm of the heavens!'"

### Folio 30 (Visual Left)

1: This radiance was willed by the Lord of the Worlds. And after
2: the passing of Nur Cahya, he returned to his father,
3: Kangjeng Prophet Shīth, peace be upon him. He dwelt
4: beside Nur Cahya and returned to his father.
5: Then Nur Cahya went to Mount Thawr,
6: which was in Mecca, bearing his radiance.
7: There he fathered children; and here ends
8: the chronicle of Kangjeng Prophet Adam, peace be upon him; and Allāh knows best
9: the truth: *ghufiralahum Allāh huwa Yā Rabbī Yā Ghafūr* ("May Allāh forgive them, O my Lord, O All-Forgiving").
10: As for the chronicle of the origin of all creatures
11: created by the Lord God Subḥānahu wa-Ta‘ālā, the One, the Subduer,
12: Mighty and Majestic, who created the beings called gods [*dewa*], descendants
13: of Batara Guru from the radiance of the angels, ordained to become
14: the seed of Nur Cahya. As for the chronicle
15: of Nur Cahya: having long dwelt
16: in the land of Mecca upon Mount Thawr,
17: he served the Lord of the Worlds, bearing his radiance,

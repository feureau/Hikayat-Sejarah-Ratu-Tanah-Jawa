### Folio 35 (Visual Right)

1: Sang Hyang Nur Rasa, who had meditated continuously,
2: was granted a boon by Subḥānahu wa-Ta‘ālā;
3: for he was loved and brought into heaven. Then Sang Hyang
4: Nur Cahya and Sang Hyang Nur Rasa, in the intermediate realm,
5: along with all their descendants, became leaders
6: among the deities. Sang Hyang Nur Cahya and Sang Hyang Nur Rasa, both of them,
7: Sang Hyang Nur Rasa becoming the elder teacher, beloved
8: of the Lord of the Worlds, were endowed with supreme spiritual power,
9: without equal among their peers. Then Sang Hyang Tunggal,
10: through the grace of Subḥānahu wa-Ta‘ālā, the One, the Subduer,
11: was made king over all the gods, because all revered
12: Sang Hyang Tunggal and Sang Hyang Nur Rasa and Sang Hyang
13: Nur Cahya. All bowed down in submission to them. Then Sang Hyang Tung-
14: gal, meditating in his heart, was filled with sorrow:
15: "Why should you, Sang Hyang Tunggal, grieve in your heart, when
16: you are favored by the Lord of the Worlds,
17: having been blessed with dominion by your grandfather and father?"

# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — IMG_0062

## Folio 123 (Visual Right)

1. Then Haris spoke to his uncle, Kyai Seta, and Pangeran Lowan:  
2. "Kyai, behold the battlefield; Haris will lead the advance,  
3. so that if the enemy attacks, we will not be taken by surprise."  
4. For it was reported that the enemy commander intended to attack at dawn,  
5. and therefore they prepared their defenses carefully. The King  
6. then sent a messenger to summon the commanders to his tent;  
7. and when they arrived, he gave them his final instructions,  
8. assigning each captain to his designated position on the battlefield.  
9. Pangeran Lowan was placed in command of the right flank,  
10. while Kyai Seta held the left wing with his veteran troops.  
11. Haris then drew his kris, swearing that he would fight valiantly  
12. and never retreat, even in the face of overwhelming odds.  
13. The herald then sounded the trumpet, signaling the advance,  
14. and the soldiers responded with a thunderous roar;  
15. they moved forward like a tidal wave crashing upon the shore,  
16. each man eager to distinguish himself before the King,  
17. who watched from his elevated vantage point on the hill.

## Folio 124 (Visual Left)

1. And having assembled, they marched towards the battlefield;  
2. the enemy forces could already be seen from a distance,  
3. entrenched along the ridges and waiting behind barricades.  
4. The King observed the disposition of their forces, noting  
5. that they had set up stakes and trenches to obstruct the cavalry.  
6. Pangeran Lowan then ordered the spearmen to advance in close formation,  
7. while the archers kept up a steady barrage of fire to cover them.  
8. The clash of arms began with great fury on both sides;  
9. the sound of battle echoed through the surrounding hills,  
10. and many fell wounded as the two lines met in fierce combat.  
11. Kyai Seta fought with remarkable valor, striking down several opponents,  
12. while Lowan pressed forward into the heart of the enemy lines.  
13. The enemy, unable to withstand the resolute royal assault,  
14. began to break ranks and fall back in disarray;  
15. their commander attempted to flee towards the forest,  
16. but was pursued and captured by the royal scouts.  
17. Thus complete victory was secured for the forces of the King.

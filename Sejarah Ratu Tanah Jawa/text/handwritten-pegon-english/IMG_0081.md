## Folio 161 (Visual Right)

1: Now they gathered together, bringing forth all the tribute from the outlying districts;
2: the messengers bowed low before the presence of the prince, offering submission.
3: The Adipati then ordered that royal rations be distributed among the soldiers,
4: and that those who were weary from the march be given rest and medical aid.
5: Then the council discussed the affairs of state and the collection of taxes,
6: ensuring that no excessive burdens were placed upon the cultivating peasantry;
7: for the prince decreed that justice and moderation were the pillars of authority,
8: and that only through equity would the blessings of peace endure in the land.
9: The local headmen were instructed to maintain the roads, canals, and bridges,
10: so that travelers and merchant caravans could move freely without impediment.
11: After these matters were settled, the Adipati reviewed the palace guard,
12: testing their readiness and discipline with various martial maneuvers;
13: he commended the officers for their vigilance and steadfast conduct,
14: bestowing robes of honor upon those who demonstrated superior skill.
15: Here begins the chronicle of the subsequent deeds of Ki Patih Gajah Mada,
16: who continued to govern the administrative machinery with unrivaled mastery;
17: his fame spread across all the realms of Nusantara as a wise and powerful statesman.

## Folio 162 (Visual Left)

1: Now the tale turns to the sovereign of Majapahit, Prabu Brawijaya,
2: who held royal audience in the inner hall surrounded by his high ministers;
3: the chief advisor reported on the state of the treasury and food stores,
4: confirming that the granaries were full and the people contented throughout Java.
5: The king expressed gratitude for the loyal service rendered by the elders,
6: granting further honors and territorial endowments to the deserving noblemen.
7: Meanwhile, reports arrived from the coastal regions regarding foreign trade,
8: noting that merchant vessels from various nations were anchoring at the ports;
9: the harbormasters ensured that proper tolls were collected and peace preserved,
10: so that commerce flourished and brought great wealth to the kingdom.
11: Prabu Brawijaya exhorted his commanders to maintain firm vigilance at sea,
12: protecting merchant fleets from corsairs and ensuring safe navigation along the coasts.
13: All the assembled lords gave their unanimous pledge of obedience,
14: promising to execute the royal will with unswerving dedication and zeal;
15: thus the realm remained orderly, prosperous, and secure under royal law,
16: as recorded by the scribes in the enduring chronicles of the ancestors,
17: which celebrate the glorious achievements of the ancient kings of Java.

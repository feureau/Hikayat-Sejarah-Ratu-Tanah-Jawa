# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — Scan IMG_0042 Translation

### Folio 83 (Visual Right)
1: And after the sage had established the boundaries, King Buddhasoca
2: divided the land among the royal princes, giving each a territory
3: to govern. To Raden Daniswara was given the realm of the east,
4: while his brother received the western marches. Both brothers
5: ruled with dedication and wisdom, maintaining the prosperity of their people.
6: King Buddhasoca watched over them with deep fatherly pride,
7: counseling them in times of need and ensuring harmony between them.
8: In those years, the arts flourished throughout the court,
9: and poets composed chronicles celebrating the heroic deeds of the ancestors.
10: Beautiful temples were constructed, dedicated to spiritual learning
11: and contemplation, drawing pilgrims from across the seas.
12: The seasons brought abundant rains, filling the rivers and reservoirs,
13: so that the rice fields yielded two crops each year without fail.
14: Markets were filled with merchandise from distant lands,
15: and traders praised the safety of the roads and waterways.
16: Thus the kingdom enjoyed a golden age of peace and culture,
17: remembered fondly in song and story throughout the realm.

### Folio 84 (Visual Left)
1: All the elders and ministers of the court held council,
2: deciding important matters of state in consultation with the king.
3: Raden Daniswara showed remarkable skill in resolving disputes,
4: listening patiently to both sides before rendering his fair judgment.
5: Even the forest dwellers and highland tribes recognized his authority,
6: bringing him tributes of rare honey, precious resins, and fragrant woods.
7: The prince received them with hospitality, granting them protection
8: and fair trade terms, so that they lived in contentment within the realm.
9: Meanwhile, the queen mother devoted herself to charitable works,
10: caring for orphans, widows, and those afflicted by sickness or poverty.
11: She established houses of healing where skilled physicians
12: administered herbal remedies and spiritual solace to all who came.
13: The people called down blessings upon her head,
14: loving her as their own mother throughout the provinces.
15: Thus the royal family was revered by all classes of society,
16: from the highest nobles to the humblest farmhands.
17: And peace rested upon the land like a gentle morning mist.

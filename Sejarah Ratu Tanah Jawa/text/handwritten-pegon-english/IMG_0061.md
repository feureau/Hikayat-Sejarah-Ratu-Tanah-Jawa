# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — IMG_0061

## Folio 121 (Visual Right)

1. and the commander said to Pangeran Lowan: "Behold,  
2. the troops of our lord have been arranged in battle order;  
3. all the ranks are steadfast, and the prince is ready."  
4. The herald then stepped forward and bowed,  
5. delivering the message that the army was ready to march;  
6. and all the Adipatis and elders bowed down,  
7. expressing their gratitude to the prince. Then Semaduk  
8. went to the right side of Kyai Buyut Seta's mount,  
9. and if the prince had any command, it was conveyed  
10. immediately; for the heralds were standing by,  
11. and Kyai Buyut received the report with contentment.  
12. Then they raised their standards and banners high,  
13. while the soldiers shouted war cries, filling the air with sound;  
14. the heralds led the van, bearing the royal insignia,  
15. and then all the companies marched forth in great numbers,  
16. each banner waving in the wind, accompanied by drums,  
17. advancing in orderly progression towards their objective.

## Folio 122 (Visual Left)

1. Now the enemy was encamped in the plain, looking out  
2. towards the royal army which was advancing steadily;  
3. the sound of their brass instruments and gong chimes  
4. echoed across the valley, striking terror into the hearts  
5. of the rebels. Kyai Roh then surveyed the enemy's positions,  
6. Senapati Mataram holding supreme command over the vanguard;  
7. he ordered the cavalry to advance along the left flank,  
8. while the spearmen held the center firmly, shields locked together;  
9. and in the vanguard, the archers and matchlockmen opened fire,  
10. sending a shower of missiles into the opposing ranks.  
11. Seeing this formidable array, the enemy ranks wavered;  
12. their leader attempted to rally his men, urging them to fight,  
13. but the royal forces pressed forward without hesitation.  
14. Then the King ordered reinforcements to be brought up,  
15. and Kyai Seta smiled, seeing that victory was at hand;  
16. the King praised Pangeran Lowan for his bravery and skill,  
17. and the entire host advanced triumphantly across the field.

# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — IMG_0070

## Folio 139 (Visual Right)

1. Now this account recounts the instruction given by the venerable Kyai Haji,  
2. who taught his disciples the paths of righteousness and devotion;  
3. then Kyai Patih also arrived to receive spiritual instruction,  
4. asking concerning the governance of the realm and the duties of leadership.  
5. The master explained that a just ruler is like a sheltering banyan tree,  
6. providing shade and comfort to all travelers beneath its branches;  
7. his words were received with reverence and committed to writing,  
8. so that future administrators might learn the virtues of humility and justice.  
9. Then the disciples departed to their respective districts across Java,  
10. spreading the teachings of piety, honesty, and mutual goodwill;  
11. and the common people embraced these principles with sincere hearts,  
12. leading to a period of unprecedented social peace and contentment.  
13. The royal court patronized schools, libraries, and houses of worship,  
14. ensuring that knowledge flourished in every corner of the realm;  
15. foreign scholars praised the King for his enlightenment and wisdom,  
16. recording his deeds in chronicles that traveled far across the eastern seas;  
17. and the kingdom stood prosperous, unified, and strong under righteous governance.

## Folio 140 (Visual Left)

1. Now after the king of Pajang had passed away,  
2. the council of nobles assembled to deliberate upon the succession;  
3. the royal princes presented their claims with dignity and decorum,  
4. and the supreme elders counseled unity and adherence to lawful tradition.  
5. Pangeran Benawa was recognized for his noble demeanor and piety,  
6. receiving the allegiance of the principal commanders and court ministers;  
7. he accepted the mantle of leadership with solemn responsibility,  
8. dedicating his reign to preserving the welfare of the people and state.  
9. Envoys were dispatched to all provinces to proclaim the ascension,  
10. and celebrations were held throughout the island of Java;  
11. the people rejoiced, offering prayers for a long and peaceful reign,  
12. while regional rulers reaffirmed their fealty to the throne.  
13. The new sovereign confirmed the existing laws and judicial customs,  
14. rewarding faithful officials and appointing capable magistrates;  
15. thus the transition of authority was accomplished without strife,  
16. and the kingdom continued in stability, order, and honor,  
17. as recounted in the enduring annals of the history of Java.

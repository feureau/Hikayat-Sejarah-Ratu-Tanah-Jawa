## Folio 149 (Visual Right)

1. Now when Prabu Brawijaya sat in full state on the throne of Majapahit,  
2. all the regional princes, commanders, and dignitaries of the realm assembled;  
3. the envoys from the western domains stood in attendance along the left wing,  
4. and the ambassadors from across the seas presented tribute before the sovereign.  
5. The palace court resounded with formal greetings, prayers, and declarations of loyalty,  
6. while the royal heralds read aloud decrees confirming land grants and appointments.  
7. The king inquired after the condition of each province, asking  
8. whether the rains had been sufficient, the rice harvests abundant, and the roads secure;  
9. the regents replied with one voice, testifying that all was prosperous and peaceful,  
10. and that the populace lived in contentment under the king's benevolent guidance.  
11. Prabu Brawijaya praised the dedication of his ministers and governors,  
12. exhorting them to continually safeguard the welfare of the weak and vulnerable;  
13. he presented fine robes of honor, gold-plated krises, and royal certificates,  
14. honoring those who had shown outstanding virtue and diligence in their office.  
15. The assembly bowed low in heartfelt gratitude, touching the king's feet,  
16. and the ceremony concluded with a great feast shared by all the dignitaries;  
17. then the regional leaders prepared to journey back to their respective domains.

## Folio 150 (Visual Left)

1. Meanwhile, in the kingdom of Demak, Pangeran Jaka Tingkir  
2. continued to earn high honor through his wisdom, courage, and nobility;  
3. he was granted authority over the principality of Pajang,  
4. with the title of Adipati Hadiwijaya, establishing his court at Pajang.  
5. Many followers, artisans, and brave warriors flocked to his banner,  
6. drawn by his upright character and generous patronage;  
7. he laid out the royal capital with splendid gateways, courtyards, and mosques,  
8. transforming Pajang into a major center of power and Islamic scholarship in Java.  
9. Adipati Hadiwijaya governed with exceptional justice and foresight,  
10. maintaining peaceful alliances with neighboring principalities like Mataram and Demak;  
11. he encouraged the cultivation of fertile lands along the Solo River,  
12. so that agricultural yields multiplied and market centers thrived.  
13. The elders and religious scholars praised his piety and reverence for tradition,  
14. recognizing that he embodied the virtues of both warrior and sage;  
15. thus the state of Pajang grew steadily in strength and spiritual renown,  
16. earning the respect of all rulers throughout the length and breadth of Java,  
17. while Sultan Hadiwijaya continued to guide his people along the path of righteous peace.

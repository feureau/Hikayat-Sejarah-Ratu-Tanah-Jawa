### Folio 17 (Visual Right)  

1. may Allāh be pleased with her, gave birth to twins, male and female:  
2. the first-born named Qābīl, then Hābīl, followed by Kayūmūrtaka.  
3. All of them were of light radiant and gleaming. And after they  
4. grew to maturity, the children of Kangjeng Prophet Adam, peace be upon him, married:  
5. Kangjeng Prophet Adam, peace be upon him, and his consort Kangjeng  
6. Babu Eve, may Allāh be pleased with her, by the will of the Prophet,  
7. wedded the children crosswise: what Kangjeng Prophet  
8. Adam wished, Babu Eve agreed to, so that  
9. in their outward appearance whatever was born was well-suited.  
10. Thus it was ordained by the Lord of the Worlds; when they were united,  
11. whatever was born was healthy and handsome, without  
12. sorrow. And Kangjeng Prophet Adam,  
13. peace be upon him, and his consort Kangjeng Babu Eve, may Allāh  
14. be pleased with her, had offspring who multiplied across the earth, all of them children  
15. of Kangjeng Prophet Adam and Kangjeng Babu Eve. Because  
16. they were blessed, their descendants grew numerous,

### Folio 18 (Visual Left)  

1. together with his consort Kangjeng Babu Eve, may Allāh be pleased with her. By the will of Kangjeng Prophet  
2. Adam, peace be upon him, the Lord God Subḥānahu wa-Ta‘ālā,  
3. the One, the Subduer, took away his life by death.  
4. In the essence of Allāh the Unseen, Kangjeng Prophet Adam, peace be upon him,  
5. returned to the presence of Allāh, for by the decree of the Lord  
6. of the Worlds, Kangjeng Prophet Adam had lived on earth,  
7. having been sent down from Paradise to this world by the will of Subḥānahu wa-Ta‘ālā,  
8. to become the father of all humankind on earth,  
9. as the Chosen One [*ṣafī Allāh*]. And Kangjeng Prophet Adam, peace be  
10. upon him, before the Lord of the Worlds, became the Friend of Allāh [*khalīl Allāh*], and  
11. gathered in Mecca with his consort Babu Eve. As for the chronicle  
12. of Kangjeng Prophet Adam, peace be upon him, his livelihood in Mecca  
13. was by farming, planting what the angel Jabrā’īl,  
14. peace be upon him, brought, so that it grew into crops. And after it grew,  
15. everything in the earthly world was made known by the angel  
16. Jabrā’īl, peace be upon him. Such is the chronicle of Kangjeng Prophet  
17. Adam, peace be upon him: he fathered children with Babu Eve,

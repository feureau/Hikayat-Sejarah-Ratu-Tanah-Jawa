## Folio 125 (Visual Right)

1: then they went before the presence of the prince, and all the chiefs
2: gathered together in the audience hall;
3: the King sat on the throne, surrounded by his dignitaries,
4: and they presented gifts of cloth and weapons.
5: When the King saw the captive leaders brought before him,
6: he ordered them to be treated with clemency,
7: saying that they should be reconciled and integrated into the state.
8: Then the King ordered a great feast to be prepared,
9: inviting all the officers and soldiers who had fought with valor;
10: the sound of gamelan music filled the palace courts,
11: and all rejoiced at the peace that had been restored.
12: The ministers praised the King's wisdom and benevolence,
13: noting that his reign brought prosperity to the land.
14: Then the King conferred titles and rewards upon Pangeran Lowan,
15: granting him territories and fertile lands in recognition of his service;
16: Kyai Seta and Haris were likewise honored with elevated ranks,
17: and all the soldiers received their shares of the spoils.

## Folio 126 (Visual Left)

1: Now after these events had transpired, the King directed his attention
2: to the administration of the realm, establishing just laws
3: and appointing upright governors across all the provinces.
4: The people flourished under this governance, engaging in agriculture
5: and commerce without fear of strife or bandits.
6: Traders came from afar to the northern harbors,
7: bringing fine fabrics, spices, and precious wares;
8: the markets were bustling with activity, and abundance prevailed everywhere.
9: Meanwhile, news arrived from the eastern regions concerning
10: disputes between local lords that threatened public order;
11: wherefore the King dispatched envoys carrying royal letters,
12: urging reconciliation and adherence to lawful authority.
13: The local rulers received the royal command with reverence,
14: bowing before the majesty of the throne and renewing their allegiance.
15: Thus harmony was maintained throughout the island of Java,
16: and the King continued to rule with righteousness and piety,
17: beloved by all his subjects from the highest to the lowest.

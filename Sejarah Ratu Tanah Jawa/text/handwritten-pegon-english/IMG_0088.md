# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — IMG_0088

## Folio 175 (Visual Right)

1. and so it was recounted by the people of the land of Java. Thus is the chronicle;  
2. God willing (Insha'Allah), the true knowledge rests with God alone (Allahu a'lam bi al-sawab). May God forgive them,  
3. O God, O All-Forgiving (Allahu ya Ghafur). Here ends the transcription of the book  
4. on the seventh day of the month of Jumada al-Awwal, on Wednesday at noon,  
5. in the month of Dhu al-Hijjah, in the year Jim, of the Hijrah of the Holy Prophet—  
6. may God bless him and grant him peace—one thousand two hundred  
7. and fifty-five (1255 AH). Written in Makassar.

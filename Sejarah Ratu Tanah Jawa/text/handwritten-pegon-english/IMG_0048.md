# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — Scan IMG_0048 Translation

### Folio 95 (Visual Right)
1: Now we tell of Ki Ageng Sela, who walked in righteousness,
2: accompanied by his faithful wife. As they walked through the fields,
3: they beheld a great crowd gathered near the edge of the grove.
4: Ki Ageng Sela went forward to see what had taken place,
5: and found that a ferocious beast was terrorizing the village.
6: The villagers cried out for help, trembling with fear.
7: Ki Ageng Sela approached the creature with a calm and fearless heart,
8: speaking words of prayer and invoking the name of the Almighty.
9: By divine power, the beast became gentle and submissive,
10: bowing down at the feet of the holy sage.
11: The villagers were filled with wonder and gratitude,
12: offering gifts and praise to Ki Ageng Sela.
13: But the sage declined their gifts, saying: "All praise belongs to God alone;
14: worship Him and live in peace with one another."
15: Then he continued on his journey toward his dwelling place,
16: while the people remembered his deed with reverence and awe.
17: And his name became famous throughout the region for his sanctity.

### Folio 96 (Visual Left)
1: After many years, Ki Ageng Sela instructed his sons
2: in the sacred mysteries of faith and virtuous governance.
3: He foretold the rise of a new dynasty from his bloodline,
4: that would rule over the entire island of Java.
5: He said: "A grandson of mine shall become a mighty king,
6: possessing great spiritual authority and temporal power.
7: Under his rule, the people shall find peace and prosperity,
8: and the kingdom shall stand as a pillar of righteousness."
9: His sons listened with deep reverence to the father's prophecy,
10: committing his words to memory and passing them down to their heirs.
11: In the court of Demak, the Sultan was troubled by dreams,
12: and consulted the royal advisors concerning the future of the state.
13: The wise counselors recalled the words of the sages of old,
14: advising the Sultan to govern with piety and seek the counsel of holy men.
15: Thus the times shifted toward the fulfillment of the divine decree,
16: as recorded in the ancient annals and sacred prophecies.
17: And God's wisdom directed the affairs of all the realms of Java.

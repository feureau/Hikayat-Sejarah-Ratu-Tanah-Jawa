# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — Scan IMG_0052 Translation

### Folio 103 (Visual Right)
1: And King Brawijaya sat upon his high seat in the audience pavilion,
2: surrounded by his brave commanders and wise counselors of state.
3: The assembly was magnificent, filled with nobles clad in gold and silk,
4: and heralds announced the arrival of petitioners from across the land.
5: Among them came a revered elder accompanied by a lovely maiden,
6: who approached the throne with humble steps and bowed down low.
7: The king inquired: "What is your purpose, and whence do you come?"
8: The elder answered: "O protector of the world, we come from the southern shore,
9: bringing news of an ominous portent that appeared above the sea."
10: King Brawijaya listened with grave attention to the elder's report,
11: and commanded the court astrologers to interpret the celestial sign.
12: The astrologers consulted their sacred charts and ancient scrolls,
13: declaring: "A momentous shift approaches the destiny of Java,
14: wherein the ancient ways shall give place to a new and enduring faith."
15: The king was thoughtful, recognizing the inevitable turning of time,
16: and ordered that prayers be made for the welfare and preservation of his people.
17: Thus was the prophecy of the coming transformation revealed to the court.

### Folio 104 (Visual Left)
1: After the assembly adjourned, King Brawijaya retired to his inner gardens,
2: accompanied by his favored queen, Dewi Suradewati.
3: They walked along the lotus ponds, conversing in quiet tones
4: about the strange omens and the future of the royal lineage.
5: Dewi Suradewati comforted the sovereign with gentle words:
6: "No power in heaven or earth can undo what the Almighty has willed;
7: let us place our trust in His infinite wisdom and mercy."
8: The king found peace in her counsel, admiring her spiritual clarity.
9: Meanwhile, in the distant provinces, rumors of change began to stir,
10: and sages in secluded hermitages prepared their disciples for the new era.
11: Scholars studied the ancient texts, seeking guidance on how to navigate
12: the turbulent currents of history that were soon to sweep over the realm.
13: Yet throughout the land, ordinary folk continued their daily labors,
14: tilling the fertile soil, harvesting rice, and trading in bustling markets.
15: The administration of justice was maintained without wavering,
16: and the king ensured that the poor and defenseless were protected from harm.
17: And so the kingdom remained tranquil under the shadow of approaching change.

# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa - English Translation
## Source: British Library Or. 14371
### Scan Concordance: IMG_0030.jpg (Folios 59 & 60)

---

### Folio 59 (Visual Right)

1: Therefore, all of Rum departed toward Ajir, cursed of Allāh.
2: There they were commanded to pay homage to the king. Then
3: they departed, forty of them, not knowing that he was the light of the cave.
4: All of them were received by Ajir, cursed of Allāh;
5: but after looking into his heart, which had no light within it,
6: they realized he was an unbeliever, cursed of Allāh, all of them.
7: And each day, those who gathered with the companions
8: of Aji Saka were commanded to turn back to Kangjeng the Prophet
9: Muḥammad, may Allāh bless him and grant him peace; they were commanded to leave the Arab land, repenting
10: to Isa. Aji Saka besought forgiveness with sincere devotion,
11: and Kangjeng the Prophet was pleased; for Iblīs, cursed of
12: Allāh, had entered their hearts. But when Aji Saka
13: realized this, by the will of the Lord of the Worlds, Aji Saka
14: did not become a follower of Iblīs. While still in the land of
15: the Arabs, not seeing the straight way, if they turned to
16: Islam, they were commanded to follow Aji Saka, seeking refuge from the cursed of Allāh:
17: *a‘ūdhu bi ’Llāhi minhā*, before Kangjeng Prophet Muḥammad, the Messenger of Allāh.

### Folio 60 (Visual Left)

1: to the children of Adam who had been tempted by Iblīs, and by the
2: truth of their faith, they turned in repentance before the Lord
3: of the Worlds, reciting the prayer of refuge [*ta‘awwudh*]. Then they entered
4: into the fellowship of all the companions. Then they gathered together;
5: those who remained faithful wept, and all gathered around
6: Kangjeng Prophet Muḥammad, may Allāh bless him and grant him peace; all his companions
7: were astonished, seeing that the spirit of Adam had departed,
8: but his knowledge remained silent. Then all
9: the companions, if they looked upon the throne, saw
10: weeping, their forms changing, turning into various
11: shapes, blossoming into flowers, and four-legged beasts,
12: all the companions. Then Sayyidinā Abū Bakr
13: al-Ṣiddīq spoke: "O Master, do not be deceived by
14: what has been said!" Kangjeng Prophet Muḥammad, the Messenger of Allāh, may Allāh bless him
15: and grant him peace, smiled, looking upon Sayyidinā Abū
16: Bakr al-Ṣiddīq, may Allāh be pleased with him. For the children of Adam
17: had their knowledge sealed, keeping silent and secret.

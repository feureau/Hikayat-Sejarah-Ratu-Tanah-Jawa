# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — Scan IMG_0046 Translation

### Folio 91 (Visual Right)
1. Then it was recorded that Raden Lembu Amiluhur had four sons,
2. who were born of the royal consort: the eldest was named Raden Panji,
3. the second was named Raden Brajanata, the third was Raden Carang Tinangluh,
4. and the fourth was named Raden Lembu Pangarang. And he had a daughter
5. who was exceedingly fair, renowned throughout the land, and loved by all.
6. When the prince came of age, he was given royal authority over Janggala,
7. and his realm flourished, filled with abundant harvests and wealthy markets.
8. In those days, Raden Panji journeyed toward Kediri,
9. seeking the princess Dewi Sekartaji who was promised to him.
10. But when he arrived, obstacles arose, for the princess had vanished
11. from the palace, wandering through forests and mountains in disguise.
12. Raden Panji, distraught with grief, set forth to search for his beloved,
13. assuming many names and disguises as he roamed through the land.
14. He performed great feats of valor, defeating bandits and wicked spirits,
15. and aiding the poor and oppressed wherever he passed.
16. The fame of his chivalry spread to every village and town,
17. and many folk joined him as loyal companions on his noble quest.

### Folio 92 (Visual Left)
1. The princess had changed her name to Endang Kencana, dwelling
2. in the hermitage of Mount Wilis with holy ascetics.
3. She devoted herself to prayer and sacred learning, hiding her royal beauty,
4. yet her radiance could not be concealed from discerning eyes.
5. When Raden Panji approached the mountain, drawn by reports of a maiden,
6. he disguised himself as a wandering bard, singing poignant tales of lost love.
7. The princess heard his voice from afar and recognized the song of her youth,
8. and her heart trembled with mingled joy and sorrow.
9. She sent her trusted handmaiden to inquire who the wandering singer was,
10. and when the maiden returned, she confirmed it was indeed the prince of Janggala.
11. Then they met beneath the shade of the ancient banyan tree,
12. weeping tears of reunion after enduring long trials of separation.
13. They renewed their vows of love and loyalty before the holy sage,
14. who blessed their union and predicted eternal peace for their descendants.
15. Word was sent to both kings, in Janggala and Kediri,
16. who rejoiced exceedingly at the joyous news of the couple's reconciliation.
17. Grand festivities were prepared to celebrate their return to the court.

# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — Scan IMG_0038 Translation

### Folio 75 (Visual Right)
1: The young man who was beloved, and also their sister,
2: the younger one, had a noble heart, longing to meet
3: the princess who lived on Mount Surapringga. She had high regard for the prince,
4: and the lady of Surapringga, named Dewi Retna, admired the youth.
5: She came before the prince and bowed low, paying homage,
6: and the youth was pleased, greeting her warmly. If she wished
7: to follow him, he would gladly take her along. Thus they journeyed
8: together, beloved by all, toward the realm of the prince.
9: Those who saw them were amazed, for Aji Saka had already
10: fashioned a realm named Medang Kamulan. Then
11: the young man took her as his bride, and they lived together in love,
12: honoring each other and obeying the commands of King
13: Sindula. Tomorrow, if they wished to wed,
14: they would be given royal garments, crowned with gold, and presented
15: with lands. All this was prepared, and the preparations were complete.
16: They were acclaimed by all the people, honoring them with praise,
17: and their wedding was celebrated for forty days and forty nights.

### Folio 76 (Visual Left)
1: And the king, who resided in Kediri, was renowned for his power,
2: and had many followers. He sent forth his messenger
3: to inquire about the state of his children and grandchildren.
4: The messenger arrived and bowed respectfully before the lord.
5: And the messenger was asked about the condition of the people,
6: whether they were in peace or distress. The messenger
7: replied that all was peaceful, and that the people
8: lived in harmony under the royal rule.
9: Then the messenger was given gifts, and returned to Kediri.
10: The king was very pleased to receive the report,
11: and thanked God for the well-being of his kingdom.
12: Because the realm was well-ordered, all the subjects
13: prospered, and their lives were filled with blessing.
14: Even the wild beasts in the forest were calm and tame.
15: Thus the land of Java became fertile and abundant,
16: and the king ruled with justice over all who dwelt in his land.
17: Thereupon he ordered that the customs and traditions of the elders be kept.

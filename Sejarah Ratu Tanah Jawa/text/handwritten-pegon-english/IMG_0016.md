# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa - English Translation
## Source: British Library Or. 14371
### Scan Concordance: IMG_0016.jpg (Folios 31 & 32)

### Folio 31 (Visual Right)

1: and their obedience to Subḥānahu wa-Ta‘ālā. For if
2: they were obedient and did not transgress, then their line would continue
3: during Laylat al-Qadr. Then Nur Cahya prostrated,
4: together with his brother Nur Rasa, before the Lord of the Worlds. After
5: they had completed their prostration, they took leave; the angel Jabrā’īl
6: departed from the earthly world to the intermediate realm [*‘ālam antara*].
7: peace be upon him, ascending upward. And after reaching the
8: intermediate realm, he established a realm on Mount
9: Purbawana. After the angel Jabrā’īl,
10: peace be upon him, had shown the way to Nur Cahya and his brother Nur
11: Rasa, the angel Jabrā’īl, peace be upon him, departed to
12: the intermediate realm. Then Nur Cahya and his
13: brother Nur Rasa worshipped the Lord God Subḥānahu wa-Ta‘ālā,
14: the One, the Subduer, Mighty and Majestic, ruling their realm and all
15: who were under their authority. Then descended the line of Nur
16: Cahya and his brother Nur Rasa, dwelling on Mount
17: Hind Purbawana, upon the peak of a high cliff

### Folio 32 (Visual Left)

1: that is why they were called gods [*dewa*]. And if
2: in the future they had offspring, they would be called
3: gods, and down to their line would be called Batara Guru
4: and Batari. On earth they would appear in human form,
5: in the kingdoms of Java; and from their line
6: would come in future times the royal rulers.
7: They were elevated in station by Subḥānahu
8: wa-Ta‘ālā in the intermediate realm, among the jinn, angels,
9: and spirits, endowed with sovereignty by the Lord of the Worlds
10: over all the races of jinn. When their work on earth was finished,
11: they chose to dwell in the intermediate realm [*‘ālam
12: antara*]. They were given authority over the multitude of
13: all types of jinn. After entering the intermediate realm,
14: they were called gods, and in the future their line would rule
15: as lords, gifted with light by the Lord of the Worlds,
16: possessing sovereignty. If they descended to earth,
17: they would appear in human form, by the decree

### Folio 24 (Visual Left)  

1. brought from Paradise. Then Kangjeng Prophet Adam,  
2. peace be upon him, and Kangjeng Babu Eve, may Allāh be pleased with her,  
3. together with their son Kangjeng Prophet Shīth, peace be upon him, sat within the Masjid  
4. al-Ḥarām. Then arrived the angel Jabrā’īl, peace be upon him,  
5. with the command of the Lord of the Worlds, bringing a heavenly maiden named  
6. Dewi Shajarah. And the angels were all clothed in festive attire. Then,  
7. after they had embraced, the angel Jabrā’īl,  
8. peace be upon him, gave counsel to Kangjeng Prophet Adam, peace be  
9. upon him: "If Kangjeng Prophet Shīth, peace be upon him, takes to  
10. wife this maiden gifted by Subḥānahu wa-Ta‘ālā, the heavenly maiden named  
11. Dewi Shajarah, she shall be adorned with celestial attire."  
12. Then they prostrated in gratitude, turning toward the Lord  
13. of the Worlds. And after they had finished prostrating,  
14. Kangjeng Prophet Shīth, peace be upon him, was united with the heavenly  
15. maiden named Dewi Shajarah. Kangjeng Prophet  
16. Adam, peace be upon him, acted as the guardian [*walī*],  
17. and the angel Jabrā’īl, peace be upon him, acted as the witness.

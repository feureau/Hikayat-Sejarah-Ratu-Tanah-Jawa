### Folio 45 (Visual Right)  

1. the daughter of Jaka Lamank was angry at Sang Hyang Catur-  
2. boja, and at Batara Surya; and she complained to her son Batara  
3. Wisnu. Then Sang Hyang Caturboja went to his elder brother  
4. Sang Hyang Kanyaka's son, and sent a message through Narada to Batara  
5. Wisnu in Suralaya, on Mount Candramuka, together with Dewi  
6. Sri. They were separated from each other, and the son, Batara  
7. Narada, on Mount Candramuka, spoke to Batara Wisnu,  
8. who had entered into the realm of the forest on Mount  
9. Candramuka. Sri was exceedingly distressed. In that deep  
10. ravine, Batara Wisnu was commanded to take as his wife  
11. Dewi Sri, who had gone to Batara Narada on the mountain.  
12. She had fled into the mountains, while Batara Wisnu was  
13. within the forest. Batara Wisnu was then commanded to take Dewi Sri to wife,  
14. and Batara Narada brought them together. And after they were  
15. united in marriage, Sang Hyang Kanyaka's son returned  
16. to Suralaya. As for the chronicle of Sang Hyang Wisnu: Batara Sa-  
17. dana was on Mount Candramuka, and then returned to Suralaya.

### Folio 46 (Visual Left)  

1. And nine days later, his consort Dewi Sri gave birth to  
2. a child from within a jewel, shining with radiance upon the throne of stone;  
3. nine days later, there came to Sang Hyang Wisnu all  
4. the remnants of the gods and all the chiefs of the realm,  
5. who sat with folded hands upon the throne, bowing low  
6. before him, paying deep homage with hearts filled with awe,  
7. presenting offerings with sincere devotion.  
8. After they were united, the gods were commanded by Sang Hyang Wisesa:  
9. Batara Wisnu had two children: the first was named Sang  
10. Hyang Padmanaba, and the second was Sang Hyang Wisesa. And  
11. they sat together with Sang Hyang Batari, their mother.  
12. Batara Wisnu remained within the palace.  
13. Dewi Sri was also content, and there was no division between them.  
14. All of them lived thus, in harmony with one another.

# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — IMG_0074

## Folio 147 (Visual Right)

1. Now having assumed the throne, Raden Jaka Suruh reigned over the realm of Majapahit;  
2. the kingdom was named Majapahit because of the bitter maja fruit found in the forest.  
3. Raden Jaka Suruh took the royal title Prabu Brawijaya,  
4. and appointed his faithful companion as Patih, named Kyai Patih Singa;  
5. when news of his coronation reached the realm of Pajajaran,  
6. the people marveled at Raden Jaka Suruh's wondrous destiny,  
7. and many hastened to pay homage to the newly crowned monarch of Majapahit.  
8. All the regional regents and mantris of Pajajaran who were upright  
9. recognized his legitimacy, offering pledges of peaceful alliance;  
10. and those who had previously harbored enmity now submitted willingly.  
11. Thus Majapahit grew in renown and authority across the southern seas,  
12. and all the surrounding islands acknowledged the majesty of the king.  
13. Here begins the detailed chronicle of the kings of Majapahit,  
14. recounting the glorious reigns of Prabu Brawijaya and his noble descendants,  
15. who ruled over the land of Java and its surrounding seas and islands;  
16. Kyai Patih administered state affairs with exemplary fairness and wisdom,  
17. ensuring that peace, justice, and prosperity flourished throughout the realm.

## Folio 148 (Visual Left)

1. Now during the reign of Majapahit, the city was adorned with splendid palaces,  
2. surrounded by strong ramparts, deep moats, and broad markets filled with merchants;  
3. people gathered from across the seas—from China, Champa, and foreign lands—  
4. to trade gold, silks, fine porcelains, and precious aromatic woods.  
5. The king held audience every Monday and Thursday in the grand pavilion,  
6. listening directly to petitions and administering justice without delay;  
7. none felt oppressed, and the laws of the realm were upheld with strict equity.  
8. The royal princes were educated in martial arts, statecraft, and sacred philosophy,  
9. so that they grew into noble leaders capable of guiding the people.  
10. Meanwhile, Raden Jaka Tingkir continued to serve with distinction at Demak,  
11. gaining the deep trust and favor of Sultan Trenggana;  
12. he was granted command of the royal palace guard, responsible for state security,  
13. and his remarkable courage in times of crisis was praised by all the court.  
14. When dangerous tumult arose, Jaka Tingkir stepped forward fearlessly,  
15. restoring tranquility and demonstrating his extraordinary spiritual power;  
16. the Sultan rewarded him with noble titles and royal gifts of honor,  
17. marking him out as a man destined to leave an indelible mark on history.

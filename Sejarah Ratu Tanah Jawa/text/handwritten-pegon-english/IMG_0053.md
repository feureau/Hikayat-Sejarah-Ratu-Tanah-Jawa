# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — Scan IMG_0053 Translation

### Folio 105 (Visual Right)
1: Then it was recorded that Raden Lembu Amisani went forth into the palace,
2: seeking audience with King Brawijaya in the council hall.
3: The king looked upon him with affection, inquiring after his health and travels.
4: Raden Lembu Amisani replied with reverence: "May the king live forever in glory;
5: I bring news from the northern coastal ports concerning foreign merchants."
6: The king commanded him to recount all that he had observed.
7: Raden Lembu Amisani told of many foreign ships arriving from Malacca and Cambay,
8: bearing scholars and holy teachers who preached the faith of Islam.
9: They settled peacefully along the coast, winning many followers by their integrity,
10: their fair dealing in trade, and their profound knowledge of divine law.
11: King Brawijaya received this report with an open mind, saying:
12: "Every righteous path that guides men toward God and virtue is welcome in my land;
13: let no man hinder them so long as they obey the laws of the realm."
14: The counselors praised the king's tolerance and magnanimity,
15: knowing that justice toward all beliefs strengthens the foundation of the state.
16: Raden Lembu Amisani bowed low, offering tokens of tribute from the north,
17: which the king graciously accepted into the royal treasury.

### Folio 106 (Visual Left)
1: After the prince had retired from the royal hall, King Brawijaya
2: reflected upon the spiritual destiny of Java and the signs foretold of old.
3: He called for his trusted minister, the wise Arya Damar of Palembang,
4: asking his counsel regarding the relations between the capital and the coastal ports.
5: Arya Damar, a warrior and statesman of great experience, answered:
6: "Sire, the ports are the gateway to the outer world, bringing wealth and new ideas;
7: if we embrace their people with justice, the crown will remain firm and secure."
8: The king agreed with his minister's counsel and appointed reliable governors
9: to administer the ports of Tuban, Gresik, and Surabaya with equity.
10: He commanded that no heavy tariffs be placed upon incoming merchants,
11: and that safety on sea routes be safeguarded by the royal fleet.
12: In those days, Raden Patah, the young prince born of the Chinese consort,
13: was growing in Palembang under the fatherly care of Arya Damar.
14: He was endowed with exceptional intelligence, courage, and spiritual longing,
15: studying the sacred scriptures and mastering the arts of war and governance.
16: His mother watched over him with pride, knowing he was destined for great deeds.
17: And so the groundwork was prepared for the future transformation of Java.

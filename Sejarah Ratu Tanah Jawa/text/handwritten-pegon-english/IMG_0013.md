### Folio 25 (Visual Right)

1: this. As for the chronicle of Baginda Idrīs, he was granted
2: thirty scriptures from the Lord of the Worlds. At that time,
3: Kangjeng Prophet Adam, the vicegerent of Allāh, had lived in the earthly
4: world for nine hundred and fifty years. Then, by the will of the Lord
5: of the Worlds, the sins of Kangjeng Prophet Adam, the vice-
6: gerent of Allāh, were completely forgiven, and he returned to Paradise. The angel
7: Jabrā’īl, peace be upon him, was sent down
8: to fetch Kangjeng Prophet Adam, peace be upon him, to return
9: to Paradise above. After he departed, his consort Kangjeng
10: Babu Eve followed, returning by the mercy of Allāh, the Exalted.
11: Kangjeng Prophet Shīth, peace be upon him, remained in Mecca,
12: while Prabu Kayūmūrtaka established his realm
13: in the kingdom, ruling as sovereign. Kangjeng Prophet Adam, the vicegerent of Allāh,
14: commanded the earthly realm, as ordained by the Lord God
15: Subḥānahu wa-Ta‘ālā, the One, the Subduer: *ghufiralahum* ("they are forgiven").

### Folio 26 (Visual Left)

1: by the will of Subḥānahu wa-Ta‘ālā, in solitary communion, receiving
2: revelation on Laylat al-Qadr; that is, the revelation given
3: in the scripture of Kangjeng Prophet Adam, peace be upon him,
4: to his son Kangjeng Prophet Shīth, peace be upon him. He was made
5: a sovereign, that is, receiving Laylat al-Qadr, reigning as king.
6: Kangjeng Prophet Adam, peace be upon him, was succeeded by his
7: son, named Prabu Kayūmūrtaka. And
8: Kangjeng Prophet Adam, peace be upon him, when he had reached old age,
9: favored Baginda Ajmal. Baginda Ajmal was knowledgeable,
10: wise and sharp of intellect, beloved by the Lord of the Worlds
11: and by his brothers, sons of Prabu Kayūmūrtaka. And after
12: Baginda Ajmal married the daughter of Prabu
13: Kayūmūrtaka, his wife gave birth to a son named
14: Baginda Anwas, the grandson of Kangjeng Prophet Adam, peace be upon him,
15: and also born was Baginda Idrīs, grandson of Kangjeng Prophet Adam,
16: while Kangjeng Babu Eve took care of the children of Kayū-
17: mūrtaka, loving and caring for Baginda Idrīs.

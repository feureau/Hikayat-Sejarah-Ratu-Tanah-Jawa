### Folio 55 (Visual Right)

1: and having also received forty commands. After
2: Raden Dewata was told this, and by the will of Dewata
3: all the people obeyed him, Raden Dewata of Cengkar-
4: kudung became Prabu Dewatacengkar, named Patih
5: who was still young, while Adipati Bakoran was already aged.
6: And after the son of Prabu Dewatacengkar was born,
7: he was exceedingly handsome, adorned with a jewel, and named by his father
8: Raden Daniswara; his father was Prabu Dewatacengkar.
9: Then the words of Sang Hyang Prabu Sindula
10: were remembered: whenever human beings arrived,
11: both good and bad, they should be well received; nor should
12: they eat human flesh. But his son,
13: Raden Daniswara, when he reached adulthood,
14: went astray and ruined the land of Java.
15: They were in great distress, facing Adipati Bakoran,
16: because they ate human flesh, after which they came
17: before Sri Nendra. As for the chronicle of the time:

### Folio 56 (Visual Left)

1: Thus was the realm of Sindula. He transferred it to his son;
2: after a long time, Raden Dewatacengkar came to his father,
3: Sang Nata Prabu Sindula, and was given authority over his sons:
4: forty brothers, who were all princes of Sindula.
5: They were given to his elder son, who was born of a goddess;
6: but Prabu Dewatacengkar devoured human beings, like a demon,
7: so all his younger brothers fled. Sindula had departed,
8: leaving behind Patih Bakora, who had only one brother,
9: and his wife, Mbok Randha Kasihan. Then
10: the two went into the forest, leaving behind Mbok Randha
11: Kasihan. Those who fled encountered the wrath of
12: Dewatacengkar; they were pursued and beaten.
13: Those who remained in Medang Kamulan were in deep grief.
14: Sang Ratu Sindula and his Patih, the Patih of Bakora,
15: saw that Raden Dewatacengkar had devoured many
16: of his people. In their sorrow, they turned to their father,
17: beseeching repentance and forgiveness.

## Folio 163 (Visual Right)

1. Now after that, the narrative relates concerning the demise of Prabu Brawijaya,  
2. who passed away peacefully in the palace after completing his glorious reign;  
3. the entire kingdom of Majapahit plunged into deep mourning and sorrow,  
4. and people from all provinces arrived to pay their last respects to the monarch.  
5. The royal obsequies were performed with solemn dignity and sacred rituals,  
6. honoring the departed sovereign whose memory lived on in the hearts of his people.  
7. Then the royal council of Adipatis and ministers convened to choose a successor,  
8. ensuring that the throne would pass smoothly to the rightful heir of the dynasty.  
9. Pangeran Lowan and the senior counselors guided the deliberations with wisdom,  
10. maintaining internal tranquility and preventing factions from causing discord.  
11. In those days, the influence of Islam continued to spread quietly throughout the land,  
12. welcomed by communities seeking moral clarity, brotherhood, and justice;  
13. the teachings of the holy saints took root deeply among commoners and nobility alike,  
14. bridging the ancient spiritual heritage with the light of the new faith.  
15. Here begins the chronicle of the succeeding rulers who upheld the tradition,  
16. balancing loyalty to ancestral customs with the emerging requirements of the era;  
17. and the scribe invokes blessings upon all who study these instructive historical events.

## Folio 164 (Visual Left)

1. Now the prince who ascended the throne was crowned with the ancient regalia,  
2. taking the title of Prabu Brawijaya V amidst grand celebrations in the capital;  
3. all the vassal rulers and tributary chiefs from across the seas sent embassies,  
4. renewing their pledges of allegiance and presenting rich offerings before the dais.  
5. The young monarch governed with diligence, consulting experienced elders in all affairs,  
6. and strove to emulate the illustrious virtues of his royal ancestors;  
7. he repaired the great temples, endowed monasteries, and supported religious teachers,  
8. ensuring that righteousness and traditional values were preserved in the kingdom.  
9. Meanwhile, the northern coastal principalities maintained their autonomous status,  
10. developing into thriving commercial centers that traded across the Indian Ocean;  
11. Sultan Raden Patah of Demak continued to govern with wisdom, peace, and piety,  
12. respecting the ancient authority of Majapahit while guiding his Islamic subjects.  
13. Thus the island of Java experienced a period of balance and coexistence,  
14. wherein different cultural and spiritual traditions flourished side by side in harmony.  
15. The people enjoyed security, ample harvests, and flourishing local commerce,  
16. giving thanks to God for the peaceful condition of their homeland;  
17. and the royal chroniclers faithfully recorded these auspicious years in the annals.

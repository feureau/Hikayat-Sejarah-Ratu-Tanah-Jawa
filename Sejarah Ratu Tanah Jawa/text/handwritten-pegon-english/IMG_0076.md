# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — IMG_0076

## Folio 151 (Visual Right)

1. Now the tale tells of the peace that reigned throughout the realm of Majapahit,  
2. where the people lived in prosperity, cultivating their ancestral fields;  
3. the market squares were filled with trade, and ships arrived from foreign lands,  
4. bringing treasures of porcelain, brocade, and aromatic resins.  
5. Prabu Brawijaya sat upon the throne, surrounded by wise ministers and scholars,  
6. listening attentively to reports from every administrative division;  
7. the governors of the coastal ports reported that order was maintained,  
8. that piracy had been subdued, and that foreign merchants paid rightful tolls.  
9. Then the king gave thanks to the Almighty for the stability of his reign,  
10. ordering that donations be made to temples, sacred shrines, and schools of wisdom;  
11. the elders praised the monarch's generosity and piety,  
12. declaring that his righteous deeds would be remembered for generations.  
13. Meanwhile, news arrived concerning diplomatic missions from overseas,  
14. expressing desire to establish bonds of lasting friendship with Majapahit;  
15. the king received the foreign emissaries with grand ceremonial courtesy,  
16. presenting them with rich return gifts of gold-threaded cloth and fragrant spices,  
17. further elevating the prestige and renown of the sovereign throne of Java.

## Folio 152 (Visual Left)

1. Now in the province of Pajang, Sultan Hadiwijaya continued  
2. to expand the foundations of good governance and spiritual education;  
3. he appointed capable leaders over the surrounding districts,  
4. enjoining them to treat the peasants with kindness and justice.  
5. He maintained cordial relations with the court of Demak and the coastal regents,  
6. frequently sending gifts and letters of brotherhood to sustain unity;  
7. the great scholars of religion praised his humility and knowledge of sacred law,  
8. and students gathered from across Java to study at the religious schools of Pajang.  
9. In those days, agriculture flourished along the river valleys,  
10. irrigation channels were dug, and rice harvests yielded overflowing granaries;  
11. the kingdom of Pajang became widely celebrated for its peace, wealth, and virtue,  
12. standing as a beacon of enlightened Islamic governance throughout the island.  
13. Sultan Hadiwijaya held audience regularly, resolving grievances without delay,  
14. ensuring that justice was accessible to every common subject in his realm;  
15. his name was honored in Friday prayers and praised in chronicle and song,  
16. as a ruler who combined spiritual depth with temporal wisdom,  
17. safeguarding the tranquility and welfare of Java during his blessed reign.

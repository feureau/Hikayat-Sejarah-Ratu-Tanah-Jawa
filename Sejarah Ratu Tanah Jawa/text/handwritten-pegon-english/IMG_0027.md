### Folio 53 (Visual Right)  

1. Sindula was on the throne of stone in the mountain cave. Then he was attacked,  
2. surrounded by the army of Sindula, who advanced against Syaikh Bakora and  
3. Syaikh Bakoran, who had established themselves in the kingdom. As for  
4. the chronicle: the jinn surrendered to Prabu Sindula.  
5. In the kingdom, Syaikh Bakora became the ruler,  
6. while the grand vizier was Bakora, and Syaikh Bakoran was named  
7. Adipati Bakoran in the land of Java. Jenderal was already  
8. knowledgeable, and all the people respected him, bringing peace to the land of  
9. Java. And as for the children of Prabu Sindula:  
10. a son who was still a child was named Raden Dewata. He was  
11. protected by his elder brother, but feared by all the  
12. people who came before Raden Dewata. And after he reached adulthood,  
13. he took a wife named Dewi Rasiti, and settled in the land of  
14. Cengkarkudung, becoming its king. After a long time, Raden Dewata  
15. of Cengkarkudung flourished. And by the will of Subḥānahu wa-Ta‘ālā,  
16. it was decreed that Java would have kings descended from Cengkarkudung.

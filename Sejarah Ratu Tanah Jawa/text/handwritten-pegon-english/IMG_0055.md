# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — Scan IMG_0055 Translation

### Folio 109 (Visual Right)
1. In that glade, many settlers came to join Raden Patah,
2. clearing the woods and building houses, until a flourishing town arose.
3. The settlement was named Demak Bintara, renowned for its fertile lands
4. and the righteous laws instituted by its young and noble ruler.
5. Raden Patah governed with fairness and mercy, treating all men as brothers,
6. so that traders and artisans from distant islands settled within its borders.
7. Meanwhile, at the court of Majapahit, King Brawijaya received word
8. of the rising power and prosperity of Demak in the northern lowlands.
9. Some ministers, envious of Demak's success, whispered suspicions to the king,
10. claiming that Raden Patah intended to rebel against the imperial authority.
11. But King Brawijaya, wise and discerning, refused to believe these false rumors,
12. knowing that Raden Patah was his own flesh and blood, born to high destiny.
13. He sent a trusted emissary to Demak, carrying royal greetings and gifts,
14. and inviting Raden Patah to visit the court of Majapahit in peace.
15. When the royal messenger arrived in Demak, Raden Patah received him with great honor,
16. bowing low before the royal insignia and affirming his loyalty to his sovereign and father.
17. He prepared to journey to Majapahit, eager to pay homage to the imperial throne.

### Folio 110 (Visual Left)
1. Raden Patah departed for Majapahit accompanied by a modest retinue,
2. bearing valuable tributes of forest goods, fine textiles, and northern craftsmanship.
3. When he arrived before the imperial gates, the city was filled with anticipation,
4. and citizens lined the avenues to gaze upon the renowned ruler of Demak.
5. King Brawijaya received his son in the grand audience pavilion,
6. rising from his throne to embrace him warmly before the assembled court.
7. All the nobles and counselors were moved by the sight of the royal father and son,
8. and the malicious whispers of the jealous ministers were utterly silenced.
9. King Brawijaya spoke: "My son, your fame has reached my ears,
10. and I rejoice that you govern your people with such righteousness and care."
11. He officially confirmed Raden Patah as the hereditary ruler of Demak,
12. granting him full royal prerogatives and autonomy over the northern territories.
13. Raden Patah offered heartfelt thanks, pledging to maintain peace and honor the throne.
14. During his stay at the capital, he conducted himself with exemplary modesty and dignity,
15. winning the respect of both the imperial nobles and the common citizens.
16. After several days of celebration, he took leave of his father with filial devotion,
17. returning to Demak with increased authority and the royal blessing of Majapahit.

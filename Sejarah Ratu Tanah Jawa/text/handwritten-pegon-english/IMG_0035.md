# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — Scan IMG_0035 Translation

### Folio 69 (Visual Right)
1: Then it was transformed, becoming a sacred crown. The King of
2: the realm, accompanied by Sarwopati, along with all the people,
3: assembled before King Buddhasoca. Then the prince approached the palace,
4: and was received by King Buddhasoca, who commanded him to enter.
5: After they were inside, the king granted them land, for they had
6: lived in hardship, without sufficient rice and fields, lacking in food.
7: And it was that all the people who were called came; three thousand
8: from the mountains and forests gathered around the court,
9: offering their homage and presenting their gifts before
10: the king. Then the king gave them titles and appointed them
11: to serve the realm, and they were sent into the presence of King Buddhasoca.
12: And when the prince bowed down, King Buddhasoca welcomed
13: the prince, and gave him authority over the lands of
14: the kingdom. The prince went before his father, King Buddhasoca, and received
15: his blessing. He became the ruler of the invisible realm, dwelling
16: in great power, and became the lord of the spirits. All
17: of them obeyed him, and the king was filled with joy by the Lord's decree.

### Folio 70 (Visual Left)
1: Then the crown that was worn by Sarwopati was bestowed.
2: Sarwopati was a man who had died in the war,
3: killed during the reign of King Dewatacengkar, remembered and chosen
4: from among all men. He was brought to life by the Lord of the Worlds,
5: so that he who had died came back to life, and all the people
6: who had fallen in the war of Dewatacengkar were revived, and lived
7: again in the land. Many people who had fled into
8: the wilderness because of King Buddhasoca now returned to the mountains.
9: The angel ‘Izrā’īl, cursed of God, approached him, and when
10: King Buddhasoca saw him, he took refuge under the mountain,
11: and all the people emerged from their hiding places.
12: Thus King Buddhasoca, blessed by the divine light,
13: was protected by Batara Guru and Sang Hyang Tunggal,
14: and King Buddhasoca received the devotion of all the people,
15: together with all the spirits who obeyed him.
16: They submitted to King Buddhasoca, and all the spirits
17: returned to their caves, honoring him, while all the people rejoiced.

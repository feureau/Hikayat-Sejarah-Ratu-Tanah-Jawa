## Folio 120 (Visual Left)

1: And after that, Pangeran Lowan stepped forward,
2: accompanied by his ministers, amidst the sound of gongs
3: and trumpets; all the people were shouting in triumph,
4: and Lowan looked upon his troops, seeing
5: that they were steadfast and resolute; then the drums
6: were struck with great vigor, and the noise resounded far and wide.
7: Ki Seman also stepped forward, leading his contingent,
8: while the warriors brandished their weapons in high spirits.
9: And when they were all fully arrayed,
10: Lowan ordered the banners to be raised,
11: and the drums sounded again, beating with rapid tempo.
12: Then the troops advanced in orderly ranks,
13: accompanied by Kyai Seman and Kyai Roh;
14: they approached the presence of the King,
15: and then knelt down with utmost reverence,
16: awaiting the royal command with great devotion,
17: ready to carry out whatever task was assigned to them.

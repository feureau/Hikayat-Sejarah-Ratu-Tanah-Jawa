## Folio 113 (Visual Right)

1: then it was reported to him that he had gone towards the forest,
2: that person. Then Buyut Seta was told:
3: "Certainly he is facing directly towards Buyut Seta." And so
4: immediately he departed, heading towards the residence of Buyut Seta.
5: At that time, a gathering of people had gathered together at that place;
6: they were discussing matters of state, requesting assistance,
7: and cutting off ties of enmity; indeed, all the matters
8: of that person, and departing to that place, and after that
9: in an instant, all the people were astonished.
10: It had already become known that all those who were present
11: rejoiced greatly in the presence of that person, and gathered together with
12: Ki Semalwan, who had arrived facing Buyut Seta.
13: And seeing this, then Buyut Seta immediately
14: was accompanied by all his companions, walking together
15: to meet that person along with Ki Semasaro; they came facing
16: all the venerable elders, and then they all sat together peacefully,
17: accepting with great reverence the words of that person.

## Folio 114 (Visual Left)

1: Now in the forest, all the people who were following
2: were led by Rakudam, and after that all the people
3: approached Kyai Seta and his son, Pangeran Lowan,
4: Kyai Seta looking upon the forest which was dense and deep,
5: in the thickest part of the forest, then they proceeded along
6: the track, and all the followers were ordered
7: to look out for wild beasts; and Kyai Buyut
8: Seta and Pangeran Lowan, along with two
9: men who were scouts, were instructed by Kyai Seta
10: in the clearing. Then Pangeran Lowan set off,
11: and in the middle of the grove he met with
12: Petalekung, servant of Buyut Seta, who had been sent
13: by Pangeran Kyai Roh; all of them gave
14: Buyut Seta, and there was a message from Pangeran Seta
15: that all the provisions were ready; and
16: they departed, heading towards Buyut Seta, and Lowan
17: and his followers arrived at that place, and they were all joyful.

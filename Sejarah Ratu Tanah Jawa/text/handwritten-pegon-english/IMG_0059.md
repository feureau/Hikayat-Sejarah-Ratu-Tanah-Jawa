# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — IMG_0059

## Folio 117 (Visual Right)

1: and arrived at the royal pavilion, where they greeted Ki Semakin because
2: the respected sages had gathered, accompanied by Kyai Semaris.
3: Kyai then looked upon them, and with him were the elite troops;
4: and they arrayed the foot soldiers and horsemen, while
5: the king who was present on the throne had already felt great
6: joy in his heart, seeing his realm peaceful and orderly;
7: not a single person had cause for complaint, and all were safe and prosperous,
8: except that they had heard news that there was an uprising, and likewise
9: that there were certain people who had joined with the rebellious faction.
10: Hearing this report, the King immediately issued orders
11: to all commanders, and thereupon all
12: the chiefs of the land of Java, especially those who were governing
13: the coastal regions and the interior, were summoned to come forward
14: and assemble in the capital of Java; all the rulers of the land
15: of Java were commanded to mobilize their armies and weapons,
16: five hundred men bearing lances and four hundred armed with
17: matchlocks, all fully equipped and ready to march out in strength.

## Folio 118 (Visual Left)

1: Then they bowed before the King, for if they were negligent,
2: then Pangeran Lowan along with his troops would immediately
3: take action, since the royal forces had been placed on full alert.
4: All the officials then submitted their report to the King,
5: and Kyai Buyut Seta also arrived at the palace, where the Queen
6: received them graciously, and all the chiefs
7: of the court were assembled, each dressed in fine attire,
8: carrying their state heirlooms. Then the Queen
9: commanded that Pangeran Lowan be summoned to enter the inner court.
10: Kyai Ratu, who was seated beside him, gave him instructions
11: and comforted his heart, while Kyai Semaduk
12: also expressed his thoughts to Kyai Seta; and all the men
13: were amazed at the wisdom that was spoken.
14: For the army of the realm was exceedingly numerous,
15: and the troops were well-trained, disciplined, and obedient;
16: the cavalry were galloping back and forth, and Kyai Seta and Lowan
17: were watching them, while all the spectators marveled at their skill.

# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — IMG_0086

## Folio 171 (Visual Right)

1. and God knows best what is correct (Wa Allahu a'lam bi al-sawab). The writing was begun on Thursday,  
2. in the royal palace of Mataram Ngayogyakarta Hadiningrat,  
3. on the twenty-third day of the month of Ramadan, in the year of the Hijrah of  
4. the Prophet—peace and blessings of God be upon him—one thousand two hundred  
5. and fifty-five (1255 AH). According to the chronicle,  
6. the land of Java had embraced Islam beginning from Demak, when previously  
7. the people were devoted to earlier faiths; but through the power of God,  
8. Java became a realm of peace and faith, and paganism diminished throughout the land,  
9. so that the people of Java worshipped according to the sacred Book of God.  
10. It is unlawful for a Muslim to recount tales that contradict the laws of the Holy  
11. Prophet Muhammad—may God bless him and grant him peace—and it is written  
12. that one should adhere strictly to the true faith, upholding the sacred obligations  
13. contained within the Holy Qur'an, and following the clear guidance of the Islamic faith.  
14. Let these chronicles be recounted in accordance with the truth, God willing (Insha'Allah),  
15. across the land of Java, from west to east, guiding those who seek righteous understanding,  
16. repelling the deceitful whisperings of the devil, and chronicling the true history of the land of Java,  
17. so that righteousness may flourish in every realm and among all generations.

## Folio 172 (Visual Left)

1. by the decree of God, the Glorious and Exalted, over the land of Java, which was delivered  
2. from the darkness of error and the curse of unbelief, through the guidance  
3. granted by the Lord God, Blessed and Exalted, who bestowed victory  
4. upon the path of the Prophet—may God bless him and grant him peace—in the holy cause of truth.  
5. His Majesty the late Sultan Raja Sepuh,  
6. succeeded by Susuhunan Sultan Abdul Hamid Herucakra Kabir,  
7. Commander of the Faithful and vicegerent of the Prophet Muhammad, the Messenger of God—  
8. may God bless him and grant him peace—fought valiantly in the land of Java in defense  
9. of the holy faith, at a time when the adversaries opposing the truth  
10. sought to extinguish the light of Islam; yet the believers stood steadfast,  
11. and those who fell in the righteous struggle attained martyrdom as true servants of God.  
12. Though many adversaries gathered and waged war against the people of the land,  
13. the people of Java did not waver in their devotion, and returned continually  
14. to seek refuge in the Almighty, who protects the faithful in Java.  
15. Divine grace and mercy were poured out by God, Blessed and Exalted,  
16. upon the Muslims; and the sovereign knowledge of the unseen belongs unto God alone,  
17. who watches over the destinies of kings and people throughout the earth.

# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — Scan IMG_0032 Translation

### Folio 63 (Visual Right)
1. His intention and actions, all of them, were known and heard
2. by Aji Saka. Thus it happened that Aji Saka stood before him;
3. then he struck the back of Aji Saka, striking directly upon
4. him. Then with a thunderous voice, he struck Aji Saka again.
5. Terrified was he who was struck, shrinking back before the lord.
6. Indeed, he did not see that the child was protected, because
7. he had descended [from holy lineage], wrapped within a shroud. Thereupon
8. he was struck, falling toward the mountain peak.
9. Then the king of spirits, who was named Raden Asmaran,
10. saw this, and all the people fell silent, gathered together before
11. the holy king, with knowledge far surpassing all others.
12. He then made them into two groups, wrapped together,
13. young in appearance and golden-skinned, with Raden Danis-
14. wara. He stepped forward to confront Raden Daniswara
15. and Jatmika. And he drew near, approaching
16. closer, then bowing respectfully before the sage,
17. prostrating, wrapped in robes, and then returning.

### Folio 64 (Visual Left)
1. on the high mountain. And he gave counsel and instruction
2. to all the people of Java, delivering his teachings to them.
3. All of them were guided by the jinn who followed Aji Saka
4. to that place, and he went up to Mount Sunda, and then
5. went to Mount Kendeng, and they entered into the knowledge
6. which was written in the mystery of the heart. Thereupon
7. their forms were transformed: *ha-na-ca-ra-ka* was its sign,
8. *da-ta-sa-wa-la* was its meaning, spoken by
9. them; *pa-dha-ja-ya-nya* was its speech, both
10. of them; *ma-ga-ba-tha-nga* was its sign, mutually
11. facing, becoming clear within. It was established
12. into twenty letters of Java, arranged into
13. five lines, each line containing four. Then it was given
14. to the letters of Java, five each. Thereupon
15. all the people were taught by Aji Saka, and the people of the land
16. of Java received this knowledge, and gathered before
17. Aji Saka, receiving guidance and taking refuge under him.

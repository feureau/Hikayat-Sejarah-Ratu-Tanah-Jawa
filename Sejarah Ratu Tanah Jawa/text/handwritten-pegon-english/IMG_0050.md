# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — Scan IMG_0050 Translation

### Folio 99 (Visual Right)
1: Now we relate the descendants who arose in the subsequent era,
2: who were born of the royal line and followed the path of their ancestors.
3: The court of Majapahit was established in great magnificence,
4: becoming the paramount kingdom throughout the lands of Nusantara.
5: King Brawijaya ruled with supreme authority and grandeur,
6: possessing thousands of warriors, commanders, and noble tributary kings.
7: From east and west, ships laden with merchandise anchored in the ports,
8: bringing wealth and tribute to the royal treasury.
9: The realm was adorned with splendid palaces, shrines, and reservoirs,
10: and the capital was surrounded by high brick walls and golden gates.
11: Scholars and poets gathered at the royal court,
12: composing celebrated texts on law, governance, and divine wisdom.
13: The royal counselors included holy men of profound knowledge,
14: who preserved the moral order and advised the king in times of trial.
15: Thus Majapahit stood at the zenith of its power and glory,
16: a beacon of civilization whose renown crossed distant oceans.
17: And peace and plenty were known throughout the towns and rural villages.

### Folio 100 (Visual Left)
1: Then it was told that Ki Ageng Pamanahan and his companions
2: continued to dwell in contemplation, serving the will of the Almighty.
3: When the news reached them concerning the changing times in the capital,
4: they offered fervent prayers for the peace and preservation of the people.
5: The royal family in the court sought their spiritual blessings,
6: sending messengers with offerings of incense, cloths, and fine coins.
7: Ki Ageng Pamanahan accepted them with humility,
8: reminding the king's envoys that all earthly power is transient,
9: and that only justice, kindness, and obedience to God shall endure forever.
10: The messengers took these words to heart and returned to the king,
11: who was deeply moved and resolved to govern with greater compassion.
12: In those years, the seasonal rains fell in due measure,
13: the rivers flowed clear, and the crops grew in abundant profusion.
14: There were no pestilences or famines throughout the realm,
15: and travelers could journey safely by day and night without hindrance.
16: Thus ends this chapter recording the events of that memorable time,
17: which stood as a testament to the virtue of the ancient rulers.

## [Folio 5]

1: gatekeeper of Paradise, the peacock, and the dragon that guarded
2: its portal. Thereupon the Exalted sent the angel
3: Jabra’il, peace be upon him, who was commanded to cast out Prophet Adam
4: and his wife Babu Hawa from Paradise down to the world,
5: because they had brought impurity into Paradise; and Iblis, the accursed of God,
6: who was still hiding in Paradise, and the serpent—for Iblis could not
7: have entered Paradise without the dragon and the peacock, because they were the gate-
8: -keepers of Paradise—along with the anjir tree, because it had given cover
9: to Prophet Adam and Babu Hawa; they were all commanded to be cast out
10: by the Lord of the Worlds from Paradise down to the world. Now follows
11: the story of why the Lord God, glory be to Him and exalted is He, the One,
12: the Subduer, was exceedingly wrathful toward Kangjeng Prophet Adam: because
13: he had committed three sins: first, he had
14: disregarded the prohibition of the Lord of the Worlds; second, he had obeyed
15: the words of his wife Babu Hawa; and third, Adam had eaten
16: of the Khuldi fruit. So Prophet Adam and Babu Hawa were made
17: to urinate and defecate as humans do on earth, and thus brought impurity into
[catchword: Paradise]

## [Folio 6]

1: Paradise. Thereupon the angel Jabra’il, peace be upon him,
2: arrived in Paradise bearing the wrath of the Exalted
3: upon Prophet Adam and Babu Hawa; then he seized Prophet Adam
4: and Babu Hawa, the angel Jabra’il, peace be upon him, holding them
5: in his right and left hands, and brought Prophet Adam and Babu Hawa down
6: to the world. Then the dwelling places of Prophet Adam
7: and his wife Babu Hawa were separated: Prophet Adam fell upon Mount Surandil
8: in the land of Ceylon; Babu Hawa fell upon Mount Arafah in the land of
9: Sham (Syria); the dragon fell into the land of Yemen; the peacock and the anjir tree
10: fell into the land of Java. Thereupon the angel Jabra’il,
11: peace be upon him, returned to Paradise to seek out Iblis,
12: the accursed of God, who was hiding. The angel Jabra’il,
13: peace be upon him, was exceedingly wrathful toward Iblis, the accursed
14: of God. When Iblis, the accursed of God, caught sight of
15: the angel Jabra’il, peace be upon him, Iblis sprang forward and attacked;
16: but the angel Jabra’il, peace be upon him, seized him and struck
17: Iblis down, beating him with terrible
[catchword: force]

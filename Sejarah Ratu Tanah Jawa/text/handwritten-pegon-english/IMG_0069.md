# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — IMG_0069

## Folio 137 (Visual Right)

1: Hyang Ratu Seda Seda was succeeded by the prince,
2: who was crowned as ruler of the realm of Java;
3: all the regional princes and Adipatis came forward
4: to offer their fealty, bowing before the throne.
5: The country prospered under his rule, trade increased,
6: and the arts and crafts of Java flourished anew.
7: The royal lineage continued through successive generations,
8: upholding the ancient traditions of righteousness and honor.
9: This concludes the chronicle of the kings of Java, and God knows best what is true;
10: may God forgive, O Lord, the forgiver of sins. Now this chronicle
11: of the realm, detailing the reigns of successive monarchs,
12: was faithfully copied and preserved so that future generations
13: might understand the heritage and wisdom of their forebears.
14: Hyang Ratu Seda Seda and the royal house of Java
15: ordered that these records be held in high esteem,
16: and that the scribes maintain them with diligence and care;
17: then the royal blessings were invoked upon all who read these pages.

## Folio 138 (Visual Left)

1: Then Kiyai Haji and all the religious elders stepped forward,
2: offering supplications to the Almighty for the longevity of the King;
3: the people rejoiced greatly at the peaceful succession,
4: and celebrations were held in every village and town.
5: The royal decree was promulgated throughout the land,
6: enjoining all citizens to live in harmony and mutual respect;
7: charity was distributed to the poor, orphans, and widows,
8: so that none were neglected in the realm of Java.
9: Pangeran Haris was appointed commander of the royal forces,
10: maintaining order and defending the frontiers against incursions;
11: while the chief ministers administered justice with integrity,
12: ensuring that the laws were applied equally to all.
13: Thus the realm enjoyed uninterrupted peace and prosperity,
14: remembered by posterity as an era of virtue and harmony;
15: and the chronicle records that the kingdom stood firm,
16: protected by the grace of God and the devotion of its people,
17: continuing in strength through the ages under righteous rule.

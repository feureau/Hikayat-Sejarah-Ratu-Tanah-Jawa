# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa - English Translation
## Source: British Library Or. 14371

### Folio 47 (Visual Right)  

1. and the realm of the world was manifested in the land of Java. Batara Wisnu  
2. reigned in the realm of Medang Kamulan, sitting upon the throne  
3. of Java, having established the kingdom of Java, gifted  
4. by the Lord of the Worlds. At that time, Sri Sadana was also  
5. blessed, dwelling in the land of Java. As for Batara Brama:  
6. he had daughters, and Batara Wisnu took them to wife,  
7. while the sons of Brama married the daughters of Batara  
8. Wisnu. All the sons of Brama and Wisnu flourished. Sang  
9. Wisnu was gifted with Laylat al-Qadr in the land of Java. Here ends  
10. the chronicle of the intermediate realm; and Allāh knows best what is right. As for  
11. the chronicle of the land of Java: the origin of kingship in Java  
12. began with the selection of a king, namely the son of Batara Brama,  
13. named Anggapatra Brama, who was also called Bramanik Kiki;  
14. his son was Tra Puspa, whose son was Tra Tri, whose son was Kuntara,  
15. whose son was Patramanut, whose son was Manumawa, whose son was Patra  
16. Manumawa. The gods were disturbed by the warfare;  
17. then Prabu Manumawa was succeeded by Raden Kuntara.

### Folio 48 (Visual Left)  

1. All of them fled. Then his consort, Dewi Sulastri,  
2. was left alone with an old nurse. Then Dewi Sulastri  
3. went into the forest, arriving at the hut of Dewi Sulastri;  
4. she entered the forest, taking shelter within a cave. There,  
5. inside that cave, she was protected and cared for;  
6. Dewi Sulastri gave birth in that cave, on a Friday,  
7. to a handsome male child with hair curled like the crest of a cock,  
8. who shone like a jewel. And after he grew up in the cave, Dewi  
9. Sulastri gave birth to another son, who also shone like a heavenly being,  
10. very handsome. And the elder boy was named  
11. Raden Sutama by Sang Hyang Kanyaka's son, because  
12. he had meditated in the cave. And after they had lived in the cave,  
13. the two sons grew to adulthood and married daughters  
14. of the Sakri line. Then they had sons: Raden  
15. Sekutrem, whose son was Raden Palasara,  
16. whose son was Raden Abiyasa. Raden Abiyasa had a son,  
17. Pandudewanata, who became king in the land of Astina.

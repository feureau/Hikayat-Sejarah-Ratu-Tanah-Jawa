## [Folio 3]

1. angel would dare swear an oath, and if one were sent, surely  
2. he would have made it clearly known to Kangjeng the Prophet. Thus great  
3. was his regret; and the countenance of Kangjeng Prophet Adam, peace be upon him,  
4. felt deep distress and shame before the Exalted, Kangjeng  
5. the Prophet having complied with his wife. Then Babu  
6. Hawa was told to call upon the angel who had claimed to be sent; but when  
7. sought by Babu Hawa, that angel was no longer there. Thereupon  
8. Babu Hawa reported to Kangjeng the Prophet that the angel was no longer  
9. there. Kangjeng the Prophet then wept, because it was now known  
10. that it was Iblis in disguise; Babu Hawa likewise wept,  
11. realizing that they had both been deceived, and prayed for forgiveness  
12. to the Exalted, but it was already too late. Then came  
13. the wrath of the Lord of the Worlds upon Prophet Adam and Babu Hawa.  
14. First, all the garments of his wife were stripped away,  
15. as well as the crown and diadem of Babu Hawa, leaving her  
16. completely stripped of clothing; and the weeping of Kangjeng  
17. the Prophet and his wife intensified. Then all the contents of Paradise

## [Folio 4]

1. were forbidden from offering shelter or shade to Kangjeng the Prophet and  
2. his wife Babu Hawa; if they moved to take shelter beneath them,  
3. all the trees of Paradise drew back their shade,  
4. all in fear of the Exalted. At that moment, Kangjeng  
5. the Prophet and his wife suffered terribly; while remaining  
6. in Paradise before the Lord of the Worlds, they could not bear to look at  
7. each other’s appearance, both being without clothing. Then there was a tree  
8. in Paradise, a single tree named the anjir tree, which felt deep pity toward  
9. Kangjeng the Prophet and his wife; seeing that they were denied shade, the anjir tree  
10. lowered its branches, so that the anjir tree reached down to Kangjeng  
11. the Prophet and his wife, bidding them take its leaves  
12. to serve as a covering for their private parts. Thereupon Kangjeng  
13. the Prophet and his wife took the leaves of the anjir tree, and wrapped  
14. and covered the nakedness of Kangjeng the Prophet and his wife; the leaves  
15. served as their covering and wrap. Then came  
16. the wrath of the Lord of the Worlds upon Kangjeng the Prophet and his wife;  
17. while Iblis was still hiding in Paradise, near the  
[catchword: peacock]

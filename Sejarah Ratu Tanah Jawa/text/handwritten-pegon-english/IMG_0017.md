### Folio 33 (Visual Right)

1: their faces resembling resplendent pearls, glowing like two radiant
2: moons, their bodies like fine silver, their eyes like blazing stars,
3: their faces gleaming like the full moon, their noses like blossoming campaka flowers,
4: their teeth like pomegranate seeds, their hair dark and glossy,
5: all their bodies perfectly formed. And after Sang Hyang Nur Cahya
6: established his sovereignty in the intermediate realm [*‘ālam antara*], on Mount
7: Hind Purbawana, upon the peak of a high cliff,
8: on the mountain peak, every day at morning and evening, Sang Hyang
9: Nur Cahya was visited by his brother Sang Hyang Nur Rasa. He had
10: the summit transformed into an assembly place of the gods, who
11: were amazed at its beauty. And after a long time,
12: Sang Hyang Nur Rasa, having performed severe asceticism,
13: was granted a vision by his father; and to his brother, Sang Hyang
14: Nur Cahya, he paid deep homage. Then Semar, the spirit servant,
15: was commanded by Sang Hyang Nur Cahya to serve
16: his brother, Sang Hyang Nur Rasa. And after a long time, Sang Hyang
17: Nur Rasa, in heaven, together with the angels of the intermediate realm,

### Folio 34 (Visual Left)

1: upon the peak of the mountain called Mount Mercukunda. There
2: Sang Hyang Nur Cahya sent an envoy to Sang Hyang Nur
3: Rasa. The envoy summoned all the jinn, and
4: after their assembly had gathered, two disciples of Sang Hyang Nur
5: Cahya selected from among all the female jinn.
6: And after all their leaders were assembled before Sang Hyang Nur
7: Cahya in the intermediate realm, and after all the
8: subjects of all the female jinn were present in the intermediate realm,
9: they were counted: all the female jinn
10: were brought before Sang Hyang Nur Cahya. The female jinn
11: bowed down in submission, each taking the form of a heavenly nymph [*dewi*]. And
12: all the female deities who were transformed into nymphs
13: were presented to Sang Hyang Nur Cahya. As for those who were made
14: divine nymphs, they were married to Sang Hyang Nur
15: Cahya. And from them were born four children:

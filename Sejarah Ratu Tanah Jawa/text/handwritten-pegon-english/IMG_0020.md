### Folio 40 (Visual Left)  

1. Sang Hyang Punggung, because all the gods turned against them.  
2. What they desired was not granted, and their requests were rejected.  
3. Both were despised, and cast out from among the gods.  
4. Therefore they departed, fleeing in fear from Sang Hyang Tunggal,  
5. fleeing in dread. Then they were discovered by the people,  
6. and sheltered by Sang Hyang Pugung and his brother,  
7. and Sang Hyang Punggung. They dwelt in the intermediate realm, among  
8. the spirits of heaven, gods and heavenly maidens.  
9. All the maidens turned away from them, and  
10. did not wish to serve them. Then the youngest brother,  
11. Sang Hyang Tunggal, who was exceedingly handsome, was loved  
12. by all his elder brothers. Then Sang Hyang Tunggal sat  
13. upon the throne of stone, while  
14. Sang Hyang Pugung and Sang Hyang Punggung departed.  
15. Then Sang Hyang Tunggal fathered children in the intermediate realm:

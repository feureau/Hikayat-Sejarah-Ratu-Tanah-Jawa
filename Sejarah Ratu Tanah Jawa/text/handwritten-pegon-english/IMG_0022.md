# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa - English Translation
## Source: British Library Or. 14371
### Scan Concordance: IMG_0022.jpg (Folios 43 & 44)

---

### Folio 43 (Visual Right)

1: Sang Hyang Wisesa Brama then descended to
2: Mount Candramuka, where Brama dwelt on the high peak, and was commanded to cross over together with
3: nine of the gods, his children, onto Mount Candramuka, upon the peak.
4: And nine of the gods, all of them who had been
5: chosen, were made leaders under Sang Hyang Narada.
6: And after they became rulers, they were known as the kings of Brama.
7: To Sang Hyang Kanyaka's children they bowed in submission, serving like
8: younger brothers to an elder. And after they established themselves upon
9: the peak, they departed; and the nine gods chose
10: to dwell in Suralaya. Brama remained, dwelling in
11: the land of Java, on the summit. And after his realm was established on Mount Sura-
12: laya, which was called the mountain of Guru, they held their court.
13: Then those who dwelt on Mount Suralaya, Candramuka,
14: and who did not dwell on the peak, were given fragrant
15: civet wood, while one went to Nilakanta. Then
16: five were transformed into heavenly maidens; while the youngest brother was
17: named Sang Hyang Manikmaya.

### Folio 44 (Visual Left)

1: Batara Guru had a daughter, Dewi Sri. Then she was brought to Dewi
2: Sri; she was presented to Batara Surya, for Batara
3: Guru was fond of looking upon her face, and named her Dewi Sri.
4: Her mother was deeply fond of her, and gave her to Batara Surya.
5: And his son, who was born afterwards, was named Batara
6: Sadana. Dewi Sri, having reached adulthood, was sought
7: in marriage; Batara Surya wished to take her to wife, but
8: Hyang Nilakanta forbade it, so that Batara Surya
9: was fearful and fled, ashamed. Then Batara Wisnu departed
10: into concealment, and then married Dewi Sri,
11: and he was named Batara Wisnu; in heaven he was named Dewi
12: Sri. And afterwards, Dewi Sri departed, and
13: Batara Sadana went in search of her. Batara Wisnu
14: was united with Dewi Sri. And after a long time, the wife of Batara
15: Nilakanta, having been taken to wife, gave birth to a daughter, Dewi
16: Sri, and a son, Sang Hyang Sadana, their youngest son.

# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — Scan IMG_0044 Translation

### Folio 87 (Visual Right)
1. This was the lineage of King Sri Makukuhan, who had sons:
2. Raden Pancadriya, who had a son named Raden Darmadriya;
3. Raden Darmadriya had a son named Raden Darmalaya; Raden Darmalaya had a son named
4. Raden Handayakusuma; Handayakusuma had a son named Raden Gandakusuma;
5. Gandakusuma had a son named Raden Panji Kusuma; Panji Kusuma had
6. a son named Raden Hardakusuma; Hardakusuma had a son named Raden Tijakusuma;
7. Tijakusuma had a son named Raden Ajatkusuma; Ajatkusuma
8. had a son named Raden Citrakusuma; Citrakusuma had a son named Raden Citrasoma;
9. and Citrasoma had a son named Raden Resi Gatayu. Completed.
10. The chronicle of King Sindula. And God knows best what is correct. May God forgive them,
11. O Lord, O Most Forgiving! Resi Gatayu had a son named Raden Lembu Amiluhur,
12. who ruled over the realm of Janggala; King Lembu Amiluhur had
13. a son named Raden Panji Inukertapati, also known as Raden
14. Kudawanengpati, who took as his bride Raden Panji Sekartaji, daughter of
15. King Banjaransari, who reigned over Kediri.
16. King Banjaransari had a daughter named Dewi Sekartaji,
17. who was renowned for her incomparable beauty throughout the land of Java.

### Folio 88 (Visual Left)
1. Now we relate the descendants who succeeded to the throne in after times.
2. After the demise of King Sindula, his son succeeded him,
3. continuing to rule with wisdom and justice, beloved by all the people.
4. The realm was at peace, and prosperity spread throughout the land.
5. In those days, people from distant islands came to trade,
6. marveling at the abundance of crops and the tranquility of the towns.
7. The memory of King Sindula remained revered among all the folk,
8. and his teachings were carefully preserved by the scribes and elders.
9. Then the lineage continued through successive generations,
10. each ruler upholding the righteous principles of their forefathers.
11. In the land of Java, people lived in security and contentment,
12. free from the fear of plagues, famines, or wicked spirits.
13. Here begins the chronicle of the subsequent royal lines,
14. detailing the noble kings who held sway across the island,
15. their heroic deeds, their alliances, and their spiritual power.
16. May this record serve as an example for all who come after,
17. demonstrating that true greatness lies in justice and devotion to God.

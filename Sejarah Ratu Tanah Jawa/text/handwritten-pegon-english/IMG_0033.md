# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — Scan IMG_0033 Translation

### Folio 65 (Visual Right)
1. Then he bowed before the queen and the sage, offering his humble respects.
2. He stood before the palace, serving respectfully, not speaking at all.
3. All the people who were left behind in Java came out before King
4. Dewatacengkar, who was on the battlefield. Thereupon,
5. the sage stood before him, and the king said: "Go forth
6. to the battlefield, bow down, and request the land of Medang Kamulan!"
7. And it was granted, but only the people who remained walked with him.
8. It was not long before he arrived in the presence of King Sri, reigning
9. on the throne. They were startled, for men and women rushed to follow
10. behind the king's procession, paying homage to the king.
11. He bowed before the king, and those who remained also bowed.
12. The king summoned his ministers and warriors, and they assembled
13. behind the king. But it was already decreed by the
14. remnants of the kingdom who were left behind. And he was followed by the queen,
15. accompanied by her retinue. Thereupon the king went forth
16. to the ocean shore, into the roaring sea, and plunged deep
17. into the sea. Thereupon the king went into the depths, and the sea parted.

### Folio 66 (Visual Left)
1. Thereupon he stood before him once again, because he had
2. seen from afar what was hidden within the sage's
3. presence, before the king. Thereupon he stood before the sage,
4. and the sage asked him: "Where do you wish to go?"
5. He replied: "I wish to go to the east, and I am very
6. weary, for I have endured great suffering." The sage
7. said to him: "Go into the presence of the sage!"
8. The sage was pleased and granted him gifts, if he
9. were willing to follow his words. Then he gave him
10. the heirloom dagger, and instructed him to go to the palace
11. of King Sri, and bow before
12. him. Above all, he was to obey the sage's commands,
13. while the rest of the people were given into his hand.
14. All of them were astonished, for the people had been scattered,
15. terrified by the spirit ruler. He was guided by
16. the king. Thereupon he went forth to
17. the throne, offering his respects to the king and sage.

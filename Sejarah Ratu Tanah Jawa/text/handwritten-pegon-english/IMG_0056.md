# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — Scan IMG_0056 Translation

### Folio 111 (Visual Right)
1. After the foundation of Demak was firmly established, Raden Patah
2. invited the holy saints, the Wali Sanga, to assist in the spiritual guidance of the people.
3. Sunan Kalijaga, Sunan Bonang, Sunan Giri, and Sunan Kudus gathered
4. in Demak to consult on the building of the Great Mosque,
5. which would serve as the central sanctuary of faith and wisdom for all Java.
6. The holy saints joined hands in constructing the sacred edifice,
7. each contributing timber and labor with their own hands.
8. Sunan Kalijaga fashioned a wondrous pillar from scattered chips of wood,
9. bound together by divine power and spiritual grace into a firm column.
10. When the Great Mosque of Demak was completed, the call to prayer resounded,
11. drawing multitudes from near and far to unite in worship of the One God.
12. The saints established traditions that harmonized the sacred teachings
13. with the noble customs of Java, utilizing wayang, gamelan, and poetry
14. to instruct the hearts of the common folk in righteousness and virtue.
15. Thus the faith spread gently and peacefully throughout the island,
16. winning devotion not by sword or coercion, but through love and wisdom.
17. And the people rejoiced under the radiant dawn of the new spiritual era.

### Folio 112 (Visual Left)
1. Meanwhile, at the court of Majapahit, the power of the ancient empire
2. was drawing toward its twilight, as foretold by the prophecies of old.
3. Internal disputes arose among the regional lords and princes of the provinces,
4. weakening the authority of the central throne in the capital.
5. King Brawijaya observed these events with philosophical equanimity,
6. knowing that all earthly dynasties rise, flourish, and eventually decline
7. according to the cyclical decree of the Supreme Creator.
8. He refrained from waging destructive wars against his own subjects,
9. choosing instead to retire into deeper contemplation and spiritual reflection.
10. In those days, many loyal followers and nobles from Majapahit
11. journeyed to Demak, pledging their allegiance to Raden Patah,
12. recognizing in him the true heir to the royal lineage of Java.
13. Raden Patah received them with honor, granting them lands and titles,
14. and preserving their ancient dignity within the new administration.
15. Thus the transition of authority was accomplished with harmony,
16. preserving the continuity of the sacred sovereignty of the island.
17. And the ancient chronicles recorded this great turning point with reverence.

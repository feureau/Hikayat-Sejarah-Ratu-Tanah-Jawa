### Folio 57 (Visual Right)  

1. Then spoke Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq, may Allāh be pleased with him: "O  
2. my Lord, do not believe him! If the Master of Kyai  
3. is deceived by Iblīs, and Iblīs succeeds in misleading him,  
4. he will deceive him completely, and your knowledge, Master, for Iblīs  
5. knows all about devotion to the Lord and worship, and perfection.  
6. But Kyai Iblīs, if he bows down, it is only outward show!"  
7. Then the Master fell silent. And Kangjeng Prophet Muḥammad, the Mes-  
8. senger of Allāh, may Allāh bless him and grant him peace, the Chosen One, turned to  
9. Sayyidinā Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq, may Allāh be pleased with him. As for the knowledge  
10. of Subḥānahu wa-Ta‘ālā, when it was revealed to Iblīs, his spirit  
11. was disturbed, and Iblīs's work became ineffective; and if  
12. his line continued, it would still bear fruit, becoming continuous.  
13. And if the children of Adam did not guard themselves from this,  
14. then within their hearts they would be inclined toward worldly darkness.  
15. But toward Iblīs, cursed of Allāh, who was the leader of falsehood,  
16. Iblīs was astonished at the spiritual state within the children of Adam:

### Folio 58 (Visual Left)  

1. into the land of Java, and the people of the Arab land, all of them,  
2. became the vicegerents of Allāh before the Lord God Subḥānahu wa-Ta‘ālā, the  
3. One, the Subduer, Mighty and Majestic; descended from Kangjeng the Prophet  
4. Muḥammad, the Beloved of Allāh, may Allāh bless him and grant him peace, the Chosen One, of Me-  
5. dina. As for the chronicle of Kangjeng Prophet Muḥammad, may Allāh bless him  
6. and grant him peace: he had four companions who were beloved: the first, Sayyid-  
7. inā Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq, may Allāh be pleased with him; the second, Sayyid-  
8. inā ‘Umar Farūq, may Allāh be pleased with him; the third, Sayyidinā ‘Uthmān  
9. Dhū ’n-Nūrayn, may Allāh be pleased with him; the fourth, Sayyidinā ‘Alī  
10. Murtaḍā, may Allāh be pleased with him; and the fifth, Najamuddin,  
11. who was cursed of Allāh. As for Najamuddin:  
12. he was cast down from among the people, because he held to the judgment  
13. of Iblīs, cursed of Allāh. Kangjeng Prophet Muḥammad, the Messenger of Allāh,  
14. may Allāh bless him and grant him peace, was tempted by the deception of Iblīs,  
15. cursed of Allāh, who came in disguise. If Kangjeng Prophet Isa  
16. had entered into the cave, he would not have perished; but he was sent  
17. to proclaim the truth, and his teachings spread throughout the world.

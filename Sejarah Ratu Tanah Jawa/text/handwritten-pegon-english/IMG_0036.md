# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — Scan IMG_0036 Translation

### Folio 71 (Visual Right)
1. and dwell in the land of Java. As for his grandchild, he wore a golden crown,
2. given by King Sindula. And after Raden
3. Daniswara had departed, he went forth into the cave of Mount Semeru.
4. Then he was summoned by Raden, who was in the cave of Mount Semeru.
5. There he held discourse with King Sindula on the mountain peak,
6. conferring on statecraft. Then Raden Nugraha, having received
7. the blessing of King Sindula, was instructed in all the secret arts.
8. Then Raden Daniswara and his sister took leave,
9. for they had been instructed by King Sindula to go to
10. the court, and reside on the northern side, while Raden Daniswara
11. and his sister remained behind. And King Sindula loved his grandchild dearly,
12. so that whoever beheld him felt deep love in their heart. King Sind-
13. ula rejoiced, for he had seen his grandchild, and he was seated upon
14. the throne. And the teachings he gave were well received by the king.
15. Thus he departed, having received permission from Aji Saka to establish
16. the royal dynasty of Java, spanning three generations.
17. And he was granted blessings, sent by the Lord of the Heavens.

### Folio 72 (Visual Left)
1. If there was a ruler in the land of Java, here begins the tale of
2. the lineage of Raden Daniswara and his younger sister,
3. who lived together in the cave, united as one from former times,
4. enduring hardship in the cave of power.
5. But now they had been brought out to the realm of light,
6. and all were astonished by King Sindula. Thereupon
7. the grandchild of Raden Daniswara and his sister was named,
8. and they were summoned by Raden
9. Daniswara. His sister was given the name Dewi
10. Maya, residing in the cave, while the brother was called
11. Raden Daniswara. Then they were embraced with great love
12. by Raden Daniswara. The younger sister was brought before
13. the king, while Raden Daniswara was received
14. by King Sindula. He was embraced and kissed by
15. King Sindula, who smiled warmly at him.
16. Thereupon King Sindula went forth into the court,
17. and greeted the grandchild with great tenderness and affection.

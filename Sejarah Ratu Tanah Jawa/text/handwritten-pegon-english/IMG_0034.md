# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — Scan IMG_0034 Translation

### Folio 67 (Visual Right)
1. Then he followed down into the cave, and it was not long before
2. he met the king who had fallen into the chasm of power.
3. Here begins the tale of the peak before the sage. Thereupon
4. the people marvelled at his white radiance, standing before
5. the king, while the sage and Aji Saka remained
6. sitting together. Then the king asked to be admitted into the realm
7. of the gods, to become King Buddhasoca, reigning over the land of Java.
8. By him, Kiyai Dogung of Gilingwesi was summoned to the presence.
9. All the people who were called came before King Buddhasoca,
10. receiving royal robes from the sage. Then all the people
11. revered him, and the people throughout the land of Java
12. feared King Buddhasoca. And his lineage descended
13. from seven generations of sages, and his reign brought peace to all.
14. He was endowed with wisdom, reigning as prince on the throne.
15. Thus King Buddhasoca governed the people
16. in the realm, ruling with justice and mercy, so that
17. whoever drew near him felt peace, and was healed of suffering.

### Folio 68 (Visual Left)
1. And the form of the queen shone bright white, pure as a pearl.
2. Her father, named Sindula, had also become a king
3. in Pajajaran, while the elder brother was King Dewata-
4. cengkar, who was drowned in the ocean.
5. As for the youngest child, Raden Danis-
6. wara, he was instructed by the sage
7. not to leave the southern sea, and to remain guarding
8. the ocean shore, dwelling upon Mount Kendeng, honored
9. by all. And his sister was taken by Raden Da-
10. niswara as his companion, and they lived together in the cave.
11. In former times, Raden Daniswara had lived in the cave,
12. and this place was called Pajajaran. He was aided by
13. divine favor, shining like a full moon. Thereupon
14. he became King of Sindula, ruling over the lands
15. of Nusajawa. Then Raden Danis-
16. wara and his sister, both of them, were honored,
17. and Raden Daniswara and his sister were called the masters of the cave.

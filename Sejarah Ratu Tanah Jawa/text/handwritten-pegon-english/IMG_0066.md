# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — IMG_0066

## Folio 131 (Visual Right)

1: Now the venerable Kiyai Buyut Seta had already departed, returning to his
2: abode, leaving behind Pangeran Lowan and the Queen;
3: the King sat on the throne, attended by all the royal princesses
4: and the court retinue, while the dignitaries remained standing in attendance.
5: At that time, news was brought to the King concerning
6: the Adipatis of the western districts, who had sent envoys
7: to pledge their loyalty and submit tribute to the sovereign.
8: The envoys were received with great courtesy in the palace court,
9: and they presented horses, precious cloths, and gold ornaments,
10: expressing the devotion of their provinces to the throne of Java.
11: The King accepted their gifts graciously and granted them honors,
12: confirming their appointments and exhorting them to govern justly;
13: then a banquet was held in their honor, with music and dancing,
14: celebrating the unity and peace of the entire country.
15: All the regional lords were delighted by the King's generosity,
16: and they prepared to return to their lands with renewed zeal;
17: while the King retired to the inner chambers to attend to state affairs.

## Folio 132 (Visual Left)

1: All the regional rulers having departed, the King turned his thoughts
2: to the welfare of the people and the building of public works;
3: he ordered the construction of roads, canals, and market centers,
4: so that commerce might flourish and communication be swift.
5: The chronicles record that during this era, the land was free from pestilence
6: and famine, and abundant crops were harvested every season.
7: The King also gave attention to religious instruction and scholarship,
8: supporting teachers of wisdom and establishing houses of study;
9: many came from distant lands to seek learning in the capital,
10: and the reputation of Java spread far and wide across the seas.
11: Meanwhile, Pangeran Lowan governed his province with great wisdom,
12: settling disputes with impartiality and protecting the common folk;
13: his fame grew throughout the kingdom as a noble and just prince,
14: highly respected by both the aristocracy and the populace.
15: Thus the land of Java remained in harmony and prosperity,
16: blessed with peace under the benevolent rule of its sovereign,
17: who was praised by all generations as a model of righteous kingship.

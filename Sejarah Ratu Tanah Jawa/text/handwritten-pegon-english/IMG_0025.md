### Folio 49 (Visual Right)  

1. By his grace, only one remained before the Lord of the  
2. Worlds in the realm of the palace. And having returned to Suralaya,  
3. all of them were transformed into ordinary human beings. Here ends  
4. the chronicle of Gowa; and Allāh knows best what is right: *ghufiralahum*.  
5. As for the chronicle of Kangjeng Sultan of Rum: he received  
6. the decree of the Lord God Subḥānahu wa-Ta‘ālā, the One, the Subduer,  
7. Mighty and Majestic. Then Kangjeng Sultan of Rum gathered  
8. all his ministers before Kangjeng Sultan of Rum,  
9. because Kangjeng Sultan of Rum was commanded to settle  
10. the land of Java, by the will of the Lord of the Worlds. Kangjeng Sultan of  
11. Rum was informed that the land of Java was empty. Therefore,  
12. he gathered all his sea captains. And the captains took  
13. twenty thousand families to the land of Java. But all of them  
14. perished, so the captains returned to Kangjeng Sultan of Rum. And having  
15. seen that the land of Java was inhabited by spirits,  
16. none dared to settle there, for they were devoured by the spirits.

### Folio 50 (Visual Left)  

1. Dewanata had a son, Arjuna Sasrabahu, whose son  
2. was Raden Abiyasa. He had a son, Raden Pandu Dewanata,  
3. whose son was Raden Pandu, whose son was Raden Yudistira,  
4. whose son was Raden Dananjaya, whose son was Raden Angkawijaya,  
5. whose son was Jayabaya, whose son was Sindula, whose son was Jayamisesa,  
6. whose son was Jayamisesa, whose son was Jayambijaya,  
7. whose son was Prabu Silacala, whose son was Prabu Silacala,  
8. whose son was Prabu Sindula. But at that time the realm of Java  
9. was not yet civilized; there was no writing, for by the decree  
10. of the Lord God Subḥānahu wa-Ta‘ālā, the One, the Subduer, Mighty and  
11. Majestic, the land of Java was still dense jungle, beloved  
12. of the Lord of the Worlds, submerged beneath water.  
13. The land was clean and fertile, but uninhabited by human beings,  
14. so it was filled with evil spirits and wild beasts.  
15. Only Sang Hyang Prabu Sindula remained, by the will of Subḥā-  
16. nahu wa-Ta‘ālā, meditating in solitary asceticism within  
17. the cave; and he became the ancestral root of the kings of Java.

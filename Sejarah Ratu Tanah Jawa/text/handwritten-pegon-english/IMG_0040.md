# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — Scan IMG_0040 Translation

### Folio 79 (Visual Right)
1: And they were followed by Raden Daniswara and his beloved sister, both of them.
2: In the land of Java, tomorrow their grandchild would arise from the royal lineage,
3: descending from King Sindula. After he had completed his ascetic retirement,
4: King Sindula passed away, and all his treasures were divided, none
5: being left behind. His realm was bestowed upon his grandchild, who reigned
6: peacefully. And all the people who were in the land of Java, none
7: was oppressed, and there was no strife or conflict. The people
8: lived in security, and the mountains and hills were rich with bounty.
9: King Buddhasoca was revered by all kings and rulers,
10: and he governed with wisdom and justice over all his lands.
11: Here begins the tale of the reign of King Buddhasoca,
12: which lasted for many years, until his successor
13: was enthroned in the land of Java. Then he retired to the mountain,
14: leaving behind an enduring legacy of peace and spiritual wisdom.
15: All his counselors were honored, and the people of Sarwopati
16: praised his righteousness, keeping his memory alive for future generations.
17: Thus ends this chapter concerning the reign of King Sindula and his successors.

### Folio 80 (Visual Left)
1: His heart was filled with joy, and all the people rejoiced because
2: their lord was righteous and fair to all his subjects, none being wronged.
3: Thus it was in the world. After that, he was granted
4: children and grandchildren, who were all beloved by the king.
5: And when the youth became king, taking the seat of King Sindula,
6: the realm flourished. Raden Daniswara was appointed as chief counselor,
7: while the queen was revered by all women throughout the court.
8: In those days, there was great friendship between all royal houses,
9: and emissaries from distant lands came to pay homage,
10: bringing gifts of gold, silver, and precious cloths.
11: The king received them graciously, bestowing royal gifts in return,
12: so that his fame spread far and wide beyond the seas.
13: King Sindula, having reached a venerable old age, retired
14: to the peaceful mountain glade, devoting himself to spiritual contemplation.
15: Then Raden Daniswara and his sister took leave,
16: returning to their domain, accompanied by their loyal retinue.
17: And the people rejoiced, celebrating their return with great festivities.

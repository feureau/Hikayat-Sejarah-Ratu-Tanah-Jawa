2. to the troops and the Adipati who had been granted high rank.

4. arranged the soldiers into companies; and all

8. standing ready, marching together towards  
9. the leader, shouting praises and beating drums, all  
10. raising loud cries, and all of them surrounded the royal

12. of the leader; they all bowed before the prince, who  
13. had arrayed the army. Then both

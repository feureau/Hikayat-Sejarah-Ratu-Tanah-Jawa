# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — Scan IMG_0043 Translation

### Folio 85 (Visual Right)
1: The angel ‘Izrā’īl, cursed of God, came down toward Aji Saka,
2: but Aji Saka was steadfast, protected by his pure devotion and faith.
3: The angel ‘Izrā’īl, cursed of God, attempted to frighten Aji Saka,
4: but Aji Saka remained calm, knowing the power of the Exalted and Most High.
5: With steadfast courage, Aji Saka faced him, and through divine decree,
6: the cursed Iblīs had no power over him, for it was as the word of
7: the Noble Prophet Muhammad, Messenger of God, peace and blessings of God be upon him, had foretold
8: to Aji Saka. Completed. And God knows best what is correct. May He forgive them,
9: O Lord, O Most Forgiving! Here begins the account of the journey
10: of Aji Saka, together with Raden Daniswara and his sister,
11: arriving in the court of his sister, who was queen to King Sindu-
12: la, in the royal palace. And the white heirloom was preserved,
13: becoming a royal regalia of the kingdom, celebrated by all.
14: Raden Daniswara and his sister went before the king,
15: and the young prince greeted them with great reverence,
16: bowing down respectfully, offering tokens of honor and devotion.
17: And they were welcomed warmly into the inner court of the palace.

### Folio 86 (Visual Left)
1: He approached near to Aji Saka and
2: Raden Daniswara. Then the angel ‘Izrā’īl, cursed of God, was
3: astonished at the radiance emanating from them, shining bright like
4: a lamp. The angel ‘Izrā’īl, cursed of God, went forth
5: toward them, and Aji Saka was joyful,
6: saying: "O Aji Saka, you have already attained
7: a pure heart, joyful in your obedience and understanding."
8: Then the angel ‘Izrā’īl, cursed of God, said:
9: "O Aji Saka, you have accomplished your mission
10: in the land of Java. Now prepare yourself, for your time draws near,
11: and your legacy shall endure in the hearts of the people."
12: Aji Saka answered with serenity and grace:
13: "All is according to the will of the Almighty, who grants wisdom
14: and guides His servants along the path of truth."
15: Thereupon the vision faded, leaving behind a tranquil silence.
16: Aji Saka returned to his disciples, teaching them the profound
17: truths of existence and the inner harmony of the universe.

### Folio 21 (Visual Right)  

1. Islam, because in truth those who believed in all  
2. that was willed by Kangjeng Prophet Adam, peace be upon him, were  
3. guided toward the path willed by Kangjeng Prophet Adam, the vicegerent  
4. of Allāh, all of them. As for the chronicle of the son of Kangjeng Prophet  
5. Adam, peace be upon him, named Baginda Shīth: when he had  
6. reached adulthood, he was granted thirty scriptures;  
7. he was named Prophet Shīth, peace be upon him. And  
8. his father, Kangjeng Prophet Adam, and his mother, Kangjeng Babu Eve,  
9. were both deeply saddened because he had reached adulthood  
10. without yet having a wife; for Kangjeng Prophet Shīth had no twin  
11. partner. In their deep sorrow, the angel  
12. directed his mother to leave him within the Masjid al-Ḥarām in Mecca.  
13. Then came the command of the Lord God Subḥānahu wa-Ta‘ālā, the One,  
14. the Subduer, to the angel Jabrā’īl, peace be upon him, who was sent  
15. down to the earthly world, bringing a heavenly maiden  
16. from Paradise named the heavenly maiden Dewi Shajarah,  
17. remarkably beautiful in form, of radiant complexion, and Kangjeng Babu

### Folio 22 (Visual Left)  

1. and some still had faith, in the midst of  
2. intense discord against Kangjeng Prophet Adam; and some  
3. were obedient in their hearts, while others turned to disbelief  
4. against the Lord of the Worlds. As for the chronicle of the people of the  
5. earth: some of the descendants all became  
6. obedient, but then turned into jinn, some joining Kangjeng  
7. the Prophet, while the unbelieving jinn remained exceedingly hostile.  
8. If they returned to good, the jinn became Muslim, but the unbelieving jinn  
9. had unbelieving offspring, and Muslim jinn had Muslim offspring. Likewise,  
10. some worshippers among men became unbelievers,  
11. and some became apostates, turning into three kinds:  
12. all the children of Adam on earth became divided: some  
13. turning into jinn, some into humans, all  
14. forgiven [*ghufiralahum*]. As for the chronicle of the children of Adam: some  
15. still remained who obeyed the divine commands,  
16. but only a few remained; there were also men

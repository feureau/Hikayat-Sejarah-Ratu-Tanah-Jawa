# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa - English Translation
## Source: British Library Or. 14371

### Folio 19 (Visual Right)

1: and the mother who had suckled them was Baginda
2: Shīth to his father Kangjeng Prophet Adam, peace be upon him.
3: After Kangjeng the Prophet had addressed his son, Baginda Shīth,
4: facing his mother, Kangjeng Babu Eve, may Allāh be pleased with her,
5: she embraced him, and the son, Baginda Shīth, was granted
6: forgiveness [*ghufiralahum*]. As for the chronicle of the children of Adam who were born,
7: they were numerous, without grief, under the divine guidance of the
8: Father; whatever they did was orderly and upright,
9: without quarrel. After they were created by the Lord,
10: Subḥānahu wa-Ta‘ālā, the One, the Subduer, there was no disbelief or
11: denial. At that time, Mecca was still wilderness,
12: and whatever was born outwardly appeared straight and proper.
13: But as for the children of Kayūmūrtaka, they were wicked,
14: and those children then wandered into the wil-
15: derness of the land of the West, the descendants of Adam living in that forest.
16: The descendants of Adam were exceedingly numerous;
17: they multiplied greatly, spreading across Mecca and beyond.

### Folio 20 (Visual Left)

1: And forty twin pairs were born; and after they
2: grew into babies, they were carefully tended,
3: raised by Prophet Adam and his consort Kangjeng Babu Eve,
4: and given clothing suitable for their states.
5: They grew up to be young men and women. Then
6: the Lord Subḥānahu wa-Ta‘ālā, the One, the Subduer, ordained that Kangjeng Prophet Adam,
7: peace be upon him, and Kangjeng Babu Eve, may Allāh be pleased with her, should
8: match each son and daughter in marriage by the command of the Lord
9: God Subḥānahu wa-Ta‘ālā, uniting them with their crosswise twins,
10: not with their own birth-mate. Then, after Kangjeng Prophet Adam,
11: peace be upon him, passed away, the leadership fell upon
12: Kangjeng Babu Eve, who took care of the children. Then Kangjeng
13: Babu Eve repented before Kangjeng Prophet Adam,
14: turning to the Lord of the Worlds; death came upon her, and
15: Babu Eve was united with Kangjeng Prophet Adam, peace be upon him,
16: granted forgiveness [*ghufiralahum*]. Her children multiplied,
17: becoming forty pairs; and the mother of them all

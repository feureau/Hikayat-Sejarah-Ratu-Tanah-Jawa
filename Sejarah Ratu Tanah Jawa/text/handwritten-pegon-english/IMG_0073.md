# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — IMG_0073

## Folio 145 (Visual Right)

1. Now they were gathered together in the council chamber, and the prince  
2. received the messengers sent by Kanjeng Ratu Kidul, who had arrived  
3. bearing greetings and celestial garments for Raden Jaka Suruh;  
4. for it was known that Kanjeng Ratu Kidul held deep spiritual affection  
5. for the descendants of the kings of Java, promising her aid in times of need.  
6. The messenger spoke words of encouragement, instructing him to build a capital  
7. at the designated sacred grove where the river meets the southern sea;  
8. Raden Jaka Suruh listened with profound humility and devotion,  
9. bowing low and accepting the celestial garments with reverence.  
10. Then Raden Jaka Suruh went forth to inspect the chosen site,  
11. finding the earth fragrant and the surrounding waters pure and sweet;  
12. he cleared the forest, planted sacred banyan trees at the central square,  
13. and laid the foundations of the new royal court of Majapahit.  
14. Many craftsmen, scholars, and warriors gathered to assist in the building,  
15. working in unity and harmony under the prince's inspired direction;  
16. within a short time, majestic halls, gates, and ramparts were completed,  
17. marking the emergence of a glorious empire destined to rule across Nusantara.

## Folio 146 (Visual Left)

1. Now the narrative relates that during that time in the forest,  
2. the disciples of the hermitage were engaged in intense spiritual practice;  
3. Raden Jaka Tingkir sat in deep meditation under a venerable tamarind tree,  
4. seeking divine clarity regarding his future role in the realm of Java.  
5. After forty days of fasting and prayer, a celestial radiance shone upon him,  
6. and a voice from the unseen realm conveyed sacred knowledge to his heart,  
7. confirming that his destiny was tied to the throne of Pajang and Mataram.  
8. Strengthening his resolve, he returned to the hermitage of Ki Ageng Tingkir,  
9. who received him with joyful tears, knowing that his training was complete.  
10. The venerable sage bestowed upon him the sacred ancestral heirloom kris,  
11. advising him to always wield power with justice, patience, and compassion.  
12. Raden Jaka Tingkir then bid farewell to his teacher and fellow disciples,  
13. setting out alone on the road leading towards the royal city of Demak;  
14. along the journey, he encountered many perils and wild beasts in the wilderness,  
15. but all submitted peacefully to his extraordinary spiritual aura and courage.  
16. At length he arrived at the capital gates, where crowds marvelled at his noble countenance,  
17. heralding the beginning of his remarkable service at the royal court of the Sultan.

### Folio 27 (Visual Right)  

1. in the intermediate realm [*‘ālam antara*], that is the realm between the heavens and the earth,  
2. among the jinn, angels, and spirits, who were called  
3. gods [*dewa*]. Their future descendants would be Batara Guru, while the pro-  
4. phets would be manifest in earthly kingdoms,  
5. in the land of Java; and if they manifested in the Arab lands,  
6. it was according to the decree of Subḥānahu wa-Ta‘ālā. After  
7. this instruction was delivered by the will of the Lord of the Worlds to Kangjeng Prophet Shīth,  
8. peace be upon him, the angel Jabrā’īl, peace be  
9. upon him, departed. Then Kangjeng Prophet  
10. Shīth, peace be upon him, prostrated before Subḥānahu wa-Ta‘ālā,  
11. prostrating in devotion. Then emerged a radiance,  
12. an incandescent light shining from the crown of his head. Then  
13. he took hold of his wife, and Kangjeng the Prophet embraced her,  
14. and she gave birth to a child. The infant was then brought to Kangjeng  
15. Prophet Shīth, peace be upon him, and named Sayyid  
16. Anwas, of pure light. And after he reached adulthood, this  
17. brilliant radiance returned to his father, Kangjeng Prophet Shīth, peace be upon him.

### Folio 28 (Visual Left)  

1. enter hellfire, for he had been purified by his father, Kangjeng Prophet Adam, peace be  
2. upon him; for in his own person he had been ordained  
3. by Subḥānahu wa-Ta‘ālā in this world and the hereafter.  
4. Such was the tale of Kangjeng Prophet Shīth, peace be upon him: he went  
5. to Mount Nūr, where Kangjeng Prophet Shīth, peace be upon him,  
6. devoted himself to the Lord God Subḥānahu wa-Ta‘ālā, the One, the Subduer,  
7. Mighty and Majestic, fasting and meditating at the cave's summit.  
8. In this manner he remained in worship. Then came the command of the Lord of the Worlds,  
9. and the angel Jabrā’īl, peace be upon him, was sent down to the  
10. mountain, carrying a radiant crown for Kangjeng Prophet Shīth, peace be upon him.  
11. After meeting him, the angel  
12. Jabrā’īl, peace be upon him, spoke to Kangjeng Prophet Shīth, peace be upon him:  
13. "If Kangjeng the Prophet desires to dwell in this world,  
14. let him take a wife; for after this world is filled,  
15. Kangjeng Prophet Shīth, peace be upon him, shall be granted a son who  
16. shall be endowed with the science of wisdom."

### Folio 11 (Visual Right)

1: ‘alayhi ’s-salām to the earth, together with forgiveness from Subḥānahu wa-Ta‘ālā,
2: the One, the Subduer, along with clothing from the garments of Paradise
3: for Prophet Adam and Babu Eve. Then in the world, the angel
4: Jabrā’īl, peace be upon him, was instructed to fetch them from Yemen;
5: he conveyed the greeting, saying: "Verily, you have been forgiven
6: by the Lord of the Worlds! Pure and upright is your heart, Adam; be patient,
7: for tomorrow, if you wish, you shall enter again into Paradise!"
8: Then they prostrated themselves, falling low in submission to the command of Subḥā-
9: nahu wa-Ta‘ālā. And after prostrating, they arose,
10: wearing fragrant garments and burning benzoin incense. Then
11: the angel Jabrā’īl, peace be upon him, departed to Mount Su-
12: randīl in the land of Ceylon. As for Kangjeng Prophet Adam, peace be
13: upon him, he was beloved of the Lord God subḥānahu wa-ta‘ālā;
14: cast down from Paradise into the earthly world upon
15: Mount Surandīl, he wept continuously day and night,
16: repenting for forty years, weeping and offering prayers of repentance
17: to the Prophet, turning toward the Lord of the Worlds. At that time

### Folio 12 (Visual Left)

1: the Lord subḥānahu wa-ta‘ālā was merciful to the peacock and the dragon,
2: who had allowed entry into Paradise, through the agency of Iblīs, who was full of deceit.
3: Marvelous was the sight of Babu Eve, who was exceedingly astonished.
4: Kangjeng Babu Eve sought forgiveness [ghufiralahum]. As for Kangjeng Prophet Adam, peace
5: be upon him, the reason he received forgiveness from the Lord of the Worlds was
6: on account of the intense enmity of the Prophet toward his adversary Iblīs,
7: the cursed one [la‘nat Allāh], seeking refuge in Allāh from him, and not trusting him. And
8: Kangjeng Babu Eve was likewise forgiven for the same reason.
9: A voice of pardon came to them: *ghufiralahum* ("they are forgiven").
10: At that time, Kangjeng Prophet Adam, peace be upon him, and Kangjeng Babu
11: Eve, may Allāh be pleased with her, wept profusely in their sorrow,
12: beseeching repentance and forgiveness from the Lord God
13: subḥānahu wa-ta‘ālā, the One, the Subduer, Mighty and Majestic, together with
14: forty days of prayer. Such is the chronicle: then
15: the Lord subḥānahu wa-ta‘ālā, the One, the Subduer,
16: granted forgiveness to Kangjeng Prophet Adam, peace be upon him, and Kangjeng Babu Eve,
17: may Allāh be pleased with her. Thereupon descended the angel Jabrā’īl, peace be upon him.

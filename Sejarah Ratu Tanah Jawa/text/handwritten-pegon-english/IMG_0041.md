# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — Scan IMG_0041 Translation

### Folio 81 (Visual Right)
1: In ancient times, there lived a holy man named Ki Ageng Pamanahan,
2: who dwelt in the forest of Mentaok, clearing the land and planting crops.
3: And many people joined him, seeking refuge and livelihood in that forest.
4: It was not long before a village grew, becoming a flourishing settlement.
5: When the people had gathered in large numbers, they sought guidance from the sage,
6: who advised them to live in peace and unity under the divine law.
7: King Buddhasoca was pleased to hear of this growing community,
8: and sent messengers with provisions and royal gifts to support them.
9: Ki Ageng Pamanahan received the messengers with deep gratitude,
10: bowing low and expressing heartfelt thanks to the king.
11: After that, the settlement grew into a prosperous town,
12: attracting merchants and scholars from all corners of the land.
13: The sage continued to guide them in spiritual and worldly matters,
14: emphasizing justice, humility, and devotion to God.
15: Thus the foundation was laid for what would become a powerful kingdom,
16: respected throughout Java for its righteousness and prosperity.
17: And the people rejoiced, offering prayers for the continued blessing of their realm.

### Folio 82 (Visual Left)
1: After they were established, the prince, Raden
2: Daniswara, was known for his courage and wisdom. He was beloved
3: by all the people, who saw in him the qualities of a great ruler.
4: In those days, emissaries from neighboring kingdoms came to establish alliances,
5: marveling at the peace and order that prevailed in the land.
6: King Sindula, seeing the wisdom of his grandson, decided
7: to entrust him with greater responsibilities in governing the realm.
8: Raden Daniswara accepted the duty with humility and resolve,
9: pledging to serve the people and maintain justice at all times.
10: Under his guidance, the laws were codified, courts were established,
11: and disputes were settled fairly without favor or prejudice.
12: Trade flourished along the rivers and roads, bringing wealth
13: to both townspeople and farmers in the countryside.
14: The prince also took care to protect the environment,
15: ensuring that forests and water sources were preserved for future generations.
16: Thus his reputation grew far beyond the borders of Java,
17: and many foreign princes came to study the art of governance at his court.

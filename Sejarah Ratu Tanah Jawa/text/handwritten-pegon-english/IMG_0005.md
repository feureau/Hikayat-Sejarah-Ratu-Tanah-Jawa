### Folio 10 (Visual Left)  

1. God, and the dragon was among the animals of the highest rank.  
2. Having entered into Paradise, their grief was twofold.  
3. Entering into Paradise, the grief became intense.  
4. And Kangjeng Prophet Adam was filled with sorrow in his heart,  
5. because he had eaten of the fruit that was forbidden by  
6. the Lord of the Worlds. As for Kangjeng Prophet Adam,  
7. peace be upon him, he was beloved of the Lord God subḥānahu wa-  
8. ta‘ālā, the One, the Subduer. His spirit was created in Paradise,  
9. then brought down into the world. He was beloved of God,  
10. while Iblīs, cursed of Allāh, was made the chief of wickedness.  
11. Iblīs was full of malice in his heart toward Prophet Adam, peace be  
12. upon him, and his heart was full of deceit toward the Lord of the Worlds,  
13. and toward Kangjeng Babu Eve, may Allāh be pleased with her.  
14. As for the peacock bird, the reason it was  
15. cursed was because it enabled Iblīs, cursed of Allāh, to enter  
16. into Paradise; the peacock bird opened the gate of  
17. Paradise, and the peacock bird listened to Iblīs.

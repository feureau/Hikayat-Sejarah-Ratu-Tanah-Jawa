# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — IMG_0071

## Folio 141 (Visual Right)

1. Now after that, the messenger proceeded directly to Jurus,  
2. delivering the royal letters to the local regents;  
3. he reported that the royal court had confirmed their appointments,  
4. enjoining them to administer their territories with fairness and vigilance.  
5. The regents bowed low, touching the royal message to their brows,  
6. and declared their absolute fidelity to the sovereign throne;  
7. they then prepared gifts of local produce, fine fabrics, and timber  
8. to be dispatched back to the capital as tribute.  
9. Meanwhile, Pangeran Lowan conducted a tour of inspection  
10. throughout the southern coastal districts and farming villages;  
11. he inspected the irrigation works, storehouses, and defense towers,  
12. ensuring that the villagers were adequately protected from marauders.  
13. The local headmen came forward to greet the prince with joy,  
14. presenting offerings of fruits, honey, and livestock;  
15. the prince listened patiently to their petitions and resolved local disputes,  
16. winning the deep admiration and affection of the countryside;  
17. after which he proceeded onward along the mountain road towards Demak.

## Folio 142 (Visual Left)

1. Then Kiyai Seta set forth from the hermitage of Jurus,  
2. accompanied by his trusted disciples and retainers;  
3. they traveled through the dense forests and steep mountain paths,  
4. arriving at the ancient settlement where the elders had gathered.  
5. The villagers came out in crowds to receive the holy man's blessing,  
6. bringing their sick and infirm to seek his prayers and guidance;  
7. Kyai Seta spoke words of comfort, instructing them in righteous conduct,  
8. encouraging charity, mutual aid, and devotion to the Almighty.  
9. He spent three days in that village, giving counsel to the headmen  
10. on how to maintain harmony and protect their communal lands;  
11. then, having accomplished his spiritual mission in that region,  
12. he prepared to depart towards the royal capital of Mataram.  
13. The villagers escorted him with tears of gratitude to the boundary stream,  
14. offering prayers that his journey might be safeguarded from all perils;  
15. and Kyai Seta blessed them once more before continuing on his way,  
16. traversing the valleys until the towers of the royal city appeared,  
17. where he was received with high honor by the sovereign and the court.

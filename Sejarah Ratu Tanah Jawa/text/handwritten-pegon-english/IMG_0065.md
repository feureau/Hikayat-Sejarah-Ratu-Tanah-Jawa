# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — IMG_0065

## Folio 129 (Visual Right)

1. All the companions of Lowan who had gathered together were assembled,  
2. and the chiefs and dignitaries presented gifts of gold and silver  
3. before the king; then the King stepped forth onto the terrace,  
4. and Pangeran Sri approached, accompanied by the commanders  
5. of the palace guard. The elders who were present received them with great reverence,  
6. and the assembly of chiefs stood in deep silence,  
7. awaiting the royal pronouncements concerning the governance of the realm.  
8. There were eight hundred and fifty men of high rank  
9. present in the pavilion, all dressed in formal ceremonial robes;  
10. and Pangeran Sri and Kyai Seta  
11. bowed low before the majesty of the throne,  
12. reporting that peace had been restored across all lands,  
13. that the harvests were bountiful and the people contented.  
14. The King expressed his deep satisfaction, praising their devotion,  
15. and commanded that a nationwide celebration be held;  
16. then the assembly dispersed, each returning to his station,  
17. carrying the royal favor and blessings to their respective provinces.

## Folio 130 (Visual Left)

1. Now Kyai Seta and the prince conferred together in the private chamber,  
2. discussing the ancient lore and genealogies of the kings of Java,  
3. tracing the lineage from the ancient rulers down to the present day;  
4. they recalled the heroic deeds of their ancestors,  
5. how they had overcome hardships to establish the realm in glory.  
6. Kyai Seta recounted the wisdom of the ancient sages,  
7. emphasizing that a ruler must always maintain justice and piety,  
8. protecting the weak and honoring the laws of Heaven;  
9. for earthly power is transient, but virtuous deeds endure forever.  
10. The prince listened with great attentiveness, taking the counsel to heart,  
11. and vowed to govern with integrity and compassion for all people.  
12. Then they offered prayers for the peace and prosperity of the land,  
13. and Kyai Seta gave his parting blessing to the young prince,  
14. commending him to the guidance of the Almighty;  
15. after which the prince took his leave and departed,  
16. accompanied by his trusted retinue of nobles and guards,  
17. riding forth towards his northern domain with a resolute heart.

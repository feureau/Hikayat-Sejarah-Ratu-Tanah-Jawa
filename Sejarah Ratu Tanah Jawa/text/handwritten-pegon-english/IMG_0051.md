# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — Scan IMG_0051 Translation

### Folio 101 (Visual Right)
1. And the king of Pajajaran desired to visit the holy shrine,
2. accompanied by Dewi Suradewati, whose heart was heavy with sorrow.
3. She offered prayers and vowed that if her distress were removed,
4. she would distribute alms of rice and gold to the destitute and poor.
5. King Brawijaya smiled gently upon her, comforting her with reassuring words:
6. "Trust in the wisdom of the Creator, for every trial has its end."
7. Then Dewi Suradewati bowed down, showing deep reverence and gratitude.
8. After they departed from the shrine, they proceeded toward the western realm,
9. resting at the hermitage of the sage on the mountain slopes.
10. When they arrived, the sage welcomed them with noble hospitality,
11. seating them upon woven mats and presenting fruits of the forest.
12. The king inquired concerning the signs of the times and the welfare of the land.
13. The sage replied: "The heavenly order remains undisturbed,
14. so long as the rulers protect the helpless and uphold righteous judgment."
15. Dewi Suradewati listened attentively, finding solace and tranquility in his speech.
16. Then King Brawijaya and his queen took their leave,
17. continuing on their royal journey through the provinces of Java.

### Folio 102 (Visual Left)
1. After three days and three nights, they reached the coastal town,
2. where the governor received the royal procession with solemn pomp.
3. The streets were decorated with colorful banners and fragrant blossoms,
4. and crowds of citizens gathered to behold the countenance of their sovereign.
5. King Brawijaya held court in the great pavilion of the town,
6. hearing petitions from townspeople, merchants, and farmers alike.
7. He redressed their grievances, abolished unjust taxes,
8. and commanded that roads and bridges be restored throughout the district.
9. The people rejoiced, praising the benevolence and equity of the monarch.
10. In those days, merchants from overseas brought rare goods to the harbor,
11. establishing flourishing trade ties under the royal protection.
12. The king inspected the harbor defenses and ordered watchtowers built,
13. ensuring that pirates and raiders could not disturb the coastal towns.
14. Then the royal procession prepared to return toward the inland capital,
15. accompanied by songs of blessing from the grateful populace.
16. Thus the king toured his realm, uniting hearts and strengthening the state.
17. And peace rested upon the towns and shores of Java.

# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — Scan IMG_0039 Translation

### Folio 77 (Visual Right)
1: The youth bowed low, taking off his royal ornaments in the presence of the king.
2: He stepped forward and knelt, touching the ground with his forehead before the king.
3: The king welcomed him, and all who were present stood respectfully.
4: Then he entered the inner chambers, accompanied by his sister.
5: By the will of the Almighty, the mountain realm remained in peace and harmony.
6: Raden Daniswara smiled gently, looking upon his sister with great affection.
7: When the time came, and all the royal gifts had been presented,
8: the prince was led to the inner court, where he was seated upon the royal seat,
9: adorned with garments made of gold and embroidered silks.
10: All the chiefs of the realm paid homage, bowing low before the throne.
11: The king wished to bestow upon him the royal title, and all agreed.
12: King Sindula, seated on his high dais, was filled with joy and satisfaction,
13: beholding his grandchild who was endowed with such noble virtues.
14: By Raden Daniswara, the royal dignity was preserved, and the kingdom of
15: King Sindula flourished. The people rejoiced, honoring the young prince.
16: King Sindula bestowed upon him the kingdom, while he himself retired,
17: and the youth was acclaimed as ruler, possessing wisdom and great renown.

### Folio 78 (Visual Left)
1: After Sarwopati had passed away, his kingdom was given
2: to his descendants, who ruled with justice. All the people
3: honored them, and the kingdom prospered under their benevolent rule.
4: The people obeyed their commands, and there was no strife or rebellion.
5: When the old king died, his son took the throne,
6: continuing the virtuous rule of his father. The people were content,
7: and the harvest was bountiful year after year.
8: In those days, there was great friendship between neighboring realms,
9: and emissaries traveled back and forth bearing peaceful greetings.
10: The young king was respected by all foreign rulers,
11: and many sought his counsel in times of dispute.
12: Raden Daniswara remained a wise advisor, guiding the state
13: along the path of righteousness and peace.
14: Thus passed many generations in happiness and abundance,
15: and the legacy of the ancient kings was remembered by the people.
16: Whenever a king passed away, his successor took the oath of justice,
17: keeping the ancient covenants and honoring the supreme creator.

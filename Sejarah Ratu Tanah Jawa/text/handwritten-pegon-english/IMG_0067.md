# Sejarah Ratu Tanah Jawa — IMG_0067

## Folio 133 (Visual Right)

1: Now there was a certain wise elder residing in the southern districts,
2: celebrated for his deep piety and knowledge of secret wisdom;
3: he was sought out by noblemen and commoners alike for spiritual guidance,
4: and his dwelling was known as a refuge for scholars and travelers.
5: News of his virtue reached the ears of the King in the capital,
6: who desired to invite the venerable master to the court;
7: therefore envoys were dispatched with gifts of honor,
8: bearing a royal invitation written on fine palm-leaf.
9: When the envoys arrived at the hermitage, they delivered the King's words,
10: bowing respectfully before the sage and presenting the gifts.
11: The elder received the emissaries with warmth and humility,
12: offering them refreshments and inquiring after the sovereign's health;
13: he acknowledged the high honor bestowed upon him,
14: but expressed his preference for the quietude of contemplation,
15: yet consented to send his most learned disciple to the court,
16: who would carry his blessing and counsel to the King;
17: wherefore the emissaries returned rejoicing to the capital.

## Folio 134 (Visual Left)

1: All the court dignitaries having gathered, the envoy presented
2: the disciple before the King, who received him with great delight;
3: the young scholar possessed profound knowledge of scriptures and law,
4: speaking with eloquence and clarity that impressed the entire council.
5: The King questioned him on diverse matters of theology and justice,
6: finding his answers full of depth, discernment, and practical wisdom;
7: he appointed him as an advisor on spiritual affairs and royal edicts,
8: granting him a residence within the precincts of the palace.
9: In those days, the administration was strengthened by wise counsel,
10: the courts of justice operated with fairness and without corruption,
11: and disputes were resolved according to established custom and law.
12: The people felt secure under such righteous stewardship,
13: and the renown of the kingdom spread to distant islands;
14: merchant vessels arrived regularly at the river ports,
15: exchanging valuable merchandise and enriching the royal treasury.
16: The King continued to give thanks to the Almighty for these blessings,
17: remaining humble amidst magnificence and ever attentive to the people's needs.

Raden Jaka Suruh remained on Ngargapura for a long time, remaining motionless. Meanwhile, *Kangjeng Ratu* of the Southern Sea (*Samodra Kidul*) felt great pity for her younger brother, Raden Jaka Suruh. *Kangjeng Ratu Kidul* then arrived at Ngargapura. Raden Jaka immediately prostrated himself at her feet weeping, and *Kangjeng Ratu* embraced her younger brother, [p. 146.] with tears welling in her eyes. / *Sang Yang Wasesa* (the Almighty) spoke through *Kangjeng Ratu Kidul*: "Be seated, my child, I am here to assist you." Raden Jaka Suruh sat down, and *Ratu Kidul* spoke: "My child, end your meditation and descend from Ngargapura. Search in the middle of the forest for a *maja* tree with bitter fruit but an exceedingly sweet fragrance, and sit there." *Kangjeng Ratu* then vanished. Raden Jaka Suruh descended from Ngargapura. He arrived in the middle of the dense forest, an utter wilderness where no human had ever trodden. Raden Jaka Suruh found the *maja* tree in the center of the forest, which emitted an extraordinarily strong fragrance. Raden Jaka Suruh sat down, leaning against the bitter *maja* tree. After about seven days, the heavens thundered, and the entire Land of Java shook—this was the sign of the unification of the [p. 147.] King of the Land of Java. Then / the *maja* tree vanished, transforming into a complete state with all its accoutrements, lacking only human inhabitants. All the villagers nearby were astonished that the forest had suddenly become a state, and they were terrified. They conferred and reported to their respective *Dipati*. When the report reached them, the nearby *Dipati* of Madiun, Ponorogo (*Panaraga*), and Kediri (*Gedhiri*) all arrived at the new state, intending only to investigate, never suspecting that the Prince was seated in the audience hall. None of the *Bupati* failed to recognize Raden Jaka Suruh's appearance, though his body was worn and his garments ragged. All the *Dipati* wept and prostrated themselves at his feet. To shorten the narrative, subsequent details are omitted for brevity. The assembled *Dipati* were then sent to summon all the *Dipati* who had not yet presented themselves, and all the *Dipati* of the eastern region from Japara [p. 148.] eastwards came to pay homage. / Raden Jaka Suruh was established in the newly visible state, which was named the state of Majapahit. Jaka Suruh's royal title was *Sang Nata Prabu* Brawijaya Dewapati. The one who became *Patih* was the son of the *Patih* who had died fighting when the Kingdom of Pajajaran collapsed. He had fled to follow Raden Jaka Suruh, but failing to find him, he became an ascetic and was given the name *Adipati* Gajahmada. All the surviving *Bupati* and *Mantri* of Pajajaran who had escaped capture also arrived.

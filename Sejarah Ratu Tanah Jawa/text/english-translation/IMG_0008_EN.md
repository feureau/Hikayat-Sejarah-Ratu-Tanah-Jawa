The one who officiated the marriage was Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*), the guardian (*wali*) was 
[p. 24.] the Angel Jibrail (*peace be upon him*), and the witnesses were all the Angels carrying the garments. After the marriage ceremony was completed, they shook hands. Once they had shaken hands, the Angel Jibrail (*peace be upon him*) and the Angels carrying the garments vanished from the Masjid al-Haram, may God forgive them. The celestial nymph named Dewi Sakhiroh was thus established as the wife of Prophet Sis (*peace be upon him*). The story of the intimacy between Prophet Sis and his wife Dewi Sakhiroh states that it occurred only once. Then, by the grace of God, the Sublime, Dewi Sakhiroh became pregnant and gave birth to a son, bringing great joy to his grandfather Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*) and his grandmother Mother Eve (*may God be pleased with her*). The grandson was named Bagindho Jamal, and it was Bagindho Jamal who was destined by the Lord of the Worlds to become the seed of all the Prophets. The story of Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*) is that two of his sons were chosen by the will of God, the Sublime: one to succeed in prophethood, and the second to succeed 
[p. 25.] in the Night of Power (*Lailatul Qadar*). The meaning of succeeding in prophethood is that the prophethood of Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*) was succeeded by his son Prophet Sis (*peace be upon him*). The meaning of the Night of Power is that the kingdom of Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*) was succeeded by the son named Prabu Kayumutu. When the grandson of Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*) named Bagindho Jamal reached adulthood, it was the will of his grandfather Bagendho Jamal that he be married, in accordance with the will of the Lord of the Worlds, to the child of Prabu Kayumutu. The marriage between Bagindho Jamal and the child of Prabu Kayumutu was deeply harmonious. She then became pregnant and gave birth to a son, who was named by his great-grandfather Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*) as Bagindho Idris. The great-grandfather Prophet Adam and Mother Eve deeply loved 
[p. 26.] their great-grandson because of the great virtue of Bagindho Idris. The story of Bagindho Idris is that he was even given fifty books by the Lord of the Worlds. At that time, the age of Prophet Adam, the Caliph of God, during his decreed time in the world, was a thousand less fifty years. Then, it was the will of the Lord of the Worlds to forgive the sins of Prophet Adam, the Caliph of God, and he was summoned back to Paradise. The Angel Jibrail (*peace be upon him*) was sent down to summon Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*) to return to Paradise once more. Not long after, Mother Eve followed, returning to the glorious mercy of God. Remaining behind was Prophet Sis (*peace be upon him*), who remained established in his prophethood, and established in the kingdom was Prabu Kayumutu; these two succeeded Prophet Adam, the Caliph of God, ruling the world as willed by God, the Sublime, the Unique, the Subduer, may God forgive them, and God knows best. The story of Prophet Sis (*peace be upon him*) is that, 
[p. 27.] by the will of the Lord of the Worlds, he was unexpectedly dissatisfied because he was not like his father Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*), as his physical manifestation, willed by God, the Sublime, in the world, had not passed through a lowly path. Therefore, Prophet Sis (*peace be upon him*) departed to Jabal Sur, where Prophet Sis (*peace be upon him*) prayed to God, the Sublime, the Unique, the Subduer, the Mighty

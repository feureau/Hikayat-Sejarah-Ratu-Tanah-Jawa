## Text

# THIS IS THE HISTORY OF THE QUEEN OF JAVA.

*Written in Arabic script meaning the Javanese Kawi language.*

Stored secretly for decades by R.M. Saleh DIPONEGORO.  
From my father, R.M. Abdumuttalib, son of --  
Prince Abdurachman, son of Prince DIPONEGORO  
in Makassar.  

The one who kept it was R.M. Saleh DIPONEGORO.  
Custodian and Key Holder of the Tomb of the Hero --  
D I P O N E G O R O in Makassar. Jl. I R I A N No. 83.

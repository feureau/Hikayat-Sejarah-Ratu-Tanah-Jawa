The Regents and Ministers of Pajajaran moved to Majapahit. The one whose father died, his son succeeded to his title.

As for the history of the Majapahit state during the reign of the Monarch *Prabu* Brawijaya Dewapati, it was extremely prosperous in the eastern part of the land of Java. He was indeed well-matched with his *Patih*, *Kyai Adipati* Gajahmada, who was also [p. 149.] still / a young man. Thus, when the will of Brawijaya was settled to attack Pajajaran, *Ki Patih* Gajahmada and all the *Adipati* (Regents) were already prepared, only waiting for the command as everything was ready. Then regarding Pajajaran, *Prabu* Siungwanara had heard the news that his older brother reigned as king in the east, and all the great *Adipati* had indeed submitted completely. The name of the Majapahit state was renowned, as if it were not made by humans, its colors and all its forms were exceedingly beautiful. This made *Prabu* Siungwanara of Pajajaran extremely angry. He wanted to attack Majalengka (Majapahit), *Prabu* Siungwanara. Then *Prabu* Siungwanara ordered all the *Bupati* of the western region to be summoned together. They arrived, a multitude of armed forces ready for war. Then they were arranged by all the *empu* (master craftsmen) and /* [meladhang] */ *meladhang* (performers/officials), as they were the ones who commanded all the *Adipati*, because he did not trust all the *Adipati* in his heart. Thus *Prabu* Siungwanara and all the great *Adipati* were indeed half-hearted, for they all had heard [p. 150.] / the news that it was true their Lord had become the Monarch in Majapahit. So all the *Adipati* of the western region discussed and gathered, but for now they just went along. Thus, *Prabu* Siungwanara ordered the sounding of the signal. The *empu* and /* [meladhang] */ *meladhang* all commanded the *Adipati*, a multitude of great troops swarmed down from the mountains. *Prabu* Siungwanara had departed from the state of Pajajaran towards Majapahit. All of them, the number of the troops was countless, like an army of *wardahanggo*. Then in Majapahit, *Prabu* Brawijaya, the time had come for his desire to attack. They were already prepared, all the *Bupati* from the eastern region and Bali, the Kings of Klungkung, Buleleng (*Guleleng*), and Karangasem were already assembled in Majapahit with their troops and ready for war. Thus, *Prabu* Brawijaya came out and immediately ordered the sounding of the signal. *Ki Patih* Gajahmada marched with all the *Adipati*, an uncountable number of men. Those who were the vanguard were the three Kings of Bali, Klungkung, Buleleng, and [p. 151.] Karangasem. / Mandura and Surabaya supported the *Patih*, leading all the coastal regions. Madiun and Kediri (*Gedhiri*) then led all the outer regions (*mancanagara*). Then His Majesty *Prabu* Brawijaya was surrounded by all the great *Adipati* inside, and his ministers, /* [kyahi] */^ki *Patih* Gajahmada was not far from the Monarch. Thus, they arrived at the state of Ngastina, *Prabu* Brawijaya arranged the encampment. Then, *Prabu* Siungwanara of Pajajaran who was marching with his troops, felt and had been informed that the King of Majalengka had encamped with his troops in Ngastina. Then *Prabu* Siungwanara immediately ordered his vanguard troops to arrange their formation to attack in battle, led by *Empu* Jerampan and /* [meladhang] */ *meladhang*. They then clashed with all the *Adipati*. Those who became the vanguard were Cirebon (*Carabon*), Banten, Bawean, Sumedang.

After they shook hands, the Angel Jibrail (*peace be upon him*) spoke to Prophet Sis (*peace be upon him*), saying that Prophet Sis's request was no longer permitted if it was exactly like his own physical form, because in the world /* [world] */ it was already complete.

The request of [p. 28.] Prophet Sis (*peace be upon him*) was granted for an offspring (*yogo*) of his own kind, but in the intermediate realm (*ngalam antoro*). The meaning of the intermediate realm is above the *jinn* and below the Angels. He was given the title *Dewo* (God), and in the future, his descendants would be *Bathoro*. There would also be a descendant destined to establish a kingdom in the world, in the land of Java. But to be born in the world in the land of Arabia was no longer permitted by God, the Sublime.

After delivering the will of the Lord of the Worlds to Prophet Sis (*peace be upon him*), the Angel Jibrail (*peace be upon him*) shook hands and vanished. Then Prophet Sis (*peace be upon him*) prostrated to God, the Sublime. After rising from his prostration, the Prophet saw a light from the sky enter the Asthagina jewel box. When the Prophet opened the box, the essence had become a baby boy, and the baby was brought back to Prophet Sis (*peace be upon him*). The baby was given the name Yang Nurcahyo by the Prophet. /* [? Prophet Sis] */

After Yang Nurcahyo grew up, he was instructed by his father [p. 29.] Prophet Sis in all the commands of the Lord of the Worlds. Once Yang Nurcahyo clearly understood, he asked for permission from his father, Prophet Sis (*peace be upon him*), to leave and perform asceticism. After gaining his father's blessing, Yang Nurcahyo departed and ascended the mountain named Jabal Sur, still in the land of Mecca. Yang Nurcahyo intensified his asceticism there.

The end. The story of Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*), God knows best the truth, may God forgive them. God is He, O my Lord, O Forgiving (twice).-

Now, the story of the one who became the seed of all the creatures of God, the Sublime, the Unique, the Subduer, the Mighty and Majestic, all those called *dewa-dewi* (gods and goddesses) and *bathoro-bathari*; they are none other than the one destined to be the seed, namely Yang Nurcahyo.

The story of Yang Nurcahyo is that after he had long intensified his asceticism on Jabal Sur in the land of Mecca, he never wavered in his devotion to the Lord of the Worlds [p. 30.] throughout his time on Jabal Sur. Then Yang Nurcahyo had an offspring on Jabal Sur in the land of Mecca, a male who was named Yang Nurroso. After Yang Nurroso grew up, by the grace of God, the Sublime, for Yang Nurcahyo and his son Yang Nurroso, the Angel Jibrail (*peace be upon him*) descended to Jabal Sur, commanded to tell Yang Nurcahyo that he was no longer permitted to be in the land of Arabia.

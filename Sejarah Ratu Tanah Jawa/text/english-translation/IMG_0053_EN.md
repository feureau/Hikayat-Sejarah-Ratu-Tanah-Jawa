The one destined by God Almighty (*Gusti Allah Subhanahu Watangala*) to reign as the Caliph of the Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) and lead the holy war in the path of Allah was the eldest son of His Highness Sinuwun Sultan Raja, named: Sinuwun Sultan Ngabdul Khamid Erucakra Kabirul Mukminin Panatagama, Caliph of the Prophet Muhammad, the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, in the land of Java, *Senopati Ngalaga* of the holy war. At that time, the wars against the infidels, may God curse them, were fierce when clashing in battle with the Muslims. But when the Muslims clashed in battle, they were safe; sometimes there were no casualties, but many Muslims also became martyrs (*sahid*). in the land of Java, it is no longer allowed. Or if Muslims are drawn to make a *wayang* story, it is forbidden, because the Prophet Muhammad, the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, has prohibited it, meaning the command of Faith. As for what is called 'faith', the command contained in the Qur'an states that it is no longer allowed for Islam to be made into a *wayang* story or images, God willing (*insja Allah*), also because the contents of the land of Java from the time of Demak until now are only three things: humans, *iblis* (devils), and animals.

[p. 173.] As for the history of the land of Java when it was still / Buddhist, the end of which in Majapahit, the story was made using wooden *wayang*. It was called *Wayang Klithik*, starting from Majapahit /* [s] */^up to Pajajaran. The end of this became the story in the land of Java, which were made as embodiments of all the people in the land of Java, big, small, bad, good, those who ended up fortunate or those who ended up miserable. These were what became imitations for all Javanese people.

As for the history from Pajajaran up to Jenggala, the story was made using leather *wayang*, named *Wayang Gedhog*. Its story was no different from the deeds of /* [xxxxxxx] */ ^Panadaran (the Pandavas?) and all humans. Then in Jenggala /* [xxxxxx] */ ^beginning with Resi Gethayu up to *Prabu* Suwela Cala, that is *Sang Yang* Sindhula, the story was made using *wayang* drawn on *daluwang* (bark paper) like a picture. Its name was *Wayang Purwa Beber*. It was named so because the beginning (*purwa*) of the presence of humans in the land of Java was His Highness Sultan of Rum. They were of the Christian (*Srani*) religion, because they followed the Sultan of Rum.

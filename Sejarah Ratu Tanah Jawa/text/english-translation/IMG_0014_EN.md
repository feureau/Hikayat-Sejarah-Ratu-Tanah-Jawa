[p. 45.] by the son, Sang Yang Padmanaba, to his heavenly abode in Jenggala manik. This caused Sang Catur Baja to become extremely angry with Batara Surya and with the son, Batara Wisnu. He summoned his older brother, Sang Yang Kaneka putra, and ordered Yang Narada to banish Batara Wisnu from Suralaya to the mortal realm (*Marcapada*), and commanded that Dewi Sri be separated from him. When Batara Narandra arrived in the mortal realm bringing Batara Wisnu, he placed him on a small mountain. In that small mountain, there was a cave of immense supernatural power called Guwa Upas (Poison Cave). Batara Narada then ordered Batara Wisnu to perform asceticism in that cave. As for Dewi Sri, she was placed on Gunung Lampit, where there was also a cave called Guwa Wesi (Iron Cave). Her uncle, Batara Narada, ordered Dewi Sri to perform asceticism in that cave. After both were settled, Sang Yang Kaneka putra returned to Suralaya. The story of Sang Yang Wisnu Batara 
[p. 46.] is that while in the mortal realm, having been banished from Suralaya and separated from his wife Dewi Sri, he never emerged from Guwa Upas. He sat on a shining stone (*Selagilang*), constantly weeping and pleading to Sang Yang Wiji Wasesa and to the Sang Yang who guards all human seeds. He sat cross-legged, his right arm crossed over his chest, his left arm covering his left calf, holding his right toe, his soul subtly focused, surrendering his true self to the immaterial. Thus, he united with the divine essence of Sang Yang Wasesa. Batara Wisnu became afflicted, not just an ordinary illness. Sang Yang Padmanaba and Sang Yang Wiji Wasesa became one with Sang Yang Amisesa. Likewise, Batara Wisnu's wife, Dewi Sri, inside Guwa Wesi, was no different from her husband in her actions, because this affliction was destined by God, the Sublime, 
[p. 47.] the Unique, the Subduer, the Mighty and Majestic, to be the divine mandate (*wahyu*) for the worldly kingdom born in the land of Java. It was Batara Wisnu who was referred to as the "treasure without a master" (*masa tanpa una*) until the end of times. The kingdom of Java will not lack successors because it is granted by the Lord of the Worlds, but Sang Yang Sri Sadana is destined to possess the land of Java. Batara Brama's son served as the vessel, while Batara Wisnu served as the contents. If Brama is the body, Wisnu is the soul; if Brama is the flower, Wisnu is its fragrance. Sang Yang Wisnu serves as the Night of Power (*Lailatul Qadar*) in the land of Java. Thus ends the story of the intermediate realm (*ngalam Antara*), and God knows best the truth. The story of the land of Java is that the first kingdom was in Gilingwesi, and its king was Batara Brama's son named Bremani. Bremani fathered Trikanan; Trikanan fathered Manumanungsa; Manumanungsa fathered Manik Mahadewa.

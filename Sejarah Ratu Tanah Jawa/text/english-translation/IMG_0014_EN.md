After Sang Yang Nurcahyo's kingdom in the intermediate realm was established—the country of Ngondar-andir buwono, the palace of Jonggring saloko, and the meeting hall of Bale si Mercu Kundho—every Monday and Thursday, Sang Yang Nurcahyo and his son Sang Yang Nuroso held court at Bale si Mercu kundho, attended by all the *dewo*, with Yang Baruno acting as his Chief Minister (*Patih*).

As time passed, Sang Yang Nuroso matured into an assertive and forceful figure; he did not hesitate to challenge his grandfather and father. He was named Sang Yang Wenang, and was fully capable and supported in all his desires as a commander of war, showing no fear toward his grandfather Sang Yang Nurcahyo or his father Sang Yang Nuroso.

Over time, Sang Yang Wenang created Heaven (*suwargo*) and Hell (*naroko*), offering them to his grandfather [p. 35.] Sang Yang Nurcahyo and his father Sang Yang Nuroso. [p. 36.] However, Sang Yang Wenang was no longer permitted [to keep them], causing distress to his grandfather and father, as they realized they had incurred the wrath of the Lord of the Worlds.

Every day, the *dewa mambang* worked to construct Hell, which was named Hendhut si Bale Gedobo. Along its edges, they planted *suket sulanjono* (sharp grass), and this grass was made of all kinds of weapons. Above Hendhut si Bale Gedobo, they placed a bridge, made of an unsteady iron plank called *wesi wot si hogal-hagil*. The gate leading to Heaven was made of stone that could open and close on its own, called Lawang Selo Matangkep. Heaven was named Tejomoyo.

Hell was the place for punishing those who were cast out, while Heaven was the dwelling place of all the *Widodari* (celestial nymphs). The *Widodari* were created from the children of the *mambang*

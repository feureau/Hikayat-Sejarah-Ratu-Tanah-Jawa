### [Folio 151]
17. Then His Highness *Prabu* Brawijaya Dewapati departed, arriving at the state of Sumedang, Karawang, and Pasundan. He then arranged to sound the signal. Thus, Brawijaya ordered Gajahmada to command the battle. Then immediately the *Patih* sounded the battle signal. And so, the three Kings of Bali, Mandura, Surabaya, Madiun, and Kediri arranged themselves, and the sound thundered sounding the signal.

### [Folio 152]

3. his troops, leaving only Pu Caro and about two thousand men. When the battle formation broke,
4. their amok charge was directed towards the King, for they had no intention of retreating.
5. So the ones who withstood them were the three Kings of Bali. They immediately
6. clashed, meeting the bravery of the Balinese which was exceedingly steadfast and victorious in combat.

8. the *pu jero* and *meladhang*, when they met, how much strength could they have?

10. *pu jero* and *meladhang* were destroyed, their movements thrashing about, whoever they encountered was destroyed.

### [Folio 153]
1. *Kyai Patih* was then ordered to make all the *Adipati*
2. retreat. They retreated. Then the King Dewapati met with *Prabu*
3. Siungwanara. All the *Adipati* were ordered only to surround
4. them. Both of them were brave with weapons, no one was wounded. They stabbed and
5. lifted each other, but Siungwanara fell behind and was trampled; *Prabu* Siungwanara
6. could no longer fight back. Thus *Prabu* Siungwanara of Pajajaran perished. But
7. his corpse then vanished. Thus *Prabu* Brawijaya Dewapati of Majapahit then
8. sat in the great pavilion. *Patih* Gajahmada was in front. On
9. the right were the *Adipati*, the three Kings of Bali, Mandura, and Surabaya, Madiun, Kediri.
10. On the left of *Ki Patih* Gajahpramada were Banten and Bawean,
11. Cirebon, Karawang, Sumedang. Behind were all the *Adipati* of the western coast,
12. and Pasundan adjoining the eastern coast and the outer regions (*Mancanagara*). After
13. all the *Adipati* of the western region had paid their respects and wept,
14. their hearts felt like a dream; after all forms of things.

### [Folio 154]
1. Thus *Prabu* Brawijaya Dewapati's will was that *Ki Patih* Gajahmada
2. was sent straight to Pajajaran, all the *Adipati* of the western region were ordered to follow.
3. The King's will for Pajajaran was ordered to be emptied... moving to the coast
4. only, to Betawi, ordered to build a fort there.
5. The Monarch was the one who made the conditions and bestowed the title *Adipati* Siungwanara,
6. and ordered to take the heirloom *Patih* and all possessions of Brawijaya.
7. Thus, when the will of the Monarch was completed, His Highness Brawijaya returned to the encampment.

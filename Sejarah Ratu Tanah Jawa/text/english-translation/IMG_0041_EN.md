Thus his inner thought was: "Truly beautiful /* [the three of them] */ ... Prabu Rara, pure and hopefully I will be granted that Prabu Rara is blinded and does not recognize me [p. 123.] there."

Then the King of Jenggala entered the sacred shrine, while his servants remained outside. The scent of Sri Nalendra's visibility oil (*lisah jayeng Katong*) was fragrant. He then stood before Sang Prabu Wanadya. It was indeed clear that he was a human man, truly the noble of Banjaransari, because the celestial nymph (*widadari*) was blinded and did not recognize the sign, and she was not allowed as a woman to associate equally with men. Then Sang Prabu Wanadya did not suspect that the human scent belonged to a mortal, but truly thought it was a God (*Dewa*). Thus Prabu Wanadya then approached the source of the scent, sitting down gracefully and paying homage to Yang Siwah (Shiva), perhaps the supreme God among Gods, appearing properly before her eyes. The King of Jenggala smiled, but only within his heart. Then the King of Jenggala answered: "Yes, I accept your words." Thus when the King of Galuh heard the words of the deity (*jawata*), her heart became exceedingly cool, and he then spoke softly: "Well, what is it, granddaughter... calling the god, so quickly I came as your incense rose." Thus Sang [p. 124.] Prabu Rara / gently spoke: "Grandfather, who are you, and please reveal yourself?" The visitor gently said: "My grandchild, I am a deity, if I reveal myself it is no longer allowed, because of your human nature. If you have a wish, come, tell me, perhaps my advice can bring relief to your heart." Then Prabu Rara said: "Lord God, why do you ask when you do not know the feelings of the heart?" The visitor smiled and spoke: "I am here, grandchild, merely sent by Yang Giri Nata to inquire of you, what your request is, ordered only to report." Thus Sang Prabu Rara gently replied: "How is it, grandfather God, I am now human, and the kingdom I guard is waiting for the one who owns the realm, unfortunately he has not truly arrived, I merely wait. If he arrives, I only wish to ask permission from the owner and clarify as man to woman, then my heart will be at peace." Then the visitor spoke: "Who is the one you are waiting for, what is his name?" Prabu Rara replied: [p. 125.] "I am waiting for the King of Java, how could I expect anyone else, grandfather God." Thus the visitor spoke: "If that is your wish, my grandchild, it is simple enough, but first I ask for a sign, then I will tell you the location of the King of Java, your ring shall be used as a proper means." Thus it was handed over, then he held the King's hand tightly, and she spoke: "Why does the God touch me, taking and squeezing my hand so that it hurts?" The visitor laughed and spoke: "Do not be angry, grandchild, don't be perturbed, it is done." Meanwhile, those outside, Manawi and Manawa, and all the jinn, with abundant offerings, chose whatever they desired. Then the three maids spoke: "Is this a God who likes to eat like humans?" Manawi and Manawa replied: "I am a new God, that is why I still like to eat." The three maids asked: "What are your names, grandfather God?" Manawi and Manawa replied: "Yang Thuwukkan, Yang Bungkukkan." The three maids laughed: "Ni Thuwukkan, Ni Bungkukkan, are they all female?" "No, I am male, my dear."

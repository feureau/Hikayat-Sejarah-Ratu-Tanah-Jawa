Then his young *Waragana* consort bore a male child named Raden Gogor. The two sons born of the *Waragana* consorts caused no disturbance in the palace; they were Raden Udara and Raden Gogor (*Geger*). Both then asked permission from their father to undertake ascetic practices, which was granted by their father. Raden Udara practiced asceticism on Mount Mahameru in the Segala-gala Cave, becoming an ascetic titled *Ajar* Tunggul Manik. Through his intense practice, he was able to project his spirit and possessed true foresight. His younger brother, Raden Gogor, practiced asceticism on Mount Ngargapura at the site of his royal father's ancient meditation. Through his severe practice, he vanished together with his physical body, and Raden Gogor [p. 159.] became one with his grandmother *Kangjeng Ratu* Pagetdhongan, / reigning as king of the male jinn, titled Sunan Lawu. As for the sons of *Prabu* Brawijaya Dewapati, only one remained, Raden Alip, who was appointed *Pangeran Dipati*. At that time, a dispute arose between a Deer and a Bison, brought before *Patih* Gajahmada. When questioned, the Deer reported: "If my child was killed by being trampled by the Bison, I ask for justice, *Sang Dipatya*." Then *Sang Dipati* Gajahmada said: "Hey Bison, you are found guilty under justice, you must surely be executed." The Bison replied: "Oh Lord, how can that be? The reason was that I was startled and accidentally trampled the fawn to death, because the lions appeared together. I was terrified, and I submit to your will." *Sang Adipati* Gajahmada then sent messengers to summon all the lions. When summoned before him, *Sang Adipati* Gajahpramada said: "Hey Lions, you are found guilty under justice, for you caused the death from beginning to end." The Lions replied: "Oh Lord, how can that be? All my companions were weeping, [p. 160.] because the dragonfly (*kinjeng*) was dancing / together; we submit to your will." So all the dragonflies were ordered to be summoned, and when they arrived, the same judgment was delivered to them as to the lions. The dragonflies replied that it was because the spider (*kemlandhingan*) set a trap. The spider was summoned and arrived, explaining when charged that it was because the firefly (*konang*) was flying about. All the fireflies were summoned and arrived, explaining when charged: "It was because the minnows (*waderpari*) were lined up in battle formation; we submit to your will." *Sang Dipati* Gajahmada said: "If that is the case, it is best that I go and inspect the minnows myself, because minnows [p. 161.] cannot walk on land." / The *Dipati* then proceeded to the river where the minnows were located, accompanied by all the judges (*jaksa*) and legal officials (*madata*). When *Sang Dipati* Gajahmada arrived at the river where the minnows were lined up, *Patih* Gajahmada questioned them: "Why are you minnows lined up in formation? Now calamity [p. 162.] has come upon you." The minnows replied: "We will cease our formation. Truly, Lord, all our companions are in fear because the crab (*yuyu*) is extremely unruly. If any companion is caught, it preys on them, and its disturbance constantly ruins everything." *Sang Dipatya* Gajahmada said: "Where is the crab now? Call it." The minnows replied: "Oh Lord, we are afraid we will surely be eaten, for its lair is extremely narrow, and it sets traps; your servant would surely die." Thus *Sang Adipati* sent the otter (*lingsang*), ordering it to summon the crab.

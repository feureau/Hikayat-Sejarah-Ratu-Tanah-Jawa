Thus it is told that Sri Naranata of Jenggala sat in the encampment, attended by all the Dukes and village elders (*buyut*) thronging together with Satma's children and grandchildren, whose hearts were all like mountains. I suspect, father, tomorrow the warriors and the Maiden King (*Prabu Rara*) will come out to battle, while I will just observe. As for you and mother, father, and Manawa and Manawi, you all just attend to me."

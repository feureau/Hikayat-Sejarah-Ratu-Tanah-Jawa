Bathoro Nilokontho because of his white neck; and Bathoro Catur Bojo because of his four arms; and Bathoro Guru Noto because he was a king who was also an ascetic. Sang Yang Papado was created by the will of his father, Sang Yang Tunggal, as a subordinate, and was given the name Bathoro Kaneko Putro, because he served as the benchmark; and Bathoro Narodo, because his vision was clear and unobstructed. After delivering his commands, Sang Yang Tunggal vanished from si Mercu kundho and returned to Ngendro sampurno. Those remaining in audience at Bale si Mercu kunda were Sang Yang Giri Nata and his older brother Sang Yang Kaneko Putro, along with all the gods and *mambang* assembled before him; after all this, Yang Guru no longer held court. Over time, Yang Guru had an eldest son named Batara Brama, then Batara Endra, then Batara Basuki, and the youngest was Batara Wisnu, who was the most handsome, 
[p. 42.] his skin shining like gold. He was the most beloved by his father and was destined to succeed him as king. There was another son of Yang Nila Kantho, but his role was not to guard like the other four sons; he was the offspring with his wife Yang Batari Durga, named Batara Kala, and he had the appearance of a giant (*buta*). Yang Kaneko also had an eldest son, Batara Surya; his younger brother Batara Bayu; his younger brother Batara Sambu; then Batara Mahadewa; and the youngest, Batara Yamadipati, who also had the appearance of a giant and was very imposing. Whenever humans suffered severe illness and performed intense asceticism, roasting themselves and causing disturbances in the mortal realm (*Marcapada*), they were taken by Yang Nurada. Mayoretna and Bisawarna were brought to Suralaya and were taken as guards by Sang Yang Jagatnata, and both were very handsome. Majoratna was given the name Batara Asmara, and Bisawarna was given the name Batara Kamajaya. Then came the will of Sang Yang Tunggal: Batara Guru was ordered to seed a king in the mortal realm, in the land of Java. The daughter of Batara Brama was chosen 
[p. 43.] by Sang Yang Tunggal, and by the will of Sang Yang Wisesa; her name was Yang Bremani, and she was then sent down to the mortal realm. Yang Narada was ordered to escort her, along with all the gods and the children, to the mortal realm. Gilingwesi was the destination chosen by Sang Yang Narada to become the kingdom. Once it was established, Yang Bremani was crowned as monarch by Sang Yang Kaneka-putra, and all humans on the left and right of Gilingwesi bowed down. After order was established, Yang Narada and all the gods vanished and returned to Suralaya. Yang Bremani remained securely as the monarch in the land of Java, in her kingdom of Gilingwesi. In Suralaya, it is told that Yang Guru possessed an excess of females, such that there were no longer any peers in Suralaya or the mortal realm. If there was a peer, it came from the fruit of the *kastuba* tree; one was picked by Yang Nilikanta and split into a celestial nymph (*widodari*) whose complexion sparkled like a diamond, but she was still small and not yet of age. She was given the name 
[p. 44.] Dewi Sri by Batara Guru, and she was then entrusted to Batara Surya because she was still small. Batara Guru could not bear to look at the beauty of Dewi Sri, which is why she was entrusted to Batara Surya. Yang Guru's most handsome son, named Yang Batara Padmanaba, often played at the residence of Batara Surya.

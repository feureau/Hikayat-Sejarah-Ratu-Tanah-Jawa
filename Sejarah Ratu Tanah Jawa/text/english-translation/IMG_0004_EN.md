As for Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*), the reason he received the forgiveness of the Lord of the Worlds was because the Prophet had genuinely forgotten for a moment about his enemy, Iblis, may God curse him, from whom we seek refuge in God. For a moment, he believed the words of his wife, Mother Eve. He was punished only because of his negligence in obeying his wife's words, may God forgive them. At that time, Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*), Mother Eve (*may God be pleased with her*), and the dragon wept profusely, repenting and begging for forgiveness to God, the Sublime, the Unique, the Subduer, the Mighty and Majestic, while they each blamed themselves. As the story goes, the forgiveness of God, the Sublime, the Unique, the Subduer, was granted to Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*), Mother Eve (*may God be pleased with her*), 
[p. 12.] and the dragon. Then the Angel Jibrail (*peace be upon him*) descended to the world carrying the grace of God, the Sublime, the Unique, the Subduer, as well as bringing garments from Paradise for Prophet Adam and Mother Eve. Upon arriving in the world, the Angel Jibrail (*peace be upon him*) went straight to Yemen, where the dragon was. He informed the dragon that it had been forgiven, but by the will of the Lord of the Worlds, it must continually protect Adam and serve as his staff, until the time comes for him to return to Paradise. The dragon immediately prostrated in gratitude and submission to the command of God, the Sublime. After prostrating, the dragon turned into an incredibly beautiful staff, shining like the *kastuba* wood. The staff was then brought by the Angel Jibrail (*peace be upon him*) to Mount Surandhil in the land of Ceylon. As for Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*), during his punishment by God, the Sublime, 
[p. 13.] he was sent down from Paradise to the world, placed on top of Mount Surandhil, sitting on a shining stone (*selo gilang*). Day and night, the Prophet constantly wept, blaming himself, repenting, and begging for forgiveness from the Lord of the Worlds. At that time, right after the Prophet had completed the Dawn (*Subuh*) prayer at the true dawn (*fajar sidiq*), he prostrated on the shining stone and wept until the stone was wet. When the sun rose, the Angel Jibrail (*peace be upon him*) arrived while Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*) was still in prostration. The Angel Jibrail (*peace be upon him*) offered greetings of peace to Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*). The Prophet was greatly startled, and he rose from his prostration. Then Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*) shook hands with the Angel Jibrail (*peace be upon him*). After shaking hands, the Angel Jibrail informed Prophet Adam that his sins had been forgiven by God, the Sublime, but Prophet Adam's time in the world was decreed for a thousand years. After a thousand years, Prophet Adam would be summoned back to Paradise. He was given garments from 
/* [Mecca] */
Paradise and the dragon staff from Paradise to serve as his tool in the world. 
[p. 14.] Furthermore, Prophet Adam was ordered to go to Husnia Malebari, which meant to perform the Hajj there, where Prophet Adam would reunite with Mother Eve. Prophet Adam then prostrated in gratitude to God, the Sublime. After prostrating, the Prophet put on the garments and wielded the dragon staff from Paradise.

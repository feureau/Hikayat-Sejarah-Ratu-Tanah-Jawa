## Text

[p. 1.] In the Name of God, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. Praise be to God, Lord of the Worlds. The Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. Master of the Day of Judgment. And the consequence is for the pious. This is the story and history of when the Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*) was punished by God, the Sublime, the Unique, the Subduer. This occurred because of the deceit of Iblis, may God curse him. This happened through his wife, Mother Eve (*may God be pleased with her*). The Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*) obeyed the words of his wife, Mother Eve. Mother Eve, in turn, obeyed the words of Iblis, may God curse him, who offered her to eat the fruit of *khuldi* (eternity), and Mother Eve complied because Iblis, may God curse him, pretended to be an angel who claimed to be sent by God, the Sublime, the Unique, the Subduer, accompanied by many oaths from Iblis. Thus it happened that Mother Eve ate [p. 2.] the fruit of *khuldi*.

Now, the fruits of the forbidden *khuldi* tree in Paradise were only three, one of which was offered to her husband, the Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*), leaving only one fruit of *khuldi*. The Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*) was greatly startled when he was offered the fruit of *khuldi* by his wife, Mother Eve. Because the Prophet Adam had already been warned by God, the Sublime, that the entirety of Paradise was permissible, except for one thing that the Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*) must not approach by order of the Lord of the Worlds, which was the *khuldi* tree. The Prophet was not allowed to eat its fruit, and unless permitted, even playing near the *khuldi* tree was forbidden. Then Mother Eve spoke to the Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*), saying that it was the will of the Lord of the Worlds, and the angel who was sent delivered the command accompanied by many oaths. Thus the Prophet obeyed his wife's words and ate the *khuldi* fruit.

[p. 3.] When the Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*) partook of it and realized the truth, he saw that there was no Prophet, Angel, or *Wali* (saint) taking an oath. And if someone had indeed been sent to summon him, it would have been conveyed to the Prophet. Thus, great was the remorse and regret of the Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*), feeling deeply ashamed before God, the Sublime, because the Prophet had obeyed his wife. He then told Mother Eve to summon the Angel who claimed to have been sent. When Mother Eve looked for the Angel, he was no longer there. Mother Eve then told the Prophet that the Angel was gone. The Prophet immediately wept, for he knew that it was Iblis in disguise. Mother Eve also wept, realizing that they had both been deceived, and they begged for forgiveness from God, the Sublime, but it was too late. Then the wrath of the Lord of the Worlds fell upon Prophet Adam and Mother Eve, the sign of which was that all their fine garments vanished, as well as the crown and ornamentation of Mother Eve. Both were left naked without any clothing, so the Prophet and his wife wept wherever they stood.

[p. 4.] Then, regarding all the contents of Paradise, the Prophet and his wife Mother Eve were forbidden from taking even the leaves of the trees, and even if they sought shelter underneath, all the trees of Paradise would move away. When asked, the trees answered that they feared God, the Sublime. At that time, the Prophet and his wife suffered greatly; they were both punished in Paradise by the Lord of the Worlds, standing silently and weeping without their garments. There was a tree in Paradise named the *"anjir"* (fig) tree, which felt great pity for the Prophet

- 53 -

Thus, all the people of Rum, upon arriving in the land of Java, changed their religion because they followed their King. King Sindhula was indeed of the Buddhist religion, and the mention of the Buddhist religion was only /* [teka] */ *Dewa Batara*. Yes, the people of Rum became the seed of humans in the land of Java until now. That is why it is called *Wayang Purwa Beber*, because the people of Rum became the seed of all humans in the land of Java until now. Then, from *Sang Yang* Sindhula until the intermediate realm (*ngalam antara*), the story was made using leather *wayang*. Its name was also *Wayang Purwa*, but it was the *Wayang Purwa* of the Gods experiencing the intermediate realm. This was what was followed and used as a designation for all the people in the land of Java, called the Buddhist Religion in the land of Java, from the intermediate realm until its end in Majapahit with the Buddhist religion for the people in the land of Java. The end, finished is the history
/* [colophon boxed in pen] */
[p. 175.] of the Kings in the land of / ^Java, as well as what became the seed of all the people in the land of Java. Its history, God willing (*insja'Allah*), is nothing other than God knows best (*wallahu 'alam bissawab ghofarolahum allahu ya ghofur3*). This is the end of copying the book of the history of the Kings of Java on Wednesday night, at nine o'clock, the fifth day, in the month of Dulkaidah, the year Jim, the Hijrah of the Prophet (peace be upon him) one thousand two hundred and fifty-three, Javanese Hijrah one thousand seven hundred and sixty-five. Written in Makassar.

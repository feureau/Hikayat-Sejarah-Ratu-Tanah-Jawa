- 26 -

Laknatullah did not leave Aji Saka, for they could not be separated, the two of them like a stone and its mold, Mulaikat Ngijajil Laknatullah and Aji Saka wherever they were. It had been destined by God Allah Subhanahu Wata' 'Ala, the Wahidulkohar, the 'Aza Wajalla, that Aji Saka was destined to become Iblis Laknatullah because he had been struck by the words of the Prophet Mohamad /* [ Sallallohu */ Rosulullah ['Alaihi Wasalam. Finished. Wallahu 'alam bisawab ghofarolahum ya Allah ya Robi ya Ghofur3.

As for the history in Mendang after the disappearance of Aji Saka, Raden Putra Daniswara then became King in Mendang, confirmed by his grandfather Sang Yang T Ratu Sindulo, with his Patih Dipati Mangkurat. The two Waraganas firmly became his wives, Sangkaningrat and Mayangkara. He was given a title by his grandfather: Raden Putra Daniswara became Sang Prabu Kaskaya. His Patih was Jaka Jugulmuda, given the title Adipati Jugulmuda. Pun Beja and Daulat became the Judges, given the titles Raja [p. 87.] Suryangalam (Daulat) and Raja Kapa2 (Beja). Pacukilan was given the title Raja Nititelu, answering to Patih Jugulmuda. Then Adipati Jugulmuda was given a wife, the daughter of Adipati Tugur, her mother was named Larasati. At that time, Mendang was extremely lively, all the glory of the world was there. Nata Ratu Sindulo still watched over his grandson in Mendang with his Patih at the wedding of Dipati Jugulmuda. After Adipati Jugulmuda was married, Nata Sindulo and his Patih Dipati Mangkurat returned to Guwa Terusan to farm. The Land of Java was extremely prosperous during the reign of Sang Prabu Kaskaya; all the people in the Land of Java were joyful. Sang Prabu Kaskaya had children with the two Waraganas. Sangkaningrat had a son named Sri Mapunggung. Mayangkara had a daughter named Suprabawati, who was taken by her great-grandfather Ratu Sindulo and brought to Guwa Terusan, Dewi Suprabawati to her great-grandfather. [p. 88.]

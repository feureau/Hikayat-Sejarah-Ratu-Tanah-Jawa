It is a sign that there is a hidden essence of Allah. Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*) was created as the manifestation of the attributes of Allah, because by the will of the Lord of the Worlds, Prophet Adam was sent down from Paradise to the world so that God, the Sublime, could make him the seed of all creatures in the world possessing human attributes, and he was given the name Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*) by the Lord of the Worlds. Prophet Adam, the Caliph of God, firmly established himself in Mecca with his wife Mother Eve. The story of Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*) relates that his work in Mecca was forging all kinds of tools for the world, with the Angel Jibrail (*peace be upon him*) serving as his assistant. Once all the tools in the world were made, the Angel Jibrail (*peace be upon him*) vanished. The story of 
[p. 18.] Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*) tells that every time Mother Eve (*may God be pleased with her*) gave birth, she bore twins, a male and a female. The firstborn was named Qabil, then Habil, then Kayumutu, while the younger ones' names are not mentioned. After the children of Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*) reached adulthood, a disagreement arose between Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*) and his wife Mother Eve (*may God be pleased with her*). Prophet Adam wished for the children's marriages to be cross-matched, while Mother Eve wished for the children to be married to their respective twins with whom they were born. This was a sign ordained by the Lord of the Worlds that these were their destined partners, and altering it would only cause distress. However, both Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*) and his wife Mother Eve (*may God be pleased with her*) were stubborn; neither was willing to concede to the other, as both claimed authority. Consequently, the dispute 
[p. 19.] and disagreement between Prophet Adam and his wife resulted in them making a pact: whoever's essence proved to have the true authority would determine the fate of the baby. Then both Prophet Adam and his wife brought forth their essences, which were placed separately in the Asthagina jewel boxes (*cupu manik hasthogino*). Both Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*) and Mother Eve (*may God be pleased with her*) then prayed to God, the Sublime, the Unique, the Subduer, asking whose will should govern the child, hoping God, the Sublime, would give a sign by turning one of their essences into a baby. When the agreed time came to open the boxes, Prophet Adam's (*peace be upon him*) essence had become a baby boy, while Mother Eve's essence had only become a clot of blood. Mother Eve then repented to Prophet Adam and to the Lord of the Worlds. This was a sign that Mother Eve could no longer oppose whatever Prophet Adam (*peace be upon him*) willed, may God forgive them.

He then stood before Sang Prabu Wanadya. It was indeed clear that he was a human man, truly the noble of Banjaransari, because the celestial nymph (*widadari*) was blinded and did not recognize the sign, and she was not allowed as a woman to associate equally with men. Then Sang Prabu Wanadya did not suspect that the human scent belonged to a mortal, but truly thought it was a God (*Dewa*). Thus Prabu Wanadya then approached the source of the scent, sitting down gracefully and paying homage to Yang Siwah (Shiva), perhaps the supreme God among Gods, appearing properly before her eyes. The King of Jenggala smiled, but only within his heart. Then the King of Jenggala answered: "Yes, I accept your words."

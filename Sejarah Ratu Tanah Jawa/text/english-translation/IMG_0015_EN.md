The kingdom of Manik Mahadewa in Gilingwesi was destroyed, and Prabu Manik Mahadewa 
[p. 48.] died and became a giant (*buta*). The giant was named Getih Banjaran; his wife, Dewi Sulastri, was heavily pregnant. Dewi Sulastri fled and was not caught by the giant. She ran into the forest and entered a cave. The cave happened to be called Guwa Wesi. The only things Dewi Sulastri took with her when she fled were three heirlooms: the Sarutama arrow, the Kalomisani keris, and the Aji Cupu Manik. Upon arriving in the cave, Dewi Sulastri gave birth to a son, assisted by all the *Widodari* (celestial nymphs). The healthy baby was named Raden Sutopo by Sang Yang Kanekoputra, because the baby immediately performed intense asceticism in Guwa Wesi. When he reached adulthood, he married and fathered a son named Raden Sakri. When he reached adulthood, he married and fathered Raden Sakutrem. Raden Sakutrem fathered Raden Palasari, who fathered Raden Abiyasa. Abiyasa 
[p. 49.] fathered Pandu Dewanata, who became the King in Ngastina. Pandu Dewanata fathered Sang Ngarjuna. Sang Ngarjuna fathered Sang Angkawijaya. Sang Angkawijaya fathered Parigesit. Parigesit fathered Gendrayana. Gendrayana fathered Udayana. Udayana fathered Kandihawan. Kandihawan fathered Jayamisena. Jayamisena fathered Jayamijaya. Jayamijaya fathered Prabu Selacala. This Prabu Selacala was named Sang Yang Prabu Sindula, handsome and young. He was untouched by old age, but his flaw was that he lacked literature. This was the will of God, the Sublime, the Unique, the Subduer, the Mighty and Majestic. At that time, the land of Java received the wrath of the Lord of the Worlds and was submerged. Submerged means it was wiped clean; there were no humans left. They all transformed into supernatural beings (*berkasakan*), some becoming fierce forest animals. The only one remaining was Sang Yang Prabu Sindula, by the will of God, the Sublime, who was left behind to perform asceticism inside Guwa Terusan 
[p. 50.] because he was the seed of the Kings of the land of Java. Bermani also remained, guarded alone by the Lord of the Worlds. However, Guwa Terusan became like Surabaya, but its flaw was that there were no humans or any other beings. Thus ends the ancient history (*purwa*), and God knows best the truth, may God forgive them. Now, the story of the Sultan of Rum, who received a divine revelation from God, the Sublime, the Unique, the Subduer, the Mighty and Majestic. The Sultan of Rum then summoned all the sea captains (*nakoda*), because the Sultan of Rum was ordered to populate the land of Java with people by the will of the Lord of the Worlds. The Sultan of Rum did not yet know the location of the land called Java. That is why he summoned all the sea captains, because they travel far and wide, in case any of them knew of the land of Java. All the sea captains informed the Sultan of Rum that they knew of the land called Java, but it was empty, devoid of humans, and no one dared to venture there because it was very terrifying. The Sultan of Rum then requested 
[p. 51.] twenty thousand families of people, and ordered them to prepare. After the twenty thousand families were prepared, they were all ordered to depart for the land of Java. Upon arriving in the land of Java, the twenty thousand families began to build their homes.

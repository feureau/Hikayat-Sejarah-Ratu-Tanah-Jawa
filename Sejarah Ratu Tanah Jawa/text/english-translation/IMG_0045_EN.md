2. the Commander (*Senapati*) in Madiyun. Do not let any of you waver regarding his destiny. His
3. twenty male servants will become the servants of Majenun, he is his servant,

5. yes, if you are off guard you will not know his servant Majenun. It is not limited to age;
6. whether old or young in appearance, day and night, you will not be able to recognize
7. Majenun. Because he can become a child or an old man, taking on the illnesses and
8. all the diseases of the people of the world, all gathered in Majenun.
9. Yet he is patient when spoken to harshly, and it is his habit to remain cool
10. without a word or retaliation, only speaking in his heart. Be very careful,
11. do not be deceived, because Majenun is the companion of life
12. and the water of life. I suspect there will be no further mistake concerning Majenun."

[p. 137.] "O Pangeran of Kudus, to you he is a son, along with the Commander (*Senapati*) in Madiyun. Do not let any of you waver regarding his destiny. His twenty male servants will become the servants of Majenun, he is his servant, yes, if you are off guard you will not know his servant Majenun, he is his servant, yes, if you are off guard you will not know his servant Majenun. It is not limited to age; whether old or young in appearance, day and night, you will not be able to recognize Majenun. Because he can become a child or an old man, taking on the illnesses and all the diseases of the people of the world, all gathered in Majenun. Yet he is patient when spoken to harshly, and it is his habit to remain cool without a word or retaliation, only speaking in his heart. Be very careful, do not be deceived, because Majenun is the companion of life and the water of life. I suspect there will be no further mistake concerning Majenun." Then Sunan Kalijaga said: "Indeed, Pangeran is right, stemming from the will of Jeng Yang. It is already the fixed intention of Sunan Cirebon /* ^that */ he does not wish the King of Demak [p. 138.] Bintara, along with Sunan of Giri, to be overly troubled in their hearts regarding Pangeran of Surabaya. So if it is your will, do not search for the right outcome." Then /* Sang */ Sunan of Giri smiled and softly said: "Therefore, do not be hasty. It is already the destiny of Yang Wasesa that the King of Bintara will lose two things now /* xxxxxxx */ for there is no shortage to replace the King of Jenggala, yes, because Uncle /* [x] */ indeed did not obey /* ^i */ the warning of the King of Bintara." 

Thus Sunan Kalijaga and Pangeran of Kudus spoke while prostrating before Sunan of Giri, and quickly the two of them withdrew while weeping.

        As for the history in Mendang after the disappearance of Aji Saka, Raden 
Putra Daniswara then became King in Mendang, confirmed by 
his grandfather Sang Yang T Ratu Sindulo, with his Patih Dipati Mangkurat. 
The two Waraganas firmly became his wives, Sangkaningrat and Mayangkara. 
He was given a title by his grandfather: Raden Putra Daniswara became Sang Prabu 
Kaskaya. His Patih was Jaka Jugulmuda, given the title Adipati 
Jugulmuda. Pun Beja and Daulat became the Judges, given the titles Raja 
[p.87.] Suryangalam (Daulat) and Raja Kapa2 (Beja). / Pacukilan was given the title 
Raja Nititelu, answering to Patih Jugulmuda. Then Adipati 
Jugulmuda was given a wife, the daughter of Adipati Tugur, her mother was named 
Larasati. At that time, Mendang was extremely lively, all the 
glory of the world was there. Nata Ratu Sindulo still watched over 
his grandson in Mendang with his Patih at the wedding of Dipati 
Jugulmuda. After Adipati Jugulmuda was married, Nata Sindulo 
and his Patih Dipati Mangkurat returned to Guwa Terusan to farm. 
The Land of Java was extremely prosperous during the reign of Sang Prabu Kaskaya; 
all the people in the Land of Java were joyful. Sang Prabu Kaskaya 
had children with the two Waraganas. Sangkaningrat had a son named Sri Mapunggung. 
Mayangkara had a daughter named Suprabawati, 
who was taken by her great-grandfather Ratu Sindulo and brought to 
Guwa Terusan, Dewi Suprabawati to her great-grandfather.
[p.88.]         As for the history after the passing of Sang Prabu Kaskaya, / 
he was succeeded by his son Mapunggung. Sri Mapunggung had a son Pancadriya. Pancadriya 
had a son Anglingdriya. Anglingdriya had a son Anglingdarma. Angling-
-darma had a son Anglingkusuma. Anglingkusuma had a son Gandakusuma. 
Gandakusuma had a son Pancakusuma. Pancakusuma had a son Ardikusuma. 
Ardikusuma had a son Tejakusuma. Tejakusuma had a son Jatikusuma. 
Jatikusuma had a son Citrakusuma. Citrakusuma had a son Sumawicitra. 
Sumawicitra had a son Resi Getayu. Finished, the ancient history of Ratu Sindula. 
Ghafarolahum bisawab ya Allah ya Rabi ya Ghofur3. Resi Getayu had a son 
Lembumiluhurhu, who became king in the country of Jenggala. Prabu Lembumiluhur had a son 
Dewakusuma. Dewakusuma had a son Raden Putra Mara Bangun. 
Marabangun had a son Panji Laleyan. Raden Panji Laleyan had a son 
Prabu Banjaransari, who was still reigning as King in Jenggala. Prabu 
Banjaransari was handsome, young, and still a bachelor. His name was Prabu Banjaran-
-sari.

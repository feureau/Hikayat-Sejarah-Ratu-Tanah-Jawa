However, if you request prophethood (*nurbuah*), you and your descendants are not permitted, only the Night of Power (*lailatul kodari*)." Then Yang Nurcahyo and his son Nuroso prostrated to the Lord of the Worlds. After both rose from their prostration, they were taken and vanished from the world to the intermediate realm by the Angel Jibrail (*peace be upon him*). After arriving in the intermediate realm, the future kingdom was named Ngondar-andir buwono for Yang Nurcahyo and his son Yang Nuraso. Then the Angel Jibrail (*peace be upon him*) vanished from the intermediate realm. Yang Nurcahyo and his son Yang Nurroso then prayed to God, the Sublime, the Unique, the Subduer, the Mighty and Majestic, for a palace and all the necessities. 
[p. 33.] The request of Yang Nurcahyo and his son Yang Nuroso was granted: the palace in Ngondar-andir buwono was named Junggring saloko, and the meeting hall (*pagelaran*) was named Bale si Mercu kundho. After that, Yang Nurcahyo sent Yang Nuroso to select all the *jinn* who had intensified their asceticism. Yang Nuroso's mission was blessed; he selected all the male and female *jinn* who had intensified their asceticism and brought them into the intermediate realm. Once all the male and female *jinn* who had intensified their asceticism were properly settled in the intermediate realm, Yang Nurcahyo gave them titles: the males became *dewa mambang* and the females *dewi mambang*. They were each assigned specific tasks by Sang Yang Nurcahyo. The one chosen by Sang Yang Nurcahyo to be the leader of the *dewa mambang* was Yang Mpu Ramadi, the leader of all the blacksmith *mambang*. Yang Siwah became the leader 
[p. 34.] of the goldsmith *mambang*. Yang Sakri became the leader of the scribe *mambang*. Yang Gono became the leader of all the porter *mambang*.

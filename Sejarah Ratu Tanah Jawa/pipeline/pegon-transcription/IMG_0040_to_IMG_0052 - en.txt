**(Page 79)**
...You shall remain here to guard the realm. Tomorrow, Raden Putra will become the ruler. Thus it was commanded, and all the warriors and giants (*ditya*) bowed respectfully. They were completely submissive. No one was left behind; all the giants and warriors were ordered to assemble in the kingdom, readying themselves as they were instructed. Everyone gathered without exception. Those who were previously dispersed now united as one body. Over time, the army of giants and men prepared for war.
When Ratu Sindula (King Sindula) gave the order, Prabu Jaka Mujika obeyed the divine command. For three years they prepared their forces. When they were finally ready, word spread through the forests and the western lands. Hearing this, the giants and the people of Medang were ordered to march out. They agreed and moved forward.

**(Page 80)**
There was a formidable force that faced them. All the giants were still attached to worldly desires. After some time, the giants turned back, and Bhairani (the terrifying spirit) advanced. Commands were issued by Ratu Sindula to all corners. They marched out to face the enemy, growing in strength and numbers. They were commanded to attack Medang. Because they were mighty and brave, they struck with great force.
Raden Putra and Jaka Mujika were unstoppable. They charged forward, their hearts resolute in their lineage. When they attacked, they destroyed everything in their path. Ratu Sindula advanced toward the skilled warriors. Prabu Jaka Mujika struck the enemies down, reducing their numbers. The two forces clashed. By the will of the Almighty, Prabu Jaka Mujika defeated them...

**(Page 81)**
Many people and soldiers were overwhelmed. On the roads, the people of Medang could not hold their ground. The forces of Medang were pushed back, seeking refuge behind their fortifications. Those who survived fled back to Medang. Seeing this, Prabu Jaka Mujika unleashed a divine power to overwhelm the enemy.
Prabu Jaka Mujika and his forces then struck simultaneously. The frontlines clashed fiercely, resulting in many falling on both sides. The soldiers of Medang fled. Raden Putra then advanced alongside Jaka Mujika toward the Kingdom of Medang. Those who survived the onslaught retreated in fear. After the Kingdom of Medang was breached, they engaged in a massive war. The forces of Medang could no longer hold out.

**(Page 82)**
Hearing the news that Raden Putra had become the Prime Minister (Patih), named Jaka Mujika, everyone realized his great power. They joined forces and submitted. It is said that they even paid respects to the Sultan of Rum, seeking his blessing. They were given the authority to rule over the lands of Java. All the people of Medang submitted entirely to Ratu Sindula. They surrendered their weapons and their forces.
When Jaka Mujika heard the news that the people of Medang had surrendered to them, they rejoiced. For three days, they discussed their victory and how to govern the western lands and the kingdom...

**(Page 83)**
...They saw that Prabu Jaka Mujika possessed extraordinary divine powers. Soon, the giant named Sangkala Ningrat arrived with his fearsome aura. He roared loudly and charged to attack Prabu Jaka Mujika. But Prabu Jaka Mujika stood firm, releasing his magical prowess. Sangkala Ningrat was struck and thrown into the ocean.
Prabu Jaka Mujika then turned his attention elsewhere. Raden Putra and Prabu Jaka Mujika met, and together they discussed their next steps. There was no longer any doubt in their minds. They had achieved absolute victory...

**(Page 84)**
All the forces fought against a Thousand Giants (*Ditya Sewu*). Jaka Mujika led the charge against the Thousand Giants, striking left and right. The Thousand Giants surrounded Jaka Mujika, but he fought fiercely, resulting in many casualties. The Thousand Giants were ultimately defeated and crushed.
Seeing the Thousand Giants defeated, Prabu Jaka Mujika unleashed his divine weapon from the sky. The power rained down upon the remaining giants, destroying them completely. The rest fled for their lives, realizing they could not win against such divine intervention.

**(Page 85)**
It is said that the Angel Azazil (Lucifer), cursed by Allah, approached Aji Saka. Aji Saka had been defeated by the decree of God, Allah Subhanahu wa Ta'ala, the One, the Supreme. It was destined that Aji Saka would become an Iblis (devil), cursed by God, because he was struck by the words of the noble Prophet Muhammad, the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him. *Tamat. Wallahu a'lam bish-shawab* (The end. And Allah knows best). O Allah, O Lord, the Forgiving.
As for the story in the land of Medang after the disappearance of Aji Saka, Raden Putra arrived in Medang. He was accepted by the people. Raden Putra was crowned King. His Prime Minister was Jaka Mujika. Thus, Jaka Mujika was given authority and the title of a mighty judge, Raden Surya Alam...

**(Page 86)**
The Angel Azazil, cursed by Allah, saw that Aji Saka had been defeated. He approached Aji Saka and said, "Since you have been defeated, you must not show your face to humans. Hide yourself in the unseen world. You and your followers must submit to the decree. Do not attempt to rule the world of men openly anymore." Thus, Aji Saka and his followers vanished into the realm of spirits (*siluman*), dwelling in the unseen realms across the lands of Java.

**(Page 87)**
*(Genealogy)* Here is the lineage of the kings: The son of Sri Pamungkas was Putra Kusuma. Putra Kusuma's son was Dewa Kusuma. Dewa Kusuma's son was Raden Putra. Raden Putra's son was Prabu Banjarsari. Prabu Banjarsari became the ruler of the Kingdom of Jenggala. Prabu Banjarsari was young but possessed great wisdom and power.

**(Page 88)**
Prabu Banjarsari appointed Jaka Mujika as his Prime Minister. The Kingdom of Jenggala became incredibly prosperous, famous throughout the world. The king and his prime minister ruled justly.
Now, the story shifts. Prabu Banjarsari had a child with his queen. They lived in great happiness. All the people of Java respected King Banjarsari of Jenggala.

**(Page 89)**
*(The Story of the Queen of the South / Ratu Kidul)*
At this time, there was the Kingdom of the South (*Kidul*). This kingdom was inhabited entirely by women (*wadon*). The Queen of the South commanded an army of female warriors. There were 80,000 of them, all incredibly powerful and feared. Among her chief commanders were Dewi Suradiwati, Wandawati, Mrawati, and Miyawati. They rode upon flying horses (*kuda sembrani*). Because of their terrifying power, no mortal could stand against them.

**(Page 90)**
In the land of Java, Ratu Kidul and her forces were greatly feared. To avoid mingling with ordinary humans, the Queen and her entire kingdom transformed themselves into invisible spirits (*siluman*). The Queen who ruled this spirit realm was Prabu Wanudya (The Queen of Women), also known as Ratu Suparawati. She commanded all the spirits and invisible beings in the Southern Ocean.

**(Page 91)**
Meanwhile, in the Kingdom of Jenggala, the gods descended from heaven to test the king. Prabu Banjarsari of Jenggala left his kingdom and wandered into the wilderness. He walked deep into the forests until he found a mystical cave. He entered the cave and fell into a deep, magical slumber.

**(Page 92)**
Back in the Southern Kingdom, Ratu Kidul's forces continued to patrol the invisible realms.
In the physical world, Prabu Banjarsari was still asleep in the cave. He was accompanied by his two loyal servants (*punakawan*), named Menwa and Menwi. They faithfully guarded their sleeping king.

**(Page 93)**
In a nearby village, there lived an elder named Ki Buyut Satima and his wife, Ni Satimi. They were searching for the missing king. Walking through the deep forest, they finally stumbled upon the cave. Inside, they saw Prabu Banjarsari sleeping soundly. Menwa and Menwi warned them not to wake the king, as he was in a deep spiritual meditation. Ki Satima and Ni Satimi sat quietly, crying tears of joy that they had found their lord, yet filled with sorrow because he would not wake.

**(Page 94)**
Menwa and Menwi explained that the king had left Jenggala and entered this slumber. The entire Kingdom of Jenggala was in chaos and mourning because their king had disappeared. The people of Java were lost without him.

**(Page 95)**
In the village of Bojong Lumangha, Ki Buyut Satima was highly respected. He was wealthy and wise. He had told his wife, Ni Satimi, that he was willing to sacrifice his life to find the king, for without the king, the world was out of balance.

**(Page 96)**
Ki Satima and Ni Satimi had sworn that they would not return home unless they found the king. They were willing to die in the forest. Now that they had found him in the cave, they wept at his feet, praying for him to awaken.

**(Page 97)**
By divine intervention and the blessings of the ancestors, Prabu Banjarsari finally awoke from his slumber. He saw Ki Satima and Ni Satimi bowing before him. He asked them gently, "Why are you here, and why are you weeping?"

**(Page 98)**
Ki Satima and Ni Satimi paid their highest respects and answered, "O Great King, the entire kingdom is in despair. We have come to beg you to return to Jenggala. The people are lost without their lord."

**(Page 99)**
The king smiled and said, "Do not weep, Satima. I know the kingdom awaits me. But my journey here in the forest is not yet complete. I have a destiny to fulfill in this southern land before I return." He commanded Satima and Satimi to gather the village leaders and wait for his instructions.

**(Page 100)**
Ki Satima and Ni Satimi agreed and left to gather the people.
Meanwhile, in the invisible Kingdom of the South, the Queen of Women (Prabu Wanudya/Ratu Kidul) had a vivid dream about a remarkably handsome man wandering in her domain. She woke up and immediately ordered her fiercest commander, Dewi Suradiwati, to search the southern lands for the man in her dream.

**(Page 101)**
Dewi Suradiwati bowed and said, "Do not worry, my Queen. I will find this man and bring him to you." She set off with her spirit soldiers.
At the same time, Prabu Banjarsari, accompanied by Menwa and Menwi, was walking through the forest and unknowingly entered the sacred, forbidden garden and bathing pool belonging to the female spirits of the South.

**(Page 102)**
The female guards saw them and immediately reported to the Queen, "Your Majesty, a human man has entered the forbidden garden!" Ratu Kidul was both angry and curious. Dewi Suradiwati went to confront the intruder. When she saw Prabu Banjarsari, she was stunned by his incredible handsomeness.

**(Page 103)**
Dewi Suradiwati and the female guards surrounded the king. "Who are you, and how dare you enter the forbidden garden of the Queen?" they demanded, drawing their weapons. Prabu Banjarsari remained calm, completely unfazed by their threats. The women, realizing he was no ordinary human, found their anger melting into awe.

**(Page 104)**
The guards told him to bathe in the pool and prepare himself to meet the Queen. Dewi Suradiwati reported back to the Queen, confirming that the man was exactly as he appeared in her dream. Prabu Banjarsari bathed and dressed in magnificent garments, looking completely divine as he prepared to meet the Queen of the South.
